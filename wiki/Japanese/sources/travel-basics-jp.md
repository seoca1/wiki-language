## Cultural Insights

- **Japan Rail Pass**: Tourist pass for unlimited JR trains (7/14/21 days)
- **Tipping**: Not customary in Japan; service is included in price
- **Shoes**: Remove shoes at ryokan, traditional inns, some temples
- **Bowing**: Standard greeting; depth/duration conveys respect
- **Quiet public spaces**: Trains, subways are quiet; talking on phone discouraged

## Notes

- 5-language matrix format enables easy cross-language comparison
- Japanese travel vocabulary shares Sino-Korean roots (空港 = 空港 in Chinese, 空港 in Korean)
- Many loanwords in JP travel: ホテル (EN), パスポート (EN), チケット (EN)
- Native 和語 terms: 切符 (kippu = ticket), 改札 (kaisatsu = ticket gate)
