# Japanese Modern Life: Konbini and Mobile

**Overview:** Modern Japanese life revolves around convenience stores (konbini) and mobile technology — quality, speed, and 24/7 availability.

## Key Points

- **Konbini (コンビニ)**: Convenience stores — 7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart dominate. Services:
  - 24/7 operation
  - ATM (bank, post office, international)
  - Bill payment (utilities, taxes)
  - Ticket purchase (concerts, sports, transport)
  - Package shipping (Yamato, Sagawa)
  - Photo printing, copy/scan
  - Clean restrooms (almost always)
  - High-quality food: onigiri, sandwiches, bento, hot snacks
  - Magazine corner, manga, weekly magazines
- **Mobile Technology**:
  - `ガラケー (garake)` — Feature phone (older)
  - `スマホ (sumaho)` — Smartphone (loanword from English "smart phone")
  - `iPhone` dominant (60%+) + Android (Samsung, Sony, Sharp, Google)
- **Communication Apps**:
  - `LINE` — Dominant messenger (>90% market share) — voice/video calls, timeline, payments
  - `Twitter/X` — Popular, especially among younger
  - `Instagram` — Food, fashion, travel photos
  - `TikTok` — Youth culture
- **Mobile Payments (モバイル決済)**:
  - `PayPay` (SoftBank/Yahoo) — #1 by users
  - `LINE Pay` — integrated
  - `Rakuten Pay` — tied to Rakuten ecosystem
  - `Suica/Pasmo` — Transit IC cards
  - Cash still common for vending machines, small shops
- **Delivery**:
  - `出前館 (Demae-can)` — Food delivery
  - `Uber Eats`, `menu`, `Wolt`
  - `Uber Eats Japan` — strong growth
- **Vending Machines (自動販売機, jidōhanbaiki)**: ~5 million in Japan — highest density globally. Drinks, food, even umbrellas.

## Language Connections

- `コンビニ` (konbini) — Convenience store
- `スマホ` (sumaho) — Smartphone
- `LINE` — Messenger
- `PayPay` — Mobile payment
- `自動販売機` (jidōhanbaiki) — Vending machine
- `出前` (demae) — Delivery
- `Suica` — Transit IC card
