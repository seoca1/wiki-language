# Japanese Food Culture

**Type:** cuisine, social-ritual, regional
**Region:** Japan (本州, 北海道, 九州, 四国, 沖縄)

## Overview

Japanese cuisine (和食, washoku) is recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage (2013). Core values include **seasonality (旬, shun)**, **freshness**, **visual presentation (盛り付け, moritsuke)**, and **balance of five flavors and five colors**. Meals are about nourishment, aesthetics, and social harmony.

## Core Principles

### 旬 (Shun) — Seasonality

The most fundamental concept. Ingredients are at peak quality and flavor during their specific season. Eating in season is a fundamental value.

| Season | Months | Key Ingredients |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **春 (Spring)** | 3-5 | 桜 (cherry), 筍 (bamboo), 鰆 (Spanish mackerel), 蛤 (clam) |
| **夏 (Summer)** | 6-8 | 鮎 (ayu), 鱧 (pike conger), 茄子 (eggplant), 素麺 (cold noodles) |
| **秋 (Autumn)** | 9-11 | 松茸 (matsutake), 銀杏 (ginkgo), 秋刀魚 (saury), 栗 (chestnut) |
| **冬 (Winter)** | 12-2 | 蟹 (crab), 河豚 (fugu), 鰤 (yellowtail), 大根 (daikon) |

### 5 Flavors (五味, Gomi)

| Flavor | Japanese | Examples |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Sweet (甘)** | 甘 (kan/ama) | Mirin, sugar, 照り焼き (teriyaki) |
| **Salty (鹹)** | 塩 (shio) | Soy sauce, miso, salt-cured |
| **Sour (酸)** | 酸 (san/sui) | 梅 (umeboshi), 酢 (vinegar), 柑橘 (citrus) |
| **Bitter (苦)** | 苦 (ku) | ゴーヤ (bitter melon), 抹茶 (matcha) |
| **Umami (鮮)** | 旨 (umi) | Dashi, soy sauce, miso, mushrooms |

### 5 Colors (五色, Goshiki)

| Color | Japanese | Examples |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Red (赤)** | 赤 (aka) | 鮪 (tuna), 梅 (plum), 人参 (carrot) |
| **Green (緑)** | 緑 (midori) | ほうれん草 (spinach), 葱 (scallion) |
| **Yellow (黄)** | 黄 (ki) | 卵 (egg), 銀杏 (ginkgo), 栗 (chestnut) |

### 5 Cooking Methods (五法, Gohou)

1. **生 (Nama)**: Raw (刺身 sashimi)
2. **煮 (Ni)**: Simmer/boil (煮物 nimono)
3. **焼 (Yaku)**: Grill (焼き物 yakimono)
4. **蒸 (Mushi)**: Steam (蒸し物 mushimono)
5. **揚 (Ageru)**: Deep-fry (揚げ物 agemono)

## Meal Structure

### Traditional Ichiju-Sansai (一汁三菜)

"One soup, three sides" — the traditional meal structure:
- **主食 (Shushoku)**: Staple (rice or noodles)
- **汁 (Shiru)**: Soup (miso or suimono)
- **主菜 (Shusai)**: Main protein (fish, meat, tofu)
- **副菜 (Fukusai)**: Side vegetables (1-2)
- **漬物 (Tsukemono)**: Pickles
- **果物 (Kudamono)**: Fruit (dessert)

### Modern Meals

- **朝食 (Breakfast)**: Rice + miso + egg + fish
- **昼食 (Lunch)**: Bento, noodle bowl, set meal
- **夕食 (Dinner)**: Family meal (most important)
- **夜食 (Yashoku)**: Late night snack (light)

## Eating Etiquette

### Chopsticks (箸, Hashi)

- **立てて (tatete)**: NEVER stick chopsticks upright in rice (resembles funeral incense)
- **渡し箸 (watashibashi)**: Pass food chopstick-to-chopstick (also funeral ritual)
- **さし箸 (sashibashi)**: Point with chopsticks (rude)
- **迷い箸 (mayoibashi)**: Hover chopsticks over dishes (rude)
- **ねぶり箸 (neburi-bashi)**: Lick chopsticks (very rude)
- **はしおき (hashioki)**: Rest chopsticks on the chopstick rest

### Slurping

- **OK for noodles** (ramen, soba, udon) — sign of appreciation
- **Not for other dishes**
- **Cooling hot food** — slurping is functional

### Saying "Itadakimasu" (いただきます)

- Said before eating
- Literally "I humbly receive"
- Gratitude to the chef, the ingredients, the cook
- Often said by all diners together

### Saying "Gochisousama" (ごちそうさま)

- Said after eating
- "Thank you for the meal"
- **Gochisousama deshita** (more polite past tense)
- Sometimes said when leaving restaurant (to chef)

### Tipping

- **NO TIPPING** — included or not done
- Attempting to tip can be offensive
- High-end restaurants: 10-15% service charge (not required)

### Itamae (板前) Etiquette

- **Say "omakase" (お任せ)** — "Chef's choice" — let chef decide
- **Don't make special requests** (especially in high-end sushi)
- **Eat promptly** — chef times dishes
- **No perfume** — interferes with sushi/sashimi aroma
- **No flash photography** — disruptive
- **Finish all rice** — respect

## Cultural Concepts

### Mottainai (もったいない)

- "What a waste" — environmental/philosophical value
- Encourages finishing food, minimalism
- Affects cooking (no waste), serving (right portions)

### Ichigo Ichie (一期一会)

- "One time, one meeting" — tea ceremony concept
- Each meal/meeting is unique
- Be present, appreciate the moment

### Wa (和) — Harmony

- Group meals = harmony maintenance
- Pouring for others before self
- Eating at same pace as group

## 例文

「いただきます」(itadakimasu) は食事を始める前の感謝の挨拶で、料理人・食材への敬意を表す。
「お好み焼き」(okonomiyaki) は関西風と広島風で作り方が異なる地域食。
「弁当」(bentō) は携帯食文化の象徴で、季節やキャラクター弁当が広く流通。
