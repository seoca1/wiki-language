# Japanese School Culture

**Type:** culture, education, social-norms
**Region:** Japan (with East Asian context)

## Overview

Japanese school culture is characterized by uniforms, strict discipline, club activities (部活), and hierarchical social structures. The school year runs April–March, and entrance ceremonies (入学式) and graduation ceremonies (卒業式) are major life events. This page complements `[[japanese-dating-culture]]`, `[[japanese-food-culture]]`, and `[[japanese-holiday-culture]]`.

## School Uniforms (制服)

### Standard Uniforms

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Gakuran (学生服)** | Male: Black high-collared jacket, matching trousers |
| **Sērā-fuku (セーラー服)** | Female: Navy sailor top + pleated skirt |
| **Bōton (ボタン)** | Metal buttons (gakuran) |
| **Beru-betto (ベルベット)** | Velvet collar (gakuran) |
| **Kyatansā (キャットサ)** | Cat-ear hoods (winter) |

### Variations

- **Elem/Middle school**: Standard uniforms (above)
- **High school**: Standard uniforms + variations (ties, badges)
- **Blazer style**: Some modern schools (especially private)
- **Plaid skirts**: Catholic school tradition
- **Sailor uniform**: Most common for girls
- **Gakuran**: Most common for boys

### Cultural Notes

- **制服 (Seifuku)**: Mandatory in ~95% of Japanese schools
- **私服 (Shifuku)**: Free dress (rare, usually alt. schools)
- **Brand restrictions**: Many schools ban dyed hair, accessories
- **Skirt length rules**: Common (especially middle school)
- **Hair color rules**: Often "natural black" only
- **Piercings/tattoos**: Usually forbidden
- **"Black School"**: Strict dress code schools (controversial)

## Classroom Culture

### Hierarchy and Address

| Address | Japanese | Used For |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **~さん** | san | Polite (default) |
| **~くん** | kun | Boys, juniors |
| **~ちゃん** | chan | Girls, children |
| **~先生** | sensei | Teachers |
| **~先輩** | senpai | Senior |
| **~後輩** | kōhai | Junior |

### Class Structure

- **担任 (Tannin)** = Homeroom teacher (one main teacher)
- **Class number system**: 1年1組 = Class 1-A (学年 gaku-nen, 組 kumi)
- **Students stay together** = Same class all day (no rotation)
- **鞋箱 (Shoe locker)**: Indoor shoes (上靴 uwabaki) vs outdoor (外靴 sotogutu)
- **Class president (委員長 iinchō)**: Elected student leader

### Speaking Up in Class

- **挙手 (Kyoshu)**: Raising hand (sometimes required)
- **はい (Hai)**: "Yes" (acknowledgment)
- **指名 (Shimei)**: Called on by teacher
- **起立 (Kiritsu)**: Stand up to answer
- **着席 (Chakuseki)**: Sit down (after answer)

## After-School Club Activities (部活)

### Types of 部活 (Bukatsu)

| Type | Examples | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Sports** (運動部) | Baseball, Soccer, Basketball, Volleyball, Track | Very common |
| **Cultural** (文化部) | Tea ceremony, Calligraphy, Art, Music | Less competitive |
| **Academic** (学術部) | Science, Math, English debate | Less common |

### Major Sports 部活

- **Baseball (野球)**: National obsession (especially 甲子園)
- **Soccer (サッカー)**: Very popular
- **Basketball (バスケ)**: Common
- **Volleyball (バレー)**: Common
- **Track and Field (陸上部)**: Common
- **Tennis (テニス)**: Both schools and clubs
- **Kendo (剣道)**: Traditional martial art
- **Judo (柔道)**: Olympic sport, popular
- **Swimming (水泳)**: Common

### 部活 Culture

- **Mandatory** at most schools (especially middle school)
- **Long hours**: 16:00-19:00 typical (post-practice)
- **Holidays reduced**: Often train even on weekends
- **3年生引退 (Sanka Intō)**: 3rd years retire early (to study for exams)
- **OB/OG (Old Boys/Old Girls)**: Alumni network
- **Vertical hierarchy**: Strong senpai/kōhai system

### 体育祭 (Taiikusai) — Sports Day

- **Annual sports event** in October
- **Class vs class competition** (紅組 vs 白組, Red vs White)
- **Events**: Relay race (リレー), Cavalry battle (騎馬戦), Tug of war (綱引き)
- **Family attendance** encouraged
- **Sakura wars**: Famous anime setting
- **Lunch**: バザー (bazaar) often concurrent

### 文化祭 (Bunkasai) — Cultural Festival

- **Annual cultural event** in October
- **Class projects**: Each class prepares a stall or show
- **Café (喫茶店 kissaten)**: Class-run cafés
- **Haunted house**: Popular for older classes
- **Plays/dramas**: Cultural performances
- **Cosplay (costume play)**: Common
- **Open to community**: Public event

### 修学旅行 (Shūgaku Ryokō) — School Trip

- **Multi-day trip** (usually 2-3 nights)
- **High school**: Often Kyoto/Tokyo (or overseas!)
- **Middle school**: Often historic sites (Kyoto, Nara, etc.)
- **Elementary school**: Often nature destinations
- **Hotel stays**: Cultural exchange component
- **Buddhist/Temple visits**: Cultural component
- **High school overseas**: Hawaii, Guam, Taiwan (popular)
- **Long bus rides**: Memorable bonding experience

## High School Details

### Daily Life

- **登下校 (Tōgekō)**: Going to/from school
- **制服 (Seifuku)**: Uniforms (mandatory)
- **頭髪 (Tōhatsu)**: Hair regulations
- **靴 (Kutsu)**: Indoor shoes (uwabaki 上靴)
- **下駄箱 (Getabako)**: Shoe locker

- **購買部 (Kōbaibu)**: School store
- **職員室 (Shokuinshitsu)**: Teachers' office
- **保健室 (Hokenshitsu)**: Nurse's office (infirmary)

### School Rules (校則 Kōsoku)

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **靴下の色 (Kutsushita no iro)** | Sock color regulations |
| **スカートの丈 (Sukāto no take)** | Skirt length rules |
| **靴下の長さ (Kutsushita no nagasa)** | Sock length |
| **下着 (Shitagi)** | Underwear color (often white only) |
| **髪 (Kami)** | Hair color (often black only) |
| **マニキュア (Manyukua)** | Nail polish forbidden |
| **ピアス (Piasu)** | Piercings forbidden |
| **スマホ (Sumaho)** | Smartphone rules (often prohibited in class) |
| **ゲーム (Gēmu)** | Game restrictions |

### Discipline and Bullying

- **いじめ (Ijime)**: Bullying (major issue)
  - Reported ~600,000 cases/year
  - Often physical, social exclusion, cyberbullying
  - School sometimes ignores or covers up
- **体罰 (Taibatsu)**: Corporal punishment
  - Officially banned but still occurs
  - Sports clubs particularly known
- **School non-disclosure**: Tradition of not reporting issues

## Bullying (いじめ Ijime) — Major Issue

### Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Physical (物理)** | Hitting, kicking, tripping |
| **Verbal (言葉)** | Insults, teasing, name-calling |
| **Social (仲間外れ)** | Exclusion from group activities |
| **Cyber (サイバー)** | Online harassment, social media |
| **Relational (関係)** | Spreading rumors, manipulating relationships |
| **Property (物的)** | Stealing/damaging belongings |

### Cultural Background

- **Group harmony (wa)**: Group takes priority over individual
- **Seniority system**: Older students have power over younger
- **Silent bullying**: Often not reported (cultural pressure)
- **Teacher involvement**: Sometimes teachers are bullies
- **Suicide rates**: School bullying → student suicide (major concern)
- **Class action lawsuits**: Growing legal pressure

### Anti-bullying Measures

- **School counselor (カウンセラー)**: Mandatory in many schools
- **定期調査 (Teiki Chōsa)**: Regular surveys
- **Anonymous reporting boxes**
- **Phone hotlines** (24-hour, child-focused)
- **Stop Bullying Action**: 2013 legislation

## Classroom Dynamics

### Question-Asking Culture

- **挙手 (Kyoshu)**: Raise hand (sometimes required)
- **当てられる (Aterareru)**: Being called on (stressful!)
- **指名 (Shimei)**: Teacher assigns question
- **Yes/No questions**: Hard to say "No" to teacher
- **Unknown word (わかりません)**: Polite way to say "I don't know"

### Student Behaviors

| Behavior | Cultural Norm |
|----------|----------------|
| **Speak up voluntarily** | Rare (especially MS) |
| **Wait to be called** | Expected |
| **Disagree with teacher** | Very rare |
| **Stay silent in class** | Common |
| **Stand when answering** | Expected (especially MS) |
| **Walk in hallway quickly** | Expected (さっさと sassato) |

## Test Culture (受験 Juken)

### Center Shiken / 共通テスト Day

- **Time**: Mid-January (cold weather)
- **Breakfast**: 弁当 (bento) + 元気ドリンク
- **Clothing**: Winter clothes (cold morning)
- **Transportation**: Parents drop off, buses
- **Praying at shrines**: Common pre-test
- **Lucky charms (お守り omamori)**: Carried
- **Post-test**: Hang out with friends

### 偏差値 (Hensachi) — Deviation Score

- **Standard score**: Average = 50, SD = 10
- **Higher = better**: 70+ = top tier
- **Common confusion**: 65 ≠ "65%" (not a percentile)
- **Per subject**: Math, Japanese, English, Science, Social
- **Combined average**: For overall school evaluation

### Result Day (発表日 Happōbi)

- **Designated release date** (varies by exam)
- **Phone lines crash** with inquiries
- **合格 (Gōkaku) / 不合格 (Fugōkaku)**
- **Family celebration/commiseration**

## Cultural Sensitivity Notes

### Do

✅ **Bow slightly** when greeting teachers
✅ **Say "いただきます" (Itadakimasu)** before meals
✅ **Remove shoes** in certain areas (especially in school)
✅ **Address seniors with -san** minimum
✅ **Try to eat school lunch** (給仕 kyūshoku)
✅ **Participate in ceremonies** respectfully
✅ **Learn proper bowing** for apology

### Don't

❌ **Stand out negatively** (目立つ) — group harmony
❌ **Speak casually to teachers** (use 先生 with です/ます)
❌ **Be late** — strict punctuality
❌ **Disrespect senpai** (先輩) directly
❌ **Dye hair** (often against rules)
❌ **Wear prohibited items** (jewelry, etc.)

## 例文
