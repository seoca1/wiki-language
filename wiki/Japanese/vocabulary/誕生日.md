# 誕生日

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Definition:** Birthday.

**Romaji:** tanjyo-bi

---

## Examples

- **誕生日おめでとう!** — Happy birthday!
- **誕生日はいつですか?** — When is your birthday?
- **誕生日パーティーをします。** — I have a birthday party.
