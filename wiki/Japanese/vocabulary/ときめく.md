# ときめく

**Part of Speech:** 動詞 (verb)

**Level:** B1

**Definition:** ときめく (tokimeku) — to flutter (heart).

**Romaji:** tokimeku

---

## Examples

- **胸ときめく** — heart flutters
- **真的会ときめく** — really flutter
