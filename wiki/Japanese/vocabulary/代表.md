# 代表

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** だいひょう (daihyou) — representative.

**Romaji:** daihyou

---

## Examples

- **公司在的代表** — company representative
- **品牌代表** — brand representative
