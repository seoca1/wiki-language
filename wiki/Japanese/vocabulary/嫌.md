# 嫌

**Part of Speech:** 形容詞 (adjective)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** いや (iya) — unpleasant/disliked.

**Romaji:** iya

---

## Examples

- **嫌な気分** — unpleasant feeling
- **真的很嫌** — I really dislike it
