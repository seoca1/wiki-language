# 発表

**Part of Speech:** 名詞/動詞 (noun/verb)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** はっぴょう (happyou) — presentation/announcement.

**Romaji:** happyou

---

## Examples

- **発表します。** — I will present.
- **卒業発表** — graduation announcement
- **データ発表** — data presentation
