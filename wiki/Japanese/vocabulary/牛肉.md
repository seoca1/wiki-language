# 牛肉

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** ぎゅうにく (gyuuniku) — beef.

**Romaji:** gyuuniku

---

## Examples

- **牛肉を食べます。** — I eat beef.
- **焼き肉** — grilled meat ( yakiniku )
- **和牛** — Wagyu beef
