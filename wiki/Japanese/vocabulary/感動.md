# 感動

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** B1

**Definition:** かんどう (kandou) — deep emotion/touched.

**Romaji:** kandou

---

## Examples

- **感動しました。** — I was deeply moved.
- **真的很感動** — really touching
