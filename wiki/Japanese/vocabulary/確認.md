# 確認

**Part of Speech:** 名詞/動詞 (noun/verb)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** かくにん (kakunin) — confirmation.

**Romaji:** kakunin

---

## Examples

- **確認します。** — I will confirm.
- **ご確認** — please confirm (polite)
