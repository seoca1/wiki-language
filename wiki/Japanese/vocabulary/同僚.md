# 同僚

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** どうりょう (douryou) — colleague/coworker.

**Romaji:** douryou

---

## Examples

- **同僚と話します。** — I talk with my colleague.
- **同僚関係** — collegial relationship
