# 怖い

**Part of Speech:** 形容詞 (adjective)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** こわいい (kowai) — scary/afraid.

**Romaji:** kowai

---

## Examples

- **怖い映画** — scary movie
- **我真的很怖い** — I'm really scared
