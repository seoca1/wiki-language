# 鶏肉

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** とりにく (toriniku) — chicken meat.

**Romaji:** toriniku

---

## Examples

- **鶏肉が好きです。** — I like chicken.
- **唐揚げ** — fried chicken (karaage)
- **焼き鳥** — grilled chicken (yakitori)
