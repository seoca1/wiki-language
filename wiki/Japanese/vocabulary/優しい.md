# 優しい

**Part of Speech:** 形容詞 (adjective)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** やさしい (yasashii) — kind/gentle.

**Romaji:** yasashii

---

## Examples

- **優しい人啊。** — He is a kind person.
- **本当に優しい** — really kind
