# 契約

**Part of Speech:** 名詞/動詞 (noun/verb)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** けいやく (keiyaku) — contract.

**Romaji:** keiyaku

---

## Examples

- **契約を結びます。** — We conclude a contract.
- **雇用契約** — employment contract
