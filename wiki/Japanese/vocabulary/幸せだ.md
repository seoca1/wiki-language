# 幸せだ

**Part of Speech:** 形容動詞 (na-adjective)

**Level:** A1

**Definition:** しあわせだ (shiawase da) — happy.

**Romaji:** shiawase da

---

## Examples

- **幸せです。** — I am happy.
- **真的很幸せ** — really happy
