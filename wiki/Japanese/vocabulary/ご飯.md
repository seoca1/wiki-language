# ご飯

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A1

**Definition:** ごはん (gohan) — rice/meal.

**Romaji:** gohan

---

## Examples

- **ご飯を食べます。** — I eat rice.
- **朝ごはん** — breakfast (asagohan)
- **お昼ごはん** — lunch (hirugohan)
- **晩ごはん** — dinner (bangohan)
