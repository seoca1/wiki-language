# 黒い靴

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** くろいくつ (kuroi kutsu) — black shoes.

**Romaji:** kuroi kutsu

---

## Examples

- **黒い靴を履きます。** — I wear black shoes.
- **本当に黒い靴** — really black shoes
