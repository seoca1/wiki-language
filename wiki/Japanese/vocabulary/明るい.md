# 明るい

**Part of Speech:** 形容詞 (adjective)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** あかるい (akarui) — bright/lively.

**Romaji:** akarui

---

## Examples

- **明るい部屋** — bright room
- **真的很明るい** — really bright
