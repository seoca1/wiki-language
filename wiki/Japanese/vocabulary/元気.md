# 元気

**Part of Speech:** 形容動詞 (na-adjective)

**Level:** A1

**Definition:** げんき (genki) — lively/healthy.

**Romaji:** genki

---

## Examples

- **元気ですか？** — Are you well?
- **真的很元気** — really lively
