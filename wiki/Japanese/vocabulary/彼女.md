# 彼女

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun) / 代名詞 (pronoun, 3rd person)
**Level:** N4

**Definition:** 여자친구 / 그녀 — Girlfriend; she.

**読み方:** かのじょ (kanojo)

## Examples (Girlfriend)

- 「彼女がいる」 — I have a girlfriend
- 「彼女にしたい」 — Want to make (her) my girlfriend
- 「彼女と公園」 — With my girlfriend, at the park

## Examples (Pronoun "she")

- 「彼女は学生です」 — She is a student
- 「彼女は何が好き?」 — What does she like?

## Related Terms

- [[彼氏]] — boyfriend
- [[恋人]] — lover (formal)
- [[付き合う]] — to date

## Cultural Notes

**주의**: 문맥에 따라 "girlfriend" (연인) vs "she" (대명사).
혼동 주의.

**명확히**:
- 私の彼女 — my girlfriend
- 彼女 (그녀) — she
