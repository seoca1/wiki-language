# 卵

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** たまご (tamago) — egg.

**Romaji:** tamago

---

## Examples

- **卵を食べます。** — I eat eggs.
- **温泉卵** — hot spring egg (onsen tamago)
- **卵焼き** — Japanese omelette (tamagoyaki)
