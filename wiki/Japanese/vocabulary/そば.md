# そば

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** そば (soba) — soba noodles.

**Romaji:** soba

---

## Examples

- **蕎麦を食べます。** — I eat soba.
- **温もり** — hot soba (atsumori)
- **もり** — cold soba with dipping sauce (mori)
