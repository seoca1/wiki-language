# 朝食

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A1

**Definition:** ちょうしょく (choushoku) — breakfast.

**Romaji:** choushoku

---

## Examples

- **朝食を食べます。** — I eat breakfast.
- **朝ごはん** — breakfast (casual)
- **朝食付き** — breakfast included
