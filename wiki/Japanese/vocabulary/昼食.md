# 昼食

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A1

**Definition:** ちゅうしょく (chuushoku) — lunch.

**Romaji:** chuushoku

---

## Examples

- **昼食を食べます。** — I eat lunch.
- **お昼ごはん** — lunch (casual)
- **昼休み** — lunch break
