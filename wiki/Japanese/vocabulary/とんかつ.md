# とんかつ

**Part of Speech:** 名詞 (noun)

**Level:** A2

**Definition:** とんかつ (tonkatsu) — tonkatsu (breaded pork cutlet).

**Romaji:** tonkatsu

---

## Examples

- **とんかつを食べます。** — I eat tonkatsu.
- **カツ丼** — tonkatsu rice bowl (katsudon)
