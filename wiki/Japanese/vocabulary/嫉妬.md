# 嫉妬

**Part of Speech:** 名詞/動詞 (noun/verb)

**Level:** B1

**Definition:** しっと (shitto) — jealousy.

**Romaji:** shitto

---

## Examples

- **嫉妬します。** — I am jealous.
- **真的太嫉妬** — really jealous
