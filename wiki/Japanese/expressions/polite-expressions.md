# Expressions: Polite & Honorific (日本語)

> **Theme:** Politeness & Honorifics
> **Level:** A1-C1
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Japanese politeness — keigo (敬語) system, honorifics, business etiquette.

---

## ありがとうございます

**Part of Speech:** expression (gratitude)
**Definition:** "Thank you very much" — most common gratitude.
**Form:** Arigatou gozaimasu.

### Variations

- **ありがとうございます (Arigatou gozaimasu)** — "Thank you" (standard)
- **ありがとうございました (Arigatou gozaimashita)** — "Thank you" (past)
- **どうも (Dōmo)** — "Thanks" (casual)
- **ありがとうございます、心から (Arigatou gozaimasu, kokoro kara)** — "Thank you, from my heart"

### Cultural Notes

- **Frequency**: Japanese use frequently
- **Levels**: ありがとう (casual) → ありがとうございます (standard) → どうもありがとうございました (formal past)
- **Response**: どういたしまして (dō itashimashite = "You're welcome")
- **Service**: Always expected in service industry
- **Apology alternative**: すみません (sumimasen) can also express thanks

## お願いします

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Please" — polite request.
**Form:** Onegaishimasu.

### Variations

- **お願いします (Onegaishimasu)** — "Please"
- **お願いします (お願いします)** — Same
- **___をお願いします (___ o onegai shimasu)** — "___ please"
- **よろしくお願いいたします (Yoroshiku onegai shimasu)** — "Please treat me well"

### Cultural Notes

- **万能表現**: One of the most versatile phrases
- **用途**: Requests, gratitude, greeting, goodbye
- **謙譲語 (kenjōgo)**: Humble form of 頼む (tanomu = "to ask")
- **Business**: Standard closing/request expression
- **Universal**: All ages, all contexts

## すみません

**Part of Speech:** expression (apology/attention)
**Definition:** "Excuse me / I'm sorry" — multiple uses.
**Form:** Sumimasen.

### Variations

- **すみません (Sumimasen)** — "Excuse me / Sorry" (most common)
- **すみませんでした (Sumimasen deshita)** — "I'm sorry" (past)
- **すいません (Suimasen)** — Casual version

### Cultural Notes

- **万能**: Excuse me, sorry, attention-getter, thank you
- **軽い謝罪**: Minor apologies (not for major mistakes)
- **重い謝罪**: 申し訳ございません (mōshiwake gozaimasen)
- **呼びかけ**: "Excuse me?" to strangers
- **感謝**: Used as "thanks" (less formal than ありがとうございます)

## ごめんなさい

**Part of Speech:** expression (apology)
**Definition:** "I'm sorry" — apology.
**Form:** Gomen nasai.

### Variations

- **ごめんなさい (Gomen nasai)** — "Sorry"
- **ごめん (Gomen)** — "Sorry" (casual)
- **ごめんなさい m(_ _)m** — "Sorry" (with emoji apology)

### Cultural Notes

- **Casual apology**: Less formal than すみません
- **Family/friends**: Common between close people
- **Business**: Avoid (too casual)
- **「ごめん」very casual**: Only close friends/family
- **Variants**: ごめんね (gomen ne — softer), ごめんよ (gomen yo — rougher)

## どういたしまして

**Part of Speech:** expression (response)
**Definition:** "You're welcome" — response to thanks.
**Form:** Dō itashimashite.

### Variations

- **どういたしまして (Dō itashimashite)** — "You're welcome" (standard)
- **いいえ、どういたしまして (Iie, dō itashimashite)** — "No, you're welcome"
- **全然大丈夫です (Zenzen daijōbu desu)** — "It's nothing"

### Cultural Notes

- **謙遜 (Kenson)**: Japanese modesty — don't fully accept thanks
- **Casual**: いいえ (Iie = "No") or ううん (Uun)
- **Frequency**: Common (but less than English "you're welcome")
- **Often no response**: Just nod, smile
- **Business**: Always respond formally

## ___してもいいですか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (permission)
**Definition:** "May I ___?" — polite permission request.
**Form:** ___ shite mo ii desu ka.

### Variations

- **___してもいいですか? (___ shite mo ii desu ka?)** — "May I ___?"
- **___してもよろしいでしょうか? (___ shite mo yoroshii deshō ka?)** — "May I ___?" (more polite)
- **___していただいてもよろしいですか? (___ shite itadaite mo yoroshii desu ka?)** — "May I ___?" (very polite)

### Cultural Notes

- **3 politeness levels**: いい (casual) → よろしい (polite) → いただく (humble)
- **Negative permission**: ___ しなくてもいい (shinakutemo ii = "don't have to ___")
- **Permission refusal**: ___ しないでください (shinaide kudasai)
- **Verb conjugation**: て-form + もいい
- **Workplace**: Always use いただく (humble form)

## お世話になっております

**Part of Speech:** expression (gratitude/humility)
**Definition:** "Thank you for your continued support" — business greeting.
**Form:** O-sewa ni natte orimasu.

### Variations

- **お世話になっております (O-sewa ni natte orimasu)** — "Thank you for your support"
- **いつもお世話になっております (Itsumo o-sewa ni natte orimasu)** — "Always thank you for your support"
- **お世話になりました (O-sewa ni narimashita)** — "Thank you for your support" (past/ending)

### Cultural Notes

- **Business standard**: Used in every business email/greeting
- **謙譲語 (Humble form)**: お世話 = support, になっております = be receiving
- **Frequency**: Almost every business interaction
- **Ending**: Past form (お世話になりました) when ending relationship
- **Translation**: "Thank you for taking care of me"

- `[[business-basics]]`

## お疲れ様でした

**Part of Speech:** expression (acknowledgment)
**Definition:** "Thank you for your hard work" — closing.
**Form:** O-tsukaresama deshita.

### Variations

- **お疲れ様でした (O-tsukaresama deshita)** — "Thank you for your hard work" (past)
- **お疲れ様です (O-tsukaresama desu)** — "Thank you for your hard work"
- **ご苦労様 (Gokurōsama)** — "Good work" (superior to subordinate)

### Cultural Notes

- **万能 closing**: After work, meetings, projects
- **職場 standard**: Every interaction ending
- **Hierarchy**: ご苦労様 = superior only; お疲れ様 = universal
- **Casual**: お疲れ (o-tsukare) for friends
- **Translation**: "Thank you for your effort"
