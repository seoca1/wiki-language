# Expressions: Technology & Internet (日本語)

> **Theme:** Technology & Internet
> **Level:** A2-C1
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Tech/internet expressions — apps, payments, social media, internet culture.

---

## Wi-Fiのパスワードは何ですか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (tech)
**Definition:** "What's the WiFi password?" — connecting to internet.
**Form:** Waifai no pasuwādo wa nan desu ka.

### Variations

- **Wi-Fiのパスワードは何ですか? (Waifai no pasuwādo wa nan desu ka?)** — "WiFi password?"
- **Wi-Fiはありますか? (Waifai wa arimasu ka?)** — "WiFi available?"
- **Wi-Fiは無料ですか? (Waifai wa muryō desu ka?)** — "WiFi free?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Free WiFi**: Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart), cafes, hotels
- **Japan Travel WiFi**: Tourist SIM rental (Ninja WiFi, Japan Wireless)
- **Pocket WiFi**: Rental device (popular for tourists)
- **eSIM**: Airalo, Ubigi (international)
- **Hotel WiFi**: Most hotels free for guests

## 連絡先を教えてください

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Please tell me your contact info" — sharing contact.
**Form:** Renrakusaki o oshiete kudasai.

### Variations

- **連絡先を教えてください (Renrakusaki o oshiete kudasai)** — "Tell me your contact"
- **LINE交換しましょう (LINE kōkan shimashō)** — "Let's exchange LINE"
- **メールアドレスは? (Mēru adoresu wa?)** — "Email address?"

### Cultural Notes

- **LINE**: Most popular messenger (replaces SMS in Japan)
- **LINE ID**: Alternative to phone number sharing
- **Email**: Less common for personal communication
- **Business**: Email + 名刺 (meishi = business card)
- **Privacy**: Japanese hesitant to share contact initially

## DMしてください

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Please DM me" — direct message on social media.
**Form:** DM shite kudasai.

### Variations

- **DMしてください (DM shite kudasai)** — "DM me"
- **DMで詳細送ります (DM de shōsai okurimasu)** — "I'll send details via DM"
- **非公開メッセージで (Hikōkai messēji de)** — "Private message"

### Cultural Notes

- **Twitter/X**: Most common for public DM
- **Instagram**: Common for young users
- **LINE**: Used for direct messaging (similar to DM)
- **Replies vs DMs**: Public replies vs private DMs
- **Etiquette**: Don't DM strangers sales pitches (spam)

## タグして

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Tag (me)" — mentioning in social media.
**Form:** Tagu shite.

### Variations

- **タグして (Tagu shite)** — "Tag me"
- **@___ (アットマーク)** — "@ ___" (mention)
- **#___ (ハッシュタグ)** — "# ___" (hashtag)

### Cultural Notes

- **Instagram @mention**: Common
- **Twitter/X @mention**: Standard
- **Privacy**: Some prefer not to be tagged
- **Notifications**: @mention = immediate notification
- **# hashtag**: カテゴリ categorize posts

## やばい

**Part of Speech:** expression (slang)
**Definition:** "Yabai" — multi-purpose (awesome/terrible/risky).
**Form:** Yabai.

### Variations

- **やばい (Yabai)** — "Awesome/Terrible" (context-dependent)
- **やばい! (Yabai!)** — Exclamation
- **やばすぎ (Yabasugi)** — "Too much" (intensifier)

### Cultural Notes

- **Versatile**: Positive or negative (context determines)
- **Originally negative**: "Dangerous/risky"
- **Now flexible**: "Awesome", "Terrible", "Risky", "Weird"
- **Gen Z**: Used extremely frequently
- **Viral word**: Became slang of the year multiple times

## 配車アプリ

**Part of Speech:** expression (urban)
**Definition:** "Ride-hailing app" — Uber/Lyft style service.
**Form:** Haisha appu.

### Variations

- **配車アプリ (Haisha appu)** — "Ride-hailing app"
- **Uber Japan** — Available in major cities
- **GO (ゴー)** — Taxi-hailing app (popular)
- **JapanTaxi** — Traditional taxi app

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps (Japan)**: GO (popular), Uber, JapanTaxi
- **Taxi culture**: Japan has excellent taxi service
- **No tipping**: Built into pricing
- **Auto-door**: Taxi doors open automatically (cool feature)
- **Receipt**: Ask for 領収書 (ryōshūsho) for business

## ライブコマース

**Part of Speech:** expression (commerce)
**Definition:** "Live commerce" — buying via live video.
**Form:** Raibu komāsu.

### Variations

- **ライブコマース (Raibu komāsu)** — "Live commerce"
- **ライブ配信 (Raibu haishin)** — "Live streaming"
- **ライバー (Raibā)** — "Live streamer"

### Cultural Notes

- **Japan market**: Growing but smaller than China/Korea
- **Platforms**: Instagram Live, YouTube Live, LINE LIVE
- **Influencers**: ライバー (live streamers) popular
- **Sales**: Often limited-time offers
- **Trust**: Japanese consumers value authenticity
