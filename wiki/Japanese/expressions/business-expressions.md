# Expressions: Business (日本語)

> **Theme:** Business & Workplace
> **Level:** B2-C1
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Business expressions — emails, meetings, contracts, titles, workplace etiquette.

---

## お目にかかれて光栄です

**Part of Speech:** expression (greeting)
**Definition:** "I'm honored to meet you" — formal first meeting.
**Form:** O-me ni kakarete kōei desu.

### Variations

- **お目にかかれて光栄です (O-me ni kakarete kōei desu)** — "I'm honored to meet you"
- **はじめまして (Hajimemashite)** — "Nice to meet you" (standard)
- **よろしくお願いいたします (Yoroshiku onegai shimasu)** — "Please treat me well"
- **どうぞよろしくお願いいたします (Dōzo yoroshiku onegai shimasu)** — "Please treat me well" (more formal)

### Cultural Notes

- **名刺交換 (Meishi kōkan)**: Business card exchange (両手 ryōte = both hands, 敬称 keishō = honorific)
- **Bow (お辞儀, ojigi)**: 15° casual, 30° standard, 45° deep (apologetic)
- **敬語 (Keigo)**: Honorific language (mandatory in business)
- **First-name basis**: NEVER in business (always + 様 sama)
- **"Yoroshiku"**: Multifunctional (greeting/closing/request)

## お時間をいただきありがとうございます

**Part of Speech:** expression (gratitude)
**Definition:** "Thank you for your time" — closing meeting or call.
**Form:** O-jikan o itadaki arigatou gozaimasu.

### Variations

- **お時間をいただきありがとうございます (O-jikan o itadaki arigatou gozaimasu)** — "Thank you for your time" (formal)
- **本日はお忙しい中ありがとうございました (Honjitsu wa o-isogashii naka arigatou gozaimashita)** — "Thank you for your time despite being busy"
- **今後ともよろしくお願いいたします (Kongotomo yoroshiku onegai itashimasu)** — "Looking forward to working with you" (formal closing)

### Cultural Notes

- **Follow-up**: Always send thank-you email within 24 hours
- **名刺**: Mention next steps from business card notes
- **Calendar**: Confirm meeting times in writing
- **Relationship (関係性)**: Building trust + 義理 (giri = obligation)
- **Humility**: Express gratitude often

## お見積もりをお願いします

**Part of Speech:** expression (business)
**Definition:** "We'd like a quote, please" — pricing inquiry.
**Form:** O-mitsumori o onegai shimasu.

### Variations

- **お見積もりをお願いします (O-mitsumori o onegai shimasu)** — "Quote please"
- **提案書をお願いします (Teiansho o onegai shimasu)** — "Proposal please"
- **___の価格は? (___ no kakaku wa?)** — "Price for ___?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Quote contents**: Itemized, valid for X days, terms
- **Negotiation**: Indirect, relationship-focused (vs. direct US)
- **MOQ**: Minimum Order Quantity
- **Payment terms**: 30日後払い (30 days later), 現金 (cash)
- **Multiple quotes**: Standard practice (相見積 aimitsumori)

## 契約条件について伺いたいのですが

**Part of Speech:** expression (legal)
**Definition:** "I'd like to ask about the contract terms" — contract negotiation.
**Form:** Keiyaku jōken ni tsuite ukagaitai no desu ga.

### Variations

- **契約条件について伺いたい (Keiyaku jōken ni tsuite ukagaitai)** — "About contract terms"
- **支払い条件は? (Shiharai jōken wa?)** — "Payment terms?"
- **キャンセルポリシーは? (Kyanseru porishī wa?)** — "Cancellation policy?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Key clauses**: 支払 (payment), 納期 (delivery), 知的財産 (IP), 解除 (termination)
- **Always have lawyer review**: Especially international contracts
- **Force majeure (不可抗力)**: Verify wording
- **Dispute resolution**: 仲裁 (chūsai = arbitration)
- **NDA (秘密保持契約)**: Sign before sharing IP

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 領収書をいただけますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (accounting)
**Definition:** "Could I have a receipt?" — billing request.
**Form:** Ryōshūsho o itadakemasu ka?

### Variations

- **領収書をいただけますか? (Ryōshūsho o itadakemasu ka?)** — "Receipt please"
- **請求書をください (Seikyūsho o kudasai)** — "Invoice please"
- **___払いで (___ barai de)** — "Paid by ___"

### Cultural Notes

- **Receipt types**: 領収書 (receipt), 請求書 (invoice)
- **Hanko (判子)**: Personal seal required for some transactions
- **Tax**: 8% or 10% consumption tax
- **Expense reports**: Itemized receipts required
- **Late payment**: 遅延損害金 (delay damages)

## お休みをいただきたいのですが

**Part of Speech:** expression (HR)
**Definition:** "I'd like to take some time off" — leave request.
**Form:** O-yasumi o itadakitai no desu ga.

### Variations

- **お休みをいただきたい (O-yasumi o itadakitai)** — "Time off please"
- **有給消化したい (Yūkyū kashō shitai)** — "Use paid leave"
- **病欠します (Byōketsu shimasu)** — "Sick leave"

### Cultural Notes

- **Japan PTO**: 10-20 days/year (often unused due to 空気 reading = kuuki)
- **有給 (Paid leave)**: Often forfeited (日本の問題)
- **Sick leave**: Separate from PTO
- **Maternity/Paternity**: Female 6 weeks before/8 weeks after; male less common
- **Pre-notice**: 2-4 weeks for vacation

## 打ち合わせのお時間をいただいてもよろしいでしょうか

**Part of Speech:** expression (communication)
**Definition:** "Could I have a meeting time?" — scheduling.
**Form:** Uchiawase no o-jikan o itadaite mo yoroshii deshō ka.

### Variations

- **打ち合わせのお時間をお願いいたします (Uchiawase no o-jikan o onegai itashimasu)** — "Meeting time please"
- **___の都合はいかがでしょうか? (___ no tsugō wa ikaga deshō ka?)** — "Does ___ work?"
- **カレンダー招待をお送りします (Karendā shōtai o o-okuri shimasu)** — "Sending calendar invite"

### Cultural Notes

- **Politeness**: Extra-long phrasing for politeness
- **Time zones**: JST always (+9 UTC)
- **Tools**: Zoom, Teams, Google Meet
- **Calendly**: Less common in Japan
- **Confirmation**: Day before + 30 min before

## 担当者を呼んでいただけますか

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Could you call the person in charge?" — escalation.
**Form:** Tantōsha o yonde itadakemasu ka.

### Variations

- **担当者を呼んでいただけますか (Tantōsha o yonde itadakemasu ka)** — "Person in charge please"
- **___さんはいますか? (___-san wa imasu ka?)** — "Is ___ here?"
- **上役の方をお願いします (Uwayaku no kata o onegai shimasu)** — "Your superior please"

### Cultural Notes

- **Hierarchy**: Japan has strong vertical hierarchy
- **受付 (Uketsuke)**: Receptionist (gatekeeper)
- **Decision-making**: Often slow (稟議書 ringisho = ringi system)
- **Top-down**: Decisions from top
- **担当 (Tantō)**: Person in charge of specific area
