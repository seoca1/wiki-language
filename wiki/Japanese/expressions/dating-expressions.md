# Expressions: Dating & Romance (日本語)

> **Theme:** Dating & Romance
> **Level:** B1-C1
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Dating/romance expressions — confession, affection, relationship stages, breakup.

---

## 好きです

**Part of Speech:** expression (confession)
**Definition:** "I like you" — direct confession.
**Form:** Suki desu.

### Variations

- **好きです (Suki desu)** — "I like you"
- **好きです、大好きです (Suki desu, daisuki desu)** — "I like you, I love you"
- **___が好きです (___ ga suki desu)** — "I like ___"

### Cultural Notes

- **Direct vs indirect**: Japan = very indirect ("あのね..." = umm...)
- **Confession (告白, kokuhaku)**: Formal event in Japanese dating culture
- **"Confession" ritual**: Often on White Day, Christmas Eve, or special date
- **Response (OK)**: "私も好きです" (Watashi mo suki desu) = "I like you too"
- **Response (NO)**: "ごめんなさい" (gomen nasai) + soft decline

## 付き合ってください

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Please go out with me" — official relationship request.
**Form:** Tsukiatte kudasai.

### Variations

- **付き合ってください (Tsukiatte kudasai)** — "Please go out with me"
- **僕と付き合ってください (Boku to tsukiatte kudasai)** — "Please go out with me" (male speaker)
- **俺と付き合ってくれ (Ore to tsukiatte kure)** — "Go out with me" (very casual)

### Cultural Notes

- **Confession first**: Must confess before becoming official couple
- **恋人 (Koibito)**: Term for boyfriend/girlfriend (post-confession)
- **付き合う (Tsukiau)**: "To go out" / "to date"
- **Rejection gracefully**: "考えさせてください" (kangaesasete kudasai) = "Let me think"
- **Indirect yes**: Silence + smile = yes (文化 bunka = cultural)

## 愛してる

**Part of Speech:** expression (love)
**Definition:** "I love you" — deep affection.
**Form:** Aishiteru.

### Variations

- **愛してる (Aishiteru)** — "I love you" (deep)
- **大好きだよ (Daisuki da yo)** — "I love you" (casual)
- **愛しています (Aishite imasu)** — "I love you" (formal)

### Cultural Notes

- **"愛してる" reserved**: Often considered heavy in Japan
- **"大好き" more common**: Used more freely
- **Cultural context**: Japanese prefer showing love over saying
- **Family**: Less common than Western cultures (until recent generations)
- **Pets/couple**: "大好き" very common

## 別れましょう

**Part of Speech:** expression (breakup)
**Definition:** "Let's break up" — ending relationship.
**Form:** Wakaremashō.

### Variations

- **別れましょう (Wakaremashō)** — "Let's break up"
- **別れてください (Wakarete kudasai)** — "Please break up with me"
- **私たちには合わないと思う (Watashitachi ni wa awanai to omou)** — "I think we're not compatible"

### Cultural Notes

- **Indirect approach**: Common in Japan (avoid confrontation)
- **"考えさせて" (Kangaesasete)**: "Let me think" (often means no)
- **Friendship maintained**: Often stays friendly
- **Honmei (本命)**: "The one" (vs. aibou = partner)
- **復帰 (Fukki)**: Getting back together (common in Japan)

## お見合い

**Part of Speech:** expression (cultural)
**Definition:** "Arranged meeting" — traditional matchmaking.
**Form:** Omiai.

### Variations

- **お見合い (Omiai)** — "Arranged meeting"
- **お見合いパーティー (Omiai pātī)** — "Matchmaking party"
- **合コン (Gōkon)** — "Group blind date"

### Cultural Notes

- **Omiai tradition**: Historical (now rare but exists)
- **Matchmaker (仲人, nakōdo)**: Introduces potential partners
- **Modern dating**: Apps dominate (Pairs, Tinder Japan, Tapple)
- **Gōkon (合コン)**: Group blind date (common in 20s-30s)
- **Office dating (社内恋愛)**: Still common (but HR sometimes discouraged)

## 同棲

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Cohabitation" — living together unmarried.
**Form:** Dōsei.

### Variations

- **同棲 (Dōsei)** — "Living together"
- **一緒に住もう (Issho ni sumō)** — "Let's live together"
- **同棲を解消する (Dōsei o kaishō suru)** — "Breaking off cohabitation"

### Cultural Notes

- **Japan**: Less common than West (more conservative traditionally)
- **Increasing**: Younger generations (20s-30s) more open
- **Marriage-first culture**: Many still expect marriage before living together
- **半同棲 (Han-dōsei)**: "Half-cohabitation" (spending nights, separate address)
- **Family pressure**: Parents may disapprove

## マッチングアプリ

**Part of Speech:** expression (modern)
**Definition:** "Matching app" — online dating.
**Form:** Matchingu appu.

### Variations

- **マッチングアプリ (Matchingu appu)** — "Matching app"
- **Pairs (ペアーズ)** — Most popular Japanese app
- **Tinder Japan** — International standard
- **Tapple (タップル)** — Younger demographic
- **with (ウィズ)** — Personality-focused

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps (Japan)**: Pairs, Tinder, Tapple, with, Omiai
- **Cultural norms**: Less casual than US; more relationship-focused
- **Footprint (足跡, ashiseki)**: Profile view notification
- **"いいね" (Like)**: Right swipe equivalent
- **Profile standards**: Hobby photos + face

## 親への挨拶

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Greeting to parents" — formal family introduction.
**Form:** Oya e no aisatsu.

### Variations

- **親への挨拶 (Oya e no aisatsu)** — "Greeting parents"
- **両家の顔合わせ (Ryōke no kao-awase)** — "Meeting of the two families"
- **結婚の挨拶 (Kekkon no aisatsu)** — "Marriage greeting"

### Cultural Notes

- **Important step**: Often before engagement
- **Gift (手土産, temiyage)**: Bring nice gift (fruit, sweets, alcohol)
- **Speech formality**: Very polite language
- **家族構成 (Kazoku kōsei)**: "Family structure" — be ready to share
- **結納 (Yuinō)**: Traditional betrothal gift exchange (now rare)
