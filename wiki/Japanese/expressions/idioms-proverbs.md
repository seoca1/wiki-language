# Expressions: Idioms & Proverbs (日本語)

> **Theme:** Idioms & Proverbs
> **Level:** B2-C2
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Japanese idioms (慣用句) and proverbs (ことわざ).

---

## 一石二鳥

**Part of Speech:** 四字熟語 (4-character idiom)
**Definition:** "Kill two birds with one stone" — efficiency.
**Form:** Isseki nichō.

### Usage

Describing efficiency.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 一 (one) + 石 (stone) + 二 (two) + 鳥 (bird)
- **Origin**: Chinese origin
- **Modern**: Common in business
- **Sample**: 一石二鳥の計画 (isseki nichō no keikaku = "two-birds plan")

---

## 蛇足

**Part of Speech:** 四字熟語
**Definition:** "Gilding the lily" — unnecessary addition.
**Form:** Dasoku.

### Usage

Criticizing unnecessary additions.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 蛇 (snake) + 足 (legs) — drawing legs on a snake
- **Origin**: Chinese idiom
- **Sample**: それは蛇足だ (Sore wa dasoku da = "That's unnecessary")

---

## 馬の耳に念仏

**Part of Speech:** 慣用句 (idiom)
**Definition:** "Casting pearls before swine" — wasted effort.
**Form:** Uma no mimi ni nenbutsu.

### Usage

When advice goes unheeded.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 馬の耳 (horse's ear) + 念仏 (Buddhist prayer)
- **意味**: Telling a prayer to a horse (who doesn't understand)
- **Sample**: 彼に注意しても馬の耳に念仏だ (Kare ni chūi shite mo uma no mimi ni nenbutsu da)

---

## 花より団子

**Part of Speech:** ことわざ (proverb)
**Definition:** "Dumplings over flowers" — practicality over aesthetics.
**Form:** Hana yori dango.

### Usage

Choosing practical over pretty.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 花 (flowers) + 団子 (dango = rice dumpling)
- **意味**: Practical > pretty (eat dumplings vs. look at flowers)
- **Origin**: Hanami (flower viewing) tradition
- **Sample**: 花より団子の私は、旅行より実用的 (Practical me prefers dumplings over flowers)

---

## 猿も木から落ちる

**Part of Speech:** ことわざ
**Definition:** "Even monkeys fall from trees" — even experts make mistakes.
**Form:** Saru mo ki kara ochiru.

### Usage

Acknowledging expert mistakes.

### Cultural Notes

- **意味**: Even experts can fail
- **Equivalent English**: "Even Homer nods"
- **Sample**: ミスしちゃったよ。猿も木から落ちるね (Mishitacha yo — saru mo ki kara ochiru ne)

---

## 七転び八起き

**Part of Speech:** ことわざ
**Definition:** "Fall seven times, stand up eight" — perseverance.
**Form:** Nanakorobi yaoki.

### Usage

Encouragement to persevere.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 七転び (7 falls) + 八起き (8 rises)
- **意味**: Resilience despite failures
- **Origin**: Buddhist concept of overcoming difficulty
- **Sample**: 七転び八起きで成功をつかもう (Persist and achieve success)

---

## 備えあれば憂いなし

**Part of Speech:** ことわざ
**Definition:** "Forewarned is forearmed" — preparation prevents worry.
**Form:** Sonae areba urei nashi.

### Usage

Recommending preparation.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 備え (preparation) + 憂い (worry)
- **意味**: Be prepared = no worries
- **Sample**: 備えあれば憂いなし — 防災グッズは大事 (Preparedness matters — disaster kit important)

---

## 二度あることは三度ある

**Part of Speech:** ことわざ
**Definition:** "Things that happen twice happen thrice" — pattern recognition.
**Form:** Nido aru koto wa sando aru.

### Usage

Warning about patterns.

### Cultural Notes

- **構造**: 二度 (twice) + 三度 (three times)
- **意味**: If it happened twice, will happen third time
- **Sample**: 二度あることは三度ある、また遅刻だ (Late again — third time!)
