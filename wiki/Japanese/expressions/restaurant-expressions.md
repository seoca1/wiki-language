# Expressions: Restaurant & Dining (日本語)

> **Theme:** Restaurant & Dining
> **Level:** A2-B2
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Restaurant/dining expressions — ordering, allergies, bill, dining etiquette.

---

## メニューをいただけますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Could I have the menu, please?" — upon sitting.
**Form:** Menyū o itadakemasu ka?

### Variations

- **メニューをいただけますか? (Menyū o itadakemasu ka?)** — "May I have the menu?"
- **英語のメニューはありますか? (Eigo no menyū wa arimasu ka?)** — "English menu?"
- **おすすめは何ですか? (Osusume wa nan desu ka?)** — "What do you recommend?"

### Cultural Notes

- **いらっしゃいませ (Irasshaimase)**: Welcome greeting from staff (no response needed)
- **お冷 (O-hiya)**: Free cold water (most restaurants)
- **おしぼり (Oshibori)**: Wet towel (free)
- **Tipping**: No tipping (would be considered rude)
- **Order timing**: Press call button (call button 呼び出し yobidashi) when ready

## これをください

**Part of Speech:** expression (order)
**Definition:** "I'll have this" — placing an order.
**Form:** Kore o kudasai.

### Variations

- **これをください (Kore o kudasai)** — "I'll have this"
- **___をお願いします (___ o onegai shimasu)** — "I'd like ___ please"
- **___を一つ (___ o hitotsu)** — "One ___"
- **同じものをください (Onaji mono o kudasai)** — "The same thing please"

### Cultural Notes

- **Politeness**: Use "お願いします" (onegaishimasu) for politeness
- **Set meals**: 定食 (teishoku) = set meal (more value)
- **Lunch sets**: ランチセット = lunch set (cheaper than dinner)
- **Quantity words**: 一つ (hitotsu), 二つ (futatsu), 三つ (mittsu)
- **Allergies**: Always inform before ordering

## ___のアレルギーがあります

**Part of Speech:** expression (health)
**Definition:** "I have a ___ allergy" — allergy disclosure.
**Form:** ___ no arerugī ga arimasu.

### Variations

- **ピーナッツのアレルギーがあります (Pīnattsu no arerugī ga arimasu)** — "Peanut allergy"
- **グルテンフリーをお願いします (Gurutenfurī o onegai shimasu)** — "Gluten-free please"
- **ベジタリアンです (Bejitarian desu)** — "I'm vegetarian"
- **___は入っていますか? (___ wa haitte imasu ka?)** — "Does this contain ___?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Severe allergies**: Always inform staff (especially for nuts, shellfish)
- **Common allergies**: Peanuts (ピーナッツ), eggs (卵 tamago), wheat (小麦 komugi), soba
- **EpiPen (エピペン)**: Carry if severe
- **Allergy cards**: Translated (SelectWisely, Equal Eats)
- **Sushi note**: Wasabi (辛子) and soy sauce allergy common

## お会計おねがいします

**Part of Speech:** expression (payment)
**Definition:** "Check, please" — asking for the bill.
**Form:** O-kaikei onegai shimasu.

### Variations

- **お会計お願いします (O-kaikei onegai shimasu)** — "Check please"
- **お勘定お願いします (O-kanjō onegai shimasu)** — "Bill please" (formal)
- **別々でお願いします (Betsubetsu de onegai shimasu)** — "Separate checks please"
- **一緒でお願いします (Issho de onegai shimasu)** — "Together please"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: お会計 (o-kaikei) = check, お勘定 (o-kanjō) = bill
- **No tipping**: Not expected, would confuse staff
- **Pay together**: Common in izakaya (one person pays, others pay back)
- **Cash common**: Many places still cash-preferred
- **Pay at table**: Bring to your table (not at register)

## 持ち帰りにできますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Can I take this to go?" — leftovers/doggy bag.
**Form:** Mochikaeri ni dekimasu ka?

### Variations

- **持ち帰りにできますか? (Mochikaeri ni dekimasu ka?)** — "Can I take it home?"
- **持ち帰りでお願いします (Mochikaeri de onegai shimasu)** — "For takeout please"
- **テイクアウトできますか? (Teikuauto dekimasu ka?)** — "Takeout?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: 持ち帰り (mochikaeri) = takeout, テイクアウト (teikuauto) = anglicism
- **Doggy bag culture**: Less common in Japan than US (smaller portions typically)
- **Container fee**: Some charge for takeout container (50-100 yen)
- **Eco-friendly trend**: Bring your own container (mottECO)
- **Restaurant permission**: Always ask first

## これは何が入っていますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "What's in this?" — ingredient inquiry.
**Form:** Kore wa nani ga haitte imasu ka?

### Variations

- **これは何ですか? (Kore wa nan desu ka?)** — "What is this?"
- **何が入っていますか? (Nani ga haitte imasu ka?)** — "What's in this?"
- **辛いですか? (Karai desu ka?)** — "Is it spicy?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Allergen awareness**: Restaurants increasingly label ingredients
- **Hidden ingredients**: Soy sauce (醤油 shōyu) = wheat, dashi = fish
- **Spice level**: 日本食 (Japanese food) generally not spicy
- **Dishes to ask**: 鍋 (nabe), お好み焼き (okonomiyaki)
- **Cross-contamination**: Shared cooking surfaces

## とても美味しいです

**Part of Speech:** expression (compliment)
**Definition:** "It's very delicious" — praising food.
**Form:** Totemo oishii desu.

### Variations

- **とても美味しいです (Totemo oishii desu)** — "Very delicious"
- **最高です! (Saikō desu!)** — "The best!"
- **また来ます! (Mata kimasu!)** — "I'll come again!"

### Cultural Notes

- **Complimenting food**: 「美味しかったです」(gochisōsama deshita = "It was a feast") at end
- **Chef acknowledgment**: Going back to compliment at end (rare but appreciated)
- **Photo rule**: Many restaurants forbid food photos (politely check)
- **Tipping**: NOT customary (would cause confusion)
