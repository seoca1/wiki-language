## すごい

**Part of Speech:** expression (admiration)
**Definition:** "Sugoi" — "Awesome/Incredible".
**Form:** Sugoi.

### Variations

- **すごい (Sugoi)** — "Amazing"
- **すごいね! (Sugoi ne!)** — "Amazing!" (with particle)
- **すっごい (Suggōi)** — "Sooo amazing" (emphasis)

### Cultural Notes

- **Most common**: Positive exclamation in Japanese
- **Versatile**: "Amazing", "Incredible", "Wow"
- **Generation**: Universal (all ages)
- **Related**: すごい (amazing), やばい (yabai), ヤバい
- **Difference**: すごい = more polite, やばい = more casual

## 頑張って

**Part of Speech:** expression (encouragement)
**Definition:** "Ganbatte" — "Do your best!" / "Hang in there!".
**Form:** Ganbatte.

### Variations

- **頑張って (Ganbatte)** — "Do your best"
- **頑張れ (Ganbare)** — "Hang in there!" (command form)
- **頑張ります (Ganbarimasu)** — "I'll do my best"
- **頑張ってるね (Ganbatteru ne)** — "You're doing great" (acknowledgment)

### Cultural Notes

- **Universal**: All ages, all contexts
- **Use**: Before challenges, exams, work
- **職場**: Common encouragement
- **スポーツ**: Standard before games
- **"お疲れ様" (Otsukaresama)**: After effort (acknowledgment)

## 草 / w

**Part of Speech:** expression (text)
**Definition:** "Kusa / w" — Internet laugh.
**Form:** Kusa / w.

### Variations

- **草 (Kusa)** — "Lol" (literally "grass")
- **w** — "Lol" (single letter)
- **www** — "Lol" (multiple w's)
- **草生える (Kusa haeru)** — "Makes me laugh" (literally "grass grows")
- **www草 (www kusa)** — Very funny

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: w = 笑え (warae = "laugh") typing quickly
- **Modern**: 草 (kusa = grass) = w (visual joke)
- **Frequency**: Very common in text
- **Tone**: Like LOL but Japanese
- **Variation**: www越多越多 funny (草生える = grass grows = LOL escalates)

- `[[emotions-reactions]]`

## フラれる

**Part of Speech:** expression (modern)
**Definition:** "Furareru" — being rejected in dating.
**Form:** Furareru.

### Variations

- **フラれた (Furareta)** — "Got rejected"
- **フラれる (Furareru)** — "To be rejected"
- **フラれる確率 (Furareru kakuritsu)** — "Probability of rejection"

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: 振られる (furareru) = "to be turned down"
- **High school term**: Originally young slang (1980s+)
- **Now universal**: All ages
- **"振られる" (Furareru)**: Original form (still used)
- **Light tone**: Often used humorously among friends
- **Rejection style**: Japan = often indirect

## ググる

**Part of Speech:** expression (tech)
**Definition:** "Guguru" — "to Google something".
**Form:** Guguru.

### Variations

- **ググる (Guguru)** — "To Google"
- **ググりました (Gugurimashita)** — "I Googled it" (past tense)
- **ググれ (Gugure)** — "Google it" (command)

### Cultural Notes

- **Popular verb**: する → ググる (verb-ification of brand name)
- **Universal**: All generations use
- **Casual**: Mostly casual (not in formal speech)
- **Similar loanwords**: コピペ (copy-paste), グーロン (go online)
- **Tip**: Even Japanese friends will "ググれ" (google it) response

## 限界 / 詰み

**Part of Speech:** expression (modern)
**Definition:** "Genkai / Tsumi" — at one's limit / checkmate.
**Form:** Genkai / Tsumi.

### Variations

- **限界 (Genkai)** — "Limit" (at breaking point)
- **詰み (Tsumi)** — "Checkmate" (game term, no way out)
- **限界です (Genkai desu)** — "I'm at my limit"
- **詰んでる (Tonderu)** — "Stuck/no way out"

### Cultural Notes

- **限界**: Universal (young + old)
- **詰み**: Originally chess (詰み = no moves left), now figurative
- **Gen Z**: Use frequently for "I'm done"
- **SNS trend**: "限界○○" memes (○○ at limit)
- **"もう限界"**: "I'm at my limit" (very common)

- `[[emotions-reactions]]`

## 大人になる

**Part of Speech:** expression (humor)
**Definition:** "Otona ni naru" — becoming an adult.
**Form:** Otona ni naru.

### Variations

- **大人になる (Otona ni naru)** — "Becoming an adult"
- **大人って大変 (Otona tte taihen)** — "Being adult is hard"
- **大人買い (Otonagai)** — "Adult purchase" (buying expensive things)

### Cultural Notes

- **Humor**: Self-deprecation about adult responsibilities
- **Tasks**: Taxes, bills, laundry, job hunting
- **20歳 (Hatachi)**: Legal adult age in Japan (since 2022)
- **"大人買い"**: Famous term (buying all items in a series)
- **Generational: Yutori/氷河期世代**: Different generation experiences

## ダチ / 仲間

**Part of Speech:** expression (address)
**Definition:** "Dachi / Nakama" — buddy / comrade.
**Form:** Dachi / Nakama.

### Variations

- **ダチ (Dachi)** — "Buddy" (slang)
- **仲間 (Nakama)** — "Comrade" (more standard)
- **フレ (Fure)** — Short for "friend" (casual)
- **マイベスト (Mai besuto)** — "My best" (close friend)

### Cultural Notes

- **ダチ**: Slang, young male
- **仲間 (Nakama)**: Anime/cultural reference (One Piece "nakama")
- **親友 (Shin'yū)**: Best friend (formal)
- **「ダチ」originated**: 大阪 dialect influence
- **Japanese friendship**: Often group-based (friends of friends)
