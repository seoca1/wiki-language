# Expressions: Travel Advanced (日本語)

> **Theme:** Travel (Advanced)
> **Level:** B1-C1
> **Standard:** 標準語 (Standard Japanese)
> **Romanization:** Romaji + Hiragana/Kanji

Advanced travel expressions — booking, ticket issues, directions, accommodation, emergencies.

---

## 予約したいんですが

**Part of Speech:** expression (booking)
**Definition:** "I'd like to make a reservation" — booking flights/hotels/restaurants.
**Form:** Yoyaku shitai n desu ga.

### Variations

- **予約したいんですが (Yoyaku shitai n desu ga)** — "I'd like to make a reservation"
- **空いていますか? (Aite imasu ka?)** — "Is it available?"
- **2名で予約お願いします (Nimei de yoyaku onegai shimasu)** — "Reservation for 2"
- **___泊お願いできますか? (___-haku onegai dekimasu ka?)** — "For ___ nights?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Booking sites**: Rakuten Travel, Jalan, Booking.com (cheapest)
- **Hotel direct**: Often matches online price + perks
- **Japanese ryokan**: Book directly (vs. via foreign platform)
- **Cancellation**: Most = 24-48 hours free
- **Peak season**: Golden Week (5월), Obon (8월), New Year = book 2-3 months ahead

## ___までの切符を一枚ください

**Part of Speech:** expression (transport)
**Definition:** "One ticket to..." — transport purchase.
**Form:** ___ made no kippu o ichimai kudasai.

### Variations

- **東京までの切符を一枚 (Tōkyō made no kippu o ichimai)** — "1 ticket to Tokyo"
- **往復でお願いします (Ōfuku de onegai shimasu)** — "Round trip please"
- **片道いくらですか? (Katamichi ikura desu ka?)** — "How much one-way?"
- **学割はありますか? (Gakuwari wa arimasu ka?)** — "Student discount?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Ticket types**: 片道 (one-way) / 往復 (round-trip)
- **JR Pass**: For foreign tourists (7/14/21 days)
- **Shinkansen**: 指定席 (reserved) / 自由席 (non-reserved)
- **IC cards**: Suica/Pasmo (Tokyo), Icoca (Osaka) — all regions
- **Booking timing**: 1 month ahead for cheaper flights

## この辺で何か面白い所はありますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Is there anything interesting around here?" — sightseeing.
**Form:** Kono hen de nanika omoshiroi tokoro wa arimasu ka?

### Variations

- **この辺で何か面白い所はありますか?** — "Anything interesting nearby?"
- **おすすめの場所は? (Osusume no basho wa?)** — "Recommended places?"
- **地元の人しか知らない所は? (Jimoto no hito shika shiranai tokoro wa?)** — "Local-only places?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: Google Maps (with Japanese reviews), Jalan, Hot Pepper
- **"穴場" (ana-ba)**: Hidden gem
- **Tourist info center (観光案内所)**: Free maps + recommendations
- **Ryokan staff**: Excellent local recommendations
- **2ch/5ch forums**: Real local advice

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## トイレはどこですか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (urgent)
**Definition:** "Where is the restroom?" — basic but essential.
**Form:** Toire wa doko desu ka?

### Variations

- **トイレはどこですか? (Toire wa doko desu ka?)** — "Where is the toilet?"
- **お手洗いは? (Otearai wa?)** — "Restroom?" (formal)
- **化粧室は? (Keshōshitsu wa?)** — "Powder room" (women)

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: トイレ (toilet — most common), お手洗い (formal)
- **Public toilets**: Free (in stations, convenience stores, parks)
- **Convenience stores (コンビニ)**: Always available (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart)
- **High-tech toilets**: Most have bidet functions (washlet)
- **Disabled toilets**: 多目的トイレ (tagen teki toilet)

## 安くなりますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (negotiation)
**Definition:** "Can it be cheaper?" — bargaining (limited use in Japan).
**Form:** Yasuku narimasu ka?

### Variations

- **安くなりますか? (Yasuku narimasu ka?)** — "Can it be cheaper?"
- **学割ありますか? (Gakuwari arimasu ka?)** — "Student discount?"
- **セット割ありますか? (Setto wari arimasu ka?)** — "Set discount?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Bargaining culture**: 
  - Japan = NO bargaining (retail fixed price)
  - Exception: flea markets, antiques, some tourist shops
  - Unlike Thailand/Vietnam
- **Discount types**: 学割 (student), シニア割 (senior), 早割 (early bird)
- **Tip**: Cash often = better deal at markets
- **Politeness**: Always polite phrasing

## 道に迷いました

**Part of Speech:** expression (problem)
**Definition:** "I'm lost" — getting help when disoriented.
**Form:** Michi ni mayoimashita.

### Variations

- **道に迷いました (Michi ni mayoimashita)** — "I'm lost"
- **助けてください (Tasukete kudasai)** — "Please help me"
- **ここはどこですか? (Koko wa doko desu ka?)** — "Where is this?"
- **英語を話せる人いますか? (Eigo o hanaseru hito imasu ka?)** — "English speaker?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: Google Maps (works in Japan), Japan Travel by Navitime
- **Police boxes (交番, kōban)**: Mini police stations — extremely helpful
- **Tourist info**: All major stations have tourist info centers
- **Japanese politeness**: People will often walk you to destination
- **Lost & found**: 忘れ物センター (wasuremono sentā)

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Wi-Fiはありますか?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Is there WiFi?" — internet access inquiry.
**Form:** Waifai wa arimasu ka?

### Variations

- **Wi-Fiはありますか? (Waifai wa arimasu ka?)** — "Is there WiFi?"
- **パスワードは何ですか? (Pasuwādo wa nan desu ka?)** — "What's the password?"
- **Wi-Fiは無料ですか? (Waifai wa muryō desu ka?)** — "Is WiFi free?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Free WiFi**: Convenience stores, cafes, hotels, some stations
- **Japan Travel WiFi**: Tourist SIM rental (Ninja WiFi, Japan Wireless)
- **Pocket WiFi**: Rental device (popular for tourists)
- **eSIM**: Airalo, Ubigi (for international travelers)
- **Hotel WiFi**: Most hotels free for guests

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## 英語が話せるガイドをお願いします

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "I'd like an English-speaking guide" — guided tour.
**Form:** Eigo ga hanaseru gaido o onegai shimasu.

### Variations

- **英語が話せるガイドをお願いします (Eigo ga hanaseru gaido o onegai shimasu)** — "English guide please"
- **英語のツアーはありますか? (Eigo no tsuā wa arimasu ka?)** — "English tour?"
- **半日/一日いくらですか? (Hannichi/Ichinichi ikura desu ka?)** — "Half-day/Full-day price?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Tour types**: Group / Private / Audio guide
- **Apps**: GetYourGuide, Viator, Klook
- **Free walking tours**: Major cities (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka)
- **Museum tours**: Often included with admission (English at major museums)
- **Local guides**: Better context than apps

- `[[travel-basics]]`
