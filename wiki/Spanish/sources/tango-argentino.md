## Summary

This source covers Argentine tango culture — its history, key vocabulary (lunfardo slang), the milonga social institution, and the unique Rioplatense Spanish spoken in Buenos Aires. Essential for understanding Argentine identity and for conversational Spanish with Argentines.

## Key Takeaways

- **Tango is Argentine national identity** — not just a dance, but a cultural ecosystem
- **Rioplatense Spanish differs** from standard Spanish: vos instead of tú, sh-sound (ll/yeísmo), distinctive rhythm
- **The milonga is a social institution** with strict etiquette (cabeceo, tandas, cortinas)
- **Lunfardo (slang)** permeates tango lyrics and everyday speech in Buenos Aires

## Cultural Insights

- **El abrazo** is non-verbal communication — the quality of the embrace determines the dance
- **Cabeceo** (nodding to invite) replaces verbal asking — preserves dignity
- **Vos** replaces tú — affects all verb conjugations
- **SH-sound** (ll/yeísmo) — calle = ca-she, lluvia = shu-bia
