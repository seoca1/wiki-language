## Summary

This source covers Mexican street food culture — taco types, ordering patterns, cultural etiquette, Mexican Spanish vocabulary differences, and Korean-Mexican food culture comparison. Essential for learning Mexican Spanish and understanding Mexico's food culture.

## Key Takeaways

- **Mexico has 130M+ Spanish speakers** — largest Spanish-speaking population
- **Tacos are eaten by hand** — no utensils; embrace the mess
- **Cilantro is polarizing** — always specify *con cilantro* or *sin cilantro*
- **Spice levels matter** — ask for *poco picoso* if unsure
- **Mexican Spanish has unique vocabulary** — *camión* (bus), *carro* (car), *chido* (cool)

## Cultural Insights

- **Eating standing** is normal at street stalls
- **Lime juice** on everything is ritual
- **Family-style sharing** — order multiple tacos per person
- **Meal timing**: lunch 14:00–16:00 (main), dinner 19:00–21:00 (lighter)
- **Korea-Mexico parallel**: both love street food, spice, communal eating
