## Cultural Insights

- **Spain time zones**: Lunch 14:00-16:00, dinner 21:00-23:00. Adjust your schedule.
- **Tapas culture**: Free small dishes with drinks, especially in Andalusia.
- **Tío/Tía**: Adults called uncle/aunt by children, even non-relatives — close family-like friendships.
- **¡Hala!**: Expresses surprise, like "Wow!" in English.
- **Besos**: 1 cheek kiss (Spain) or 2 cheeks (LatAm) when greeting close friends.
- **Tipping**: Not required, but rounding up appreciated (5-10%).
