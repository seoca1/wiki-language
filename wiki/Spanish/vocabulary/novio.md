# novio

**Part of Speech:** sustantivo (noun, masculine)
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 남자친구 / 신랑 — Boyfriend; groom.

## Examples

- "Mi novio es ingeniero" — My boyfriend is an engineer
- "¿Es tu novio?" — Is he your boyfriend?
- "Estoy con mi novio" — I'm with my boyfriend

## Related Terms

- [[novia]] — girlfriend / bride
- [[enamorado]] — in love
- [[cita]] — date

## Cultural Notes

**"Novio" tiene dos significados**:
1. **연인 (boyfriend)** — 교제 중인 남자
2. **신랑 (groom)** — 결혼식 당일 또는 약혼 후

**"Tener novio" = 교제 중** (가장 흔한 의미).

**Equivalentes de género**: novio/novia son heteronormativos.
"Mi pareja" (my partner) es neutro.
