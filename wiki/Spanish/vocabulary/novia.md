# novia

**Part of Speech:** sustantivo (noun, feminine)
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 여자친구 / 신부 — Girlfriend; bride.

## Examples

- "Mi novia se llama Ana" — My girlfriend's name is Ana
- "¿Es tu novia?" — Is she your girlfriend?
- "Estoy con mi novia" — I'm with my girlfriend

## Related Terms

- [[novio]] — boyfriend / groom
- [[enamorada]] — in love (fem.)
- [[cita]] — date

## Cultural Notes

**"Novia" tiene dos significados**:
1. **여자친구 (girlfriend)** — 교제 중인 여자
2. **신부 (bride)** — 결혼식 당일

**Equivalentes de género neutro**: "Pareja" / "Compañero/a".
