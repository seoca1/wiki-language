# beso

**Part of Speech:** sustantivo (noun, masculine) / verbo (verb, "besar")
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** Kiss. The action of pressing one's lips against someone/something as a gesture of affection, love, or greeting.

**Gender:** masculine (el beso, los besos)

**Etymology:** From Latin "basium" (kiss). Possibly from Greek "basilēus" (king) — the kissing of the king's ring? Or from Proto-Italic *bassjum. Modern Spanish "beso" / "besar" (to kiss).

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈbeso/
- Syllables: be-so
- Stress: 1st syllable (BE-so)
- The "s" is soft (voiceless alveolar fricative)

**Memory Tip:** "**BE**co **SO**" — "Be so sweet, give me a kiss!" Or: "**BESO**" = "**B**ig **E**arnest **S**weet **O**utpour" = a kiss.

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "La beso" → "**El beso**" (masculine, not feminine)
- ❌ "Beso a tu" → "**Beso a tu madre**" (a + person = personal "a" required in Spanish)
- ❌ "Un beso grande" → "**Un beso enorme**" or "**Un gran beso**" (idiomatic; "grande" + noun needs "gran")
- ❌ Confusing types: "beso en la mejilla" (cheek) vs "beso en los labios" (lips) vs "beso en la mano" (hand) — context essential
- ✅ "Un beso" (A kiss)
- ✅ "Dame un beso" (Give me a kiss)
- ✅ "El primer beso" (The first kiss)
- ✅ "Un beso en la mejilla" (A kiss on the cheek — greeting)
- ✅ "Besar" (to kiss — verb)

**Register:** casual to romantic. Different uses have different registers.
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500 ES words)

## Examples (Noun)

- "Un beso" — A kiss
- "Dame un beso" — Give me a kiss
- "El primer beso" — The first kiss
- "Un beso en la mejilla" — A kiss on the cheek (greeting)
- "Un beso en los labios" — A kiss on the lips (romantic)
- "Beso robado" — Stolen kiss (literary)
- "Beso de película" — Movie kiss

## Examples (Verb "besar")

- "Te beso" — I kiss you
- "¿Puedo besarte?" — May I kiss you?
- "Me besó en la mejilla" — He/she kissed me on the cheek
- "Besarse" — to kiss each other (reflexive)

## Types of Kisses in Spanish Culture

| Type | Spanish | Context | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheek (Spain) | Beso en la mejilla | Greeting (1 per person) | Casual, universal |
| Cheek (LatAm) | Beso en la mejilla | Greeting (2 per person) | Casual, universal |
| Lips | Beso en los labios | Romantic | Intimate |
| Hand | Beso en la mano | Chivalry, old-fashioned | Formal/romantic |
| Forehead | Beso en la frente | Affection, parental | Tender |
| Air kiss | Beso al aire | Greeting, social events | Polite |
| Stolen | Beso robado | Surprise, romantic | Literary |

## Cross-Reference: Greeting Customs

- **Spain**: 1 kiss (left cheek first, or right — varies by region)
- **Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, etc.**: 1 kiss (right cheek) or 2 (one each side)
- **Business contexts**: handshake often replaces kiss in Spain/LatAm
- **Family**: very common, all ages
- **Between friends**: women-to-women and women-to-men common; men-to-men = handshake in most countries (except Argentina where it's a hug + cheek kiss)

## Cultural Notes

- **"Beso" en contexto hispano**:
  - Beso en la mejilla = saludo (1 vez en España, 2 en LatAm, 1 en many parts)
  - Beso en los labios = romantic
  - Beso en la mano = cortesía/galantería (old-fashioned, charming)
- **Consent**: Antes de un beso romántico, es respetuoso preguntar "¿Puedo darte un beso?" — la norma actual (current norm). Important in modern dating.
- **첫 키스 (First kiss)**: Latin cultures often more openly discuss first kisses than Anglo cultures — a milestone, celebrated in songs/poems.
- **스페인어의 키스 시**: 처음 만난 사이, 친구, 가족 모두에게 cheek kiss. 자연스러움.
- **Consent culture**: Preguntar antes de un beso romántico es **siempre apropiado** (always appropriate), no importa la confianza.
