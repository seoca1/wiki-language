# amar

**Part of Speech:** verbo (verb, irregular)
**Level:** B1

**Definition:** To love (deep, romantic, unconditional). The strongest expression of love in Spanish.

**Conjugation:**
- yo amo (presente)
- tú amas
- él/ella ama
- nosotros amamos
- vosotros amáis (Spain)
- ellos/ellas aman
- Pretérito: yo amé
- Imperfecto: yo amaba
- Futuro: yo amaré
- Condicional: yo amaría
- Subjuntivo: yo ame, ames, ame, amemos, améis, amen

**Etymology:** From Latin "amare" (to love). Same root as French "aimer" and English "amiable/amateur." PIE root *h₂em- (to take).

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /aˈmaɾ/
- Syllables: a-mar
- Stress: 2nd syllable (a-MAR)
- Final "r" is soft (single tap)

**Memory Tip:** "**A**-**MAR**" = "to the sea (mar)" = to love deeply, like diving into the ocean. Or: "**AM**o" = "I love" sounds like "**am**" = I am = essence of being = love.

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "Te amar" → "**Te amo**" (1st person, not infinitive)
- ❌ "Yo te amar" → "**Yo te amo**" (always 1st person singular)
- ❌ "Yo amar tú" → "**Yo te amo**" (pronoun "te" required, "tú" not used as object)
- ❌ "Yo te amo mucho mucho" → redundant; one "mucho" is enough
- ❌ "Amar a Juan" without context → in Spanish, "amar a alguien" needs the "a" personal; "**Amo a Juan**" (I love Juan)
- ❌ "Estoy amando" → "**Te amo**" (amAR is non-progressive in Spanish; you don't say "I'm loving")
- ✅ "Te amo" (I love you — deep, romantic)
- ✅ "Te amaré siempre" (I will always love you)
- ✅ "Nos amamos" (We love each other)
- ✅ "Amo a mi familia" (I love my family)
- Note: **"Te quiero" vs "Te amo"** — querer = lighter (family, close friends, romantic); amar = deep (romantic, very close family). Choose based on relationship level.

**Register:** serious, deep, romantic. Use carefully.
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000 ES words)

**Visual:**
```
   amar (infinitive)
   ├── a- (root, "to love")
   └── -mar (verbal ending)
   ─────
   am-o (yo amo) = I love
   am-as (tú amas) = you love
   am-a (él ama) = he/she loves
```

## Examples

- "Te amo" — I love you (deep)
- "Te amaré siempre" — I'll always love you
- "Nos amamos desde hace 10 años" — We've loved each other for 10 years
- "Amo a mi madre" — I love my mother
- "Amar no es posesivo" — To love is not possessive
- "Te amo con todo mi corazón" — I love you with all my heart

## Mini-Dialogue (Confession)

```
A: Tengo que decirte algo.   ← 할 말이 있어.
B: ¿Qué pasa? Me asustas.   ← 무슨 일? 무서워.
A: No, tranquila. Te amo. Quiero estar contigo.   ← 아니, 괜찮아. 사랑해. 너와 함께하고 싶어.
B: (emocionada) Yo también te amo.   ← (감동) 나도 사랑해.
A: ¿En serio?   ← 정말?
B: Sí, desde hace tiempo.   ← 응, 한참 전부터.
```

## Cultural Notes

- **"Te amo" = 가장 깊은 로맨틱 표현**. 가족/친한 친구에게도 사용하지만, 주 사용은 연인 사이.
- **첫 "Te amo"**: 보통 관계 후 3-6개월. 너무 빠르면 부담.
- **스페인어 특유의 "Querer vs Amar" 구분** — 한국어 "좋아해/사랑해"와 유사한 단계 구조.
- **"Te quiero mucho"** = "I love you a lot" — between light and deep; safe for family, close friends, new relationships.
- **드라마, 노래**: "Te amo"는 보통 5-10 에피소드 이후 등장. 진지한 관계의 신호.
- **결혼**: 부부 사이에서는 일상적으로 사용. 가족에게도 자주.
- **남자가 여자친구에게**: 흔함. 여자가 남자친구에게도 점점 더 흔해짐 (세대 변화).
- **문자 메시지**: "tkm" (te quiero mucho) 줄임말 사용.
