# enamorado

**Part of Speech:** adjetivo (adjective) / sustantivo (noun)
**Level:** B1

**Definition:** 사랑에 빠진 — In love.

**Género:** enamorado (masc.) / enamorada (fem.)

## Examples

- "Estoy enamorado de ti" — I'm in love with you
- "Llevamos un año enamorados" — We've been in love for a year
- "Es mi enamorado" — He's my lover

## Related Terms

- [[amor]] — love
- [[amar]] — to love
- [[novio]] / [[novia]] — boyfriend/girlfriend

## Cultural Notes

**Diferencia entre "enamorado" y "novio"**:
- Enamorado = 감정 상태 ("I am in love")
- Novio = 관계 ("I am his boyfriend")

**Verbo 관련**: "enamorarse" (to fall in love) — reflexive.

**Variantes**:
- Enamorado/a — in love
- Enamoradísimo/a — madly in love
- Desenamorado/a — out of love
