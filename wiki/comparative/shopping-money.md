## Price & Discount Expressions

| Expression | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|------------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **How much?** | How much is this? | ¿Cuánto cuesta? / ¿Cuánto es? | いくらですか？ (Ikura desu ka?) | 얼마예요? (Eolmayeyo?) | 多少钱? (Duōshǎo qián?) |
| **Too expensive** | It's too expensive. | Es muy caro. / Me parece caro. | 高すぎます。 (Takasugimasu.) | 너무 비싸요. (Neomu bissayo.) | 太贵了。 (Tài guì le.) |
| **Can you lower it?** | Can you lower the price? | ¿Me puede hacer un descuento? / ¿Me lo deja en...? | 安くできますか？ (Yasuku dekimasu ka?) | 깎아 주세요. (Kkakka juseyo.) | 能便宜点吗? (Néng piányi diǎn ma?) |
| **Discount** | discount / % off | descuento / % de descuento | 割引 (waribiki) / ○%オフ (○% ofu) | 할인 (harin) / ○% 세일 | 打折 (dǎzhé) / ○折 (○ zhé) / 优惠 (yōuhuì) |
| **On sale** | on sale / on clearance | en oferta / rebajas / liquidación | セール (seeru) / バーゲン (baagen) | 세일 (seil) / 할인 행사 | 促销 (cùxiāo) / 打折 (dǎzhé) / 清仓 (qīngcāng) |
| **Buy X get Y** | buy one get one free | compra 1 lleva 1 / 2x1 | 1つ買うと1つ無料 (hitotsu kau to hitotsu muryou) | 1+1 / 원플원 (wonpeulwon) | 买一送一 (mǎi yī sòng yī) / 第二件半价 |
| **Final price** | final price / out the door | precio final / total | 税込み (zeikomi) / 合計 (goukei) | 최종가 (choejongga) / 총액 (chong'aek) | 实付 (shífù) / 总价 (zǒngjià) |
| **Tax included** | tax included | IVA incluido | 税込み (zeikomi) | 세금 포함 (segum poham) | 含税 (hánshuì) / 价格含税 |

## Payment Interactions

### Cash Payment
| Step | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Hand over cash** | Here you go. | Aquí tiene. | はい、これです。 (Hai, kore desu.) | 여기요. (Yeogiyo.) | 给您。 (Gěi nín.) |
| **Clerk counts** | Your change is $X. | Su cambio son $X. | お釣りはX円です。 (Otsuri wa X-en desu.) | 거스름돈 X원입니다. (Geoseureumdon X-won-imnida.) | 找您X元。 (Zhǎo nín X yuán.) |
| **Confirm** | Thank you. | Gracias. | ありがとうございます。 (Arigatou gozaimasu.) | 감사합니다. (Gamsahamnida.) | 谢谢。 (Xièxiè.) |

## Return/Exchange Policies

| Aspect | English (US) | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|--------|--------------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Window** | 30 days typical | 14-30 days (EU law: 14) | 7-30 days (store policy) | 7-30 days (law: 7 online) | 7 days (law: 7 no-reason online) |
| **Condition** | Unworn, tags on | Unused, packaging intact | 未使用・タグ付き (mishiyou tagutsuki) | 미착용, 택 부착 (michak-yong, taek buchak) | 未拆封, 吊牌完好 (wèi chāifēng, diàopái wánhǎo) |
| **Receipt required** | Yes (gift receipt ok) | Yes | 必要 (hitsuyou) / レシート必須 | 필수 (pil-su) / 영수증 지참 | 必须 (bìxū) / 发票/小票 |
| **Refund method** | Original payment | Original payment | 現金/カード返金 (genkin/kaado henkin) | 원결제수단 환불 (wongyeoljesudan hwanbul) | 原路退款 (yuánlù tuìkuǎn) |
| **Sale items** | Often final sale | Often no return | セール品返品不可 (seeru hin henpin fuka) | 세일 상품 교환/환불 불가 | 打折商品不退换 (dǎzhé shāngpǐn bù tuìhuàn) |
| **Online returns** | Free return label common | Free return (EU) | 送料自己負担 common | 무료 반품 common (Coupang etc) | 运费险 (yùnfei xiǎn) — return shipping insurance |

## Cultural Shopping Norms

| Norm | English (US/UK) | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|------|-----------------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Bargaining** | Flea markets only | Markets, street vendors | **Rare** (fixed price) | Markets (Namdaemun, Dongdaemun) | **Markets, street vendors** expected |
| **Trying on** | Expected for clothes | Expected | Expected (ask staff first) | Expected | Expected |
| **Bagging** | Self-bag (US) / staff (UK) | Staff bags | **Staff bags meticulously** | Staff bags | Self-bag or pay for bag |
| **Gift wrapping** | DIY or paid | Free/paid at dept stores | **Free, beautiful** (dept stores) | Free/paid | Free/paid (dept stores) |
| **Loyalty cards** | Common (points) | Common (puntos) | **Universal** (T-point, Ponta, Rakuten) | **Universal** (L.Point, Happy Point) | **WeChat/Alipay integrated** |
| **Sales tax** | Added at register (US) / Included (UK) | **Included** (IVA) | **Included** (zeikomi) | **Included** (VAT 10%) | **Included** (VAT 13%) |
| **Tipping** | No (retail) | No | **No** (rude) | **No** | **No** (Mainland) |
