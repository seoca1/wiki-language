# Variación diatópica del español (cross-linguistic comparison)
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

**Scope:** Comparison of Spanish dialectal variation across Spain + Latin America.
**Languages compared:** Español castellano, español mexicano, español rioplatense, español andino, español caribeño.
**Theme:** regional-linguistics, dialectal-marking, language-identity.

## Overview

The 500+ million speakers of Spanish across 20+ countries exhibit significant diatopic (regional) variation in pronunciation, lexicon, grammar, and pragmatics. This page synthesizes the key regional differences for the daily-exposure pool.

## Key dialectal axes

| Feature | España (castellano) | México | Argentina (rioplatense) | Caribe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd person plural | vosotros | ustedes | ustedes | ustedes |
| Vosotros use | universal | absent | absent | absent |
| "Vos" (voseo) | absent | absent | universal (rioplatense) | partial (Chile, etc.) |
| "Coger" meaning | "to take" | taboo usage | taboo usage | taboo usage |
| "Carro/coche/auto" | coche | carro | auto | carro |
| "Cena" (supper) | 21:00 | 19:00-20:00 | 21:00-22:00 | 20:00 |
