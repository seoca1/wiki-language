## Confession Expressions (The "Big Moment")

| Language | Standard Confession | Casual/Modern | Response (Accept) | Response (Reject) |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **English** | "I like you. Will you go out with me?" | "I'm into you. Wanna date?" | "Yes, I'd love to." / "I like you too." | "I'm flattered, but..." / "I see you as a friend." |
| **Spanish** | "Me gustas. ¿Quieres salir conmigo?" | "Me molas. ¿Quedamos?" | "Sí, me gustas tú también." | "Lo siento, no siento lo mismo." / "Solo amigos." |
| **Japanese** | "好きです。付き合ってください。" (Suki desu. Tsukiatte kudasai.) | "好きやねん。付き合おうや。" (Kansai) | "はい、お願いします。" / "私も好きです。" | "すみません、お付き合いは…" / "友達として好きです。" |
| **Korean** | "좋아해요. 사귀어요." (Joahaeyo. Sagwieoyo.) | "좋아해. 사귈래?" (Banmal) | "네, 저도 좋아해요." / "좋아요." | "미안해요, 전 그냥 친구로…" / "마음이 안 가요." |
| **Chinese** | "我喜欢你。我们交往吧。" (Wǒ xǐhuan nǐ. Wǒmen jiāowǎng ba.) | "我喜欢你。在一起吧。" (Wǒ xǐhuan nǐ. Zài yīqǐ ba.) | "好啊，我也喜欢你。" (Hǎo a, wǒ yě xǐhuan nǐ.) | "对不起，我只把你当朋友。" / "我不合适。" |

### Cultural Notes on Confession

| Culture | Confession Norm | Who Confesses | Timing |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| **English (US/UK)** | Direct or gradual; "talk" to define relationship | Either | After several dates (3-5 typical) |
| **Spanish** | Direct verbal; *novio/novia* = official | Traditionally man, now either | After chemistry confirmed; *beso* often seals it |
| **Japanese** | **Formal confession (告白 - kokuhaku) required** — no "dating" without it | Traditionally man; women ~30% | After 2-3 dates; *kokuhaku* = relationship start |
| **Korean** | **Confession (고백 - gobaek) expected** — "some" (썸) phase before | Either; men slightly more | After *some* phase (2-4 weeks); *gobaek* = official |
| **Chinese** | Verbal confession common; less ritualized than JP/KR | Either | After mutual interest clear; varies by generation |

## Breakup Expressions & Etiquette

| Language | Initiator Says | Recipient Says | Etiquette |
|----------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| **English** | "We need to talk." / "It's not working." / "I think we should break up." | "Okay." / "Why?" / "I understand." | In person preferred; text acceptable <3 months; ghosting frowned upon |
| **Spanish** | "Tenemos que hablar." / "Ya no funciona." / "Mejor lo dejamos." | "Vale." / "¿Por qué?" / "Lo entiendo." | In person mandatory; *cortar por WhatsApp* = very rude |
| **Japanese** | "話がある。別れよう。" (Hanashi ga aru. Wakareyou.) / "これ以上は無理。" | "わかった。" / "理由は？" / "うん。" | **In person mandatory**; *line de wakare* = extremely rude; return gifts (*omiyage*) |
| **Korean** | "할 말 있어. 헤어지자." (Hal mal isseo. Heeojaja.) / "더 이상 안 돼." | "알겠어." / "이유가 뭐야?" / "그래." | **In person mandatory**; *kakaotalk de heeojida* = very rude; return couple items |
| **Chinese** | "我们谈谈吧。分手吧。" (Wǒmen tán tán ba. Fēnshǒu ba.) / "不合适了。" | "好吧。" / "为什么？" / "我知道了。" | In person preferred; WeChat breakup = rude; return gifts less formal |
