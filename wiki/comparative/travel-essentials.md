## Cultural Notes (Travel Context)

| Culture | Key Travel Norms |
|---------|------------------|
| **English (UK/US/CA/AU)** | Queue strictly; tip 15-20% (US), 10-15% (UK/CA), none (AU); small talk with service staff |
| **Spanish (Spain)** | Late meals (lunch 14:00, dinner 21:00+); *sobremesa*; two kisses greeting; siesta closures (14-17) |
| **Spanish (LatAm)** | *Usted* default; bargaining in markets; *la cuenta* must ask; propina 10% |
| **Japanese** | No tipping (rude); cash preferred; IC cards (Suica); quiet on transport; *omotenashi* service |
| **Korean** | T-money card; *ppali ppali* (hurry); metal chopsticks; no tipping; age hierarchy in service |
| **Chinese** | Mobile pay (WeChat/Alipay) universal; no tipping; loud = lively; queue jumping common; *guanxi* helps |
