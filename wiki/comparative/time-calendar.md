### 12h vs 24h Format

| Language | Default Format | 24h Context |
|----------|----------------|-------------|
| **English (US)** | 12h (am/pm) | Military, medical, transport |
| **English (UK)** | 12h common, 24h growing | Transport, TV schedules, military |
| **Spanish** | 12h spoken, 24h written/schedules | Timetables, official, military |
| **Japanese** | **24h standard** (13:00, 23:59) | 12h only in casual speech with *gozen/gogo* |
| **Korean** | **24h standard** (13시, 23시 59분) | 12h with *am/pm* or *오전/오후* in speech |
| **Chinese** | **24h standard** (13:00, 23:59) | 12h with *am/pm* or *上午/下午* in speech |
