# Daily Life Activities — Cross-Language Comparison

**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese
**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

> How the 5 languages describe and conjugate routine verbs: eat, sleep, work, exercise, study.

## Conjugation Patterns Across Languages

| Pattern | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Tense marking** | Inflection (eat/ate) | Inflection (-ar/-er/-ir) | Inflection (た-form / て-form) | Inflection (았/었 + 다) | Aspect particle (了/过/着) |
| **Word order** | SVO | SVO | SOV | SOV | SVO |
| **Verb type** | Weak/strong (irregular) | -ar / -er / -ir (3 classes) | Group 1 (u-verbs), Group 2 (ru-verbs), irregular | 하다 (do-), regular, irregular | Aspect-verb compounds |
| **Politeness form** | none | usted/tú distinction | です/ます (desu/masu) | 해요/합쇼체 (-yeo / -seumnida) | 您 (nín) honorific |
| **Progressive** | be + -ing | estar + -ndo | て-form + いる (-te iru) | -고 있다 (-go itda) | 着 (zhe) |

## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: eat, sleep, work, exercise, study, walk, run, read, write, talk
- **Patterns**: Weak verbs (work-worked-worked) vs strong (eat-ate-eaten); progressive uses "be + V-ing"
- **Register notes**: No politeness marking on verbs; politeness comes from pronouns and intonation
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/English/vocabulary/daily-life-basics]]`

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: comer, dormir (o→ue), trabajar, estudiar, hacer ejercicio
- **Patterns**: Three conjugation classes (-ar / -er / -ir); stem-changing verbs (dormir → duermo)
- **Regional variations**: Vosotros (Spain) vs ustedes (LatAm) for "you all"; *vos* in Rioplatense
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Spanish/vocabulary/daily-life-basics]]`

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: 食べる (taberu), 寝る (neru), 働く (hataraku), 運動する (undō), 勉強する (benkyō)
- **Patterns**: Verb groups: Group 1 (godan: 食べる→食べた) and Group 2 (ichidan: 寝る→寝た); て-form for progressive
- **Keigo / politeness level**: ます-form (neutral polite) → plain form (casual); 尊敬語/謙譲語 for business
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Japanese/vocabulary/daily-life-basics]]`

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 먹다 (meokda), 자다 (jada), 일하다 (ilhada), 운동하다, 공부하다
- **Patterns**: 하다 (hada) attached to Sino-Korean nouns (공부 + 하다); vowel harmony on past tense (았/었)
- **Speech level**: 해요체 (-yeo, polite casual) / 합쇼체 (-seumnida, formal) / 해체 (plain)
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Korean/vocabulary/daily-life-basics]]`

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 吃 (chī), 睡 (shuì), 工作 (gōngzuò), 锻炼 (duànliàn), 学习 (xuéxí)
- **Patterns**: Bounded by aspect particles 了 (le = completed) and 过 (guò = experienced); 着 (zhe) for progressive
- **Register / honorifics**: 您 (nín) honorific "you"; verbs themselves don't inflect
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Chinese/vocabulary/daily-life-basics]]`

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Implication for Learners |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **Verb richness** | Spanish/Japanese/Korean conjugate extensively; Chinese barely conjugates (aspect particles instead) |
| **Past tense marker** | EN/ES/JP/KR all have distinct past forms; CH uses 了 (le) particle after verb |
| **Progressive** | EN "be+V-ing"; JP/KR use auxiliary verb (ている/고 있다); CH uses 着 (zhe) |
| **Politeness on verbs** | JP and KR conjugate politeness into verb form; EN/ES/CH leave it to pronouns/word choice |
