## Ordering Patterns (Grammar)

### English
- **Direct**: "I'll have the salmon." / "Give me the burger."
- **Polite**: "Could I get the...?" / "I'd like to order the..."
- **Modifiers**: "with/without", "on the side", "well-done/medium-rare"

### Spanish
- **Quisiera** + noun: *Quisiera la paella.* (conditional polite)
- **Me pone** + noun: *Me pone un café.* (familiar, common in Spain)
- **Para mí** + noun: *Para mí, el pescado.* (colloquial)
- **Sin / Con**: *Sin cebolla, con queso extra.*

### Japanese
- **~をお願いします** (~o onegaishimasu) — standard polite
- **~をください** (~o kudasai) — slightly more direct
- **~はありますか** (~wa arimasu ka) — "Do you have...?"
- **Counter + つ/個/本/枚** — mandatory for quantity

### Korean
- **~ 주세요** (~ juseyo) — standard polite request
- **~ 하나 주세요** (~ hana juseyo) — one please (counter often omitted in speech)
- **~ 빼주세요** (~ ppaejuseyo) — "without..." (remove)
- **~ 더 주세요** (~ deo juseyo) — "more please" (refills)

### Chinese
- **我要...** (Wǒ yào...) — direct "I want..."
- **给我来个...** (Gěi wǒ lái gè...) — colloquial "Give me a..."
- **来份...** (Lái fèn...) — "Bring a portion of..."
- **不要...** (Bú yào...) / **不放...** (Bú fàng...) — "no..." / "don't put..."

## Dining Etiquette Comparison

| Behavior | English (US/UK) | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|-----------------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Start eating** | "Bon appétit" / "Enjoy" | *Buen provecho* | *Itadakimasu* (hands together) | *Jal meokkesseumnida* (잘 먹겠습니다) | *Man man chi* (慢慢吃) / *Kuai chi* (快吃) |
| **Finish eating** | "Thank you" | *Gracias* | *Gochisousama deshita* | *Jal meogeosseumnida* (잘 먹었습니다) | *Chi bao le* (吃饱了) |
| **Pouring drinks** | Self or offer | Pour for others | Pour for others (never self) | Pour for others (two hands) | Pour for elders/superiors first |
| **Refusing drink** | "No thanks" | *No, gracias* | *Kekkou desu* (hand over glass) | *Jeongjunghi* (정중히) / hand over glass | *Bu yao le* (不要了) / cover glass |
| **Splitting bill** | Common (Venmo) | *A escote* / separate | Rare (senior pays) | *N빵* (N-way split) / senior pays | *AA制* (AA zhi) / host pays |
| **Tipping** | 15-20% (US) | 5-10% (Spain) / 10% (LatAm) | **None** (rude) | **None** (rare) | **None** (Mainland) / 10% (HK/TW) |
| **Slurping noodles** | Rude | Rude | **Polite** (enjoyment) | Acceptable | Acceptable (northern) |
| **Lifting bowl** | Rare | Rare | **Yes** (rice/miso) | **Yes** (rice/soup) | **Yes** (rice) |
| **Chopsticks in rice** | N/A | N/A | **Taboo** (funeral) | **Taboo** (funeral) | **Taboo** (funeral) |
| **Passing food** | Hands/utensils | Hands/utensils | Chopsticks→chopsticks = **taboo** | Spoon/chopsticks | Serving chop | Serving chopsticks (公筷) |

## Useful Phrases by Situation

### "I'm allergic to..."
- EN: "I'm allergic to [nuts/shellfish/gluten]."
- ES: "Soy alérgico/a a [los frutos secos/mariscos/gluten]."
- JP: "[ナッツ/甲殻類/グルテン]アレルギーがあります。" (*[nattsu/kakakurui/guruten] arerugī ga arimasu.*)
- KR: "[견과류/갑각류/글루텐] 알레르기가 있어요." (*[gyeongwaryu/kapgangryu/geulluten] alleogiga isseoyo.*)
- CH: "我对[坚果/甲壳类/麸质]过敏。" (*Wǒ duì [jiānguó/jiǎqiáolèi/fūzhì] guòmǐn.*)

### "Is this vegetarian/halal?"
- EN: "Is this vegetarian?" / "Is this halal?"
- ES: "¿Es vegetariano?" / "¿Es halal?"
- JP: "これはベジタリアン向けですか？" / "ハラール対応ですか？"
- KR: "이거 채식인가요?" / "할랄인가요?"
- CH: "这是素的吗？" / "这是清真的吗？"

### "Can you make it less spicy?"
- EN: "Can you make it less spicy?"
- ES: "¿Puede hacerlo menos picante?"
- JP: "辛くないようにできますか？" (*Karaku nai yō ni dekimasu ka?*)
- KR: "안 맵게 해주실 수 있어요?" (*An maepge haejusil su isseoyo?*)
- CH: "能不能不放辣椒？" (*Néng bùnéng bù fàng làjiāo?*)

### "I'd like to take this home."
- EN: "Can I get a to-go box?" / "Box this up, please."
- ES: "¿Me lo pone para llevar?" / "Para llevar, por favor."
- JP: "お持ち帰りできますか？" (*Omochikaeri dekimasu ka?*)
- KR: "포장해 주세요." (*Pojanghae juseyo.*)
- CH: "打包。" (*Dāobāo.*) / "打包带走。" (*Dāobāo dàizǒu.*)
