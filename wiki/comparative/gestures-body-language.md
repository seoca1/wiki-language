# Gestures & Body Language — Cross-Language Comparison

**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese
**Last updated**: 2026-07-19

> Nonverbal communication varies drastically. Same gesture = opposite meaning across cultures.

---

## Greeting Gestures

| Gesture | English (US/UK) | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|-----------------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Handshake** | Standard business | Business (firm, brief) | **Rare** (Westernized only) | Business (two hands, slight bow) | Business (light, brief) |
| **Bow** | No (except royalty) | No | **Standard** (15° casual, 30° business, 45° formal) | **Standard** (30° business, 45° formal, 90° 세배) | Slight nod (historical) |
| **Cheek kiss** | No (friends only) | **Standard** (2 kisses Spain, 1 LatAm) | **No** | **No** | **No** |
| **Hug** | Friends/family | Friends/family (close) | **No** | **No** (same-sex rare) | **No** |
| **Wave** | Hello/goodbye | Hello/goodbye | Hello/goodbye (palm down) | Hello/goodbye (palm down) | Hello/goodbye |
| **Nod** | Yes / acknowledgement | Yes / acknowledgement | Yes / greeting (slight) | Yes / greeting (slight) | Yes / greeting |

### Bowing Specifics

| Culture | Angle | When | Hands |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| **Japan** | 15° (eshaku) | Casual greeting, passing | At sides |
| | 30° (keirei) | Business, meeting | At sides |
| | 45° (saikeirei) | Formal apology, deep respect | At sides |
| **Korea** | 30° | Business, elders | At sides or clasped front |
| | 45° | Formal, seniors | Clasped front |
| | 90° (keunjeol/세배) | New Year, weddings, funerals | Clasped front, knees down |
| **China** | Slight nod | Business (historical bow) | At sides or clasped |

## Facial Expressions & Eye Contact

| Behavior | EN | ES | JP | KR | CH |
|----------|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Eye contact (speaking)** | Direct = honest | Direct = sincere | **Avoid** = respect (superiors) | **Avoid** = respect (elders) | **Avoid** = respect (seniors) |
| **Eye contact (listening)** | Direct = attention | Direct = interest | **Lower gaze** = attention | **Lower gaze** = respect | **Lower gaze** = respect |
| **Smiling** | Frequent, default | Frequent, social | **Mask** negative emotion | Polite, social | Polite, social |
| **Nodding while listening** | "I follow" | "I follow" | **"I hear you"** (not necessarily agree) | **"I hear you"** | "I hear you" |
| **Raising eyebrows** | Surprise / Question | Surprise / Question | Surprise / "Eh?" | Surprise / "Huh?" | Surprise |
| **Sucking teeth** | — | Annoyance / Disapproval | — | Annoyance (쯧) | Annoyance (啧啧) |
| **Clicking tongue** | — | — | — | Disapproval / Pity (쯧쯧) | — |

## Body Posture & Space

| Behavior | EN | ES | JP | KR | CH |
|----------|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Personal space** | Large (arm's length) | **Small** (close talk) | Medium (respect distance) | Small-Medium | **Small** (crowded norm) |
| **Touching (same sex)** | Minimal | **Common** (arm, shoulder) | Minimal | Minimal (changing) | Common (friends) |
| **Touching (opposite sex)** | Handshake / hug | Kiss / hug (friends) | **None** (business) | **None** (business) | Minimal |
| **Sitting posture** | Relaxed / cross-legged | Cross-legged OK | **Seiza** (formal) / legs together | Legs together / cross (casual) | Cross-legged OK |
| **Feet on furniture** | Casual (rude formal) | Rude | **Very rude** (feet = dirty) | **Rude** | Rude |
| **Showing soles** | Rude | Rude | **Very rude** | **Rude** | Rude |
| **Crossing arms** | Defensive / Cold | Defensive / Cold | Defensive / Thinking | Defensive / Cold | Defensive |
| **Leaning in** | Interest | Interest / Intimacy | **Intrusive** | Interest (close) | Interest |
| **Standing close in line** | Uncomfortable | **Normal** | **Normal** (ordered) | **Normal** | **Normal** (crowded) |
