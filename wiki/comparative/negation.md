# Negation — Cross-Language Comparison

**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese
**Last updated**: 2026-07-19

---

## Negation Architecture Overview

| Feature | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Sentential negation** | Auxiliary + not | *no* + verb | Verb suffix *-nai* / *-masen* | Verb suffix *-ji anta* / *-ji anhseumnida* | *bù* (不) / *méi* (没) + verb |
| **Constituent negation** | *not* + constituent | *no* + constituent | *wa* + *nai* / *dewa nai* | *an(i)* + verb / *mot* + verb | *bù* + adj/verb; *wú* (无) + noun |
| **Negative concord** | **No** (double neg = positive) | **Yes** (double neg = negative) | **No** (single neg) | **No** (single neg) | **No** (single neg) |
| **Negative polarity items** | any, ever, anymore | *nadie, nada, nunca* | *dare mo...nai, nani mo...nai* | *amudo...an(i), amugeotdo...an(i)* | *shéi dōu...bù, shénme dōu...bù* |

## Sentential Negation

### English
- **Structure**: Auxiliary + *not* (*n't*)
- **Present**: I **do not** / **don't** know. She **does not** / **doesn't** go.
- **Past**: I **did not** / **didn't** go.
- **Future**: I **will not** / **won't** go.
- **Modal**: I **cannot** / **can't** / **could not** / **couldn't** go.
- **Be**: I **am not** / **'m not** / **is not** / **isn't** / **are not** / **aren't** ready.
- **Have (aux)**: I **have not** / **haven't** seen it.
- **Have (main, BrE)**: I **haven't** a car. (AmE: I **don't have** a car.)

### Spanish
- **Structure**: *no* + verb (pre-verbal)
- **Present**: *No sé.* / *No voy.*
- **Past**: *No supe.* / *No fui.* / *No he ido.* (perfect)
- **Future**: *No iré.* / *No voy a ir.*
- **Subjunctive trigger**: *No creo que venga.* (subj), *No es verdad que venga.*
- **Double negation**: *No vi **nada**.* (I didn't see **anything**.) *Nadie **no** vino.* → *Nadie vino.* (Nobody came.)

### Japanese
- **Plain negative**: Verb stem + *nai* (ない) — *tabenai* (食べない = don't eat)
- **Polite negative**: Verb stem + *masen* (ません) — *tabemasen* (食べません)
- **Past negative**: *tabenakatta* (食べなかった) / *tabemasen deshita* (食べませんでした)
- **Adjectives**:
  - *i-adj*: *takai* → *takakunai* (高くない) / *takaku arimasen*
  - *na-adj/noun*: *kirei* → *kirei dewa nai* (綺麗ではない) / *kirei dewa arimasen*
- **Existential**: *aru* → *nai* (ない) / *arimasen*; *iru* → *inai* (いない) / *imasen*
- **Prohibitive**: *taberu na* (食べるな) — plain; *tabenaide kudasai* (食べないでください) — polite request

### Korean
- **Short form (plain)**: Verb stem + *ji anta* (지 않다) — *meokji anta* (먹지 않다)
- **Long form (polite)**: Verb stem + *ji anhseumnida* (지 않습니다) — *meokji anhseumnida*
- **Past**: *meokji anatda* (먹지 않았다) / *meokji anhasseumnida*
- **Adjectives**: Same pattern — *yeppeuji anta* (예쁘지 않다)
- **Existential**: *itda* (있다) → *eopda* (없다) — separate negative verb!
- **Prohibitive**: *meokji maseyo* (먹지 마세요) / *meokjima* (먹지 마)
- **Cannot (ability)**: *mot* (못) + verb — *mot meokda* (못 먹다) — distinct from *an(i)*

### Chinese
- **Standard negation**: *bù* (不) + verb/adj — *bù chī* (不吃), *bù hǎo* (不好)
- **Perfective/experiential negation**: *méi* (没) / *méiyǒu* (没有) + verb — *méi chī* (没吃), *méiyǒu qù* (没有去)
- **Future/volitional negation**: *bù* — *bù qù* (不去 = won't go)
- **Imperative negation**: *bié* (别) + verb — *bié chī* (别吃 = don't eat)
- **Adjectives**: *bù* — *bù dà* (不大), *bù cōngming* (不聪明)
- **Existential**: *méiyǒu* (没有) — *méiyǒu qián* (没有钱 = no money)

## Constituent Negation

### English
- *Not* + NP: **Not** John but Mary came.
- *Not* + PP: I saw him **not** in Paris but in London.
- *Not* + Adv: He drove **not** carefully but recklessly.
- *No* + N: **No** student passed. (determiner)

### Spanish
- *No* + constituent: *No Juan, sino María vino.*
- *Ningún* + N (negative determiner): *Ningún estudiante aprobó.*
- *Ni*... *ni* (neither...nor): *Ni Juan ni María vinieron.*

### Japanese
- *Wa* + negative: *Jon wa konakatta.* (As for John, he didn't come — contrastive)
- *De wa nai* (copula negation): *Jon de wa nai.* (It's not John.)
- *Mo* in negative: *Dare mo konakatta.* (Nobody came.)

### Korean
- *An(i)* / *mot* + verb: *an(i) meokda* (안/못 먹다)
- *An(i)* = don't/won't; *mot* = can't
- *Ani* as copula negative: *Jon-i aniya.* (존이 아니야 = It's not John.)

### Chinese
- *Bù* + VP/Adj: *bù shì* (不是 = not be), *bù xǐhuan* (不喜欢)
- *Méi* + V (perfective): *méi qù* (没去)
- *Wú* (无) + N (formal/written): *wú rén* (无人 = no one), *wú fǎ* (无法 = no way)

## Negative Polarity Items (NPIs) & Negative Concord

### English (No Negative Concord)
| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| something | **anything** / nothing |
| someone | **anyone** / nobody |
| somewhere | **anywhere** / nowhere |
| already | **any more** / **no longer** |
| somewhat | **at all** |

- *I didn't see **anything**.* (not *nothing*)
- *I **don't** have **any** money.* (not *no money* — though colloquial *I don't got no money* exists)

### Spanish (Negative Concord Obligatory)
| Positive | Negative (with *no*) | Negative (fronted) |
|----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| algo (something) | **nada** | **Nada** vi. |
| alguien (someone) | **nadie** | **Nadie** vino. |
| algún/alguno (some) | **ningún/ninguno** | **Ninguno** sirve. |
| también (also) | **tampoco** | **Tampoco** voy. |
| siempre (always) | **nunca / jamás** | **Nunca** voy. |
| algo de (some) | **nada de** | — |

- *No vi **nada**.* (I didn't see **nothing** = I saw nothing.)
- *Nadie **no** sabe.* → *Nadie sabe.* (Nobody knows.)

### Japanese (No Negative Concord — NPIs require negation)
| Positive | NPI (requires negation) | Negative fronted |
|----------|------------------------|------------------|
| 何か (nanika) | 何も...ない (nani mo...nai) | 何もない (nanika mo nai) |
| 誰か (dareka) | 誰も...ない (dare mo...nai) | 誰もいない (dare mo inai) |
| どこか (dokoka) | どこも...ない (doko mo...nai) | どこにもない (doko ni mo nai) |
| いつも (itsumo) | 決して...ない (kesshite...nai) / 全然...ない (zenzen...nai) | — |
| ちょっと (chotto) | 全く...ない (mattaku...nai) | — |

- *Nani mo tabenakatta.* (何も食べなかった = I didn't eat **anything**.)
- *Dare mo inai.* (誰もいない = **Nobody** is here.)

### Korean (No Negative Concord — NPIs require negation)
| Positive | NPI (requires negation) | Negative fronted |
|----------|------------------------|------------------|
| 무언가 (mueonga) / 뭐 (mwo) | 아무것도...안/못 (amugeotdo...an/mot) | 아무것도 없다 (amugeotdo eopda) |
| 누군가 (nugunga) | 아무도...안/못 (amudo...an/mot) | 아무도 없다 (amudo eopda) |
| 어딘가 (eodinga) | 아무데도...안/못 (amudeo...an/mot) | 아무데도 없다 (amudeo eopda) |
| 항상 (hangsang) | 결코...안/못 (gyeolko...an/mot) / 전적으로...안/못 | — |
| 조금 (jogeum) | 전혀...안/못 (jeonhyeo...an/mot) / 하나도...안/못 (hana do...an/mot) | — |

- *Amugeotdo an meogeosseo.* (아무것도 안 먹었어 = I didn't eat **anything**.)
- *Amudo an wasseo.* (아무도 안 왔어 = **Nobody** came.)

### Chinese (No Negative Concord — NPIs require negation)
| Positive | NPI (requires negation) | Negative fronted |
|----------|------------------------|------------------|
| 什么 (shénme) | 什么都不/没 (shénme dōu bù/méi) | 什么都没有 (shénme dōu méiyǒu) |
| 谁 (shéi) | 谁都不/没 (shéi dōu bù/méi) | 谁也没有 (shéi yě méiyǒu) |
| 哪里 (nǎlǐ) | 哪里都不/没 (nǎlǐ dōu bù/méi) | 哪里都没有 (nǎlǐ dōu méiyǒu) |
| 总是 (zǒngshì) | 从不/没 (cóng bù/méi) | — |
| 一点儿 (yīdiǎnr) | 根本不/没 (gēnběn bù/méi) / 一点儿都不/没 | — |

- *Wǒ shénme dōu méi chī.* (我什么都没吃 = I didn't eat **anything**.)
- *Shéi yě méi lái.* (谁也没来 = **Nobody** came.)

## Special Negation Constructions

### English
| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| **Negative question** | *Don't you like it?* / *Isn't she coming?* |
| **Tag question** | *You're coming, **aren't you**?* / *You're not coming, **are you**?* |
| **Negative imperative** | *Don't go.* / *Let's not go.* |
| **Negative infinitive** | *I told him **not to go**.* |
| **Negative gerund** | ***Not knowing** what to do, I waited.* |
| **Neither/Nor** | *I don't like it. **Neither do I**.* / ***Neither** John **nor** Mary came.* |

### Spanish
| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| **Negative question** | *¿No vienes?* / *¿No te gusta?* |
| **Tag question** | *Vienes, **¿verdad?** / **¿no?** / **¿verdad que sí?** |
| **Negative imperative** | *No vayas.* / *No te vayas.* (subjunctive) |
| **Negative infinitive** | *Le dije **que no fuera**.* (subj) / *Le dije **no ir**.* |
| **Ni...ni** | ***Ni** Juan **ni** María vinieron.* |
| **Tampoco** (neither) | *Yo **tampoco** quiero ir.* |

### Japanese
| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| **Negative question** | *Tabemasen ka?* (食べませんか？= Won't you eat?) |
| **Tag question** | *Taberu deshou?* (食べるでしょう？) / *Tabenai deshou?* |
| **Negative request** | *Tabenaide kudasai.* (食べないでください = Please don't eat.) |
| **Negative te-form** | *Tabenakute* (食べなくて = not eating, and...) |
| **Negative conditional** | *Tabenakereba* (食べなければ = if don't eat) |
| **Mo...nai** (not even) | *Ichido mo ikanai.* (一度も行かない = Never go even once.) |
| **Wa...nai** (contrastive) | *Kore wa tabenai.* (これは食べない = **This** I won't eat.) |

### Korean
| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| **Negative question** | *An meogeoyo?* (안 먹어요? = Don't you eat?) / *Meokji anayo?* |
| **Tag question** | *Meogeoyo, geureochji?* (먹어요, 그렇죠?) |
| **Negative request** | *Meokji maseyo.* (먹지 마세요 = Please don't eat.) |
| **Negative connective** | *Meokji anko* (먹지 않고 = not eating, and...) |
| **Negative conditional** | *Meokji aneumyeon* (먹지 않으면 = if don't eat) |
| **Aniya** (copula neg) | *Jon-i aniya.* (존이 아니야 = It's not John.) |
| **Mot** (cannot) vs **An** (don't) | *Mot meokda* (못 먹다 = can't eat) vs *An meokda* (안 먹다 = don't eat) |

### Chinese
| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| **Negative question** | *Bù chī ma?* (不吃吗? = Not eating?) / *Méi chī ma?* (没吃吗?) |
| **Tag question** | *Nǐ qù, duì ba?* (你去，对吧?) / *Nǐ bù qù, shì ba?* |
| **Negative imperative** | *Bié chī!* (别吃! = Don't eat!) / *Bù yào chī!* (不要吃!) |
| **Negative purposive** | *Wèile bù chī* (为了不吃 = in order not to eat) |
| **Negative conditional** | *Bù chī de huà* (不吃的话 = if not eat) / *Yào bù chī* (要不吃) |
| **Bù... yě** (not even) | *Yī cì yě méi qù.* (一次也没去 = Not even once went.) |
| **Lián... dōu bù/méi** (not even) | *Lián tā dōu bù zhīdào.* (连他都不知道 = Even he doesn't know.) |
| **Wú** (无) formal | *Wú fǎ* (无法 = no way/cannot), *wú rén* (无人 = no one) |

## Negation Scope & Ambiguity

### English
- *I didn't go because he called.* → Ambiguous: (1) Reason I didn't go = he called. (2) I went, but not because he called.
- *All that glitters is not gold.* → Traditional reading: Not all that glitters is gold. (Scope: ¬∀)

### Spanish
- *No fui porque me llamó.* → Same ambiguity. Disambiguate: *No fui **porque** me llamó* (reason) vs *Fui, **pero no** porque me llamó.*
- *No todo lo que brilla es oro.* (Not all that glitters is gold — clear ¬∀)

### Japanese
- *Kare ga yonda kara ikanakatta.* (彼が呼んだから行かなかった) — Scope ambiguous.
- *Zenbu ga kin dewa nai.* (全部が金ではない = Not all is gold.) — Clear ¬∀.

### Korean
- *Geu-ga bulleoseo an gasseo.* (그가 불러서 안 갔어) — Ambiguous.
- *Modeum ge keum-i aniya.* (모두가 금이 아니야 = Not all is gold.)

### Chinese
- *Wǒ bù qù shì yīnwèi tā jiào wǒ.* (我不去是因为他叫我 = The reason I'm not going is he called me.) — Scope clarified by *shì...de* focus construction.
- *Bù shì yīqiè shǎnshǎn de dōu shì jīn.* (不是一切闪闪的都是金 = Not all that glitters is gold.)

## Negation in Politeness / Face-Saving

| Language | Softening Negation | Example |
|----------|-------------------|---------|
| **English** | *I'm afraid not* / *I don't think so* / *Not really* | *Q: "Can you come?" A: "I'm afraid I can't."* |
| **Spanish** | *Creo que no* / *Me temo que no* / *No creo que pueda* | *¿Vienes? — Creo que no puedo.* |
| **Japanese** | *Chotto...* (implied no) / *Kangaete okimasu* (I'll consider it) | *Ashita kimasu ka? — Chotto... / Kangaete okimasu.* |
| **Korean** | *Geureoke hagi jom...* (그렇게 하긴 좀...) / *Jom...* | *Naeil wayo? — Geureoke hagi jom...* |
| **Chinese** | *Bù tài hǎo shuō* (不太好说) / *Kěngpà bù xíng* (恐怕不行) | *Nǐ néng lái ma? — Kěngpà bù xíng.* |
