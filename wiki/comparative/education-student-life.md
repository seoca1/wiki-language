### Korean Hakwon (학원) Phenomenon

- **Korea has world's highest private tutoring rate**
- **70%+ of students** attend hakwon (cram schools)
- **Parents spend** ~$2,000-10,000/year per child
- **"과외 (Gwaoe)"** = Private tutoring
- **"입시 지옥 (Ipsi Jiog)"** = Entrance exam hell
- **Late-night schools (심야 학교)** — study till 2 AM

### Chinese 高考 (Gaokao) Pressure

- **One exam** on June 7-8 (or 6/7 in famous memes)
- **9+ million students** per year
- **985/211 工程 (Programs)** = Top university designation
- **省份 (Province)** = Major score differences by region
- **高考状元 (Provincial #1)** = Fame, scholarships, university offers
- **Mobile phones banned** during exam week

### Japanese 受験 (Juken) Culture

- **塾 (Juku)** = Cram school
- **浪人 (Rōnin)** = Failed exam students (gap year)
- **東大 (Tōdai)** = University of Tokyo (target for many)
- **多浪 (Taran)** = Multiple gap years (negative connotation)
- **受験戦争 (Juken sensō)** = "Exam war" — intense competition

## School Cafeteria / 급식 / 給食

### Korean 급식 (School Lunch)

- **급식 (Geupsik)** = School lunch (standardized)
- **Menu planned months in advance** by nutritionists
- **Free for all students** (since 2021, all Korean schools provide free meals)
- **Salty, often criticized** — "해장국, 짜장면 금지" rules
- **급식 폭행 (School lunch bullying)** — social hierarchy in cafeteria

### Japanese 給食 (Kyushoku)

- **給食 (Kyūshoku)** = School lunch
- **Nutritionist-designed menus**
- **Customized for allergies**
- **Students serve each other** (当番制度)
- **No choice** = Eat what's served

### American School Lunch

- **Cafeteria or bring own**
- **Free/Reduced lunch** for low-income
- **Pizza Friday, Taco Tuesday** = common
- **Competitive food brands**

### Chinese School Lunch

- **食堂 (Shítáng)** = Cafeteria
- **Often unhealthy** (fried, oily)
- **"光明小卖部" memes** = backstreet vendors around schools
- **Strict lunch times** (30-40 min)
- **Parents worried** about food safety scandals

### Spanish School Lunch (Comedor)

- **Comedor escolar** = School cafeteria
- **Fixed menu** (Mediterranean diet emphasis)
- **Religious accommodations** common
- **Family-style seating**
