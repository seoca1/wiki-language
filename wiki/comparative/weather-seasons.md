**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese

## Per-Language Detail

### English

**Weather small talk**:
- "How's the weather today?"
- "It's raining cats and dogs."
- "It's a beautiful day."
- "I wish it would stop raining."

**Adjective forms**:
- **-y ending**: sunny, cloudy, rainy, snowy, windy
- **Irregular**: hot (not "hotty"), cold (not "coldy")

**Verb forms**:
- It's + adjective (it's hot/cold/raining)
- Verb + ing (it's raining/snowing)

### Spanish

**Weather small talk**:
- "¿Qué tiempo hace?" (What weather is there?)
- "Hace calor/frío" (It's hot/cold) — *hacer* = "to make/do"
- "Está lloviendo" (It's raining) — *estar* + gerund
- "Hace sol" (It's sunny) — *hacer sol* = "make sun"

**Verb patterns**:
- **Hacer + weather noun**: Hace calor/frio/sol/viento (It's hot/cold/sunny/windy)
- **Estar + gerundio**: Está lloviendo/nievando (It's raining/snowing)
- **Haber + noun**: Hay sol/nubes (There's sun/clouds)

**Idioms**:
- "Llueve a cántaros" — "It rains in pitchers" (heavy rain)
- "Hace un sol de justicia" — "It makes a sun of justice" (very sunny)

### Japanese

**Weather small talk**:
- 天気いいですね (Tenki ii desu ne) — "Nice weather, isn't it?"
- 今日はいい天気ですね (Kyou wa ii tenki desu ne) — "Today is nice weather, isn't it?"

**Adjective forms**:
- **i-adjectives**: 暑い (atsui), 寒い (samui), 涼しい (suzushii), 暖かい (atatakai)
- **na-adjectives**: 蒸し暑い (mushiatsui), 蒸し (mushi)
- **Counter for days**: 今日 (today), 明日 (tomorrow), 昨日 (yesterday)

**Compound weather**:
- 蒸し暑い (mushiatsui) — humid + hot (signature Japanese summer)
- 肌寒い (hadasamui) — slightly cold (skin-cold)
- 底冷え (sokobie) — cold to the bone

**Special features**:
- **梅雨 (tsuyu/baiu)**: Rainy season (June-July) — uniquely Japanese phenomenon
- **雪国 (yukiguni)**: Snow country (rural Japan, heavy snowfall regions)
- **桜前線 (sakura zensen)**: Cherry blossom front (spring indicator)

### Korean

**Weather small talk**:
- 날씨 좋네요 (Nalssi johneyo) — "Nice weather, isn't it?"
- 오늘 날씨 어때요? (Oneul nalssi eottaeyo?) — "How's today's weather?"

**Adjective forms**:
- **ㅂ irregulars**: 덥다 (hot) → 더워 (informal), 춥다 (cold) → 추워 (informal)
- **Regular**: 따뜻하다 (warm), 시원하다 (cool), 습하다 (humid), 건조하다 (dry)

**Special features**:
- **장마 (jangma)**: Monsoon season (June-August)
- **꽃가루 알레르기 (kkotgaru allergy)**: Spring pollen allergy (huge in Korea!)
- **초미세먼지 (chomise-myeonji)**: Fine dust / Yellow dust from China (Korea-specific concern)
- **장마 (jangma)**: Plum rain / Monsoon (Korea's rainy season)

### Chinese

**Weather small talk**:
- 今天天气真好 (Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo) — "Today's weather is really nice"
- 天气怎么样? (Tiānqì zěnmeyàng?) — "How's the weather?"

**Adjective forms**:
- **Verb patterns**: 下雨 (xià yǔ) = "rain" (literally "fall rain"), 下雪 (xià xuě) = "snow"
- **很 + adjective**: 很热 (hěn rè) = "very hot"
- **Nouns**: 晴天 (qíngtiān), 雨天 (yǔtiān)

**Special features**:
- **梅雨 (méiyǔ)**: Plum rain season (southern China, June-July)
- **沙尘暴 (shāchénbào)**: Sandstorm/dust storm (northern China, spring)
- **台风 (táifēng)**: Typhoon (southern coastal, summer/autumn)
- **二十四节气 (24 solar terms)**: 24 traditional Chinese seasonal divisions (春分, 秋分, 立夏, 立冬, etc.)

## Weather Small Talk Patterns

### Universal Pattern: "Nice weather today"

| English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| "Nice weather today, isn't it?" | "Buen tiempo hoy, ¿verdad?" | "今日いい天気ですね" | "오늘 날씨 좋네요" | "今天天气真好" |
| "Terrible weather" | "Tiempo horrible" | "いやな天気ですね" | "날씨 안 좋네요" | "天气真差" |

### Conversational Pattern (Japan)

Japanese weather conversation is famously formulaic:
1. **Opening**: 天気いいですね (Tenki ii desu ne) — "Nice weather, isn't it?"
2. **Confirmation**: はい、ほんとうによい天気ですね (Hai, hontou ni yoi tenki desu ne) — "Yes, really nice weather"
3. **Continuation**: こんな日は外出したいです (Konna hi wa gaishutsu shitai desu) — "On days like this I want to go out"

### Conversational Pattern (Korea)

Korean has similar formula:
1. **오늘 날씨 좋다 (Oneul nalssi jota)** — "Today's weather is nice"
2. **정말 좋은 날씨예요 (Jeongmal joeun nalssiyeyo)** — "Really nice weather"

### Conversational Pattern (China)

Chinese weather talk is very common:
1. **今天天气真好** (Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo) — "Today's weather is really nice"
2. **是啊，适合出游** (Shì a, shìhé chūyóu) — "Yeah, good for outings"
