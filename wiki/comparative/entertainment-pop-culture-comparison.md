## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: "Blockbuster", "Cult classic", "Indie film"
- **Patterns**: "Movie" vs "Film" — formal/informal distinction
- **Sources**: `[[entertainment-vocabulary]]`

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: "Telenovela" (LatAm TV staple, 100+ episodes common), "Cortometraje" (short film)
- **Patterns**: Heavy dubbing tradition; Spanish dubs often differ from subtitles
- **Sources**: N/A — Spanish lacks entertainment theme yet

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: "Anime" = specifically Japanese animation; "Manga" = Japanese comics
- **Patterns**: Industry vertical integration (manga → anime → merchandise); voice actors (声優 = seiyū) are celebrities
- **Sources**: `[[entertainment-vocabulary]]`

### Korean
- **Key terms**: "K-drama", "K-pop", "Webtoon" (digital comic); "Manhwa" = Korean comics
- **Patterns**: Streaming-driven industry; 정주행 (full-marathon viewing) cultural concept
- **Sources**: `[[entertainment-vocabulary]]`

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: "武侠" (wǔxiá = martial arts genre), "仙侠" (xiānxiá = xianxia/fantasy), "古风" (gǔfēng = classical style)
- **Patterns**: Genre-blending common (drama + variety show + games); 饭圈 (fànquān = fandom) culture
- **Sources**: N/A — Chinese lacks entertainment theme yet

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Insight |
|----------|---------|
| **Industry vertical** | JP anime/manga has unique manga-first pipeline; KR has webtoon-first; CN has web novel → drama pipeline |
| **Fandom vocabulary** | 推し活 (JP), 팬덤 (KR borrowed from EN), 饭圈 (CN) — different cultural vocabularies for same concept |
| **Subtitling vs dubbing** | JP/EN prefer subtitles; ES/KR heavy dubbing tradition; CN subtitles standard |
| **Genre hybridity** | C-drama often blends historical + fantasy; JP anime has more genre purity |

## Quick Reference Card

> **Watching foreign content**:
> - JP: Look for 声優 (seiyū = voice actor) credits — they're celebrities
> - KR: 정주행 = binge-watching all episodes at once
> - CN: 饭圈 = active fandom culture around series/stars
> - ES: Telenovelas are culturally unique to LatAm — different from US TV
