## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: "Pet" vs "Domestic animal"; "Livestock" (farm animals); "Wildlife"
- **Patterns**: Animal + sound verb ("the dog barks", "the cat meows")
- **Sources**: `[[animals-vocabulary]]`

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: "Mascota" (pet), "Animal doméstico" (domestic), "Fauna" (wildlife)
- **Patterns**: Gender agreement: gato/gata, perro/perra; -ar verb conjugation
- **Sources**: `[[animals-vocabulary]]`

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: 動物 (dōbutsu = animal); ペット (petto = pet); 野生動物 (yasei dōbutsu = wildlife)
- **Patterns**: Verb: 〜が鳴く (ga naku = animal cries); noun+の: 猫の鳴き声
- **Sources**: `[[animals-vocabulary]]`

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 동물 (dongmur), 반려동물 (baeryu-dongmur = companion animal); 가축 (gachuk = livestock)
- **Patterns**: Verb: 〜이/가 울다 (cry); noun compound: 강아지 (dog + puppy)
- **Sources**: `[[animals-vocabulary]]`

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 动物 (dòngwù), 宠物 (chǒngwù), 家畜 (jiāchù = livestock)
- **Patterns**: Verb: 〜叫 (jiào = to call/cry); noun + noun: 老虎 (lǎohǔ)
- **Sources**: N/A — Chinese lacks explicit animals theme yet

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Insight |
|----------|---------|
| **Hanja/漢字 shared vocabulary** | JP/KR/ZH share ~60% animal vocabulary (犬/개/狗, 馬/말/马, 魚/물고기/鱼) |
| **Native terms vs 漢字** | Korean often has native 순우리말 + 한자어 (소 vs 牛); JP similar (うま vs 馬) |
| **Pet culture** | All cultures have increasing pet ownership; specific species popular varies |
| **Mythological animals** | East Asian cultures (JP/KR/ZH) share 龍/용/龙 myth; Western cultures have different mythology |
