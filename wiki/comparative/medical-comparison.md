## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: "Patient" / "Doctor" / "Physician" distinction; "ER" (Emergency Room) vs "A&E" (UK)
- **Patterns**: Loanword "doctor" used universally; medical jargon often Greek/Latin (cardiology, neurology)
- **Sources**: N/A — English wiki lacks explicit medical theme yet

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: "Médico" (physician) vs "Doctor"; "Enfermo" (sick) vs "Enfermedad" (illness); "Salud" (health)
- **Patterns**: "Estoy enfermo" = I am sick; gender agreement: médico/médica, enfermo/enferma
- **Sources**: N/A — Spanish wiki lacks explicit medical theme yet

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: 病院 (byōin = hospital); 医院 (iin = clinic); 診療所 (shinryōjo = clinic/practice)
- **Patterns**: 医者 (isha) casual; 医師 (ishi) formal; "お大事に" (odaiji ni) = "take care" — common parting phrase
- **Sources**: N/A — Japanese wiki lacks explicit medical theme yet

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 병원 (byeongwon = hospital); 의원 (uiwon = clinic); 환자 (hwanja = patient)
- **Patterns**: 아프다 (apeuda = to be sick/painful); native word for body part + 아프다 pattern (배가 아파요 = stomach hurts)
- **Sources**: N/A — Korean wiki lacks explicit medical theme yet

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 医院 (yīyuàn = hospital); 诊所 (zhěnsuǒ = clinic); 大夫 (dàifu) = doctor (colloquial, esp. Northern)
- **Patterns**: 看 (kàn) used for "to see (a doctor)": 看病 = to see a doctor; 中医 (zhōngyī) = Traditional Chinese Medicine
- **Sources**: N/A — Chinese wiki lacks explicit medical theme yet

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Insight |
|----------|---------|
| **Native vs loanword doctor** | EN "doctor" (Latin), ES "médico" (Latin), but JP 医者 / KR 의사 / CN 医生 are native/Sino compound — no Latin loanword |
| **Sino character sharing** | JP/KR/CN share characters for medical terms (医, 病院/医院, 内科/내과/内科) — same written roots, different pronunciations |
| **Specialist naming** | CJK often use compound: 内科 (internal + department); EN/ES use Latin/Greek (cardiology / cardiología) |
| **Traditional medicine** | CN has 中医 (zhōngyī); KR has 한의학 (hanuihak); JP has 漢方 (kanpō); ES/EN lack equivalent integrated traditional system |
| **Body part + 아프다** | Korean unique pattern: noun + 아프다 (e.g., 배가 아파요 = stomach hurts); other languages use "have a [noun] ache" |
