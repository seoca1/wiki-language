## Cultural Descriptions

### Chinese New Year (春节)

**Chinese (CN)**: 春节 / 新年 (Chūnjié / Xīnián) — "Spring Festival"
**When**: Lunar calendar (Jan/Feb)
**Duration**: 7-15 days
**Activities**:
- Reunion dinner (年夜饭)
- Red envelopes (红包 hóngbāo)
- Firecrackers (鞭炮 biānpào)
- Spring couplets (春联 chūnlián)
- Lion/dragon dance

**Foods**: Dumplings (饺子), fish (鱼 — surplus), niangao (年糕 — high year), tangerines

### Korean New Year (설날)

**Korean (KR)**: 설날 (Seollal) — "New Year"
**When**: Lunar calendar (Jan/Feb)
**Duration**: 3 days
**Activities**:
- Sebae (세배) — formal bow to elders
- Tteokguk (떡국) — rice cake soup (mandatory!)
- Hanbok (한복) — traditional dress
- Yut nori (윷놀이) — traditional game
- Ancestor memorial (차례 jarye)

**Foods**: Tteokguk (rice cake soup), jeon (전 pancakes), yakgwa (약과), hangwa (한과)

### Japanese New Year (正月)

**Japanese (JP)**: お正月 (Oshōgatsu) / 正月 (Shōgatsu)
**When**: Jan 1-3
**Duration**: 3-7 days
**Activities**:
- Hatsumode (初詣) — first shrine visit
- Osechi ryori (おせち料理) — traditional foods
- Otoshidama (お年玉) — money gift for children
- Hatsu-nyu (初売り) — first sale of the year

**Foods**: Osechi (おせち), toshi-koshi soba (年越しそば — year-end noodles), ozoni (お雑煮)

### Spanish/Hispanic New Year

**Spanish (ES)**: Año Nuevo / Nochevieja
**When**: Dec 31 - Jan 1
**Duration**: 1 day celebration
**Activities**:
- 12 grapes at midnight (12 uvas) — one per bell chime
- Nochevieja in Spain (eat 12 grapes)
- Año Nuevo in Latin America (family gathering)

**Foods**: 12 grapes, traditional dishes per region

### English/Western New Year

**English (EN)**: New Year's Day / New Year's Eve
**When**: Jan 1 / Dec 31
**Activities**:
- Countdown (10...9...8...)
- Auld Lang Syne (Scotland origin)
- First-footing (UK tradition)
- Ball Drop (Times Square, US)
- Fireworks
