### Key Structural Differences

| Feature | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Base** | 1,000 (thousand) | 1,000 (mil) | 10,000 (万) | 10,000 (만) | 10,000 (万) |
| **Large number grouping** | 3 digits (thousand, million, billion) | 3 digits | 4 digits (万, 億, 兆) | 4 digits (만, 억, 조) | 4 digits (万, 亿, 兆) |
| **Two systems** | No | No | No | **Yes** (Sino-Korean + Native) | No (but 两 vs 二) |
| **Zero in compound** | "one hundred **and** one" | "ciento uno" | "hyaku ichi" | "baek il" / "baek hana" | "yībǎi líng yī" |

## Ordinal Numbers

| Position | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| 1st | first | primero / 1º | 一番目 (いちばんめ) | 첫째 / 제1 | 第一 (dì yī) |
| 2nd | second | segundo / 2º | 二番目 (にばんめ) | 둘째 / 제2 | 第二 (dì èr) |
| 3rd | third | tercero / 3º | 三番目 (さんばんめ) | 셋째 / 제3 | 第三 (dì sān) |
| nth | -th | -º / -ª | -番目 (-ばんめ) | -째 / 제- | 第- (dì-) |

- **Spanish**: *primero/tercero* drop -o before masculine noun (*primer libro, tercer piso*)
- **Japanese**: *dai-* prefix for formal (*dai-ikkai* = 第1回)
- **Korean**: *je-* (Sino) + *beonchae* for formal; native *cheot-/du-/se-* for informal
- **Chinese**: *dì-* prefix universally

## Counters / Classifiers (The Big Divergence)

> **English/Spanish**: No obligatory counters — "three apples" = *tres manzanas*
> **Japanese/Korean/Chinese**: **Mandatory** — noun cannot be counted without counter

### Japanese Counters (助数詞) — from `[[wiki/Japanese/vocabulary/jp-counters]]`

| Counter | Kanji | Use For | 1 | 2 | 3 | 10 |
|---------|-------|---------|---|---|---|---|
| General objects | 個 | Small objects, apples, eggs | ひとつ | ふたつ | みっつ | とお |
| People | 人 | Humans | ひとり | ふたり | さんにん | じゅうにん |
| Long objects | 本 | Pens, bottles, umbrellas | いっぽん | にほん | さんぼん | じゅっぽん |
| Flat objects | 枚 | Paper, tickets, shirts | いちまい | にまい | さんまい | じゅうまい |
| Machines/Cars | 台 | Cars, computers, TVs | いちだい | にだい | さんだい | じゅうだい |
| Floors | 階 | Building floors | いっかい | にかい | さんがい | じゅっかい |
| Times/Occurrences | 回 | Times doing something | いっかい | にかい | さんかい | じゅっかい |
| Minutes | 分 | Minutes | いっぷん | にふん | さんぷん | じゅっぷん |
| Hours (duration) | 時間 | Hours | いちじかん | にじかん | さんじかん | じゅうじかん |
| Age | 歳 | Years old | いっさい | にさい | さんさい | じゅっさい |
| Animals (small) | 匹 | Cats, dogs, fish | いっぴき | にひき | さんびき | じゅっぴき |
| Books | 冊 | Books, magazines | いっさつ | にさつ | さんさつ | じゅっさつ |
| Cups/Bowls | 杯 | Drinks, bowls of rice | いっぱい | にはい | さんばい | じゅっぱい |

**Phonological rules**: 
- *h/b/p* alternation (本: *hon/bon/pon*)
- *s/sh* alternation (分: *fun/pun*)
- *k/g* alternation (階: *kai/gai*)

| 마리 (mari) | Animals | 한 마리, 두 마리 | - | |
| 권 (gwon) | Books | 한 권, 두 권 | - | |
| 장 (jang) | Flat things (paper, tickets) | 한 장, 두 장 | - | |
| 대 (dae) | Machines, cars | 한 대, 두 대 | - | |
| 병 (byeong) | Bottles | 한 병, 두 병 | - | |
| 잔 (jan) | Cups/glasses | 한 잔, 두 잔 | - | |
| 그릇 (geureut) | Bowls | 한 그릇, 두 그릇 | - | |
| 번 (beon) | Times | 한 번, 두 번 | - | |
| 시 (si) | Hour (o'clock) | 한 시, 두 시 | - | Native for hour |

**Key rule**: Native Korean numbers (1-99) + native counters; Sino-Korean + Sino counters. *Age* uses native (*sal*) or Sino (*se*).

| 本 | běn | Books | 两本书 (2 books) |

## Number-Specific Cultural Notes

### Japanese
- **4 (shi/yon)** & **9 (ku/kyuu)** — avoided in hospitals, hotels, gifts (*shini* = death, *ku* = suffering)
- **Gift money**: odd numbers preferred (3, 5, 7); 4, 9, even numbers avoided
- **Counting gesture**: index finger = 1, index+middle = 2... thumb tucked = 5

### Korean
- **4 (sa)** — *tetraphobia*, 4th floor often labeled "F" in elevators
- **Chuseok/Seollal**: money in white envelopes, odd amounts
- **Age**: Korean age = (current year - birth year) + 1 (everyone gains a year on Jan 1)

### Chinese
- **4 (sì)** — avoided (sounds like 死 *sǐ* = death); 8 (bā) = lucky (发 *fā* = prosper); 6 (liù) = smooth (流 *liú*)
- **Phone numbers/license plates**: pay premium for 8s, avoid 4s
- **Red envelopes (hongbao)**: even amounts (except 4); 666, 888, 999 lucky

### Spanish
- **Billions**: *billón* = 10¹² (long scale) vs US *billion* = 10⁹ — financial translation trap
- **Decimal separator**: comma (1,5 = one point five); thousands separator: dot or space (1.000 or 1 000)

### English
- **And**: "one hundred **and** one" (UK) vs "one hundred one" (US)
- **Large numbers**: short scale (million=10⁶, billion=10⁹, trillion=10¹²)

## Practical Decision Guide for Learners

| If you learn... | Master first | Because |
|-----------------|--------------|---------|
| **Japanese** | *hon, mai, hiki, kai, fun, sai* + 1-10 irregular forms | Covers 80% of daily counting |
| **Korean** | Native 1-10 + *gae, myeong, mari, jang, beon, si, sal* | Native numbers + top 7 counters = survival |
| **Chinese** | *ge, wei, zhang, ben, tiao, liang, bei, ci* + 两 vs 二 rule | *ge* is universal fallback; 两 is mandatory before classifiers |
| **Spanish** | Cardinal 1-100 + *millón/millones* agreement (*un millón DE*) | *Millón* requires *de*; *cien* vs *ciento* |
| **English** | Ordinal suffixes (-st, -nd, -rd, -th) + "a/an" before numbers | "a hundred" vs "one hundred" |

## Cross-Language Practice Scenarios

### Scenario: Ordering 3 beers
- EN: "Three beers, please."
- ES: "Tres cervezas, por favor."
- JP: "ビールを三本ください。" (*biiru o san-bon kudasai*)
- KR: "맥주 세 병 주세요." (*maekju se byeong juseyo*)
- CH: "来三瓶啤酒。" (*lái sān píng píjiǔ*)

### Scenario: "I have 2 younger brothers"
- EN: "I have two younger brothers."
- ES: "Tengo dos hermanos menores."
- JP: "弟が二人います。" (*otouto ga futari imasu*) — *futari* (2 people)
- KR: "남동생이 둘 있어요." (*namdongsaeng-i dul isseoyo*) — native *dul*
- CH: "我有两个弟弟。" (*wǒ yǒu liǎng gè dìdi*) — *liǎng* + *ge*

### Scenario: "It's the 5th time I've been to Japan"
- EN: "This is my fifth time in Japan."
- ES: "Es la quinta vez que voy a Japón."
- JP: "日本に来るのは五回目です。" (*nihon ni kuru no wa go-kaime desu*)
- KR: "일본에 온 게 다섯 번째예요." (*ilbon-e on ge daseot beonjjae-yeyo*)
- CH: "这是我第五次来日本。" (*zhè shì wǒ dì wǔ cì lái Rìběn*)
