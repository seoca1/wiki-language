## Politeness Register — Quick Map

| Register | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Very formal** | (rare — "Sir/Madam") | usted | ございます / 敬語 | 합쇼체 (-seumnida) | 您 (nín) honorific |
| **Polite standard** | "Could you...?" | por favor + usted | です/ます (desu/masu) | 해요체 (-yeo) | 请 (qǐng) |
| **Casual** | "Gimme..." | tú + informal | Plain form (だ/である) | 반말 (banmal) | 无请 (wú qǐng) |

## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: please, thank you, sorry, excuse me, you're welcome, no problem
- **Patterns**: Politeness via modal verbs ("Could you...?"); "please" position flexible; "thanks" = informal
- **Register notes**: Minimal verb-based honorifics; register comes from word choice and tone
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/English/vocabulary/politeness]]`

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: por favor, gracias, lo siento, de nada, buenos días, mucho gusto
- **Patterns**: Usted (formal) vs tú (casual) pronoun drives politeness; *vos* in Rioplatense
- **Regional variations**: "Disculpe" (formal) vs "perdón" (less formal); Mexico favors "disculpe"; Spain prefers "perdone"
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Spanish/vocabulary/politeness]]`

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: お願いします, ありがとう, すみません, どういたしまして
- **Patterns**: ありがとう (casual) → ありがとうございます (polite) → ありがとうございました (past polite)
- **Keigo / politeness level**: 尊敬語 (sonkeigo = respect), 謙譲語 (kenjōgo = humble), 丁寧語 (teineigo = polite)
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Japanese/vocabulary/politeness]]`

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 주세요, 감사합니다, 죄송합니다, 실례합니다, 안녕하세요
- **Patterns**: Speech levels encoded in verb endings; 합쇼체 (-seumnida) most formal, 해체 (banmal) plain
- **Speech level**: Formal honorific (시/-si-), casual (반말/banmal), polite (해요체/-yeo)
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Korean/vocabulary/politeness]]`

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 请 (qǐng), 谢谢 (xièxiè), 对不起 (duìbuqǐ), 不好意思, 不客气
- **Patterns**: 请 (qǐng) softens commands; 您 (nín) honorific "you"; apology 双 level (对不起 formal > 不好意思 casual)
- **Register / honorifics**: 您 (nín) for elders; verbs don't conjugate; tone particles 吧/啊 soften requests
- **Sources**: `[[wiki/Chinese/vocabulary/politeness-zh]]`

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Implication for Learners |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **Politeness system** | JP/KR embed politeness in verb morphology; EN/ES/CH use pronouns or word choice |
| **"Sorry" split** | JP/KR/CN distinguish apology (对不起) vs "excuse me" (不好意思); EN/ES often overlap |
| **Thank you layers** | JP: ありがとう/ありがとうございます/ございました (3 levels); EN: thank you / thanks |
| **Honorific pronoun** | ES (usted) / JP (none, but verb honorifics) / KR (none, but 시 suffix) / CH (您/nín) |
