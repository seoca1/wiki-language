# Business Email — Cross-Language Comparison

**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese
**Last updated**: 2026-07-19

---

## Email Structure Comparison

| Component | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|-----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Subject line** | Concise, specific | Concise, specific | 【件名】 + topic | [제목] + topic | 【主题】 + topic |
| **Salutation** | Dear [Name], | Estimado/a [Name]: | [Name] 様 / [Company] [Title] 様 | [Name] [Title]님 / [Company] [Title]님 | 尊敬的 [Title] [Surname]： |
| **Opening** | I hope you're well. | Espero que esté bien. | いつもお世話になっております。 | 항상 수고가 많으십니다. | 希望您一切安好。 |
| **Purpose statement** | I'm writing to... | Le escribo para... | この度は〜の件でご連絡いたしました。 | 〜건으로 메일 드립니다. | 写信是关于…… |
| **Body** | Direct, bullet-friendly | Direct, formal | Context-first, then request | Context-first, then request | Context-first, then request |
| **Closing** | Best regards, | Atentamente / Cordialmente, | よろしくお願いいたします。 | 잘 부탁드립니다. | 顺颂商祺 / 此致 敬礼 |
| **Signature** | Name, Title, Company, Phone | Name, Cargo, Empresa, Tel | Name, 所属, 役職, 会社, TEL | 이름, 직함, 회사, 연락처 | 姓名, 职位, 公司, 电话 |

## Salutation Details

### English
| Relationship | Salutation |
|--------------|------------|
| Formal (unknown name) | Dear Sir/Madam, / To Whom It May Concern, |
| Formal (known name) | Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. [Last], |
| Colleague/known | Hi [First], / Hello [First], |
| Team/group | Dear Team, / Hi All, |

### Spanish
| Relationship | Salutation |
|--------------|------------|
| Formal (unknown) | Estimado Señor/Señora: / Muy señor mío: |
| Formal (known) | Estimado/a Sr./Sra./Dr./Dra. [Apellido]: |
| Known contact | Estimado/a [Nombre]: |
| Less formal | Hola [Nombre]: / Querido/a [Nombre]: |

**Regional**: *Estimado* (Spain) vs *Estimado/a* (LatAm gender agreement); *Usted* implied in formal

### Japanese (from `[Japanese/sources/business-email]`)
| Relationship | Salutation |
|--------------|------------|
| External (standard) | 株式会社〇〇 〇〇部 〇〇様 |
| External (known name) | 〇〇株式会社 〇〇様 |
| Internal (superior) | 〇〇部長 / 〇〇課長 / 〇〇様 |
| Internal (peer) | 〇〇さん |
| Reply to external | 〇〇様 いつもお世話になっております。 |

**Key**: Company → Department → Title/Name → 様 — never just name-sama for external

### Korean (from `[Korean/sources/daily-life-basics]`)
| Relationship | Salutation |
|--------------|------------|
| External (standard) | ㈜○○ ○○팀 ○○님 / ○○부장님 |
| External (known) | ○○님 |
| Internal (superior) | ○○팀장님 / ○○부장님 / ○○선배님 |
| Internal (peer) | ○○님 / ○○씨 |
| Reply to external | ○○님, 항상 수고가 많으십니다. |

**Key**: Company → Team → Title + 님 — *님* mandatory for respect; *씨* only for peers/subordinates

### Chinese
| Relationship | Salutation |
|--------------|------------|
| Formal (unknown) | 尊敬的先生/女士： |
| Formal (known) | 尊敬的 [职称] [姓]： (经理/总监/教授) |
| Known contact | 尊敬的 [姓] [职称]： |
| Less formal | [姓] [职称] 你好： / 各位同事： |

**Key**: Title follows surname (王经理, 李总监) — never first name in business

## Standard Phrases by Function

### Opening / Context Setting

| Function | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Reference previous contact** | Further to our conversation... | En referencia a nuestra conversación... | 先日はお話しいただき、ありがとうございました。 | 앞서 통화한 내용과 관련하여... | 承接我们上次的沟通…… |
| **Reference attachment** | Please find attached... | Adjunto encontrará... | 添付ファイルをご確認ください。 | 첨부파일 확인 부탁드립니다. | 请查收附件…… |
| **Reply acknowledgment** | Thank you for your email. | Gracias por su correo. | メールをいただき、ありがとうございます。 | 메일 주셔서 감사합니다. | 收到您的邮件，谢谢。 |
| **Apology for delay** | Apologies for the late reply. | Disculpe la demora. | 返信が遅くなり、申し訳ございません。 | 답장이 늦어져 죄송합니다. | 回复较晚，抱歉。 |

### Requests

| Function | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Polite request** | Could you please...? | ¿Podría... por favor? | 〜していただけますでしょうか。 | 〜해 주시겠어요? | 能否请您…… |
| **Formal request** | I would appreciate it if... | Agradecería que... | 〜ていただけますと幸いです。 | 〜해 주시면 감사하겠습니다. | 如果能……将不胜感激。 |
| **Ask for confirmation** | Please confirm... | Por favor confirme... | ご確認のほど、よろしくお願いいたします。 | 확인 부탁드립니다. | 请确认…… |
| **Ask for reply by date** | Please reply by [date]. | Responda antes del [fecha]. | [日付]までにご返信いただけますでしょうか。 | [날짜]까지 회신 부탁드립니다. | 请在[日期]前回复。 |

### Providing Information

| Function | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Inform** | I'd like to inform you that... | Le informo que... | 〜ことをお知らせいたします。 | 〜을 알려드립니다. | 特此通知…… |
| **Update** | Update on [topic]... | Actualización sobre... | 〜について進捗をご報告します。 | 〜 관련 진행상황 보고드립니다. | 关于……的进展更新： |
| **Share document** | I'm sharing... | Les comparto... | 〜を共有いたします。 | 〜를 공유합니다. | 分享…… |

### Closing / Next Steps

| Function | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Call to action** | Please let me know... | Por favor indíqueme... | ご検討のほど、よろしくお願いいたします。 | 검토 부탁드립니다. | 请告知…… |
| **Availability** | I'm available for a call... | Quedo a su disposición... | お時間のある際にご連絡いただけますと幸いです。 | 시간 되실 때 연락 주시기 바랍니다. | 方便时请联系…… |
| **Follow-up** | I'll follow up on [date]. | Haré seguimiento el [fecha]. | [日付]に再度ご連絡させていただきます。 | [날짜]에 다시 연락드리겠습니다. | 将于[日期]跟进。 |
| **Thank you** | Thank you for your time. | Gracias por su tiempo. | お時間をいただき、ありがとうございました。 | 시간 내주셔서 감사합니다. | 谢谢您的时间。 |

## Sign-off Formulas

### English
| Formality | Closing |
|-----------|---------|
| Very formal | Respectfully, / Yours faithfully, (UK: unknown recipient) |
| Formal | Sincerely, / Yours sincerely, (UK: known recipient) |
| Standard professional | Best regards, / Kind regards, / Regards, |
| Warm/known | Best, / Warm regards, / All the best, |
| Casual/Internal | Thanks, / Cheers, (UK/AU) |

### Spanish
| Formality | Closing |
|-----------|---------|
| Very formal | Atentamente, / Muy atentamente, |
| Formal | Cordialmente, / Un cordial saludo, |
| Standard professional | Saludos cordiales, / Atentamente, |
| Warm/known | Un abrazo, / Un saludo afectuoso, |
| Casual/Internal | Saludos, / Hasta luego, |

**LatAm**: *Quedo a su disposición* common before closing

### Japanese (from `[Japanese/sources/business-email]`)
| Formality | Closing |
|-----------|---------|
| Standard external | よろしくお願いいたします。 |
| Requesting action | ご検討のほど、よろしくお願いいたします。 |
| After meeting | お会いできましたら幸いです。よろしくお願いいたします。 |
| Internal (superior) | 以上、よろしくお願いいたします。 |
| Internal (peer) | よろしくお願いします。 / 以上です。 |

**Never omit** — even internal emails end with *yoroshiku onegaishimasu*

### Korean (from `[Korean/sources/daily-life-basics]`)
| Formality | Closing |
|-----------|---------|
| Standard external | 잘 부탁드립니다. / 감사합니다. |
| Requesting action | 검토 부탁드립니다. / 확인 부탁드립니다. |
| After meeting | 뵙기를 희망합니다. 잘 부탁드립니다. |
| Internal (superior) | 이상입니다. 잘 부탁드립니다. |
| Internal (peer) | 잘 부탁해요. / 수고하세요. |

**Note**: *수고하세요* (to someone staying) vs *수고하십시오* (to someone leaving) — email closing uses *수고하세요*

### Chinese
| Formality | Closing |
|-----------|---------|
| Very formal | 顺颂商祺 / 此致 敬礼 |
| Formal | 此致 敬礼 / 顺祝 工作顺利 |
| Standard professional | 祝好 / 顺祝 安好 |
| Known contact | 祝好 / 期待回复 |
| Casual/Internal | 谢谢 / 祝顺利 |

## Cultural Norms & Taboos

| Culture | Norm | Taboo |
|---------|------|-------|
| **English (US/UK)** | First-name basis after 1-2 exchanges | Overly stiff language with peers |
| **Spanish (Spain)** | *Usted* default; *tú* after invitation | *Tú* with strangers; omitting *Estimado* |
| **Spanish (LatAm)** | *Usted* longer; titles important | First name without title |
| **Japanese** | *Yoroshiku* mandatory; reply same day | Omitting *yoroshiku*; cc'ing without notice |
| **Korean** | *님* mandatory; hierarchy in cc order | *씨* for superiors; replying above superior |
| **Chinese** | Title + surname; *qing* (请) for requests | First name; omitting closing formula |

## Email Thread Etiquette

| Practice | EN | ES | JP | KR | CH |
|----------|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Reply-All default** | Context-dependent | Context-dependent | **Yes** (keep all in loop) | **Yes** (hierarchy) | Context-dependent |
| **Quote original** | Inline or top-post | Inline preferred | Top-post (full history) | Top-post (full history) | Top-post |
| **Subject prefix** | Re: | Re: / R: | Re: / 返信: | Re: / 회신: | Re: / 回复: |
| **Forward prefix** | Fwd: | Fwd: / Rv: | 転送: | 전달: | 转发: |
| **BCC for privacy** | Common | Common | Rare (groupware used) | Rare | Common |
| **Read receipt request** | Rare (pushy) | Rare | Rare | Rare | Sometimes |

The meeting would be [30/60] minutes via [Zoom/Teams/in-person].

Please let me know what works best.

Best regards,
[Name]

## Out-of-Office / Auto-Reply

| Language | Template |
|----------|----------|
| **English** | Thank you for your email. I am out of the office from [date] to [date] with limited email access. For urgent matters, please contact [Name] at [email/phone]. I will respond upon my return. |
| **Spanish** | Gracias por su correo. Estoy fuera de la oficina del [fecha] al [fecha] con acceso limitado al email. Para asuntos urgentes, contacte a [Nombre] en [email/teléfono]. Responderé a mi regreso. |
| **Japanese** | ご連絡ありがとうございます。[日付]〜[日付]まで不在にしております。緊急の場合は [名前] ([メール/電話]) までご連絡ください。戻り次第、順次ご返信いたします。 |
| **Korean** | 메일 감사합니다. [날짜]부터 [날짜]까지 부재중입니다. 긴급한 용무는 [이름] ([이메일/전화])로 연락 주시기 바랍니다. 복귀 후 순차적으로 회신드리겠습니다. |

## Quick Reference: Do / Don't

| Language | ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|----------|------|---------|
| **English** | Use "please" and "thank you"; be concise | Over-apologize; use "kindly" (dated) |
| **Spanish** | Use *usted* until invited to *tú*; include *Estimado* | Skip salutation; use *tú* with clients |
| **Japanese** | Always *yoroshiku onegaishimasu*; full signature | Omit *yoroshiku*; reply without context |
| **Korean** | Use *님*; hierarchy-aware cc; *수고하세요* closing | Use *씨* for seniors; reply-all without thought |
| **Chinese** | Title + surname; *qing* for requests; closing formula | First name only; skip closing; blunt requests |
