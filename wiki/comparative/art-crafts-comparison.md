## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: "Art" (general), "Fine arts" vs "Crafts" distinction; "Canvas" borrowed from Latin
- **Patterns**: "Arts and crafts" is a single compound concept; "visual arts" umbrella term
- **Sources**: N/A — English wiki lacks explicit art theme yet

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: "Arte" (art, gender-shifting: el arte / las artes); "Artesanía" = craftsmanship; "Obra" (work)
- **Patterns**: Strong distinction between "bellas artes" (fine arts) and "artesanía" (crafts); rich tradition of muralismo (muralism)
- **Sources**: N/A — Spanish wiki lacks explicit art theme yet

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: 美術 (bijutsu = fine art); 工芸 (kōgei = crafts); 芸術 (geijutsu = art broadly)
- **Patterns**: Strong craft tradition: 茶道 (sadō = tea ceremony), 華道 (kadō = flower arranging) treated as art forms; ukiyo-e (浮世絵) = woodblock prints
- **Sources**: N/A — Japanese wiki lacks explicit art theme yet

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 미술 (misul = fine art); 공예 (gongye = crafts); 예술 (yesul = art broadly)
- **Patterns**: Strong craft tradition: 瓷器 (celadon porcelain), 韩纸 (hanji paper); 분청사기 (bunchaengsagi) ceramics
- **Sources**: N/A — Korean wiki lacks explicit art theme yet

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 艺术 (yìshù = art); 工艺 (gōngyì = craft); 美术 (měishù = fine art)
- **Patterns**: 文房四宝 (wénfáng sìbǎo = Four Treasures of Study: brush, ink, paper, inkstone); 国画 (guóhuà = traditional Chinese painting)
- **Sources**: N/A — Chinese wiki lacks explicit art theme yet

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Insight |
|----------|---------|
| **Shared character roots** | JP/KR/CN share 藝/艺 characters for "art" via cultural sphere; ES "arte" and EN "art" share Latin root |
| **Fine arts vs crafts boundary** | EN maintains strict separation; CJK cultures often treat crafts (茶道, calligraphy) as high art |
| **Calligraphy status** | CN/JP/KR treat calligraphy as major art form; ES/EN treat it as decorative or hobby skill |
| **Traditional dress revival** | Hanfu (CN), Hanbok (KR), Kimono (JP) all see modern revival movements; ES/EN no equivalent contemporary folk dress |
| **Ink-based traditions** | CJK share brush-and-ink tradition; ES has "tinta china"; EN has no equivalent cultural practice |
