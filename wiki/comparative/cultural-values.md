## Core Value Clusters

### English (Anglo) — "Autonomy & Achievement"
| Value | Manifestation | Language Reflex |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Personal autonomy** | Self-reliance, choice, privacy | "My life, my choices"; *boundaries* |
| **Achievement** | Meritocracy, measurable success | *Goals, metrics, KPIs, hustle* |
| **Directness** | Say what you mean | *Call a spade a spade; no beating around the bush* |
| **Egalitarianism** | Flat hierarchies, first names | *First-name basis; "What do you think?"* |
| **Time = Money** | Linear, scheduled, punctual | *Don't waste my time; schedule it* |
| **Universalism** | Rules apply to all | *No exceptions; fair play* |

### Spanish (Hispanic) — "Relational & Expressive"
| Value | Manifestation | Language Reflex |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Personalismo** | Relationships > rules | *Confianza; "es mi amigo" = trust* |
| **Simpatía** | Pleasantness, harmony | *Ser simpático; evitar conflicto* |
| **Familismo** | Family as anchor | *La familia ante todo; compadrazgo* |
| **Honor / Vergüenza** | Reputation, shame | *Qué dirán; vergüenza ajena* |
| **Presentismo** | Present > future | *Mañana; "ahora" = flexible* |
| **Expresividad** | Emotion shown = authenticity | *Gesticular; tono alto = passion* |

### Japanese — "Harmony & Context"
| Value | Manifestation | Language Reflex |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Wa (和)** | Group harmony | *Kuuki o yomu; enryo; nemawashi* |
| **Omotenashi** | Anticipatory service | *O-sewa ni narimasu; kizukai* |
| **Giri / Ninjo** | Duty vs feeling | *Giri-choco; honne/tatemae* |
| **Mono no aware** | Impermanence appreciation | *Setsunai; shibui; wabi-sabi* |
| **Shikata ga nai** | Acceptance | *Shou ga nai; akirame* |
| **Shuudan seikatsu** | Collective lifestyle | *Ringi-seido; nemawashi* |

## Culture-Specific "Unspoken Rules"

### English (Anglo)
- ✅ Speak up in meetings
- ✅ Disagree with boss privately
- ✅ Leave on time = good boundaries
- ❌ Don't ask salary in interview
- ❌ Don't overshare personal problems at work

### Spanish
- ✅ Build *confianza* before business
- ✅ Accept late starts / long lunches
- ✅ Use *usted* until invited to *tú*
- ❌ Don't criticize family
- ❌ Don't rush relationship-building

### Japanese
- ✅ *Nemawashi* before meetings
- ✅ Say *sumimasen* constantly
- ✅ Accept business cards with two hands
- ❌ Don't point with chopsticks
- ❌ Don't blow nose in public
- ❌ Don't say "no" directly

### Korean
- ✅ Pour drinks for seniors (two hands)
- ✅ Bow depth = respect level
- ✅ Use honorifics by default
- ❌ Don't write names in red ink
- ❌ Don't stick chopsticks in rice
- ❌ Don't refuse first drink offer

### Chinese
- ✅ Give/receive cards with two hands
- ✅ Toast seniors first (*ganbei*)
- ✅ Decline praise modestly (*na li na li*)
- ❌ Don't give clocks (送钟 = 送终)
- ❌ Don't give green hats (戴绿帽 = cuckold)
- ❌ Don't split pears (分梨 = 分离)
