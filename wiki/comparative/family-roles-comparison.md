# Family Roles & Kinship — Cross-Language Comparison

**Languages**: English · Spanish · Japanese · Korean · Chinese
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

> How 5 languages describe family relationships. East Asian languages have particularly complex kinship systems; Western languages are simpler.

## Cultural Concepts

| Concept | English | Spanish | Japanese | Korean | Chinese |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Kinship complexity** | Simple (uncle = uncle) | Medium (tío/tía) | High (舅/姑 differentiated) | Highest (시/외 family separated) | High (舅父/姑父 differentiated) |
| **Seniority** | Less emphasized | Tío mayor vs menor | Very important (兄/弟, 姉/妹) | Critical (형/동생, 누나/동생) | Important (哥哥/弟弟) |
| **Honorific family terms** | Casual | Casual | Many (お父さん/お母さん) | Many (아버지/어머니) | Casual (爸爸/妈妈) |
| **Family register** | Limited | Tío vs tía | Extensive | Most extensive | Moderate |

## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Key terms**: "Relative" (general); "Immediate family" vs "Extended family"
- **Patterns**: Simple address; first name commonly used among family members
- **Sources**: `[[family-vocabulary]]`

### Spanish
- **Key terms**: "Familia" (family), "Pariente" (relative), "Primo" (cousin)
- **Patterns**: Gender agreement: tío/tía, primo/prima; "Sobrino" vs "sobrino" by gender
- **Sources**: `[[family-vocabulary]]`

### Japanese
- **Key terms**: 家族 (kazoku = family), 親戚 (shinseki = relatives); 義理 (giri = in-law)
- **Patterns**: Multiple words for same relation (兄/兄さん); strict seniority marking (兄/弟, 姉/妹)
- **Sources**: `[[family-vocabulary]]`

### Korean
- **Key terms**: 가족 (gajok), 친척 (chincheok); 시 (in-laws wife's family) vs 외 (in-laws husband's family)
- **Patterns**: Gender of speaker matters for sibling terms (형/오빠); strict seniority
- **Sources**: `[[family-vocabulary]]`

### Chinese
- **Key terms**: 家庭 (jiātíng = family), 亲戚 (qīnqi = relatives); 长辈 (zhǎngbèi = elder generation)
- **Patterns**: 舅/姑 distinction (maternal vs paternal in-laws); regional variations
- **Sources**: N/A — Chinese lacks family theme yet

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Insight |
|----------|---------|
| **Kinship complexity** | KR > JP > ZH > ES > EN — Korean has most differentiated kinship terms |
| **Seniority language** | All East Asian languages mark sibling seniority; Western languages don't |
| **In-law precision** | East Asian languages distinguish maternal vs paternal in-laws; English merges |
| **Family values** | East Asian cultures (especially CN/KR) emphasize hierarchy; Western cultures more egalitarian |
