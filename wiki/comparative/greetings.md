## Per-Language Detail

### English
- **Time-based**: Good morning (until ~12), Good afternoon (12-17), Good evening (17+), Good night (parting only)
- **Register**: Hi/Hey (casual) → Hello (neutral) → Good morning/afternoon/evening (formal)
- **Phatic**: "How are you?" = greeting, not literal question. Response: "Good, you?" / "Fine, thanks."
- **Body language**: Handshake (business), wave/nod (casual), hug (close)
- **Sources**: `[English/vocabulary/basic-vocabulary]`, `[English/culture/english-dating-culture]`

### Spanish
- **Time-based**: 
  - *Buenos días* (dawn–14:00)
  - *Buenas tardes* (14:00–sunset/20:00)
  - *Buenas noches* (after dark — both greeting & farewell)
- **Register**: *Hola* (universal) + time phrase; *Buenos días/tardes/noches* more formal
- **Regional**: 
  - Spain: *¿Qué tal?*, *¿Cómo vas?*
  - Mexico: *¿Qué onda?*, *¿Cómo estás?*
  - Argentina: *¿Cómo andás?*
- **Kiss greeting**: *Dos besos* (Spain, some LatAm) — women/women & women/men; men/men handshake or hug
- **Phone**: *¿Diga?*, *¿Aló?*, *Bueno* (Mexico)
- **Sources**: `[Spanish/vocabulary/basic-vocabulary]`, `[Spanish/culture/espana-vs-latinoamerica-registro]`

### Japanese
- **Time-based**: 
  - *Ohayou gozaimasu* (〜10:00/11:00)
  - *Konnichiwa* (11:00〜17:00)
  - *Konbanwa* (17:00〜)
- **Register**: 
  - Casual: *Ohayou*, *Yaa*, *Ossu* (male)
  - Polite: *Ohayou gozaimasu*, *Konnichiwa*, *Konbanwa*
  - Business: *Ohayou gozaimasu* (all day in some offices)
- **Bowing**: Angle = respect (15° casual, 30° standard, 45° deep apology/respect)
- **Phone**: *Moshi moshi* (casual) / *Hai, [Name] desu* (business)
- **Sources**: `[Japanese/vocabulary/basic-vocabulary]`, `[Japanese/culture/japanese-dating-culture]`

### Korean
- **Time-based**: No distinct morning/evening greetings — *Annyeonghaseyo* all day
- **Register**:
  - *Annyeong* (해체) — close friends, children
  - *Annyeonghaseyo* (해요체) — default polite (all ages, strangers, coworkers)
  - *Annyeonghasimnikka* (합쇼체) — formal (military, broadcast, very formal)
- **Bowing**: 30-45° standard; 90° (큰절) — New Year's, weddings, funerals
- **Phone**: *Yeoboseyo* (여보세요) — universal
- **Age check**: First meeting → "연세가 어떻게 되세요?" / "몇 살이세요?" determines speech level
- **Sources**: `[Korean/vocabulary/basic-vocabulary]`, `[Korean/culture/korean-dating-culture]`

### Chinese
- **Time-based**: 
  - *Zǎoshang hǎo* (早上好) — 5:00-10:00
  - *Xiàwǔ hǎo* (下午好) — 12:00-18:00
  - *Wǎnshang hǎo* (晚上好) — 18:00+
- **Register**:
  - *Nǐ hǎo* (neutral)
  - *Nín hǎo* (您好) — elders, superiors, formal
  - Title + *hǎo*: *Lǎoshī hǎo* (老师好), *Jīnglǐ hǎo* (经理好)
- **Phatic**: *Chī le ma?* (吃了吗？— "Have you eaten?") — classic greeting, esp. older gen
- **Body language**: Nod / slight bow (historical); handshake (business); no cheek kisses
- **Phone**: *Wéi* (喂) — answer; *Nín hǎo* — formal answer
- **Sources**: `[Chinese/vocabulary/body-zh]`, `[Chinese/sources/greetings-zh]`

## Key Contrasts (Synthesis)

| Contrast | Implication for Learners |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **Time-specific vs all-day** — EN/ES/JP/CH have time-based; KR uses *annyeonghaseyo* all day | In KR, don't overthink time. In JP, using *ohayou* at 3pm = awkward. |
| **Phatic "How are you?"** — EN/ES expect ritual response; JP/KR/CH less so | EN: "Good, you?" is mandatory. JP: *Ogenki desu ka* = genuine inquiry (often skipped). KR: *Jal jinaeseyo?* = genuine. CH: *Chi le ma?* = ritual but fading. |
| **Bowing depth hierarchy** — JP (15°/30°/45°) vs KR (30°/45°/90°) vs CH (slight nod) | JP: match partner's depth + 5°. KR: age gap = deeper bow. CH: business = handshake + slight nod. |
| **Kiss vs bow vs handshake** — ES (kiss), JP/KR (bow), EN/CH (handshake) | ES: mirror the other person (1 kiss? 2?). JP/KR: bow, don't offer handshake first unless they do. |
| **Phone greeting** — *Moshi moshi* (JP) vs *Yeoboseyo* (KR) vs *Wei* (CH) vs *Diga/Aló* (ES) vs *Hello* (EN) | Never use *moshi moshi* in business calls. *Yeoboseyo* is universal in KR. *Wei* can sound blunt — add *nín hǎo*. |

## Learner Decision Guide

- **First 10 phrases to master**:
  - EN: Hello / Hi, Good morning, Goodbye, Thank you, Sorry, How are you?, Nice to meet you, See you, Good night, Excuse me
  - ES: Hola, Buenos días/tardes/noches, Adiós, Gracias, Perdón, ¿Qué tal?, Mucho gusto, Hasta luego, Buenas noches, Disculpe
  - JP: こんにちは, おはようございます, こんばんは, さようなら, ありがとうございます, すみません, はじめまして, またね, おやすみなさい, 失礼します
  - KR: 안녕하세요, 안녕, 감사합니다, 미안합니다, 반갑습니다, 잘 가세요, 안녕히 계세요, 나중에 봐요, 잘 자요, 실례합니다
  - CH: 你好, 您好, 早上好, 晚上好, 再见, 谢谢, 不客气, 对不起, 很高兴认识你, 晚安
