# Korean Modern Life: KakaoTalk and Convenience

**Overview:** Modern Korean life revolves around mobile technology and convenience culture — KakaoTalk messenger, delivery services, and 24-hour convenience stores.

## Key Points

- **KakaoTalk (카카오톡)**: The dominant messaging app (>90% market share). Features:
  - Free messaging + voice/video calls
  - KakaoTalk Chat (open chat rooms) for groups
  - KakaoPay (mobile payment)
  - KakaoMap (navigation)
  - KakaoTaxi (ride-hailing, dominates market)
  - Emoticons and stickers (Kakao Friends characters: Ryan, Apeach, etc.)
- **Delivery Culture (배달 문화)**:
  - **배달의 민족 (Baemin)**: #1 food delivery app (acquired by Delivery Hero)
  - **쿠팡 (Coupang)**: E-commerce + delivery (Rocket delivery, same-day)
  - **배민커넥트 (Baemin Connect)**: Mart delivery, ultrafast
  - **치킨 (chikin, chicken)**: Late-night delivery staple — "치느님 (치킨 is god)" meme
  - **배달의 민족 (delivery people)**: Tip culture (사장님/sajangnim, boss)
- **24-Hour Convenience (편의점, pyeonuijeom)**:
  - Major chains: CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, Emart24
  - Services: ATM, ticket purchase, delivery pickup, photo printing
  - Food: kimbap, ramyeon, gyeran-mari, dosirak (boxed meals)
- **Online Shopping**: Coupang dominates, "로켓배송" (Rocket delivery) = same-day.
- **Streaming**: Netflix Korean content, YouTube, watcha, wavve.
- **Public Transport**: T-money card, subway (Seoul Metro 1-9), bus — T-money works everywhere.

## Language Connections

- `카카오톡` (KakaoTalk) — Messenger
- `배달` (baedal) — Delivery
- `치킨` (chikin) — Chicken (loanword)
- `편의점` (pyeonuijeom) — Convenience store
- `쿠팡` (Coupang) — E-commerce
- `지하철` (jihacheol) — Subway
- `택시` (taeksi) — Taxi
