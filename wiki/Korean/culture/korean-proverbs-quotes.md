# 한국 속담과 명언 (Korean Proverbs and Quotations)

**Overview:** Korean wisdom literature spans 1,600+ years — from 고려시대 (Goryeo era, 918–1392) poetry through 조선왕조 (Joseon Dynasty, 1392–1897) Confucian sayings to modern 격언 (gyeogeon, motto-style quotations). 속담 (sokdam, folk proverbs) reflect peasant farming wisdom + family values + Confucian ethics. 명언 (myeongeon, famous quotations) come from philosophers, independence fighters, presidents, K-pop/K-drama lines, and modern authors like 한강 (Han Kang, 2024 Nobel Literature laureate).

## Key Points

- **속담 (俗談, sokdam)**: Folk proverbs, often 4-syllable (사자성어-like). Rooted in agricultural life + Confucian family ethics. Categories:
  - **자연 (nature) wisdom**: "가뭄에 콩 나듯" (rare as beans in drought = very rare). "소와 돼지도 사이좋게 살다" (even cow and pig live in harmony = unlikely peace).
  - **인간 관계 (relationships)**: "발 없는 말이 천 리 간다" (words without feet travel 1,000 li = gossip spreads fast). "원숭이도 나무에서 떨어진다" (even monkeys fall from trees = experts err too).
  - **노력 + 인내 (effort + patience)**: "천 리 길도 한 걸음부터" (a 1,000-li journey starts with one step). "티끌 모아 태산" (dust piling up makes a mountain = small efforts accumulate).
  - **시간 (time)**: "오늘도 평범한 하루였다" (today was an ordinary day again = daily gratitude).
- **사자성어 (四字成語, sajaseongeo)**: 4-character idioms from Chinese classics but widely used in Korean. Examples: 일석이조 (一石二鳥, one stone two birds), 우물 안 개구리 (frog in the well = narrow-minded person).
- **살어리랏다 (Sae-ri-rat-da, Goryeo song)**: Famous Goryeo-era (918–1392) poem by 이성부 (Yi Seong-bu). Reflects the Korean literary imagination of the natural world — "If you ask me how I am, I live by flowing water, sleeping on stones."
- **고전 명언 (Classical Quotations)**: Yi Hwang (이황, Toegye 퇴계) + Yi I (이이, Yulgok 율곡) — Joseon Confucian scholars. Park Chung-hee (박정희) "되찾은祖国, 다시 뜨는 아시아" (recovered nation, rising Asia). Kim Gu (김구) "독립은 우리의 것이요, 통일은 우리의 책무다" (Independence is ours, unification is our duty).
- **현대 명언 (Modern Quotations)**: 1인칭 일상 명언 — "오늘도 평범한 하루였다" (today was an ordinary day). Author 한강 (Han Kang) — 2024 노벨 문학상 수상 — "인간이 온다는 건 좋은 일이다" (It is a good thing that humans come). K-pop quotes like BTS "Love myself" (나는 나를 사랑한다).
- **문학 + 시 (literature + poetry)**: 한국 현대 시 — 윤동주 (Yun Dong-ju) "서시 (서시)" — "죽는 날까지 하늘을 우러러 한 점 부끄럼이 없기를" (until the day I die, may I have no shame looking up at the sky). 한강 — 2024 Nobel Prize in Literature.

## Cross-language Connections

- **English**: "A stitch in time saves nine" ≈ Korean "오늘 먹으면 내일 굶는다" (eat today, starve tomorrow — about savings). "Practice makes perfect" ≈ "백 번 찍어 안 넘은 나무 없다" (no tree is too hard to chop after 100 strikes).
- **Spanish**: "Más vale tarde que nunca" (better late than never) ≈ Korean "늦더라도 하지 않는 것보다 낫다". Spanish refranes (folk proverbs) ≈ Korean 속담 — both rooted in rural daily life.
- **Japanese**: 日本ことわざ (kotowaza) ≈ 한국 속담 — both 4-syllable preference, share agricultural roots, Confucian influence. 차이: Japanese has 百聞は一見に如かず (hyakubun wa ikken ni shikazu, "100 hearings are not worth 1 seeing"), Korean has "백 번 듣는 것이 한 번 보는 것만 못하다".

## Ejemplos

1. **결혼식 축사 (wedding speech)**: "살어리랏다" (Goryeo poem) often quoted at Korean weddings — wishing the couple to live as harmoniously as nature flows.
2. **시험 + 공부 (test + study motivation)**: "천 리 길도 한 걸음부터" (a 1,000-li journey starts with one step) — common on exam prep flashcards. Also "티끌 모아 태산" (dust pile makes a mountain) = small efforts compound.
3. **K-pop 응원 (K-pop cheering)**: BTS song "Answer: Love Myself" lyrics quoted as inspirational quotes: "You showed me I have reasons I should love myself" (Korean: 나 자신을 사랑할 이유를 내게 보여줬어).
