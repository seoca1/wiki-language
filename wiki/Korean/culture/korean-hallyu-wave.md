# Korean Wave (한류 / Hallyu)

**Type:** culture, media, entertainment, social-phenomenon
**Region:** South Korea → global (Asia, Americas, Europe, Middle East)

## Overview

한류 (Hallyu, Korean Wave) refers to the global surge in popularity of South Korean culture since the late 1990s. It encompasses K-pop, K-dramas, K-films, Korean food, beauty, fashion, language, and lifestyle. Korea's cultural soft power has become a major economic driver, contributing billions to the GDP and shaping global youth culture.

## Timeline of Hallyu

### First Wave (1990s-2000s): K-Dramas

- **1997**: "신세대" (New Generation) term coined
- **1999-2000**: "가을동화" (Autumn Fairy Tale) — K-drama hits East Asia
- **2002**: "겨울연가" (Winter Sonata) — Japan mania, "Yonsama" Bae Yong-joon phenomenon
- **2003**: "대장금" (Jewel in the Palace) — historical drama, pan-Asian success
- **2004-2010**: First Hallyu peak in Japan, China, Taiwan, Southeast Asia

### Second Wave (2010-2015): K-Pop Globalization

- **2009-2012**: "Gangnam Style" (강남스타일) by PSY — 2012, first YouTube video to reach 1 billion views
- **2010-2012**: Wonder Girls, BIGBANG, 2NE1 — K-pop in America
- **2012**: "江南STYLE" → 1B views, then 2B, 3B, 4B+ — global phenomenon
- **2013**: K-pop in Billboard Hot 100 (PSY)
- **2014-2015**: "K-pop in Latin America" via YouTube

### Third Wave (2016-2019): BTS + Cultural Power

- **2013-2017**: BTS (방탄소년단) growth from small label to global
- **2017**: BTS "DNA" enters Billboard Hot 100
- **2018-2019**: BTS performs at American Music Awards, Billboard Music Awards
- **2019**: BTS "Map of the Soul: Persona" — first Korean album to go #1 Billboard 200
- **2017-2019**: BLACKPINK formation, "Ddu-Du Ddu-Du" (1B+ views)
- **2019**: K-pop in United Nations (BTS speech on youth)
- **2019**: "Parasite" — Bong Joon-ho film wins Palme d'Or + 4 Oscars
- **2019**: "K-pop wave 3.0" peak in global awareness

### Fourth Wave (2020-2024): Diversification

- **2020-2021**: BTS "Dynamite" (full English), "Butter" #1 on Billboard Hot 100
- **2021**: "Squid Game" (오징어 게임) — Netflix, #1 most-watched series
- **2022**: "Extraordinary Attorney Woo" (이상한 변호사 우영우) — autism representation
- **2022**: BLACKPINK, NewJeans, IVE, (G)I-DLE — fourth-gen explosion
- **2023**: "KPop Demon Hunters" — first Western animation featuring K-pop
- **2024**: BTS military service begins; solo careers accelerate (V/Jungkook solos)

### Training System (연습생 제도)

- **연습생 (Trainee)**: Pre-debut member, 2-7 years training
- **Age at debut**: Usually 15-19 (some as young as 14)
- **Daily schedule**: 12-18 hours dance/vocal/language lessons
- **Diet control**: Strict (some "diet pills" scandals)
- **Monthly evaluations**: Ranked
- **Survival shows**: Produce 101, I-LAND, Girls Planet
- **Trainee-to-debut ratio**: ~1 in 10
- **Contract length**: 5-7 years
- **Termination disputes**: 30+ legal battles (BTS-JH vs. HYBE, EXO members vs SM, etc.)

### Drama Industry Facts

- **16-episode format** — typical (vs. US 8-13)
- **Pre-produced or live** — both
- **Simultaneous filming** — actors juggle 2-3 dramas
- **Shooting hours**: 24-hour days common
- **Lead actor fee**: ~100M won (~$75K) per drama
- **Ratings**: Live ratings matter less (streaming era); but brand value high
- **Kissing scenes**: rare until recently (2010s)
- **No nudity / minimal profanity** — broadcast rules

## Cultural Concepts (관련 개념)

### 정 (Jeong, 情)

- Deep relational bond built over time
- "Korean soul" — deepest social glue
- Distinct from Western "love" — broader, deeper
- Sources: [../comparative/untranslatable-concepts](../../comparative/untranslatable-concepts.md)

### 눈치 (Nunchi, 眼識)

- Social awareness / reading the room
- Crucial for Korean social navigation
- Sources: [../comparative/untranslatable-concepts](../../comparative/untranslatable-concepts.md)

### 한 (Han, 恨)

- Collective sorrow / unresolved grief
- Historical trauma (colonialism, war, division)
- Sources: [../comparative/untranslatable-concepts](../../comparative/untranslatable-concepts.md)

### 화 (Hwa, 火) vs Cool (쿨)

- **Hot (화끈)**: Passionate, intense
- **Cool (쿨하다)**: Calm, reserved (opposite)
- Korean "cool" = emotional control

### 빨리빨리 (Ppali-ppali)

- "Hurry hurry" — speed culture
- Economic miracle driver
- Fast delivery, fast food, fast everything
- Sources: [../comparative/cultural-values](../../comparative/cultural-values.md)

- **Study pressure (학업 스트레스)**: High suicide rate among teens
- **Hell Joseon (헬조선)**: "Hell Korea" — youth discontent
- **Gender inequality**: Still significant
- **병역 (military service)**: 18-21 months (men) — disrupts careers
- **Chaebol concentration**: 10 families own 50% of economy
- **N-po generation** (N포세대): Giving up dating, marriage, kids, jobs, etc.
