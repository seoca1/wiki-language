# 오늘 날씨가 좋아요

**Part of Speech:** sentence (statement)

**Definition:** The weather is good today.

**Etymology:** 오늘 (today) + 날씨 (weather) + 가 (subject marker) + 좋 (good) + 아요 (polite ending).

**Romaja:** oneul nalssiga joayo

> 발음 변동:
> - 날씨가: ㄹ + ㅆ → ㄹㅆ (날씨)
> - 좋아요: ㅎ + ㅏ → ㅏ (좋 → 조)

## Examples

- 오늘 날씨가 좋아요. — The weather is good today.
- 오늘 날씨가 나빠요. — The weather is bad today.
