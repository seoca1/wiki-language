# 싸다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Cheap / inexpensive.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** ssada

---

## Examples

- **싸고 좋아요.** — Cheap and good.
- **이거 싸요?** — Is this cheap?
- **더 싼 거 있어요?** — Is there something cheaper?
