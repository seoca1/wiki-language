# 신호등

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Traffic light.

**Etymology:** 한자 (信號燈) 기반

**Romaja:** sinhodeung

---

## Examples

- **신호등을 건너요.** — Cross at the traffic light.
- **신호등 빨간불이에요.** — The light is red.
- **신호등에서 기다리세요.** — Wait at the traffic light.
