# 방

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Room.

**Etymology:** 한자 (房) 기반

**Romaja:** bang

---

## Examples

- **방이 어디예요?** — Where is the room?
- **제 방이에요.** — This is my room.
- **방이 깨끗해요.** — The room is clean.
