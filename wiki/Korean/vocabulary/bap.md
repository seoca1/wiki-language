# 밥

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** Rice; meal (cooked rice as staple food).

**Etymology:** Pure Korean (순 우리말).

**Romaja:** bap

## Examples

- 밥 먹었어요? — Have you eaten? (common greeting)
- 밥 먹자. — Let's eat.

## Related Terms

- [[물]] — water
- [[음식]] — food
- [[아침]] — morning

## Cultural Notes

"밥 먹었어요?"는 한국式 인사로 "How are you?"에 가까움. 한국 식문화에서 밥 = 끼니(meal) 자체를 의미.
