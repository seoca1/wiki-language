# 맵다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Spicy / hot (in flavor).

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** maepda

---

## Examples

- **너무 매워요.** — Too spicy.
- **매운 거 좋아해요.** — I like spicy food.
- **안 매운 거 주세요.** — Not spicy, please.
