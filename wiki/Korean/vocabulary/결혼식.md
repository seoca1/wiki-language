# 결혼식

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Wedding ceremony.

**Etymology:** 한자 (結婚式) 기반

**Romaja:** gyeolhonsik

---

## Examples

- **결혼식에 초대받았어요.** — I was invited to a wedding.
- **결혼식 축하해요!** — Congratulations on your wedding!
- **결혼식이 내일이에요.** — The wedding is tomorrow.
