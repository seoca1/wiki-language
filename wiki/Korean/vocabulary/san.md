# 산

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Mountain.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** san

---

## Examples

- **산에 가요.** — Let's go to the mountain.
- **산이 높아요.** — The mountain is high.
- **한국 산이 유명해요.** — Korean mountains are famous.
