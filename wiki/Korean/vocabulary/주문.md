# 주문

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun) / 동사 (verb)

**Definition:** Order (food/services).

**Etymology:** 한자 (注文) 기반

**Romaja:** jumun

---

## Examples

- **주문할게요.** — I'll order.
- **주문하신 거 나왔어요.** — Your order is ready.
- **메뉴 주문할게요.** — I'd like to order from the menu.
