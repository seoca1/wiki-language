# 가격

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Price / cost.

**Etymology:** 한자 (價格) 기반

**Romaja:** gagyeok

---

## Examples

- **가격이 얼마예요?** — How much is it?
- **가격이 비싸요.** — The price is high.
- **싼 가격이에요.** — It's a low price.
