# 비싸다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Expensive / costly.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** bissada

---

## Examples

- **너무 비싸요.** — Too expensive.
- **비싼 거예요.** — It's expensive.
- **비싸지 마세요.** — Don't make it expensive.
