# 오늘

**Part of Speech:** adverb / noun

**Definition:** Today.

**Etymology:** Pure Korean (순 우리말).

**Romaja:** oneul

## Examples

- 오늘 뭐 해요? — What are you doing today?
- 오늘 날씨 좋아요. — Today's weather is nice.
