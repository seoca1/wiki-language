# 짐

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Luggage / baggage.

**Etymology:** 한자 (짐) 기반

**Romaja:** jim

---

## Examples

- **짐이 많아요.** — I have a lot of luggage.
- **짐 봐주세요.** — Please watch my luggage.
- **짐 찾으러 가요.** — I'm going to pick up my luggage.
