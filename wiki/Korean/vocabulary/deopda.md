# 덥다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Hot (weather) / warm.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** deopda

---

## Examples

- **오늘 너무 더워요.** — Today is too hot.
- **여름엔 더워요.** — It's hot in summer.
- **더운 날씨 싫어요.** — I don't like hot weather.
