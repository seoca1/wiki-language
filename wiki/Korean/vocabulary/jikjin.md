# 직진

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun) / 부사 (adverb)

**Definition:** Straight ahead.

**Etymology:** 한자 (直進) 기반

**Romaja:** jikjin

---

## Examples

- **직진하세요.** — Go straight.
- **직진하면 보여요.** — It's straight ahead.
- **100미터 직진이에요.** — It's straight for 100 meters.
