# 예약

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun) / 동사 (verb)

**Definition:** Reservation / to reserve.

**Etymology:** 한자 (豫約) 기반

**Romaja:** yeyak

---

## Examples

- **예약했어요.** — I have a reservation.
- **예약하고 싶어요.** — I want to make a reservation.
- **예약 취소할게요.** — I'll cancel the reservation.
