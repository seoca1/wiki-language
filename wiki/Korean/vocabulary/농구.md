# 농구

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Basketball.

**Etymology:** 한자 (籠球) 기반

**Romaja:** nonggu

---

## Examples

- **농구를 해요.** — I play basketball.
- **농구 선수** — basketball player
- **농구 좋아하세요?** — Do you like basketball?
