# 입국심사

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Immigration (inspection at airport/border).

**Etymology:** 한자 (入國審查) 기반

**Romaja:** ipguk-simsa

---

## Examples

- **입국심사 어디예요?** — Where is immigration?
- **입국심사 기다리세요.** — Wait for immigration.
- **여권 보여주세요 (입국심사).** — Show your passport (immigration).
