# 환전

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun) / 동사 (verb)

**Definition:** Currency exchange / to exchange money.

**Etymology:** 한자 (換錢) 기반

**Romaja:** hwanjeon

---

## Examples

- **환전 어디서 해요?** — Where do I exchange money?
- **환전해 주세요.** — Please exchange money.
- **환전소가 어디예요?** — Where is the exchange counter?
