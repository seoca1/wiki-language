# 맛있다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Delicious / tasty.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** masissda

---

## Examples

- **너무 맛있어요!** — So delicious!
- **이거 맛있어요?** — Is this tasty?
- **한국 음식이 맛있어요.** — Korean food is delicious.
