# 달다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Sweet.

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** dalda

---

## Examples

- **너무 달아요.** — Too sweet.
- **달고 맛있어요.** — Sweet and delicious.
- **덜 달게 해주세요.** — Make it less sweet, please.
