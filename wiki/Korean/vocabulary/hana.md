# 하나

**Part of Speech:** numeral

**Definition:** One (native Korean, used standalone or for hours).

**Etymology:** Pure Korean.

**Romaja:** hana

> One of 두 native Korean counting systems (순 우리말 vs Sino-Korean 일).

## Examples

- 하나, 둘, 셋 — one, two, three
- 한 시 — 1 o'clock (Sino-Korean 시)
- 하나 주세요. — One, please.

## Cultural Notes

Native Korean numbers (하나~열) are used for hours, age, and small counts. Sino-Korean numbers (일~십) are used for dates, money, addresses, minutes.
