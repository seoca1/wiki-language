# 아침밥

**Part of Speech:** 명사 (noun)

**Definition:** Breakfast.

**Etymology:** 한자 (아침) + 순 우리말 (밥)

**Romaja:** achim-bap

---

## Examples

- **아침밥 먹었어요?** — Did you eat breakfast?
- **아침밥 같이 먹어요.** — Let's have breakfast together.
- **아침밥 뭐 먹어요?** — What do you have for breakfast?
