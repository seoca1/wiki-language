# 춥다

**Part of Speech:** 형용사 (adjective)

**Definition:** Cold (temperature).

**Etymology:** 순 우리말

**Romaja:** chupda

---

## Examples

- **오늘 너무 추워요.** — Today is too cold.
- **겨울엔 추워요.** — It's cold in winter.
- **추운 날씨 싫어요.** — I don't like cold weather.
