## Cultural Insights

- **한복 (Hanbok)**: Traditional Korean dress with distinct design for men (저고리+바지) and women (저고리+치마)
- **Fashion industry**: K-fashion is global force; Seoul Fashion Week is major international event
- **Dress codes**: Korean workplace formal (정장), casual (캐주얼); university students favor 깔끔+트렌디 style
- **Sizes**: 한국 사이즈 = Asian sizing; US/EU sizes run larger (US 6 = KR 55-66)
