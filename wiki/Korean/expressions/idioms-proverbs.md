# Expressions: Idioms & Proverbs (한국어)

> **Theme:** Idioms & Proverbs
> **Level:** B2-C2
> **Standard:** 표준어 (Standard Korean)
> **Romanization:** Romaja

Korean idioms (관용구) and proverbs (속담).

---

## 일석이조

**Part of Speech:** 사자성어 (4-character idiom)
**Definition:** "One stone, two birds" — efficiency.
**Form**: Ilseogi-jo.

### Usage

Describing efficiency.

### Cultural Notes

- **구조**: 一 (one) + 石 (stone) + 二 (two) + 鳥 (birds)
- **Origin**: Chinese origin
- **Modern**: Common in business
- **Sample**: 일석이조의 효과를 얻다 (Gain two-birds effect)

---

## 그림의 떡

**Part of Speech:** 관용구 (idiom)
**Definition:** "Rice cake in a painting" — useless thing.
**Form**: Geurim-ui tteok.

### Usage

Something useless/unreachable.

### Cultural Notes

- **구조**: 그림 (painting) + 떡 (rice cake)
- **의미**: Edible only if real (not in painting)
- **Sample**: 내 꿈은 그림의 떡이야 (My dream is unattainable)

---

## 우물 안 개구리

**Part of Speech:** 속담 (proverb)
**Definition:** "Frog in a well" — narrow perspective.
**Form**: Umul-an gaeguri.

### Usage

Criticizing narrow worldview.

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: Chuang Tzu (Chinese philosopher)
- **의미**: Limited perspective = thinks world is small
- **Sample**: 우물 안 개구리가 되지 말자 (Don't be a frog in a well)

---

## 등잔 밑이 어둡다

**Part of Speech:** 속담
**Definition:** "It's dark under the lamp" — closest relationships often ignored.
**Form**: Deungjan mit-i eodupda.

### Usage

What we overlook.

### Cultural Notes

- **의미**: Closest = most overlooked
- **Sample**: 부모님 건강, 등잔 밑이 어둡다 (Parents' health — overlooked)

---

## 살어리 살어리랏다

**Part of Speech:** 시가 (poetry)
**Definition:** "Live! Live!" — ancient Goryeo poem.
**Form**: Sal-eori sal-eoriratda.

### Usage

Celebration of life.

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: Goryeo era ( 고려 시대) folk song
- **의미**: Celebrating life, joy
- **Sample**: 살어리살어리랏다 청산에 살어리랏다 (Live! Live! On the blue mountain)

---

## 가는 말이 고와야 오는 말이 곱다

**Part of Speech:** 속담
**Definition:** "If outgoing words are beautiful, returning words are beautiful" — kindness begets kindness.
**Form**: Ganeun mari gowaya oneun mari gopda.

### Usage

Encouraging kind speech.

### Cultural Notes

- **의미**: Speak kindly, get kindness back
- **Sample**: 가는 말이 고와야... (kind words...)
- **Universal**: All cultures have equivalent

---

## 호랑이도 제 말 하면 온다

**Part of Speech:** 속담
**Definition:** "Speak of the tiger and it appears" — superstitious coincidence.
**Form**: Horangi-do je mal hamyeon onda.

### Usage

When mentioned person appears.

### Cultural Notes

- **의미**: Mentioning someone brings them
- **English equivalent**: "Speak of the devil"
- **Sample**: 호랑이도 제 말 하면 온다 — 진짜 왔네 (Tiger came — really here)

---

## 벼는 익을수록 고개를 숙인다

**Part of Speech:** 속담
**Definition:** "The riper the rice, the lower it bows" — humility in wisdom.
**Form**: Byeneun ig-eulsurok gogaereul su-ginda.

### Usage

Encouraging humility.

### Cultural Notes

- **의미**: Wise people are humble
- **Sample**: 벼는 익을수록 고개를 숙인다 (The wiser, the humbler)
