# Expressions: Restaurant & Dining (한국어)

> **Theme:** Restaurant & Dining
> **Level:** A2-B2
> **Standard:** 표준어 (Pyojuneo, Standard Korean)
> **Romanization:** Romaja

Restaurant/dining expressions — ordering, allergies, bill, Korean dining etiquette.

---

## 메뉴판 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Menu, please" — upon sitting at restaurant.
**Form:** Menupan juseyo.

### Variations

- **메뉴판 주세요 (Menupan juseyo)** — "Menu please"
- **영어 메뉴 있어요? (Yeongeo menyu isseoyo?)** — "English menu?"
- **추천 메뉴가 뭐예요? (Chucheon menyuga mwoyeyo?)** — "What's your recommendation?"

### Cultural Notes

- **한국 식당 입장**: "어서오세요!" (Welcome!) — 인사치레
- **물 (Mul)**: 무료 물 (most Korean restaurants)
- **반찬 (Banchan)**: 무료 반찬 (side dishes, refillable)
- **Tipping**: NO tipping (would be refused or confused)
- **Order timing**: Call staff "저기요!" (Jeogiyo! = "Excuse me!")

## 이거 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (order)
**Definition:** "I'll have this" — placing an order.
**Form:** Igeo juseyo.

### Variations

- **이거 주세요 (Igeo juseyo)** — "This one please"
- **___ 주세요 (___ juseyo)** — "___ please"
- **___ 한 개 주세요 (___ han gae juseyo)** — "One ___"
- **같은 거 주세요 (Gateun geo juseyo)** — "Same thing please"

### Cultural Notes

- **Quantity counter**: 한 개 (one), 두 개 (two), 세 개 (three)
- **Set menus**: 세트 (set) often better value
- **Lunch sets**: 점심 특선 (lunch special) — cheaper than dinner
- **Customization**: 한국 = less customization (정해진 메뉴 fixed)
- **Allergies**: Always inform before ordering

## ___ 알레르기 있어요

**Part of Speech:** expression (health)
**Definition:** "I have a ___ allergy" — allergy disclosure.
**Form:** ___ allereugi isseoyo.

### Variations

- **땅콩 알레르기 있어요 (Ttangkong allereugi isseoyo)** — "Peanut allergy"
- **글루텐 프리요 (Geuluten peuriyo)** — "Gluten-free"
- **비건이에요 (Bigeon-ieyo)** — "I'm vegan"
- **___ 있어요? (___ isseoyo?)** — "Does it have ___?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Severe allergies**: Always inform (especially nuts, shellfish)
- **Common allergies**: 땅콩 (peanut), 우유 (milk), 새우 (shrimp), 게 (crab), 밀 (wheat)
- **EpiPen (에피펜)**: Carry if severe
- **Allergy cards**: Translated (very helpful)
- **Korean food note**: 고추장 (gochujang), 된장 (doenjang) often contain allergens

## 계산서 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (payment)
**Definition:** "Check, please" — asking for the bill.
**Form:** Gyesanseo juseyo.

### Variations

- **계산서 주세요 (Gyesanseo juseyo)** — "Check please"
- **계산해 주세요 (Gyesanhae juseyo)** — "Please calculate"
- **따로 계산해 주세요 (Ttaro gyesanhae juseyo)** — "Separate checks please"
- **같이 계산할게요 (Gachi gyesanhalgeyo)** — "Pay together"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: 계산서 (check), 영수증 (receipt)
- **No tipping**: Service included
- **Card payment**: Almost universal (현금 receipt less common)
- **Pay together**: Common in Korean BBQ (one pays, others transfer)
- **이체 (Transfer)**: Friends split via bank transfer apps (토스, 카카오페이)

## 포장해 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Please wrap it up" — leftovers/doggy bag.
**Form:** Pojanghae juseyo.

### Variations

- **포장해 주세요 (Pojanghae juseyo)** — "Please wrap it"
- **포장 가능해요? (Pojang ganeunghaeyo?)** — "Can I take it home?"
- **일부만 포장 (Ilbu-man pojang)** — "Just part wrapped"

### Cultural Notes

- **Doggy bag culture**: Common in Korea (반찬 leftover often wrapped)
- **Cost**: Usually free or 500-1000원 for container
- **Eco-friendly**: Many restaurants use disposable containers
- **Korean BBQ**: Take-home raw meat sometimes questioned (but allowed)
- **반찬 (Banchan)**: Take-home banchan is normal

## 이게 뭐예요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "What is this?" — ingredient inquiry.
**Form:** Ige mwoyeyo?

### Variations

- **이게 뭐예요? (Ige mwoyeyo?)** — "What is this?"
- **안에 뭐 들어있어요? (Ane mwo deureoisseoyo?)** — "What's inside?"
- **매워요? (Maewoyo?)** — "Is it spicy?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Allergen awareness**: Korean restaurants increasingly label ingredients
- **Hidden ingredients**: 고추장 (gochujang), 된장 (doenjang), 간장 (ganjang) all have allergens
- **Spice level**: 한국 음식 often spicy (very spicy 매운맛, mild 안매운맛)
- **Cross-contamination**: Shared kitchen surfaces
- **Ask**: Better to ask than assume

## 정말 맛있어요!

**Part of Speech:** expression (compliment)
**Definition:** "It's really delicious!" — praising food.
**Form:** Jeongmal masisseoyo!

### Variations

- **정말 맛있어요! (Jeongmal masisseoyo!)** — "Really delicious!"
- **맛있게 잘 먹었습니다! (Masisge jal meogeotseumnida!)** — "I ate well!" (after meal)
- **또 올게요! (Tto olgeyo!)** — "I'll come again!"

### Cultural Notes

- **Complimenting food**: "잘 먹었습니다" (jal meogeotseumnida = "I ate well") after meal
- **사장님 (Owner) compliment**: Going back to thank owner appreciated
- **Photo rule**: Some restaurants forbid food photos (rare but check)
- **Tipping**: NOT customary
- **Korean BBQ**: Complimenting the meat quality appreciated
