# Expressions: Business (한국어)

> **Theme:** Business & Workplace
> **Level:** B2-C1
> **Standard:** 표준어 (Business Korean)
> **Romanization:** Romaja

Business expressions — emails, meetings, contracts, titles, workplace etiquette.

---

## 처음 뵙겠습니다

**Part of Speech:** expression (greeting)
**Definition:** "Nice to meet you (first time)" — formal first meeting.
**Form:** Cheoeum bwopseumnida.

### Variations

- **처음 뵙겠습니다 (Cheoeum bwopseumnida)** — "First time meeting" (very formal)
- **만나서 반갑습니다 (Mannaseo bangapseumnida)** — "Glad to meet you"
- **잘 부탁드립니다 (Jal butakdeumnida)** — "Please treat me well"
- **환영합니다 (Hwan-yeonghamnida)** — "Welcome"

### Cultural Notes

- **명함 교환 (Myeongham gyohwan)**: Business card exchange (with 두 손)
- **인사 (Insa)**: Bow (15° casual, 30° standard)
- **존댓말 (Jondaetmal)**: Honorific language (mandatory)
- **First-name basis**: NEVER in business (always + 님 nim)
- **"잘 부탁드립니다"**: Multifunctional closing/greeting

## 시간 내 주셔서 감사합니다

**Part of Speech:** expression (gratitude)
**Definition:** "Thank you for taking the time" — closing meeting.
**Form:** Sigan nae jusyeoseo gamsahamnida.

### Variations

- **시간 내 주셔서 감사합니다 (Sigan nae jusyeoseo gamsahamnida)** — "Thank you for your time"
- **바쁘신 중에도 시간 내 주셔서 감사합니다 (Bappeusin jung-edo sigan nae jusyeoseo gamsahamnida)** — "Thank you for your time despite being busy"
- **앞으로도 잘 부탁드립니다 (Apeurodo jal butakdeumnida)** — "Looking forward to working together"

### Cultural Notes

- **Follow-up**: Always send thank-you email within 24 hours
- **명함**: Take notes on business cards during meeting
- **Calendar**: Confirm meetings in writing
- **Networking (인맥, inmack)**: Building relationships key
- **Humility**: Express gratitude often

## 견적서 부탁드립니다

**Part of Speech:** expression (business)
**Definition:** "Could I get a quote, please?" — pricing inquiry.
**Form:** Gyeokjeokseo butakdeumnida.

### Variations

- **견적서 부탁드립니다 (Gyeokjeokseo butakdeumnida)** — "Quote please"
- **제안서 부탁드려요 (Jeanseo butakdeulyeoyo)** — "Proposal please"
- **___ 가격 얼마예요? (___ gagyeok eolmayeyo?)** — "Price for ___?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Quote contents**: Itemized, valid for X days, terms
- **Negotiation**: Common for large orders
- **MOQ**: Minimum Order Quantity
- **Payment terms**: 30일 후불 (30 days later), 선불 (prepaid)
- **Multiple quotes**: Standard (비교 견적)

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 계약 조건이 어떻게 되나요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (legal)
**Definition:** "What are the contract terms?" — contract negotiation.
**Form:** Gyeyak jogeon-i eotteoke doenayo?

### Variations

- **계약 조건이 어떻게 되나요? (Gyeyak jogeon-i eotteoke doenayo?)** — "What are contract terms?"
- **결제 조건은? (Gyeolje jogeon-eun?)** — "Payment terms?"
- **취소 정책은? (Chwisso jeongchaek-eun?)** — "Cancellation policy?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Key clauses**: 결제 (payment), 인도 (delivery), 지식재산권 (IP), 해지 (termination)
- **Always have lawyer review**: Especially international contracts
- **Force majeure (불가항력)**: Verify wording
- **Dispute resolution**: 분쟁 해결 (mediation), 조정
- **NDA (비밀유지계약)**: Sign before sharing IP

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 세금계산서 발행해 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (accounting)
**Definition:** "Please issue a tax invoice" — billing request.
**Form:** Saegeumgyesanseo balhaenghae juseyo.

### Variations

- **세금계산서 발행해 주세요 (Saegeumgyesanseo balhaenghae juseyo)** — "Tax invoice please"
- **영수증 주세요 (Yeongsujeung juseyo)** — "Receipt please"
- **카드 결제 (Kadeu gyeolje)** — "Card payment"

### Cultural Notes

- **세금계산서 (Tax invoice)**: Required for business expenses (부가세 환급)
- **Hometax (홈택스)**: Government tax system
- **전자세금계산서**: Electronic tax invoice (standard)
- **Expense reports**: 영수증 receipts required
- **Late payment**: 연체료 (delay fees)

### Sources

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 연차 사용하고 싶습니다

**Part of Speech:** expression (HR)
**Definition:** "I'd like to use annual leave" — leave request.
**Form:** Yeoncha sayonghago sipseumnida.

### Variations

- **연차 사용하고 싶습니다 (Yeoncha sayonghago sipseumnida)** — "Use annual leave"
- **휴가 신청합니다 (Hyuga sincheonghamnida)** — "Apply for leave"
- **병가 사용 (Byeon-ga sayong)** — "Sick leave"

### Cultural Notes

- **연차 (Annual leave)**: 15 days minimum (per Korean Labor Standards Act)
- **5인 이상 사업장**: Mandatory 15 days
- **Sick leave**: 별도 (separate from annual)
- **Maternity/Paternity**: 출산 전후 90일 (maternity), 10일 (paternity)
- **Pre-notice**: 2-4 weeks for vacation

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 미팅 시간 잡아 주실 수 있나요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (communication)
**Definition:** "Can we schedule a meeting?" — scheduling.
**Form:** Miting sigan jaba jusil su issnayo?

### Variations

- **미팅 시간 잡아 주실 수 있나요? (Miting sigan jaba jusil su issnayo?)** — "Schedule meeting?"
- **___ 시간 어때요? (___ sigan eottaeyo?)** — "How about ___ time?"
- **일정 조율해 주세요 (Iljeong joyulhae juseyo)** — "Coordinate schedule"

### Cultural Notes

- **Time zones**: Always KST (UTC+9)
- **Tools**: Zoom, Teams, Google Meet (standard)
- **네이버 캘린더 / 카카오 캘린더**: Korean calendar apps
- **Confirmation**: Day before + 30 min before
- **Rescheduling (일정 조율)**: Common in Korean business

### Sources

- `[[business-basics]]`

## 담당자 좀 바꿔 주시겠어요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Can I speak to the person in charge?" — escalation.
**Form**: Damdangja jom bakkweo jusigesseoyo?

### Variations

- **담당자 좀 바꿔 주시겠어요? (Damdangja jom bakkweo jusigesseoyo?)** — "Person in charge?"
- **___ 님 계세요? (___ nim gyeseyo?)** — "Is ___ here?"
- **팀장님 부탁드려요 (Timjangnim butakdeulyeoyo)** — "Team leader please"

### Cultural Notes

- **Hierarchy**: Strong vertical hierarchy in Korean business
- **수령 (Suryeong)**: Receptionist
- **Decision-making**: Often slow (보고 cheolyeo = reporting chain)
- **Top-down (수직 문화)**: Decisions from top
- **담당 (Damdang)**: Person in charge
