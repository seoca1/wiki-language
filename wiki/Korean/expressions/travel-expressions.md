# Expressions: Travel Advanced (한국어)

> **Theme:** Travel (Advanced)
> **Level:** B1-C1
> **Standard:** 표준어 (Pyojuneo, Standard Korean)
> **Romanization:** Romaja

Advanced travel expressions — booking, ticket issues, directions, accommodation problems, emergencies.

---

## 예약하고 싶은데요

**Part of Speech:** expression (booking)
**Definition:** "I'd like to make a reservation" — booking flights/hotels/restaurants.
**Form:** Yeyakago sip-eundeyo.

### Variations

- **예약하고 싶은데요 (Yeyakago sip-eundeyo)** — "I'd like to make a reservation"
- **빈 자리 있어요? (Bin jari isseoyo?)** — "Is there availability?"
- **2명 예약할게요 (Du myeong yeyakalgeyo)** — "Reservation for 2"
- **___박 예약 가능한가요? (___-bak yeyak ganeung-ganyo?)** — "Can I book ___ nights?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Booking platforms**: 야놀자 (yanolja), 여기어때 (yeogi-eo-ttae), Booking.com
- **Hotel direct**: Often matches price + perks (free parking)
- **Restaurant reservations**: 캐치테이블 (CatchTable), 네이버 예약
- **Cancellation**: Most = 24-48 hours free
- **Peak season**: 설/추석/여름 = book 1-2 months ahead

## ___까지 표 한 장 주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (transport)
**Definition:** "One ticket to..." — transport purchase.
**Form:** ___ -kkaji pyo han jang juseyo.

### Variations

- **부산까지 표 한 장 주세요 (Busan-kkaji pyo han jang juseyo)** — "1 ticket to Busan"
- **왕복으로 주세요 (Wangbogeuro juseyo)** — "Round-trip please"
- **편도 얼마예요? (Pyeondo eolmayeyo?)** — "How much one-way?"
- **학생 할인 있어요? (Haksaeng harin isseoyo?)** — "Student discount?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Ticket types**: 편도 (one-way) / 왕복 (round-trip)
- **KTX/SRT**: 고속열차 (Seoul-Busan 2시간)
- **코레일 (Korail)**: Train booking app
- **Student discount**: 청소년/대학생 할인
- **Booking timing**: 1 month ahead for KTX reserved seats

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## 여기 근처에 뭐 볼 거 있어요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Is there anything to see around here?" — sightseeing.
**Form:** Yeogi geuncheoe mwo bol geo isseoyo?

### Variations

- **여기 근처에 뭐 볼 거 있어요?** — "Anything to see nearby?"
- **가볼 만한 곳 추천해 주세요 (Gabol-manhan got chucheonhae juseyo)** — "Recommend places to go"
- **현지인만 아는 곳 있어요? (Jeonjijin-man aneun got isseoyo?)** — "Local-only places?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: 네이버 지도, 카카오맵, 망설임
- **여행 앱**: 야놀자, 트립어드바이저
- **관광안내소**: Tourist info centers (every major station)
- **펜션/한옥 사장님**: Excellent local recommendations
- **블로그**: Naver blogs (real local advice)

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## 화장실 어디예요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (urgent)
**Definition:** "Where is the restroom?" — basic but essential.
**Form:** Hwajangsil eodiyeyo?

### Variations

- **화장실 어디예요? (Hwajangsil eodiyeyo?)** — "Where is the restroom?"
- **화장실 좀 써도 돼요? (Hwajangsil jom sseodo dwaeyo?)** — "Can I use the restroom?"
- **남자/여자 화장실 (Namja/Yeoja hwajangsil)** — "Men's/Women's restroom"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: 화장실 (most common), 변소 (formal), WC (anglicism)
- **Public restrooms**: Free in stations, parks, buildings
- **Cafe/식당**: Use restroom freely (most welcome)
- **Disabled toilet**: 장애인 화장실 (universal access)
- **Smart toilets**: 비데 (bidet) common in modern facilities

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## 깎아주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (negotiation)
**Definition:** "Please give me a discount" — bargaining (limited in Korea).
**Form:** Kkakkajuseyo.

### Variations

- **깎아주세요 (Kkakkajuseyo)** — "Please give a discount"
- **학생 할인이에요? (Haksaeng hal-inieyo?)** — "Student discount?"
- **싸게 해주세요 (Ssage haejuseyo)** — "Make it cheaper"
- **깎아주실 수 없어요? (Kkakkajusil su eopseoyo?)** — "Can't you give a discount?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Bargaining culture**: 
  - 한국 = NO bargaining (retail fixed)
  - Exception: 시장 (traditional markets), 노점상
- **Discount types**: 학생 (student), 경로 (senior), 단체 (group)
- **Tip**: Buy multiple items = bulk discount possible
- **Politeness**: Always polite phrasing

## 길을 잃었어요

**Part of Speech:** expression (problem)
**Definition:** "I'm lost" — getting help when disoriented.
**Form:** Gireul ireosseoyo.

### Variations

- **길을 잃었어요 (Gireul ireosseoyo)** — "I'm lost"
- **도와주세요 (Dowajuseyo)** — "Please help me"
- **여기가 어디예요? (Yeogi ga eodiyeyo?)** — "Where is this?"
- **영어 할 줄 아세요? (Yeongeo hal jul aseyo?)** — "Do you speak English?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: 네이버 지도, 카카오맵 (Korean-first)
- **Google Maps**: Works but limited in Korea
- **경찰/파출소**: Police stations very helpful
- **Tourist info (1330)**: 24-hour English line
- **Korean politeness**: People often walk you to destination

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Wi-Fi 있어요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Is there WiFi?" — internet access inquiry.
**Form:** Waipai isseoyo?

### Variations

- **Wi-Fi 있어요? (Waipai isseoyo?)** — "Is there WiFi?"
- **비밀번호 뭐예요? (Bimilbeonho mwoyeyo?)** — "What's the password?"
- **Wi-Fi 무료예요? (Waipai mulyoeyo?)** — "Is WiFi free?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Free WiFi**: Cafes, hotels, stations, some parks
- **KT/Giga LTE**: Tourist SIM rental (most convenient)
- **Portable WiFi**: Pocket WiFi rental at airport
- **eSIM**: Airalo, Ubigi (international)
- **Hotel WiFi**: Most hotels free for guests

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## 영어 가이드 부탁해요

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "I'd like an English guide" — guided tour.
**Form:** Yeongeo gaideu butakhaeyo.

### Variations

- **영어 가이드 부탁해요 (Yeongeo gaideu butakhaeyo)** — "English guide please"
- **영어 투어 있어요? (Yeongeo tueo isseoyo?)** — "English tour?"
- **하루/반나절 얼마예요? (Haru/bannajeol eolmayeyo?)** — "Full day/Half day price?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: GetYourGuide, Viator, Klook
- **Free walking tours**: Seoul, Busan (tip-based)
- **Palace tours**: Free English tours (Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung)
- **Local guides**: Better context than apps
- **Personal guides**: 캐치테이블 (private guides)

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`
