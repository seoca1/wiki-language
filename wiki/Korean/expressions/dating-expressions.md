# Expressions: Dating & Romance (한국어)

> **Theme:** Dating & Romance
> **Level:** B1-C1
> **Standard:** 표준어 (Pyojuneo, Standard Korean)
> **Romanization:** Romaja

Dating/romance expressions — confession, affection, relationship stages, breakup.

---

## 좋아해요

**Part of Speech:** expression (confession)
**Definition:** "I like you" — direct confession.
**Form:** Johahyeoyo.

### Variations

- **좋아해요 (Johahyeoyo)** — "I like you"
- **너무 좋아해요 (Neomu johahyeoyo)** — "I really like you"
- **사랑해요 (Saranghaeyo)** — "I love you"
- **___ 좋아해요 (___ johahyeoyo)** — "I like ___"

### Cultural Notes

- **Direct vs indirect**: Korea = quite direct (compared to Japan)
- **고백 (Confession)**: Often before Valentine's/White Day
- **"고백할래?"**: "Will you confess?"
- **First date**: Often group double date (4인 4중식)
- **계산 (Bill)**: Often one person pays (especially first few dates)

## 사귀어 줄래요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Will you go out with me?" — official relationship request.
**Form**: Sagwieo julraeyo?

### Variations

- **사귀어 줄래요? (Sagwieo julraeyo?)** — "Will you date me?"
- **나랑 사귀자 (Narang sagwijeo)** — "Let's date" (very casual)
- **연인으로 지내요 (Yeon-in-euro jinaeyo)** — "Let's be lovers"

### Cultural Notes

- **연인 (Yeonin)**: Term for boyfriend/girlfriend
- **사귀다 (Sagwida)**: "To date" / "to go out"
- **"좋아해 → 사귀자"**: Sequence (confess → official)
- **Rejection**: "우리 그냥 친구로 하자" (Let's stay friends)
- **Direct vs indirect**: Sometimes indirect, depends on person

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 사랑해

**Part of Speech:** expression (love)
**Definition:** "I love you" — deep affection.
**Form:** Saranghae.

### Variations

- **사랑해 (Saranghae)** — "I love you" (casual)
- **사랑해요 (Saranghaeyo)** — "I love you" (standard)
- **사랑합니다 (Saranghamnida)** — "I love you" (formal)
- **너무 사랑해 (Neomu saranghae)** — "I love you so much"

### Cultural Notes

- **Frequency**: Korean couples say 자주 (often)
- **매일 사랑해**: "I love you every day" (common in relationships)
- **100일 (100 days)**: 100-day anniversary = milestone
- **기념일**: Anniversary (monthly/100-day/yearly)
- **White Day (3/14)**: Men give candy (White Day)

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 헤어져요

**Part of Speech:** expression (breakup)
**Definition:** "Let's break up" — ending relationship.
**Form:** Heeojyeoyo.

### Variations

- **헤어져요 (Heeojyeoyo)** — "Let's break up"
- **우리 헤어지자 (Uri heeojijeo)** — "Let's break up" (intimate)
- **이게 잘 안 맞는 것 같아 (Ige jal an manneun geot gata)** — "This doesn't seem to fit"

### Cultural Notes

- **Direct vs indirect**: Often direct (especially younger generation)
- **연락처 차단 (Contact blocking)**: Common post-breakup
- **친구 관계 (Friend relationship)**: Sometimes maintained
- **재회 (Reunion)**: Getting back together possible
- **"좀 더 생각해 볼게"**: "Let me think more" (often means no)

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 소개팅

**Part of Speech:** expression (cultural)
**Definition:** "Blind date (introduced)" — setup date.
**Form**: Sogeting.

### Variations

- **소개팅 (Sogeting)** — "Blind date"
- **미팅 (Miting)** — "Meeting" (less formal)
- **맞선 (Matseon)** — Traditional matchmaking

### Cultural Notes

- **소개팅 culture**: Very common in Korea
- **Matchmaker (소개자)**: Friend/family/colleague
- **Group dates (4:4, 6:6)**: Common in 20s
- **Modern apps**: Tinder, Amanda, Pairs
- **Age (나이)**: Important factor in Korean dating

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 동거

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Cohabitation" — living together unmarried.
**Form:** Donggeo.

### Variations

- **동거 (Donggeo)** — "Cohabitation"
- **같이 살자 (Gachi salja)** — "Let's live together"
- **동거를 시작하다 (Dongeoreul sijakhada)** — "Start living together"

### Cultural Notes

- **Korea**: Less common than West (conservative traditionally)
- **Increasing**: Younger generation (20s-30s) more accepting
- **Family reaction**: 부모님 opposition common
- **결혼 전 동거**: Pre-marriage cohabitation
- **반반 (Banban)**: Splitting rent 50/50 (common)

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 만남 어플

**Part of Speech:** expression (modern)
**Definition:** "Dating app" — online dating.
**Form**: Mannam eopul.

### Variations

- **만남 어플 (Mannam eopul)** — "Dating app"
- **아만다 (Amanda)** — Korean dating app (popular)
- **글램 (Glam)** — Premium Korean app
- **티나 (Tina)** — Casual app
- **틴더 (Tindeo)** — Tinder (international)

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps (Korea)**: Amanda, Glam, Tinder, Pairs
- **Cultural norms**: Less casual than US
- **Profile requirements**: Real name verification (본인 인증)
- **무료 vs 유료**: Free vs paid tiers
- **Korean dating market**: $1B+ industry

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## 부모님 인사

**Part of Speech:** expression (relationship)
**Definition:** "Greeting to parents" — formal family introduction.
**Form**: Bumonim insa.

### Variations

- **부모님 인사 (Bumonim insa)** — "Parents greeting"
- **양가 부모님 (Yangga bumonim)** — "Both sets of parents"
- **혼인 신고 (Honin sin'go)** — "Marriage registration"

### Cultural Notes

- **Important step**: Often before engagement
- **선물 (Gift)**: 과일/한약재/고급 차 (fruit/herbal tea)
- **정중함**: Very polite language
- **Inheritance (가문, gamun)**: Family lineage matters
- **Caste-like hierarchy**: Old families still considered
