# Expressions: Technology & Internet (한국어)

> **Theme:** Technology & Internet
> **Level:** A2-C1

## Wi-Fi 비밀번호 뭐예요?

**Part of Speech:** expression (tech)
**Definition:** "What's the WiFi password?" — connecting to internet.
**Form:** Waipai bimilbeonho mwoyeyo?

### Variations

- **Wi-Fi 비밀번호 뭐예요? (Waipai bimilbeonho mwoyeyo?)** — "WiFi password?"
- **Wi-Fi 있어요? (Waipai isseoyo?)** — "WiFi available?"
- **Wi-Fi 무료예요? (Waipai mulyoeyo?)** — "WiFi free?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Free WiFi**: Cafes, hotels, stations, subway
- **SKT/KT/LG**: Mobile carriers (5G widespread)
- **Portable WiFi**: Reservation at airport (most popular for tourists)
- **eSIM**: Airalo, Ubigi (international)
- **Hotel WiFi**: Most hotels free for guests

## 연락처 알려주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Please give me your contact" — sharing contact.
**Form**: Yeollakcheo allyeojuseyo.

### Variations

- **연락처 알려주세요 (Yeollakcheo allyeojuseyo)** — "Tell me your contact"
- **카톡 아이디 뭐예요? (Katalk aidi mwoyeyo?)** — "KakaoTalk ID?"
- **전화번호 주세요 (Jeonhwabeonho juseyo)** — "Phone number please"

### Cultural Notes

- **카카오톡 (KakaoTalk)**: #1 messenger in Korea (essential)
- **카톡 = Katalk**: Universal term for messaging
- **Phone number sharing**: Common even for strangers (vs. West)
- **Email**: Less personal (used for business)
- **Instagram**: Popular for younger users

## DM 보내주세요

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Please DM me" — direct message on social media.
**Form**: DM bonaejuseyo.

### Variations

- **DM 보내주세요 (DM bonaejuseyo)** — "DM me"
- **DM으로 연락 주세요 (DM-euro yeollak juseyo)** — "Contact via DM"
- **쪽지 보내주세요 (Jjokji bonaejuseyo)** — "Send message" (older)

### Cultural Notes

- **Instagram DM**: Most common
- **Twitter DM**: Less common in Korea
- **카톡 (Katalk)**: Used instead of DM usually
- **Public reply**: Public vs private (DM = private)
- **Etiquette**: Don't DM strangers (spam)

## ㅋㅋㅋ / ㅎㅎㅎ

**Part of Speech:** expression (text)
**Definition:** "kkk / hhh" — Internet laugh.
**Form**: ㅋㅋㅋ / ㅎㅎㅎ.

### Variations

- **ㅋㅋㅋ (kkk)** — "Lol" (Korean "ㅋ" = k sound)
- **ㅎㅎㅎ (hhh)** — "Haha" (older style)
- **ㅋㅋㅋㅋ** — More laughing
- **ㅋㅋ (kk)** — Light laugh

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: ㅋ = ㄱㅋㅋ in Hangul (most "k-like" consonant)
- **Frequency**: Very common in text
- **ㅎ**: More polite (older, formal)
- **Tone**: More ㅋ's = louder/funnier
- **Variation**: ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ (6+) = "ROFL"

## 택시 어플

**Part of Speech:** expression (urban)
**Definition:** "Taxi app" — Korean ride-hailing app.
**Form**: Taeksi eopul.

### Variations

- **택시 어플 (Taeksi eopul)** — "Taxi app"
- **카카오택시 (Kakao Taeksi)** — Most popular
- **타다 (Tada)** — Premium (suspended 2020, returned 2022)
- **UT (유티)** — Black cab premium
- **i.M (아이엠)** — Taxi call

### Cultural Notes

- **카카오택시 = Kakao Taxi**: Industry standard
- **Type**: 일반 (regular), 모범 (deluxe), 블랙 (black cab)
- **Payment**: Card, cash, 카카오페이
- **Surge pricing**: 야간 할증 (night surcharge 20-40%)
- **Tipping**: NO (built-in)

## 라이브커머스

**Part of Speech:** expression (commerce)
**Definition:** "Live commerce" — buying via live video.
**Form**: Raibeukomeoseu.

### Variations

- **라이브커머스 (Raibeukomeoseu)** — "Live commerce"
- **라이브 방송 (Raibeu bangsong)** — "Live broadcast"
- **라이버 (Raibeo)** — "Live streamer"

### Cultural Notes

- **Korea = leader**: Pioneer in live commerce (2019+)
- **Platforms**: Coupang Live, Naver Shopping Live, Instagram Live
- **인플루언서**: Influencers + brands
- **한정 특가 (Limited specials)**: Create urgency
- **한국 화장품**: Korean cosmetics especially popular
