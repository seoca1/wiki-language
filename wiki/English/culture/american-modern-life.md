# American Modern Life: Streaming, Social Media, and Delivery

**Overview:** Modern American life revolves around streaming entertainment, social media, and on-demand delivery services.

## Key Points

- **Streaming**:
  - **Netflix** — #1 (started 2007, streaming 2008)
  - **Disney+** — Disney/Pixar/Marvel/Star Wars
  - **HBO Max / Max** — HBO content
  - **Hulu** — TV shows, next-day broadcast
  - **Amazon Prime Video** — bundled with Prime
  - **Apple TV+** — premium original content
  - **Paramount+, Peacock, Disney+ Hotstar** — various
- **Social Media**:
  - **Facebook** — Older demographics, family
  - **Instagram** — Visual content, influencers
  - **Twitter/X** — News, politics, real-time
  - **TikTok** — Younger, short video
  - **YouTube** — Long-form video
  - **Snapchat** — Younger, ephemeral
  - **Reddit** — Communities
  - **LinkedIn** — Professional
- **Delivery Apps**:
  - **Uber Eats** — #1 (acquired by Uber)
  - **DoorDash** — #2 (rapid growth)
  - **Grubhub** — Older brand
  - **Postmates** — Owned by Uber
  - **Instacart** — Grocery delivery
- **Smartphone**:
  - **iPhone** — Dominant (~60% market)
  - **Android** (Samsung, Google Pixel) — ~40%
  - **Apps**: Social, maps, banking, dating, food
- **Dating Apps**:
  - **Tinder** — Casual
  - **Bumble** — Women message first
  - **Hinge** — Serious relationships ("designed to be deleted")
  - **Match** — Older demographic
- **Public Transit** (varies by city):
  - NYC: Subway (24/7)
  - LA: Car-centric
  - Chicago: L (elevated train)
  - Boston: T (subway)
  - Many cities: Bus + light rail
- **Tipping Culture**: 15-20% standard at restaurants, 20-25% expected at bars, $1-2/drink at coffee. Other: Uber/taxi, hotel housekeeping, hairdresser.
