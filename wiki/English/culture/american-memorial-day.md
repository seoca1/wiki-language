# American Memorial Day

**Overview:** American Memorial Day is a federal holiday on the last Monday of May, originally established in 1868 to honor Civil War dead. It has expanded to remember all U.S. military personnel who died in service, and unofficially marks the start of summer with barbecues, beach trips, and retail sales.

## Key Points

- **1868 Origin**: General John A. Logan of the Grand Army of the Republic (GAR) proclaimed May 30, 1868, as "Decoration Day" — a day to decorate the graves of Civil War dead with flowers. The first observance was at Arlington National Cemetery.
- **Civil War Roots**: More Americans died in the Civil War (1861-1865) than in any other U.S. conflict before modern times. Communities on both sides lost fathers, brothers, and sons. Decoration Day was a reconciliation gesture.
- **Date Change to Last Monday**: The 1968 Uniform Monday Holiday Act moved Memorial Day, Veterans Day (later moved back), and other holidays to designated Mondays to create three-day weekends. Effective 1971.
- **Two-Minute Silence at 3 PM**: The "National Moment of Remembrance," established by Congress in 2000, asks Americans to pause at 3:00 PM local time on Memorial Day.
- **Poppy Tradition**: Veterans and supporters wear red poppies (inspired by the WWI poem "In Flanders Fields") distributed by the Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW) and American Legion. Buddy Poppy program since 1922.
- **BBQ and Summer Kickoff**: Unofficially, Memorial Day is the start of summer. ~62% of Americans grill or barbecue on Memorial Day. Indianapolis 500 race is held on Memorial Day weekend. Pools and beaches open.
- **Confederate Memorial Day**: Several Southern states observe a separate Confederate Memorial Day in late April or early May, often without federal recognition, and it has become a source of contemporary debate.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국의 현충일 (顯忠日, 6월 6일) — 전몰 장병과 호국 영령을 추모. 미국 Memorial Day 와 같은 충성/추모 명절이지만 가족 BBQ 분위기는 약함. 한국의 봄 명절 단오 (端午, 음력 5월 5일) 는 가족+피서 의식.
- **Japonés**: 일본의 戦没者追悼記念日 (8월 15일) — 전몰 장병 추모. 미국 Memorial Day 와 같은 추모 명절이지만 BBQ 나 휴가 분위기는 없음. 일본의 5월 골든위크 (5월 초) 가 비슷한 여름 개막 분위기.
- **Chino**: 중국에는 공식 Memorial Day 와 같은 추모 명절 없음. 清明절 (4월 4-6일) 이 조상 추모 (성묘) 명절이지만 가족 등산/소풍 의식도 강함. 미국 Memorial Day BBQ 와 비슷한 가족 야외 활동 구조.

## Examples

«Memorial Day is observed on the last Monday of May to honor the men and women of the United States armed forces who died in military service.»

Memorial Day 는 미국 군에서 군 복무 중 사망한 남녀를 기리기 위해 5월 마지막 주 월요일에 기념됩니다.

«General John A. Logan officially established Decoration Day in 1868, choosing May 30 as a day to decorate the graves of Civil War soldiers with flowers.»

John A. Logan 장군은 1868년에 공식적으로 Decoration Day 를 제정하며, 5월 30일을 남북전쟁 장병의 무덤을 꽃으로 장식하는 날로 지정했습니다.

«Many Americans treat Memorial Day weekend as the unofficial start of summer, hosting barbecues, going to the beach, and watching the Indianapolis 500.»

많은 미국인들은 Memorial Day 주말을 여름의 비공식적인 시작으로 여기며, 바비큐를 열고 바닷가에 가거나 인디애나폴리스 500 경기를 관람합니다.
