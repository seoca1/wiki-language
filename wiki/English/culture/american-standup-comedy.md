# American Stand-Up Comedy

**Overview:** Stand-up comedy is a distinctive American entertainment form in which a single comedian performs live, telling jokes and observational commentary directly to an audience. Originating in vaudeville and Borscht Belt resorts, modern stand-up has evolved through the New York and Los Angeles club scenes into a podcast-dominated era where comedians build global audiences without traditional TV deals.

## Key Points

- **NYC and LA Club Scenes**: New York (Comedy Cellar, Caroline's, Stand Up NY) and Los Angeles (Comedy Store, Laugh Factory, Improv) are the two epicenters. The Comedy Cellar has featured nearly every major comedian of the last 40 years.
- **Open Mic Culture**: Open mics (free or low-cost performances where aspiring comedians try new material) are the entry point. NYC's Comic Strip Live, The Stand, and countless bars host nightly open mics. Comics typically perform 5-10 minutes and refine jokes over dozens of sets.
- **Mitzvah Circuit**: Comics, especially Jewish-American ones, performed at bar mitzvahs and weddings during lean years. The term "working the Borscht Belt" refers to the 1920s-1980s Catskills comedy resorts. Don Rickles, Mel Brooks, Jerry Seinfeld all worked the circuit.
- **Late Night TV as Springboard**: The Tonight Show, Late Show, and SNL have launched countless careers. Jerry Seinfeld's "The Tonight Show" appearances in the 1980s are credited with helping build his material for "Seinfeld."
- **Movers and Shakers**:
  - **Lenny Bruce (1950s-60s)**: Transformed stand-up with social commentary; arrested for obscenity (1961, 1964).
  - **Richard Pryor (1970s-80s)**: Brought raw personal storytelling to mainstream.
  - **George Carlin (1970s-2000s)**: Wordplay and counterculture commentary.
  - **Eddie Murphy (1980s)**: Made comedy concerts into major films (Delirious, 1983; Raw, 1987).
  - **Dave Chappelle (2000s-present)**: His Chappelle's Show (2003-2006) and Netflix specials define modern stand-up.
- **Podcast Era**: Since the 2010s, comedians have built audiences through podcasts (Marc Maron's WTF, Joe Rogan's The Joe Rogan Experience). Rogan signed a $100M Spotify deal in 2020. The 2024 Netflix-curated comedy boom continued the trend.
- **Tight Five / Tight Ten**: A "tight five" is 5 minutes of well-rehearsed, polished material. Most working comedians have multiple tight fives for different sets (clubs, TV, corporate gigs).

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 개그 문화 — 코미디 프로그램 "개그 콘서트" (1999-2020) + 토크쇼. KBS 개그맨 + "웃찾사" (웃음찾기). 미국 stand-up 보다는 TV 개그 (sitcom) 중심. 한국에는 stand-up 클럽 문화가 약함 (홍대 소수).
- **Japonés**: 일본 お笑い (owarai) — 漫才 (manzai, 둘이서 농담 주고받기), コント (skit), ピン芸 (single comedian). 일본형 stand-up 은 漫才 중심. 미국과 다른 둘이서 호흡 구조. 吉本興業 (Yoshimoto Kogyo) 가 업계 지배.
- **Chino**: 중국 脱口秀 (tuōkǒu xiù, "탈口秀") — 미국식 stand-up 의 중국 수입. 上海, 北京 의 클럽 중심. 杨笠, 何广智, 周奇墨 등이 인기. 2023년 House (喜剧之王) 같은 TV 쇼가 인플루언스. 중국 정치적 한계 (정치 풍자 금지).
