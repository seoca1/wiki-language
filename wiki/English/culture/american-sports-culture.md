# American Sports Culture

**Overview:** Sports are a major part of American identity. Baseball is called "the national pastime," but American football (NFL) draws the largest TV audiences, and basketball (NBA) and stock car racing (NASCAR) have massive followings. For many Americans, supporting a team is a family tradition passed across generations.

## Key Points

- **Baseball (national pastime)**: Called this since the mid-1800s. MLB (Major League Baseball) plays a 162-game season from April to October, capped by the World Series. Pace is slow compared to football or basketball.
- **American football (NFL)**: The most popular spectator sport by TV ratings. Super Bowl in February draws ~100 million viewers; players are the country's most recognizable athletes.
- **Basketball (NBA)**: Invented by James Naisley in 1891. Globally influential through figures like Michael Jordan and LeBron James. College basketball (March Madness) is also huge.
- **NASCAR**: Stock car racing, especially popular in the South. Daytona 500 (February) is its biggest race. Distinct from open-wheel Formula 1.
- **Sports as identity**: Many Americans inherit team loyalty from parents or hometown. "I'm a Yankees fan" or "Go Cowboys!" is a quick social identifier, similar to declaring a hometown.
- **High school + college sports**: In smaller towns, Friday-night high school football is the main social event. College football (especially SEC schools) draws stadiums of 100,000+.
- **Tailgating**: Pre-game parking-lot BBQ tradition, especially for football. Combines food, beer, and team colors into a ritual that often lasts longer than the game itself.
- **Fantasy sports**: Millions of Americans play fantasy football/baseball/basketball, turning games into a personal investment even when their favorite team plays poorly.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국은 야구 (KBO) 가 1980년대부터 인기를 끌었지만, 축구 (K-League) 와 e스포츠가 더 큰 문화적 영향력을 가짐. 한국에선 선수 응원 문화 (응원단, 싸이렌) 가 매우 적극적, 미국 cheerleading 과 비슷하지만 규모가 더 크다.
- **Japonés**: 일본은 야구 (NPB) 가 national pastime 와 비슷한 위치. 프로 야구 12구단, 고교 야구 (甲子園) 가 사회적 이벤트. 일본 축구 (J-League) 와 럭비 (대학 럭비) 도 인기가 있지만 NFL 스타일 미식축구는 거의 없음.
- **Chino**: 중국은 NBA 가 가장 인기 있는 미국 스포츠 (야오밍 효과). 축구 (中超) 와 탁구, 배드가 인기. 미국식 "national pastime" 개념 자체가 강하지 않음.
- **Español**: 스페인 축구 (La Liga, 레알 마드리드 vs 바르셀로나) 가 미국 NFL 만큼이나 도시 정체성과 결부. 라틴아메리카에서는 축구가 종교에 가깝다. "Tailgating" 같은 주차장聚会 문화는 없음.

## Examples

«Baseball is still called "America's national pastime," even though the NFL gets much higher TV ratings.» (야구는 아직도 "미국의 국민 오락" 이라고 불리지만, 사실 NFL 이 TV 시청률 면에서 훨씬 앞선다.)

«Every Sunday in fall, millions of Americans watch NFL games on TV, often with friends and a lot of snacks.» (가을의 매 일요일마다 수백만 미국인이 친구들과 함께 간식을 먹으며 NFL 경기를 TV로 본다.)

«I grew up a Cowboys fan because my dad was a Cowboys fan — it's just what we do in Texas.» (아버지가 카우보이스 팬이셨기 때문에 나도 자연히 카우보이스 팬으로 자랐다. 텍사스에서는 그냥 그런 거다.)
