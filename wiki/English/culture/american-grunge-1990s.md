# American Grunge and 1990s Music

**Overview:** Grunge (also called the Seattle Sound) was a rock music movement emerging from the Pacific Northwest in the late 1980s and peaking commercially in 1991-1996. Defined by distorted guitars, introspective lyrics, flannel shirts, and disaffected youth culture, it was the last major rock movement before the internet reshaped music distribution.

## Key Points

- **Seattle Scene Origins**: Bands like Soundgarden (formed 1984), Green River (1984, with members of Mudhoney and Pearl Jam), and the Melvins developed the heavy, low-tuned, distorted guitar sound in the Seattle area. Sub Pop Records (founded 1988) signed and promoted the local scene.
- **Nirvana Breakthrough**: Nirvana's "Nevermind" (released September 24, 1991) sold over 30 million copies worldwide. The single "Smells Like Teen Spirit" became an anthem of Generation X. Frontman Kurt Cobain died by suicide on April 5, 1994.
- **Pearl Jam (1990-)**: Formed by Stone Gossard and Jeff Ament (ex-Green River) with Eddie Vedder. "Ten" (1991) sold 13+ million copies. Vedder's social activism (anti-war, pro-environment) defined Pearl Jam's identity. Fought Ticketmaster over service fees in 1994.
- **Flannel Fashion**: Flannel shirts, ripped jeans, and Doc Martens became the visual signature of grunge. Marc Jacobs's 1992 Perry Ellis grunge collection for fashion week was influential but cost him his job.
- **Lollapalooza (1991-1997, revived 2005)**: Perry Farrell's alternative rock touring festival helped spread the genre nationwide. Originally a farewell tour for Jane's Addiction.
- **"Nevermind" as Cultural Pivot**: "Nevermind" knocked Michael Jackson's "Dangerous" off #1 on the Billboard 200 (January 1992), symbolically ending 1980s pop dominance.
- **Post-Grunge Mainstream**: Foo Fighters (formed by Dave Grohl after Nirvana), Creed, Nickelback, and 3 Doors Down dominated the late 1990s but were often dismissed by grunge purists.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 1990년대 록 — 신촌블루스 (Shinchon Blues), 넥스트 (NEXT) 등이 비슷한 시기에 활동. 미국 그룬지보다 10년 일찍한 1980년대 한국 대학가 록과 더 가깝음. 1990년대 한국은 미국 그룬지를 라이브 클럽으로 수입.
- **Japonés**: 일본 1990년대 비주얼계 (Visual Kei) — X JAPAN, BUCK-TICK, LUNA SEA. 미국 그룬지와 비슷한 "어둡고, 헤어스타일 강렬한 록" 구조이지만 일본 고유의 코스프레/반전 미학. Sub Pop 이 일본 아티스트를 시그닝.
- **Chino**: 중국 1990년대 魔岩三杰 (Magic Rock Three) — 唐朝, 黑豹, 何勇. 미국 그룬지와 동시에 베이징 록 붐을 일으켰지만 상업적 성취는 미국보다 작음. 중국 본토 록의 첫 전성기.

## Examples

«Nirvana's 1991 album "Nevermind" sold over 30 million copies worldwide and is considered the album that brought grunge music into the mainstream.»

Nirvana 의 1991년 앨범 "Nevermind"는 전 세계적으로 3천만 장 이상 판매되었으며, grunge 음악을 주류로 끌어올린 앨범으로 간주됩니다.

«Frontman Kurt Cobain's death on April 5, 1994 marked the symbolic end of the grunge era, even as bands like Pearl Jam continued to release albums.»

Kurt Cobain 의 1994년 4월 5일 사망은 Pearl Jam 같은 밴드가 계속 앨범을 발매했지만 grunge 시대의 상징적인 끝을 알렸습니다.

«The flannel shirt, ripped jeans, and Doc Martens boots of the grunge era stood in sharp contrast to the colorful, neon styles of 1980s pop music.»

Grunge 시대의 플란넬 셔츠, 찢어진 진, 닥터마틴 부츠는 1980년대 팝 음악의 화려하고 네온 빛깔의 스타일과 극명한 대조를 이루었습니다.
