# American Mother's Day and Father's Day

**Overview:** Mother's Day (second Sunday of May) and Father's Day (third Sunday of June) are American holidays honoring mothers and fathers, respectively. Both originated in the early 1900s through grassroots campaigns and became commercialized into major retail events by the mid-20th century. Anna Jarvis (Mother's Day founder) and Sonora Smart Dodd (Father's Day founder) both later criticized the commercialization they had helped create.

## Key Points

- **Mother's Day 1914 Official**: President Woodrow Wilson signed a bill making Mother's Day a federal holiday on May 9, 1914, observed the second Sunday of May. The first widespread Mother's Day observance was in 1908, organized by Anna Jarvis in Grafton, West Virginia.
- **Anna Jarvis's Background**: Jarvis campaigned for Mother's Day as a memorial to her own mother, Ann Reeves Jarvis, who had founded "Mothers' Friendship Day" units to promote peace during the Civil War.
- **Father's Day 1966-1972 Official**: Father's Day became a federal holiday under President Lyndon B. Johnson (1966 proclamation), and was made a permanent federal holiday by Congress in 1972. The first Father's Day was celebrated on June 19, 1910, in Spokane, Washington, organized by Sonora Smart Dodd.
- **Sonora Smart Dodd's Origin**: Dodd, raised by a single father (Civil War veteran William Jackson Smart) after her mother died in childbirth, proposed a day to honor fathers while listening to a Mother's Day sermon in 1909.
- **Commercial Scale**: Mother's Day is the third-largest holiday for card and gift sales (after Christmas and Valentine's). Americans spend ~$30 billion on Mother's Day and ~$20 billion on Father's Day annually.
- **Anna Jarvis's Critique**: Jarvis spent her later life fighting Mother's Day commercialization. She protested a 1923 candy convention and called florists' price-fixing schemes "a stench in the nostrils of decent folk." She died in 1948 in poverty.
- **Common Gifts**: Mother's Day — flowers, greeting cards, jewelry, spa days, brunch. Father's Day — greeting cards, neckties (a long-running joke), tools, BBQ equipment, sports tickets, "World's Best Dad" mugs.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국에는 어버이날 (5월 8일) — 부모 합동 명절. 5월 가정의 달의 핵심. 미국 Mother/Father's Day 의 분리와 다름. 한국에서 카네이션 (어머니) 은 미국 Mother's Day 와 비슷하지만 한국 고유 명절.
- **Japonés**: 일본 母の日 (5월 두 번째 일요일) + 父の日 (6월 세 번째 일요일) — 미국과 같은 분리 명절이지만 연방공휴일 아님. 일본 상업화의 비판론도 미국과 비슷하게 발생.
- **Chino**: 중국에는 어버이날 없음. 重阳节 (음력 9월 9일, "노인절") 이 부모/어르신 경애 명절로 더 가까움. 미국식 Mother/Father's Day 의 분리된 소비 명절 구조와 매우 다름.
