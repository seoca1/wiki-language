# American Halloween History

**Overview:** American Halloween (October 31) is a secular holiday blending Celtic Samhain roots, Irish immigrant customs, Christian All Saints' Day, and modern American commercialization. It is the second-largest commercial holiday after Christmas, focused on costumes, candy, and decoration.

## Key Points

- **Celtic Samhain Origins**: The ancient Celtic festival of Samhain (pronounced "SAH-win"), celebrated October 31-November 1 in Ireland, Scotland, and Wales, marked the end of harvest and the beginning of winter. The veil between living and dead was believed to be thinnest.
- **Irish Immigration Wave**: Irish refugees fleeing the potato famine (1845-1852) brought their Halloween customs to American cities. The holiday was already being celebrated in parts of colonial America but grew dramatically in scale.
- **Trick-or-Treat Evolution**: The phrase and practice date to the 1920s-1930s in the U.S. and Canada, partly inspired by older "souling" and "guising" customs. Mass-produced candy made it practical by the 1950s. It displaced earlier pranks and vandalism.
- **Costume Culture**: Americans spend more on Halloween costumes than any other country — over $3 billion annually (second only to Christmas decorations). Costumes range from traditional witches and ghosts to pop-culture parodies.
- **Jack-o'-Lantern**: Carved pumpkins come from the Irish legend of "Stingy Jack" and the turnip-carving tradition. Pumpkins replaced turnips in America because they were native and larger.
- **Haunted Houses and Horror Films**: Commercial haunted houses emerged in the 1960s-1970s. Halloween horror film franchises (Halloween, Friday the 13th, A Nightmare on Elm Street) made the holiday synonymous with horror cinema.
- **Religious Tensions**: Some Christian groups (historically, some Reformed and evangelical) have objected to Halloween's pagan roots, promoting "harvest festivals" or "trunk-or-treat" alternatives in the 1990s-2000s.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국은 10월 31일을 큰 명절로 두지 않음. 최근 코스프레 (cosplay) 와 이디야 (Ediya) 할로윈 마케팅, 홍대 클럽 파티 등 소비 문화로만 유입. 추석 (Chuseok) 같은 명절이 가을 가족의 식사를 담당.
- **Japonés**: 일본은 9월 "오분 (お盆, Obon)" 이 조상귀환 명절로 가을 핵심. Halloween 은 2000년대 이후 도쿄 디즈니랜드와 시부야 (Shibuya) 코스프레 문화로 수입됨. 한국과 같이 미국 Halloween 을 native 명절로 흡수하지 않음.
- **Chino**: 중국은 10월 31일을 공식 명절로 안 봄. 中元節 (음력 7월 15일, "맹혼절") 이 조상귀환 명절로 더 가깝지만 현대 도시에서는 점점 약해짐. 미국 Halloween 의 코스프레 + 사탕 구조와는 매우 다름.

«Halloween originated from the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain, which marked the end of the harvest season and the beginning of winter in Ireland and Scotland.»

«Children in American neighborhoods dress up in costumes and go door to door on Halloween night, shouting "trick or treat" to collect candy from each house.»
