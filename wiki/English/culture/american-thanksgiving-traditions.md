# American Thanksgiving Traditions

**Overview:** American Thanksgiving is a federal holiday on the fourth Thursday of November, centered on a feast of turkey, stuffing, and pumpkin pie with family. It blends the 1621 Plymouth Pilgrim-Wampanoag harvest meal mythology with the 1863 Lincoln proclamation that made it a national holiday.

## Key Points

- **Lincoln's 1863 Proclamation**: Abraham Lincoln declared Thanksgiving a national holiday on October 3, 1863, midway through the Civil War, to foster unity. He set the date as the last Thursday in November.
- **Plymouth 1621 Mythology**: The "First Thanksgiving" story of Pilgrims and Wampanoag sharing a fall harvest meal is taught in schools but is partly reconstructed mythology. The Wampanoag had been helping the Pilgrims survive.
- **Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade (NYC, 1924+)**: Massive parade with giant character balloons, floats, marching bands, and Broadway performances. Ends with Santa Claus, marking the start of Christmas season. Aired on NBC since 1948.
- **NFL Football Tradition**: The Detroit Lions have played on Thanksgiving since 1934 (with breaks). The Dallas Cowboys joined in 1966. Three NFL games now air on Thanksgiving Day.
- **Classic Menu**: Roast turkey (centerpiece), stuffing/dressing, mashed potatoes, gravy, cranberry sauce, sweet potatoes, green bean casserole, dinner rolls, pumpkin pie. Regional variations: Southern cornbread dressing, New England oyster stuffing.
- **Family Ritual**: Travel is the busiest of the year. Roughly 50+ million Americans travel. "Going around the table" to say what you're grateful for is a common ritual.
- **Black Friday**: The day after Thanksgiving launches the Christmas shopping season, historically with early-morning store openings (now mostly online).

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국의 추석 (Chuseok) — 음력 8월 15일, 가족이 고향에 모여 차례 (차례/제사) 를 지내고 송편 (songpyeon) 을 나눠 먹는 가을 명절. 미국 Thanksgiving 과 같이 "가족 + 가을 수확 + 음식" 의 결합.
- **Japonés**: 日本の勤労感謝の日 (Kinro Kansha no Hi, 11월 23일) — 일의 가치를 기리는 날. Thanksgiving 과 같이 11월 가을에 있지만 가족 식사 축제 보다는 노동 감사 중심. 유사점은 "은혜에 감사" 라는 의식의 구조.
- **Chino**: 中国的感恩节 — 1941 년부터 미국 영향으로 존재하지만 미국식 대형 가족 식사보다 쇼핑 축제 (사이버 월요일) 에 가까움. 명절 강도는 중추절 (中秋節) 이 담당.

## Examples

«Every fourth Thursday in November, families across the United States gather for a roasted turkey dinner with stuffing, mashed potatoes, and pumpkin pie.»

매년 11월 넷째 주 목요일, 미국 가족들은 모두 모여서 구운 칠면조와 stuffing, mashed potatoes, pumpkin pie 로 저녁 식사를 합니다.

«The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City features giant balloons, marching bands, and floats, ending with Santa Claus at the front of the line.»

뉴욕의 Macy's 추수감사절 퍼레이드에는 거대한 풍선, marching band, 그리고 float 가 등장하고, 마지막에 산타클로스가 줄의 맨 앞에 등장합니다.

«The Detroit Lions have played an NFL game on Thanksgiving Day every year since 1934, making football a central part of the holiday for many Americans.»

디트로이트 라이온즈는 1934년 이후 매년 추수감사절에 NFL 경기를 해왔으며, 이는 많은 미국인에게 휴일의 중심 요소가 되었습니다.
