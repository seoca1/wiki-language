## Expressions (Romance/Dating theme)

- [[nice-to-meet-you]] - "만나서 반가워요" — First-time greeting.
- [[want-coffee]] - "커피 한 잔 할래요?" — Common low-pressure date invitation.
- [[really-like-you]] - "난 정말 너를 좋아해" — Strong but not full confession.
- [[be-my-girlfriend]] - "내 여자친구 돼줘" — Formal proposal for exclusive relationship.
- [[i-love-you]] - "사랑해" — Deep romantic confession.
- [[hold-your-hand]] - "손 잡아도 돼?" — Consent request for first physical affection.
- [[had-a-great-time]] - "정말 즐거웠어" — Standard post-date appreciation.
- [[see-you-again]] - "또 만나자" — Direct expression of wanting to meet again.
- [[stay-friends]] - "친구로 지내자" — Polite but firm decline.
- [[im-busy]] - "바빠" — Evasive way to decline.
