# greetings (English) expressions

**Overview:** English greetings 표현. 일상적 인사 패턴 모음.

### Hello!

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 안녕하세요!

**Example:** Hello! How are you?

---

### Good morning!

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 좋은 아침입니다!

**Example:** Good morning! Did you sleep well?

---

### Good afternoon!

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 좋은 오후입니다!

**Example:** Good afternoon! How is your day?

---

### Good evening!

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 좋은 저녁입니다!

**Example:** Good evening! How was work?

---

### Good night!

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 안녕히 주무세요!

**Example:** Good night! See you tomorrow.
