# Expressions: Restaurant & Dining (English)

> **Theme:** Restaurant & Dining
> **Level:** A2-B2
> **Standard:** US/UK English

Restaurant/dining expressions — ordering, allergies, bill, dining etiquette.

---

## Could I see the menu, please?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Could I see the menu, please?" — upon sitting at restaurant.

### Usage

First interaction at most restaurants.

### Variations

- **Could I see the menu, please?** — "Menu please" (polite)
- **May I see the wine list?** — "Wine list?"
- **Do you have a kids' menu?** — "Kids' menu?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Waiter approach**: US = immediate, EU = wait until greeted
- **Reservation vs walk-in**: US often accepts walk-ins, EU = reservation recommended
- **Tip (US)**: 18-22% expected (not included)
- **Tip (EU)**: Service often included (5-10% extra for great service)

## I'll have ___

**Part of Speech:** expression (order)
**Definition:** "I'll have ___" — placing an order.

### Usage

When ready to order.

### Variations

- **I'll have the steak, medium rare** — "Steak, medium rare"
- **I'll go with the salmon** — "Salmon, please"
- **Can I get the burger with no onions?** — "Burger, no onions"
- **The same thing, please** — "Same as them"

### Cultural Notes

- **Cooking temperature** (US): Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done
- **Customization**: US = encouraged (substitutions welcome), EU = less common
- **Dietary restrictions**: Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, kosher
- **Servers**: "What can I get you?" / "Are you ready to order?"

- `[[food-dining]]`

## I'm allergic to ___

**Part of Speech:** expression (health)
**Definition:** "I'm allergic to ___" — food allergy disclosure.

### Usage

CRITICAL — disclose to server before ordering.

### Variations

- **I'm allergic to peanuts** — "Peanut allergy"
- **I'm gluten intolerant** — "Gluten-free needed"
- **I'm vegetarian** — "No meat"
- **Does this contain nuts?** — "Nuts in this?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Severe allergies**: Always inform server (cross-contamination risk)
- **Common allergies**: Peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, dairy, gluten, soy
- **EpiPen**: Carry if severe (especially when traveling)
- **Allergy cards**: Translated cards for traveling (Celiac.org, SelectWisely)
- **Hidden ingredients**: Sauces, marinades, frying oil (cross-contamination)

- `[[food-dining]]`

## Could I have the check, please?

**Part of Speech:** expression (payment)
**Definition:** "Could I have the check, please?" — asking for the bill.

### Usage

End of meal.

### Variations

- **Could I have the check, please?** — "Check please" (US)
- **Could I have the bill, please?** — "Bill please" (UK)
- **Can we split the check?** — "Split the bill?"
- **Can I pay separately?** — "Pay separately?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: US = "check", UK = "bill"
- **No rush**: US servers often bring check without asking; EU = ask
- **Tipping**: US 18-22%, EU often included (round up optional)
- **Paying together**: Common in US (one person pays + others Venmo/Zelle)
- **Split evenly**: "Let's just split it" (vs. itemized)

- `[[food-dining]]`

## Can I get this to go?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Can I get this to go?" — leftovers/doggy bag.

### Usage

End of meal, leaving with food.

### Variations

- **Can I get this to go?** — "To go?" (US)
- **Can I get this to take away?** — "Takeaway" (UK)
- **Can you wrap this up?** — "Wrap it up?"
- **Can I get a box for this?** — "Box for this?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: US = "to go" / "doggy bag" (informal), UK = "takeaway"
- **Container fee**: Some eco-friendly restaurants charge $0.50-1 for boxes
- **Custom**: US = completely normal, EU = less common (smaller portions)
- **Eco-friendly trend**: Many restaurants now use compostable containers

### Sources

- `[[food-dining]]`

## What is in this dish?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "What is in this dish?" — ingredient inquiry.

### Usage

For allergies, dietary restrictions, curiosity.

### Variations

- **What is in this dish?** — "What's in this?"
- **Does this contain dairy?** — "Dairy?"
- **Is this gluten-free?** — "Gluten-free?"
- **Is this spicy?** — "Spicy?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Allergen awareness**: Restaurants increasingly label (GF, V, vegan)
- **Hidden allergens**: Soy sauce (gluten), Worcestershire (anchovy), Caesar dressing (anchovy/egg)
- **Spice level**: US restaurants often adjust (mild/medium/hot)
- **Cross-contamination**: Shared fryers, cutting boards (always ask)

### Sources

- `[[food-dining]]`

## This is delicious!

**Part of Speech:** expression (compliment)
**Definition:** "This is delicious!" — praising food.

### Usage

During or after meal.

### Variations

- **This is delicious!** — "Delicious!"
- **This is amazing!** — "Amazing!"
- **The chef is a genius!** — "Chef is great"
- **I'd come back for this!** — "Worth returning"

### Cultural Notes

- **Complimenting the chef**: Goes a long way (especially in Italy, France, Japan)
- **Tipping extra**: Great meal + great service = tip 25%+
- **Reviews**: Yelp/Google reviews matter to restaurants
- **Returning**: Mention you'd come back to server (appreciated)
