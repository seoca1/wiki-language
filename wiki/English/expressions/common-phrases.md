# common-phrases (English) expressions

**Overview:** English common-phrases 패턴. 일상 대화에서 자주 사용되는 핵심 표현.

### How are you?

**Category:** greeting

**Usage:** 어떻게 지내세요?

**Example:** How are you? — Fine, thank you.

---

### I do not understand

**Category:** clarification

**Usage:** 이해가 안 됩니다

**Example:** I do not understand. Please speak slowly.

---

### Could you help me?

**Category:** request

**Usage:** 도와주실 수 있나요?

**Example:** Could you help me? — Of course!

---

### How much is this?

**Category:** question

**Usage:** 이거 얼마예요?

**Example:** How much is this? — 5 dollars.

---

### Where is the bathroom?

**Category:** question

**Usage:** 화장실 어디예요?

**Example:** Where is the bathroom? — Over there.
