# apologies (English) expressions

**Overview:** English apologies 표현. 일상적 사과 패턴 모음.

### I am sorry.

**Category:** apology

**Usage:** 죄송합니다.

**Example:** I am sorry for being late.

---

### Excuse me.

**Category:** apology

**Usage:** 실례합니다.

**Example:** Excuse me, may I pass?

---

### I beg your pardon.

**Category:** apology

**Usage:** 실례합니다.

**Example:** I beg your pardon, I did not hear you.

---

### Please forgive me.

**Category:** apology

**Usage:** 용서해 주세요.

**Example:** Please forgive me for the mistake.

---

### It was my fault.

**Category:** apology

**Usage:** 제 잘못입니다.

**Example:** It was my fault, I apologize.
