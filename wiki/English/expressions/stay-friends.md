# stay-friends

**Part of Speech:** expression (decline, soft)
**Definition:** "친구로 지내자" — Polite but potentially painful decline.

**Form:** Let's just stay friends.

## Usage

호감은 있지만 연애 감정은 아닐 때. 부드러운 거절.

## Variations

- **Let's just stay friends.** — 캐주얼
- **I really value our friendship.** — 더 부드러움
- **I don't see us as more than friends.** — 직접적
- **I think we're better as friends.** — 정중

## Cultural Notes

**"Friend zone"** concern: this phrase can feel dismissive, like the
speaker is reducing the relationship. **Use carefully** — sometimes
just saying "I don't think we're a match" is more respectful than
the "let's stay friends" cliché.

**Best practice**: honesty + warmth + offer of genuine friendship
if you mean it.
