# Expressions: Business Basics (English)

> **Theme:** Business & Professional
> **Level:** B1-C1 (formal)
> **Standard:** US/UK English

English business communication — greetings, email/meeting phrases, negotiations, contracts.

## Thank you for your email

**Part of Speech:** expression (greeting, formal)
**Definition:** "Thanks for your email" — 정중한 이메일/문자 시작 인사.
**Form:** Thank you for your email.

### Usage

이메일, 문자 (Slack/Teams) 시작.

### Variations

- **Thank you for your email** — "Thanks for your email" (default)
- **Thanks for your message** — "Thanks for your message" (문자)
- **Thank you for reaching out** — "Thanks for contacting"
- **Thank you for your prompt response** — 강조 (응답 빠른 경우)

### Sample Sentences

- **Thank you for your email. I'm writing to confirm...** — 기본 구조
- **Thank you for reaching out. I would be happy to discuss...** — 미팅 응답
- **Thank you for your quick response.** — 빠른 응답 감사

### Follow-up

- **I look forward to hearing from you** — 다음 연락 기대
- **Please let me know if you have any questions** — 추가 문의 환영
- **Best regards** / **Sincerely** / **Thanks** — 마무리 인사

### Cultural Notes

- **영어 이메일**: 본문 전형적으로 3-단락 (인사 → 본론 → 마무리)
- **수신자 인지**: 첫 단락에 수신자 정보 정확히 반영
- **한국어 "감사합니다"**와 같지만 정중한 톤 추가
- **영국 비즈니스 이메일**: 더 격식 ("Dear Sir/Madam" 흔함)
- **미국 비즈니스 이메일**: 더 캐주얼 ("Hi [Name]" 가능)

## I would appreciate it if...

**Part of Speech:** expression (formal request)
**Definition:** "I would appreciate if..." — 정중한 부탁 표현.
**Form:** I would appreciate it if...

### Usage

이메일, 회의, 부탁.

### Variations

- **I would appreciate it if you could...** — "I would appreciate if you could..."
- **I would appreciate your prompt response** — "I would appreciate your quick response"
- **It would be greatly appreciated if...** — "It would be very much appreciated if..."
- **We would appreciate...** — "We would appreciate..."

### Sample Sentences

- **I would appreciate it if you could review the document by Friday.** — "If you could review by Friday, I would appreciate"
- **I would appreciate your feedback at your earliest convenience.** — "Please give feedback when you can"
- **It would be greatly appreciated if you could send the report.** — "Please send the report"

### Variations by Formality

- **Direct**: "Please review the document by Friday."
- **Polite**: "Could you please review the document by Friday?"
- **More polite**: "I would appreciate it if you could review the document by Friday."
- **Most polite**: "It would be greatly appreciated if you could review the document by Friday."

### Cultural Notes

- **영어 비즈니스**: 정중함 단계 (Modal verbs, conditional, subjunctive)
- **"I would appreciate if..."**: 정중함의 안전한 표현
- **한국어 "부탁드립니다"**와 일치
- **이메일 필수**: 영어 비즈니스 이메일에서 정중함이 매우 중요

## Best regards

**Part of Speech:** expression (closing, formal)
**Definition:** "Best regards" — 정중한 이메일 마무리 인사.
**Form:** Best regards.

### Usage

이메일 끝 서명 위 인사말.

### Variations (Most → Least Formal)

- **Sincerely (sincerely yours)** — 가장 격식
- **Respectfully** — 매우 격식
- **Regards** — 격식 (간결)
- **Best regards** — 정중 (default 비즈니스)
- **Kind regards** — 정중 (따뜻)
- **Warm regards** — 캐주얼 (친근)
- **Thanks** — 캐주얼
- **Best** — 매우 캐주얼
- **Cheers (UK)** — 캐주얼 (특히 영국/호주)

### Sample Sign-off

```
Best regards,

John Smith
Senior Manager
ABC Company
Phone: +1-555-123-4567
Email: john.smith@company.com
```

### Cultural Notes

- **이메일 서명**: 격식 인사 + 이름 + 직함 + 회사 + 연락처
- **첫 이메일**: "Sincerely" / "Best regards"
- **반복 이메일**: "Thanks" / "Best" 가능 (덜 격식)
- **비즈니스**: "Best regards" 안전한 선택
- **한국어 "감사합니다"**와 같지만 격식 추가

## I'll follow up next week

**Part of Speech:** expression (action)
**Definition:** "I'll follow up next week" — 후속 조치.
**Form:** I'll follow up next week.

### Usage

이메일/문자 — 다음 연락 일정.

### Variations

- **I'll follow up next week** — "I'll follow up next week"
- **I will circle back next week** — "I'll circle back next week" (비즈니스 캐주얼)
- **I'll be in touch next week** — "I'll be in touch next week"
- **Let's reconnect next week** — "Let's reconnect"
- **I'll get back to you by Friday** — "I'll respond by Friday"

### Sample Sentences

- **I'll follow up next week with more details.** — "I'll follow up with more details next week"
- **Let's reconnect next Tuesday.** — "Let's reconnect next Tuesday"

### Cultural Notes

- **정확한 날짜**: "next Tuesday" > "next week" (더 명확)
- **이메일 follow-up**: 1주 후 답변 없으면 정중하게 follow-up
- **링크/문서 첨부**: 다음 연락 시 첨부 파일 참조
- **한국어 "다음 주에 다시 연락드릴게요"**와 일치

## I'll get back to you on that

**Part of Speech:** expression (response, delay)
**Definition:** "I'll get back to you on that" — 부드러운 보류.
**Form:** I'll get back to you on that.

### Usage

이메일/통화에서 즉시 답 못할 때.

### Variations

- **I'll get back to you on that** — "I'll get back to you"
- **I'll look into that and get back to you** — "I'll look into it"
- **I'll have an answer for you by tomorrow** — "I'll answer by tomorrow"
- **I'll need to check on that** — "I'll need to check"
- **Let me confirm and get back to you** — "Let me confirm and respond"

### Sample Sentences

- **I'll get back to you on that by end of day.** — "I'll respond by EOD"
- **Let me look into that and get back to you tomorrow.** — "Let me check and respond tomorrow"

### Cultural Notes

- **이메일 응답 시간**: 24시간 이내 (US), 4시간 (UK)
- **응답 불가 시**: 즉시 "I'll get back to you" + 예상 시간
- **침묵 = 무례**: 응답 시간 명시 필수
- **한국어 "확인 후 답변드릴게요"**와 일치

## Per our discussion

**Part of Speech:** expression (reference)
**Definition:** "Per our discussion" — 이전 논의 참조.
**Form:** Per our discussion.

### Usage

이메일/문서 — 이전 대화/미팅 참조.

### Variations

- **Per our discussion** — "Per our discussion" (default)
- **As discussed** — "As discussed" (캐주얼)
- **Following our conversation** — "Following our conversation"
- **As per our agreement** — "Per our agreement" (계약/협상)
- **Following up on our conversation** — "Following up"

### Sample Sentences

- **Per our discussion yesterday, I'm sending the proposal for review.** — 이메일 응답
- **As per our agreement, the deadline has been extended to Friday.** — 계약 변경
- **Following up on our conversation about the Q3 budget.** — follow-up 이메일

### Cultural Notes

- **이메일 reference**: 명확한 회상 시점 + 화제
- **구체성**: "Per our discussion about [topic] on [date]" — 더 명확
- **한국어 "지난번 대화에서"**와 일치
- **법적/계약적**: "as per our agreement" 정형화

## I'd like to schedule a meeting

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "I'd like to schedule a meeting" — 미팅 요청.
**Form:** I'd like to schedule a meeting.

### Usage

이메일, 미팅 요청.

### Variations

- **I'd like to schedule a meeting** — "I'd like to schedule a meeting" (default)
- **Would you be available for a meeting?** — "Would you be available?"
- **Let's set up a time to discuss** — "Let's set up a time"
- **Can we find time to meet?** — "Can we find time to meet?"

### Sample Sentences

- **I'd like to schedule a meeting to discuss the Q3 budget. Are you available next Tuesday?**

- **미팅 도구 제안**: "I can send a calendar invite" / "Would Zoom work?"
- **한국어 "미팅을 잡고 싶습니다"**와 일치

## Could you please...

**Part of Speech:** expression (formal request)
**Definition:** "Could you please..." — 정중한 부탁.
**Form:** Could you please...

### Usage

이메일/통화 — 부드러운 요청.

### Variations (Casual → Formal)

- **Do X** — "Send the file" (직접)
- **Please do X** — "Please send the file"
- **Can you do X?** — "Can you send the file?"
- **Could you do X?** — "Could you send the file?" (정중)
- **Could you please do X?** — "Could you please send the file?" (더 정중)
- **Would you mind doing X?** — "Would you mind..." (매우 정중)
- **I would appreciate it if you could do X** — 가장 정중

### Sample Sentences

- **Could you please send me the latest version?** — 정중한 요청
- **Could you please review the document by Friday?** — 정중한 마감
- **Would you mind sharing your feedback at your earliest convenience?** — 매우 정중

### Cultural Notes

- **"Please"**: 영어 비즈니스에서 자주 사용 (필수 아님)
- **Modal verbs**: can / could / would / might (정중함 단계)
- **한국어 "부탁드립니다"**와 일치
