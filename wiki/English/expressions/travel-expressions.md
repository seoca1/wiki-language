# Expressions: Travel Advanced (English)

> **Theme:** Travel (Advanced)
> **Level:** B1-C1
> **Standard:** US/UK English

Advanced travel expressions — booking, ticket issues, deep directions, accommodation problems, emergencies. Follow-up to `[[travel-basics]]`.

---

## I'd like to make a reservation

**Part of Speech:** expression (booking)
**Definition:** "I'd like to make a reservation" — booking flights/hotels/restaurants.

### Usage

Pre-trip reservation request.

### Variations

- **I'd like to make a reservation** — "I want to make a reservation"
- **Do you have any availability for ___?** — "Is there availability for ___?"
- **I'd like to book a table for two** — "Reserve a table for 2"
- **Can I reserve a room for ___ nights?** — "Reserve a room for ___ nights"

### Cultural Notes

- **Booking platforms**: Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb (cheapest)
- **Hotel direct**: Often matches online price + perks (free breakfast/wifi)
- **Restaurant reservations**: OpenTable, Resy (US), TheFork (EU)
- **Cancellation policy**: Most = 24-48 hours free cancellation
- **Peak season**: Summer/Christmas = book 2-3 months ahead

## A one-way / round-trip ticket to ___

**Part of Speech:** expression (transport)
**Definition:** "One-way / round-trip ticket to..." — transport purchase.

### Usage

Buying transport tickets.

### Variations

- **One ticket to Chicago, please** — "1 ticket to Chicago"
- **Round-trip to New York** — "Round trip to NYC"
- **How much is a one-way to Boston?** — "How much for one-way?"
- **Are there any discounts for students?** — Student discount inquiry

### Cultural Notes

- **Ticket types**: One-way / Round-trip / Open-jaw (return from different city)
- **Booking apps**: Skyscanner, Google Flights (compare)
- **Train**: Amtrak (US), Eurail (Europe), Shinkansen (Japan)
- **Student discounts**: ISIC, Student ID (10-50% off)
- **Booking timing**: 6-8 weeks out for cheapest flights

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## What's there to do around here?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "What's there to do around here?" — sightseeing inquiry.

### Usage

Hotel/cafe/taxi inside.

### Variations

- **What's there to do around here?** — "What to do nearby?"
- **Any must-see attractions?** — "Must-see places?"
- **Where do locals hang out?** — "Local spots?"
- **Any hidden gems you'd recommend?** — "Hidden gems?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: TripAdvisor, Google Maps (reviews), Yelp (US)
- **"Off the beaten path"**: Local favorites (avoid tourist traps)
- **Free activities**: Walking tours, parks, museums (free days)
- **Local tourism office**: Free maps + recommendations
- **Reddit / forums**: r/travel, r/solotravel (real advice)

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Where is the restroom?

**Part of Speech:** expression (urgent)
**Definition:** "Where is the restroom?" — basic but essential.

### Usage

Restaurant, museum, public spaces.

### Variations

- **Where is the restroom?** — "Where is the bathroom?" (formal)
- **Where is the bathroom?** — "Where is the bathroom?" (general)
- **Where is the toilet?** — "Where is the toilet?" (UK)
- **Where's the loo?** — "Where's the loo?" (UK slang)

### Cultural Notes

- **Terminology**: US = "restroom"/"bathroom", UK = "toilet"/"loo"
- **Public restrooms**: Free in malls/museums, paid in some EU cities (€0.50)
- **Disabled access**: ADA-compliant (US), accessible (EU)
- **Starbucks**: Famous for letting non-customers use restrooms (US policy)

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Can I get a discount?

**Part of Speech:** expression (negotiation)
**Definition:** "Can I get a discount?" — bargaining at markets (NOT at retail).

### Usage

Markets, tourist shops, some attractions.

### Variations

- **Can I get a discount?** — "Is there a discount?"
- **Is there a bulk discount?** — "Bulk discount?"
- **Student discount available?** — "Student discount?"
- **Do you have any specials?** — "Any specials?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Bargaining culture**: 
  - US/UK/Canada = NO bargaining (retail fixed price)
  - Mexico/Turkey/Morocco/Southeast Asia = YES (markets)
  - China/HK = YES (markets, NOT malls)
- **Tip**: Cash often = better deal
- **Walk away**: Vendor may call you back with lower price (Asia)

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## I'm lost

**Part of Speech:** expression (problem)
**Definition:** "I'm lost" — getting help when disoriented.

### Usage

When needing directions or help.

### Variations

- **I'm lost** — "I'm lost"
- **Can you help me?** — "Can you help me?"
- **Where am I?** — "Where am I?"
- **Do you speak English?** — "Do you speak English?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: Google Maps (offline download), Maps.me (offline)
- **Hotel card**: Show taxi driver your hotel card (works worldwide)
- **Tourist info**: Most cities have tourist info kiosks
- **English in non-English countries**: Younger people often speak basic English
- **Police**: Helpful + often speak English (tourist areas)

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Is there WiFi here?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Is there WiFi here?" — internet access inquiry.

### Usage

Cafes, hotels, airports, public spaces.

### Variations

- **Is there WiFi here?** — "WiFi available?"
- **What's the WiFi password?** — "WiFi password?"
- **Is the WiFi free?** — "Free WiFi?"
- **Do you have an ethernet cable?** — "Wired connection?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Free WiFi**: Most cafes/restaurants worldwide (McDonald's, Starbucks)
- **Public WiFi**: Parks, libraries, transit stations (EU/US)
- **VPN**: Recommended for public networks (security)
- **Hotel WiFi**: Often free for guests; some charge ($10-20/night)
- **Mobile data**: eSIM (Airalo, Holafly) for international travel

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## I need an English-speaking guide

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "I need an English-speaking guide" — guided tour request.

### Usage

Tourist sites, museums, historical areas.

### Variations

- **I need an English-speaking guide** — "Need English guide"
- **Is there an English tour available?** — "English tour?"
- **How much for a half-day tour?** — "Half-day price?"
- **Where do I meet the tour group?** — "Meeting point?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Tour types**: Group / Private / Self-guided audio
- **Apps**: GetYourGuide, Viator, Klook (Asia)
- **Free walking tours**: Most major cities (tip-based)
- **Museum tours**: Often included with admission (English at major museums)
- **Local guides**: Better context + cultural insights than apps

- `[[travel-basics]]`
