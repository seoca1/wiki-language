# agreement (English) expressions

**Overview:** English agreement 표현. 일상적 동의/찬성 패턴 모음.

### Yes, I agree.

**Category:** agreement

**Usage:** 네, 동의합니다.

**Example:** Yes, I agree with your plan.

---

### That sounds good.

**Category:** agreement

**Usage:** 좋은 생각이네요.

**Example:** That sounds good to me.

---

### I think so too.

**Category:** agreement

**Usage:** 저도 그렇게 생각해요.

**Example:** I think so too, actually.

---

### Exactly!

**Category:** agreement

**Usage:** 맞아요!

**Example:** Exactly! That is what I meant.

---

### You are right.

**Category:** agreement

**Usage:** 당신 맞아요.

**Example:** You are right, I made a mistake.
