# see-you-again

**Part of Speech:** expression (continuation, post-date)
**Definition:** "또 만나자" — Direct expression of wanting to meet again.

**Form:** Can I see you again? / Let's do this again.

## Usage

**관계 지속의 명시적 신호**. "great time" 표현과 함께 사용.

## Variations

- **Can I see you again?** — 직접적 질문
- **Let's do this again.** — 캐주얼
- **I'd love to do this again.** — 정중
- **Same time next week?** — 구체적 제안

## Cultural Notes

Saying this **at the end of a first date** is the standard way to
indicate interest in continuing. Without it, the other person may
wonder.

**Tone matters**: confidence + warmth, not desperation.
