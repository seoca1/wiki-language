# Expressions: Technology & Internet (English)

> **Theme:** Technology & Internet
> **Level:** A2-C1
> **Standard:** US/UK English

Tech/internet expressions — apps, payments, social media, online culture.

---

## What's the WiFi password?

**Part of Speech:** expression (tech)
**Definition:** "What's the WiFi password?" — connecting to internet.

### Usage

Cafes, hotels, friends' homes, offices.

### Variations

- **What's the WiFi password?** — "WiFi password?"
- **Is there WiFi here?** — "WiFi available?"
- **Do you have the WiFi password?** — "Got the WiFi password?"
- **Is the WiFi free?** — "Free WiFi?"

### Cultural Notes

- **Coffee shops**: Most offer free WiFi (ask barista for password)
- **Hotels**: Often on key card holder or reception
- **Public WiFi**: Most cities offer free public WiFi (parks, transit)
- **VPN**: Always use on public WiFi (security)
- **Mobile hotspot**: Backup option (use sparingly — drains battery/data)

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Can I get your number?

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Can I get your number?" — sharing contact info.

### Usage

Meeting new people, dating, networking.

### Variations

- **Can I get your number?** — "Your number?"
- **Can I add you on Instagram?** — "Add on IG?"
- **Can I get your email?** — "Your email?"
- **Let's connect on LinkedIn** — "Connect on LinkedIn"

### Cultural Notes

- **Phone vs. social**: Younger = Instagram/Snapchat, professional = LinkedIn
- **Area code**: US/Canada = regional identity
- **WhatsApp**: International standard
- **Signal**: Privacy-focused (Europe/US popular)
- **iMessage vs SMS**: Green bubble = Android (some social stigma US teens)

### Sources

- `[[dating-romance]]`

## DM me

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "DM me" — direct message on social media.

### Usage

Social media communication.

### Variations

- **DM me** — "Direct message me"
- **Slide into my DMs** — "Message me" (flirty)
- **Send me a DM** — "DM me"
- **I'll DM you the details** — "I'll message you"

### Cultural Notes

- **Platform-specific**: Instagram DMs, Twitter DMs, LinkedIn messages
- **DM etiquette**: Don't DM strangers sales pitches (spam)
- **"Slide into DMs"**: Often used humorously (flirting)
- **Verification**: Blue checkmark = verified account (Twitter, Instagram)
- **Read receipts**: Some apps show (iMessage), some don't

- `[[daily-life]]`

## Tag me

**Part of Speech:** expression (social)
**Definition:** "Tag me" — mentioning someone in social media post.

### Usage

Sharing content with friends.

### Variations

- **Tag me** — "Tag me in the photo"
- **Tag everyone** — "Tag your friends"
- **Don't tag me** — "Please don't tag"
- **I was tagged in ___** — "I was tagged"

### Cultural Notes

- **@mention**: Twitter/Instagram mention system
- **#hashtag**: Categorizes content
- **Privacy**: Some people prefer not to be tagged (especially in unflattering photos)
- **"Tag yourself"**: Memes where people tag themselves
- **Notifications**: Tag = immediate notification to tagged user

## No cap

**Part of Speech:** expression (slang)
**Definition:** "No lie / For real" — affirmation.

### Usage

Gen Z slang (since ~2020).

### Variations

- **No cap** — "For real"
- **On god** — "I swear"
- **Fr (For real)** — "Seriously"
- **Deadass** — "Really" (informal)

### Cultural Notes

- **Origin**: AAVE (African American Vernacular English), now mainstream Gen Z
- **Usage**: Affirming truthfulness ("That was the best movie, no cap")
- **Generations**: Gen Z/Gen Alpha = mainstream, older = may not understand
- **Internet slang**: Evolves rapidly (TikTok influence)

### Sources

- `[[slang-colloquial]]`

## Rideshare

**Part of Speech:** expression (urban)
**Definition:** "Rideshare" — Uber/Lyft style service.

### Usage

Modern transportation.

### Variations

- **Rideshare** — "Uber/Lyft"
- **Ride-hailing** — "Ride-hailing"
- **Can you call me a Lyft?** — "Get me a Lyft"
- **Surge pricing** — "Higher prices due to demand"

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps (US)**: Uber, Lyft (main)
- **Apps (UK)**: Uber, Bolt, FreeNow
- **Apps (Asia)**: Grab (SE Asia), Didi (China), Gojek (Indonesia)
- **Driver ratings**: Both rider and driver rate each other
- **Tipping (US)**: Optional but appreciated (added in app)
- **Safety**: Share trip with friend, verify license plate

### Sources

- `[[travel-basics]]`

## Live-stream shopping

**Part of Speech:** expression (commerce)
**Definition:** "Live-stream shopping" — buying via live video.

### Usage

Modern e-commerce.

### Variations

- **Live-stream shopping** — "Live shopping"
- **WhatNot** — "Live auction app"
- **Twitch shopping** — "Gaming stream shopping"
- **QVC-style live** — "QVC live"

### Cultural Notes

- **Asia dominant**: China/Korea/Japan (more mature market)
- **US growing**: TikTok Shop, Amazon Live, WhatNot
- **Influencer-led**: Host demonstrates product live
- **Real-time chat**: Viewers ask questions, comment
- **Limited-time deals**: Creates urgency
