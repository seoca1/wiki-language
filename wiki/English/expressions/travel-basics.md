## Where is the airport?

**Part of Speech:** expression (direction)
**Definition:** "Where is the airport?" — 가장 흔한 방향 문의.
**Form:** Where is the airport?

### Usage

새 도시 도착 시 첫 질문.

### Variations

- **Where is the airport?** — "Where is the airport?"
- **How do I get to the airport?** — "How do I get to the airport?"
- **Is there a shuttle to the airport?** — "Is there a shuttle?"
- **Can you call me a taxi to the airport?** — "Can you call me a taxi?"

### Sample Conversations

- **Excuse me, where is the airport?** — 직원에게
- **How much does a taxi to the airport cost?** — 택시 비용
- **How long does it take to get to the airport?** — 소요 시간

### Cultural Notes

- **Airport signs**: 모든 영어 표기 (보편)
- **City airport**: 대부분 도심 30-60분 거리
- **Shuttle**: 호텔/대형시설 무료 셔틀 흔함
- **Rideshare**: Uber/Lyft 보편 (US), Grab/Gojek (아시아)

## Can I have a ticket to ___?

**Part of Speech:** expression (request)
**Definition:** "Can I have a ticket to ___?" — 교통 티켓 구매.
**Form:** Can I have a ticket to ___?

### Usage

기차역, 버스터미널, 공항.

### Variations

- **Can I have a ticket to ___?** — "Can I have a ticket to ___?"
- **One ticket to ___, please** — "One ticket, please"
- **Two tickets to ___, please** — "Two tickets, please"
- **Round-trip / One-way?** — "Round trip or one way?"

### Sample Conversations

- **Can I have a ticket to Chicago?** — 단거리
- **Two tickets to Boston, please. Round-trip.** — 왕복
- **How much is a ticket to New York?** — 가격 문의

### Cultural Notes

- **Ticket types**: One-way / Round-trip / Open-jaw (다른 도시에서 돌아오기)
- **Booking**: 앱/웹 = 최저가 (Kiwi, Skyscanner, Booking.com)
- **Same-day**: Walk-up 가격 > 사전 예매
- **Student/AAA discounts**: 신분증 제시 시 할인

- `[[Language/wiki/comparative/travel-essentials]]`

## How much is a taxi to ___?

**Part of Speech:** expression (price)
**Definition:** "How much is a taxi to ___?" — 택시 비용.
**Form:** How much is a taxi to ___?

### Usage

택시 이용 전 비용 확인.

### Variations

- **How much is a taxi to ___?** — "How much is a taxi to ___?"
- **How much would a taxi cost to ___?** — "How much would a taxi cost?"
- **Can I get a taxi to ___?** — "Can I get a taxi?"

### Sample Conversations

- **How much is a taxi to the airport?** — 공항 택시
- **How much to downtown?** — 시내 택시
- **Can you take me to ___?** — 운전사에게

### Cultural Notes

- **Tipping**: US 15-20%, UK 10-12% (often included), EU varies, AU 10%, JP/KR/CN 0%
- **Meter vs. fixed**: 대부분 미터제, 일부 공항/관광지는 정액
- **Apps**: Uber/Lyft (US), Grab (SE Asia), Didi (China)
- **Taxis in tourist areas**: 높은 가격 가능 (사전 가격 확인)

## How do I get to ___?

**Part of Speech:** expression (direction)
**Definition:** "How do I get to ___?" — 가장 흔한 길 묻기.
**Form:** How do I get to ___?

### Usage

도시, 관광지.

### Variations

- **How do I get to ___?** — "How do I get to ___?"
- **What's the best way to get to ___?** — "What's the best way?"
- **Can you give me directions to ___?** — "Can you give me directions?"
- **Is it within walking distance?** — "Can I walk there?"

### Sample Conversations

- **How do I get to the museum?** — 박물관
- **How do I get to the airport from here?** — 공항
- **Is the train station within walking distance?** — 도보 거리

### Cultural Notes

- **"Here"**: 현지 — 명확히 표시 필요
- **Public transport**: 대중교통 표시 (영어)
- **Walking distance**: 보통 15-20분 이내
- **스마트폰**: Google Maps 즉시 길 찾기

## Call 911!

**Part of Speech:** expression (emergency)
**Definition:** "Call 911!" — 응급 호출 (미국/캐나다).
**Form:** Call 911!

### Usage

응급 (의료, 화재, 범죄).

### Variations

- **Call 911!** — "Call 911!"
- **Call an ambulance!** — "구급차 불러주세요!"
- **Call the police!** — "경찰 불러주세요!"
- **I need help!** — "도와주세요!"

### Emergency Numbers by Country

| Country | Number |
|---------|--------|
| **US** | **911** |
| **Canada** | **911** |
| **UK** | **999 / 112** |
| **Australia** | **000** |
| **EU** | **112** |
| **Japan** | **110 (police) / 119 (fire/ambulance)** |
| **Korea** | **112 (police) / 119 (fire/ambulance)** |
| **China** | **110 (police) / 120 (ambulance) / 119 (fire)** |

### Sample Situations

- **Call 911! Someone is hurt!** — 부상자 발생
- **Call 911! There's a fire!** — 화재
- **Call the police!** — 범죄

### Cultural Notes

- **공공장소**: CCTV 광범위, 즉시 도움
- **영어 소통**: 911 호출 시 영어 가능
- **여행자 보험**: 강력 권장
- **호텔 카드**: 응급 번호 적힌 카드
- **한국어 "119 불러주세요"**와 일치

## Where can I find a good restaurant?

**Part of Speech:** expression (question)
**Definition:** "Where can I find a good restaurant?" — 식당 추천 요청.
**Form:** Where can I find a good restaurant?

### Usage

도시, 관광지에서 현지 식당 찾기.

### Variations

- **Where can I find a good restaurant?** — "Where can I find a good restaurant?"
- **What's a good place to eat around here?** — "What's a good place?"
- **Any recommendations for local food?** — "Any recommendations for local food?"

### Sample Conversations

- **Excuse me, where can I find a good restaurant nearby?** — 관광지
- **Any recommendations for local food?** — 현지 음식
- **Where do locals eat?** — 로컬 추천

### Cultural Notes

- **Apps**: Yelp, TripAdvisor, Google Maps (현지 리뷰)
- **Hotel concierge**: 호텔 직원에게 추천 요청 (보통 최고의 옵션)
- **Local specialties**: 도시/지역 특색 음식 (여행의 하이라이트)
- **"Tourist trap"**: 관광객 표적 식당 주의
