# Expressions: Daily Basics

> **Theme:** Daily Basics
> **Level:** A1-B2 (idioms)

Universal phrases for everyday situations — greetings, directions, simple inquiries.

---

## how-much

**Part of Speech:** expression (question phrase)
**Definition:** "얼마예요?" — 가격을 물을 때 쓰는 가장 기본 영어 표현.
**Form:** How much [is it]?

### Usage


상점, 식당, 시장 등에서 가격 문의.


### Variations


- **How much is it?** — 얼마예요?
- **How much does this cost?** — 이거 얼마예요?
- **How much for one?** — 하나에 얼마예요?
- **How much in total?** — 전부 얼마예요?


### Related Expressions


- **What is the price?** — 가격이 어떻게 되나요?
- **Can I get a discount?** — 깎아주세요
- **Too expensive** — 너무 비싸요


### Cultural Notes


미국에서는 "check, please"로 계산 요청, 영국에서는 "bill, please" 사용.

## where-is

**Part of Speech:** expression (question phrase)
**Definition:** "어디인가요?" — 위치·방향 물을 때 쓰는 가장 기본 영어 표현.
**Form:** Where is [place]?

### Usage


여행, 길 묻기, 사물 위치 확인 시 사용.


### Variations


- **Where is the hotel?** — 호텔은 어디인가요?
- **Where is the airport?** — 공항은 어디인가요?
- **Where is the bathroom?** — 화장실은 어디인가요?
- **Excuse me, where is the station?** — 실례합니다, 역은 어디인가요?


### Related Expressions


- **Where are you from?** — 어디서 오셨어요?
- **How do I get to...?** — 어떻게 가나요?
- **Is it far?** — 멀어요?


### Cultural Notes


영어권에서는 "Excuse me"로 시작하는 것이 예의. 바로 "Where is"로 시작하면 약간 무례하게 들릴 수 있음.

## nice-to-meet-you

**Part of Speech:** expression (greeting)
**Definition:** "만나서 반가워요" — Standard polite greeting on first meeting.
**Form:** Nice to meet you.

### Usage


처음 만났을 때 한 번. 그 후로는 "Hi" / "Hey" / "Good to see you".


### Variations


- **Nice to meet you.** — 정중, 격식
- **Nice meeting you.** — 헤어질 때 (변형)
- **Pleased to meet you.** — 격식, 비즈니스
- **Good to meet you.** — 캐주얼


### Formal vs Casual


- Formal: "It's a pleasure to meet you."
- Casual: "Hey, nice to meet ya!"


### Cultural Notes


**Only said once**. Repeating "nice to meet you" on a second
meeting is awkward. Use "Hi again" or "Good to see you" instead.

## im-busy

**Part of Speech:** expression (decline, evasive)
**Definition:** "바빠" — Evasive way to decline without giving specific reason.
**Form:** I'm busy.

### Usage


**거절의 회피적 표현**. 명확한 이유 없이 거절.


### Variations


- **I'm busy.** — 단순
- **I'm busy that day.** — 구체적 날짜
- **I have plans.** — 회피
- **I can't that day.** — 직접
- **Maybe another time.** — 부드러운 회피


### Cultural Notes


**"I'm busy" is often a polite no**, especially in dating. The
speaker is signaling they don't want to commit to a meeting without
saying "no" directly.

**Honest alternative**: "I appreciate it, but I'm not interested
in dating right now" is more respectful.

**If you ARE busy**: be specific. "I have a work thing Tuesday
night — how about Thursday?" shows real interest.
