## Summary

English vocabulary for sports, hobbies, recreation, fitness. Useful for casual conversation and active lifestyle.

## Key Takeaways

- Common sports (soccer, basketball, tennis, swimming, running)
- Hobby vocabulary (reading, cooking, painting, music)
- Fitness (gym, yoga, exercise, workout)
- Outdoor activities (hiking, camping, fishing, cycling)
- Sports terms (team, score, win, lose, champion)

## Cultural Insights

- Sports culture: very important in US (football, basketball, baseball)
- "Football" means different things: soccer in UK, football in US
- March Madness: US college basketball tournament, huge
- World Cup: globally watched
- Hiking: very popular in Colorado, California, Pacific Northwest
- Yoga: very common in US, often 1+ class per week
- Fantasy sports: very popular in US
