- Nature metaphors pervasive in English idioms
- "Sun/star" = celebrity; "rain/cloud" = gloom; "mountain" = challenge
- Weather talk = universal small talk (British especially)
- "Fall" (US) vs "autumn" (UK) for season
- Environmental vocabulary increasingly relevant (deforestation, climate)
