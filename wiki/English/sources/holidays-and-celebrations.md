## Cultural Insights

- Christmas: Dec 25, family gathering, gift exchange
- Thanksgiving: 4th Thursday of November, US/Canada only
- Halloween: Oct 31, costumes, trick-or-treat
- Weddings: usually 6+ months planning, expensive ($30K+ in US)
- Birthdays: cake with candles, "Happy Birthday" song
- "Black Friday": day after Thanksgiving, biggest sale day in US
- Public holidays: 10-11 federal holidays in US
