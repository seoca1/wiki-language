## Cultural Insights

- "Reply all" vs "reply" distinction critical in corporate email culture
- "CC" (carbon copy) and "BCC" (blind carbon copy) standard fields
- "Out of office" auto-reply standard for absences
- Meeting culture varies: US (direct, time-boxed) vs UK (consensus-building) vs AU (informal)
- "Action items" = tasks assigned in meetings
- "Circle back" = follow up later (US corporate speak)
