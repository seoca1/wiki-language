# Shopping and Money — English (Source Hub)

**Type:** Shopping / Money
**Date Added:** 2026-06-20
**Language Level:** A1-A2 (Beginner)

## Summary

English for shopping, money, prices, bargaining. Useful for travelers and daily life.

## Key Takeaways

- Types of shops (grocery, department, convenience, market)
- Money vocabulary (cash, card, change, refund, receipt)
- Price phrases (how much, expensive, cheap, discount)
- Shopping actions (buy, sell, return, exchange, try on)
- Common products (clothes, electronics, food, gifts)

## Expressions Extracted

- "How much is it?" — 얼마예요?
- "Do you take credit cards?" — 카드 받나요?
- "Can I have a discount?" — 할인 되나요?
- "Can I try it on?" — 입어볼 수 있나요?
- "Where is the fitting room?" — 탈의실 어디예요?

## Cultural Insights

- Sales tax: varies by state in US (0-10%)
- Tipping: not required in retail (but appreciated for great service)
- "Free gift with purchase": common marketing tactic
- Returns: usually 30-90 days with receipt
- Haggling: not common in US retail, only at markets/garage sales
- Sales tax is added at checkout, NOT on the price tag
