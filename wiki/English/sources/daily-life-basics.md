# Daily Life Basics — English (Source Hub)

**Type:** Daily Life
**Date Added:** 2026-06-20
**Language Level:** A1-A2 (Beginner)

## Summary

Everyday English for daily routines — greetings, time, weather, family, hobbies, work. The most commonly used words in everyday conversation.

## Key Takeaways

- Greetings (hello, good morning, how are you)
- Time expressions (today, tomorrow, yesterday, now)
- Family members (mother, father, sister, brother)
- Common verbs (go, come, eat, drink, sleep, work)
- Adjectives (good, bad, big, small, hot, cold)
- Numbers (1-100, ordinals)

## Cultural Insights

- Small talk: weather, weekend plans, sports teams
- "How are you?" is rhetorical — short answer expected ("Fine, thanks")
- American optimism: "Great!" / "Awesome!" / "Awesome sauce" (slang)
- British understatement: "Not bad" / "Quite good" / "Lovely"
