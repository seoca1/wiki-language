## Summary

English vocabulary for technology, internet, computers, smartphones. Modern essential terms.

## Key Takeaways

- Computer hardware (keyboard, mouse, screen, laptop)
- Internet terms (website, email, app, password, wifi)
- Social media (post, like, share, follow, comment)
- Common tech verbs (download, upload, search, click, scroll)
- Tech problems (broken, slow, error, update, virus)

- "How do I connect?" — 어떻게 연결해요?

## Cultural Insights

- Smartphone addiction: very common in US/Asia, less so in Europe
- Tipping for tech support: NOT customary
- Tech etiquette: don't take calls in restaurants/movies
- "I'm off the grid" = no phone/internet
- Battery life: carry a portable charger
- Streaming: Netflix/Hulu/Disney+ dominant in US
