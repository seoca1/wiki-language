# Travel Adventure — English (Source Hub)

**Type:** Travel / Adventure
**Date Added:** 2026-06-20
**Language Level:** B1-B2 (Intermediate)

## Summary

English for travel adventures — hiking, camping, outdoor activities, weather, emergencies. Intermediate vocabulary for active travelers.

## Key Takeaways

- Outdoor activities (hike, climb, swim, ski)
- Camping gear (tent, sleeping bag, backpack, flashlight)
- Weather conditions (sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy, foggy)
- Emergencies (injury, lost, stranded, evacuation)
- Directions in nature (north, south, east, west, trail)

## Expressions Extracted

- "How far is it?" — 얼마나 먼가요?
- "I'm lost" — 길을 잃었어요
- "Can you help me?" — 도와주세요
- "Where is the trail?" — 산책로는 어디예요?
- "I need a doctor" — 의사가 필요해요

## Cultural Insights

- Hiking culture: very popular in US (Appalachian, Pacific Crest trails)
- National Parks: $20-35 entry, many have annual passes
- Bear safety: "If it's black, fight back. If it's brown, lie down. If it's white, say goodnight."
- Weather: always check forecast before outdoor activities
- Cell service: often no signal in mountains/national parks
