## Cultural Insights

- "Afraid" predicative only (not *an afraid child*)
- "Angry" directness varies by culture (US/UK > East Asian)
- "Jealous" vs "envious" distinction often lost
- "Happy" in greetings = performative politeness
- "Kind" = baseline expectation, not exceptional
- "Cool" = ultimate multivalent slang (AAVE origin)
