# Work and Career — English (Source Hub)

**Type:** Work / Professional
**Date Added:** 2026-06-20
**Language Level:** A2-B2 (Intermediate)

## Summary

English vocabulary for the workplace — job titles, meetings, emails, business etiquette. Useful for professionals working in international environments.

## Key Takeaways

- Job titles and roles
- Office vocabulary (meeting, deadline, project, report)
- Email phrases (please find attached, regards, kindly)
- Interview language (experience, skills, salary)
- Business meeting phrases (agenda, action items, follow up)

## Vocabulary Extracted

- work — 일

- project — 프로젝트
- deadline — 마감
- email — 이메일
- salary — 급여
- colleague — 동료
- boss — 상사

## Expressions Extracted

- "Let's schedule a meeting" — 회의를 잡아보죠
- "I'll get back to you" — 답장드릴게요
- "Per my last email" — 지난 이메일에 따르면
- "Please find attached" — 첨부 파일 확인 부탁드립니다
- "Best regards" — 감사합니다 (closing)

## Cultural Insights

- Direct vs indirect: US is direct, UK is more indirect
- Email formality: American business more casual than Japanese/Korean
- Networking: critical in US, less so in some European countries
- "Let's grab coffee" = informal meeting, common in US business
- Work-life balance: varies widely; US tends to overwork
