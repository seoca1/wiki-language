## 핵심 추출 사항 (Key Extractions)

### 어휘 카테고리
1. **공항 (Airport)**: airport, passport, immigration, customs, luggage
2. **호텔 (Hotel)**: hotel, reservation, room, breakfast, key
3. **식당 (Restaurant)**: restaurant, menu, order, bill, tip
4. **교통 (Transport)**: station, subway, train, bus, taxi
5. **관광 (Sightseeing)**: temple, shrine, museum, park, mountain

### 표현 (Expressions)
- "Where is the hotel?" — 위치 묻기
- "How much is it?" — 가격 묻기
- "Thank you very much" — 감사
- "Excuse me" — 사과/호출

### 문화 노트 (Cultural Notes)
- No tipping in Japan
- Remove shoes at homes/temples
- Chopstick etiquette
- Photo restrictions
- Onsen manners
