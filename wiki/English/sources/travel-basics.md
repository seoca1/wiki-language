# Travel Basics — English (Source Hub)

**Type:** Travel / Daily Life
**Date Added:** 2026-06-20
**Language Level:** A1-B1 (Beginner)

## Summary

Essential English vocabulary and phrases for travelers — airports, hotels, restaurants, transportation, emergencies. Covers the top 50 most-used travel words.

## Key Takeaways

- Greetings and politeness (please, thank you, sorry)
- Numbers and money (cash, card, change)
- Directions (left, right, near, far)
- Transportation (bus, train, taxi, plane)
- Food and drink (menu, water, coffee, check)
- Emergencies (help, police, hospital, lost)

## Expressions Extracted

- "How much is it?" — 얼마예요?
- "Where is...?" — ~는 어디인가요?
- "Can you help me?" — 도와주실 수 있나요?
- "I don't understand" — 이해하지 못합니다
- "Do you speak English?" — 영어 하세요?

## Cultural Insights

- Tipping: 15-20% in US, 10-12% in UK, not expected in many other English-speaking countries
- Personal space: ~45cm in conversation
- Eye contact: direct eye contact = honesty; avoiding = shifty/lying
- Loud talking: Americans louder than British/Australians
