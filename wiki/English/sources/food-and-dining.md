## Summary

English vocabulary for restaurants, cooking, food types, dietary restrictions. Useful for travelers and food enthusiasts.

## Key Takeaways

- Restaurant vocabulary (menu, waiter, bill, tip)
- Common foods (bread, meat, fish, vegetables, fruit)
- Drinks (water, coffee, tea, juice, beer, wine)
- Cooking methods (boil, fry, bake, grill)
- Flavors (sweet, sour, salty, bitter, spicy)
- Dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free)

## Cultural Insights

- Tipping: 15-20% standard in US, included in UK/EU
- Fast food culture: very different from European sit-down dining
- Coffee: drip (US) vs espresso (Europe) — order differently
- "Grab a bite" = quick meal, often fast food
- Brunch: late morning (10am-1pm) — popular weekend meal
