**Type:** Health

## Summary

English vocabulary for health, body parts, illness, doctor visits. Essential for emergencies and daily life.

## Key Takeaways

- Body parts (head, hand, foot, eye, ear)
- Common illnesses (cold, flu, fever, headache, stomachache)
- Doctor/hospital vocabulary (appointment, prescription, pharmacy)
- Health verbs (hurt, sick, tired, better, worse)
- Wellness (exercise, sleep, water, food)

## Cultural Insights

- Healthcare: very expensive in US without insurance
- Pharmacy: CVS, Walgreens common in US
- "Take your medicine" often means OTC drugs (Tylenol, Advil)
- Mental health: increasingly discussed openly in US
- "Man flu" (slang): men exaggerating cold symptoms
- "I'm sick" = general illness; "I have a cold" = specific
