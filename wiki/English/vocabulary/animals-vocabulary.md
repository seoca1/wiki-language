## Pets & Farm Animals

### dog

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** A domesticated carnivorous mammal (Canis familiaris), typically kept as a pet or for work.

**IPA / Pronunciation:** /dɒɡ/

**Etymology:** Old English *docga*, of unknown origin.

#### Examples

- I have a **dog** named Max. — Pet ownership
- The **dog** barks at strangers. — Behavior description

#### Related Terms

- ''puppy'' — Young dog
- [[cat]] — Common household pet

#### Cultural Notes

Dogs are among the most popular pets in English-speaking countries. "Man's best friend" is a common phrase.
