# Fish

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A limbless cold-blooded vertebrate animal living in water.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /fɪʃ/
- Syllables: fish (1 syllable)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000)

## Examples

- "I eat fish twice a week." — 일주일에 두 번 생선 먹어
