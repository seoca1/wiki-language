## Basic Natural Phenomena

### sun

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** The star at the center of our solar system; the source of light and heat for Earth.

**IPA / Pronunciation:** /sʌn/

**Etymology:** Old English *sunne*, from Proto-Germanic *sunnōn*.

#### Examples

- The **sun** rises in the east. — Fact
- Enjoy the **sun**. — Leisure

#### Related Terms

- ''sunrise'' / ''sunset'' — Daily events
- ''sunlight'' — Light from sun
- ''solar'' — Adjective

#### Cultural Notes

"Sun" = life source in many cultures. "Make hay while the sun shines" = seize opportunity. "Sun worship" = historical religion.
