# smart

**Part of Speech:** adjective
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 똑똑한 — Having or showing a quick-witted intelligence.

## Examples

- "You're really smart." — 지적 매력
- "That's a smart idea." — 아이디어 칭찬
- "He's smart and hardworking." — 종합

## Related Terms

- [[kind]] — kindness
- [[funny]] — humor
- [[clever]] — UK English equivalent

## Cultural Notes

"Smart" = US/Canada. "Clever" = UK (often implies trickery). Modern
"smart" is gender-neutral. Highly valued trait, especially in
long-term partner context.
