# Vegetable

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A plant or part of a plant used as food, typically excluding fruits and sweets.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈvɛdʒtəbəl/
- Syllables: veg-e-ta-ble (4 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (VEG-e-ta-ble)

**Etymology:** From Latin "vegetabilis" meaning "animating" or "freshening." The word originally meant any plant, not just edible ones.

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "Tomato is a vegetable" → botanically a fruit, but commonly called a vegetable in cooking
- ❌ "I eat many vegetable" → use plural "vegetables"
- ✅ "Eat more vegetables."
- ✅ "Fresh vegetables are healthy."

**Types:**
- **Leafy greens:** lettuce, spinach, kale
- **Root vegetables:** carrot, potato, beet
- **Cruciferous:** broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500 English words)

## Examples

- "I don't like vegetables." — 나는 채소를 싫어해
- "Eat your vegetables!" — 채소 먹어!
- "Vegetables are good for you." — 채소는 몸에 좋아
