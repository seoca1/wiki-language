## Basic Clothing

### shirt

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** A garment for the upper body, typically with sleeves, collar, and buttons.

**IPA / Pronunciation:** /ʃɜːrt/

**Etymology:** Old English *scyrte* "short garment," from *scort* "short."

#### Examples

- I'm wearing a blue **shirt**. — Description
- **Shirts** go in the top drawer. — Storage

#### Related Terms

- ''t-shirt'' — Casual shirt
- ''blouse'' — Women's shirt
- ''polo shirt'' — Collared casual

#### Cultural Notes

"Keep your shirt on" = stay calm. "Shirt off my back" = extreme generosity.

### pants

**Part of Speech:** noun (plural)

**Definition:** A garment covering each leg separately, from waist to ankles.

**IPA / Pronunciation:** /pænts/

**Etymology:** Short for *pantaloons*, from Italian *Pantalone* (commedia dell'arte character).

#### Examples

- These **pants** are too long. — Fit
- **Pants** or **trousers**? — US vs UK

#### Related Terms

- ''jeans'' — Denim pants
- ''trousers'' — UK/formal term
- ''shorts'' — Short pants

#### Cultural Notes

US: "pants" = trousers. UK: "pants" = underwear. "Fancy pants" = showing off.
