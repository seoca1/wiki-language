## Essential Phrases

- "What's your hobby?" — 취미가 뭐예요?
- "I like playing soccer." — 축구하는 것 좋아해요
- "Do you want to play tennis?" — 테니스 칠래요?
- "Let's watch the game!" — 경기를 보자!
- "What time does the game start?" — 경기 시작 몇 시예요?
- "Who's playing?" — 누가 나와요?
- "What's the score?" — 점수가 어떻게 돼요?
- "It was a great game!" — 좋은 경기였어요!
