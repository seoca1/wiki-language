## Email & Correspondence

### email

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** Electronic mail; messages distributed by electronic means from one computer user to one or more recipients.

**IPA / Pronunciation:** /ˈiːmeɪl/

**Etymology:** 1970s: from *e-* (electronic) + *mail*.

#### Examples

- I sent an **email** to the client. — Communication
- Check your **email** for the attachment. — Instruction

#### Related Terms

- [[attachment]] — File sent with email
- [[sender]] / [[recipient]] — Email parties

#### Cultural Notes

Standard business communication. "Email chain" = thread of replies.
