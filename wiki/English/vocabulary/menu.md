# Menu

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A list of dishes available at a restaurant, or a set of options in a computer program.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈmɛnjuː/
- Syllables: me-nu (2 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (ME-nu)

**Etymology:** From French "menu" (small, detailed), from Latin "minutus" (little).

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 2000)

## Examples

- "May I see the menu?" — 메뉴 볼 수 있나요?
- "The menu has many options." — 메뉴에 많은 선택지가 있어
