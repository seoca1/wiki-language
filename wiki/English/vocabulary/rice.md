# Rice

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A cereal grain widely grown and used as food, staple in many cuisines.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /raɪs/
- Syllables: rice (1 syllable)
- Sound: same as "rise" (without the 'e')

**Etymology:** From Latin "oryza", from Greek "oruza". The word traveled through Arabic ("aruzz") to Old French ("ris").

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I want some rices" → uncountable in English
- ❌ "The rice is undercooked" → correct usage
- ✅ "Rice is a staple food in Asia."
- ✅ "Boil the rice."

**Types:**
- **White rice** — milled, most common
- **Brown rice** — whole grain, more fiber
- **Jasmine rice** — aromatic, Thai
- **Basmati rice** — long grain, Indian

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500 English words)

## Examples

- "Rice is a staple food in Japan." — 쌀은 일본의 주료야
- "I'll have rice with my meal." — 밥이랑 먹을게
