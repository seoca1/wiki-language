# Milk

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A white liquid produced by mammals, commonly used as food and drink.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /mɪlk/
- Syllables: milk (1 syllable)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000)

## Examples

- "I drink milk every morning." — 매일 아침 우유를 마셔
