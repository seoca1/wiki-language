# Fruit

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** The sweet and fleshy product of a tree or plant that contains seeds and can be eaten as food.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /fruːt/
- Syllables: fruit (1 syllable)
- Sound: same as "root"

**Etymology:** From Latin "fructus" meaning "enjoyment" or "produce." Originally meant any harvest product, not just sweet fruits.

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I ate a fruit" → generic, use "some fruit" or specify "an apple"
- ❌ "Tomato is a fruit" → botanically true, but cooking-wise it's a vegetable
- ✅ "An apple is a fruit."
- ✅ "Do you like fruit?"

**Types:**
- **Citrus:** orange, lemon, lime
- **Berries:** strawberry, blueberry, grape
- **Tropical:** banana, mango, pineapple

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500 English words)

## Examples

- "An apple a day keeps the doctor away." — 매일 사과 하나면 의사 필요 없어
- "I prefer fruit to candy." — 사탕보다 과일을 더 좋아해
