# hello

**Part of Speech:** interjection / noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** "Hello" — a common spoken greeting used when meeting someone.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /həˈloʊ/ (AmE) /həˈləʊ/ (BrE)
- Syllables: hel-lo (2 syllables)
- Stress: 2nd syllable (hel-LO)

**Memory Tip:** "Hel-lo!" — the friendlier you sound, the warmer the greeting. Or: HELLO = "Hell O" (greeting from a friendly place).

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "Hell-o" (no clear enunciation) → "Heh-LOW" or "Huh-LOW"
- ❌ "Hello my friend" → "Hello, my friend" (comma after greeting)
- ❌ "Hello John" (no greeting context) → "Hello, John!" (with comma + exclamation)
- ✅ "Hello!" (general)
- ✅ "Hello, how are you?" (with follow-up)
- ✅ "Say hello to your wife" (give greetings)
- Note: In US English, "hi" is more casual; "hello" is more formal/neutral. In UK, "hello" is universal. In phone calls: "Hello?" to check connection.

**Register:** neutral, universal. Works in all contexts (formal/informal).
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 100 English words)

## Examples

- "Hello! How are you?" — 안녕! 어떻게 지내?
- "Hello, nice to meet you." — 안녕하세요, 만나서 반가워요.
- "She said hello and walked past." — 그녀는 인사하고 지나갔다.
- "Hello, is anyone there?" — (전화) 여보세요, 계신가요?
- "Hello world!" (programming cliché) — 첫 프로그램 출력

## Mini-Dialogue

```
A: Hello! I'm Jane.   ← 안녕하세요! 제인입니다.
B: Hi Jane, nice to meet you. I'm Tom.   ← 안녕하세요 제인, 만나서 반가워요. 톰이에요.
A: Hello Tom. How are you?   ← 안녕하세요 톰. 잘 지내세요?
B: I'm good, thanks. And you?   ← 잘 지내요, 감사해요. 당신은요?
A: Doing well, thanks.   ← 잘 지내요, 감사.
```

## Cultural Notes

- **"Hi" vs "Hello"**: "Hi" is more casual/American. "Hello" is universal/British-leaning formal.
- **Phone answering**: "Hello?" to check if connection. Some countries (e.g., Japan) say "moshimoshi".
- **First meeting**: Often "Hello, nice to meet you" or "Hi, I'm [name]" with handshake.
- **Time of day**: "Good morning/afternoon/evening" more formal than "hello" for specific times.
- **Global spread**: "Hello" used worldwide as English lingua franca greeting.
- **Spanish**: "Hola", Korean: "안녕하세요", Japanese: "こんにちは", French: "Bonjour", German: "Hallo"
