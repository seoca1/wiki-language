# hobby

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 취미 — An activity done regularly in one's leisure time for pleasure.

## Examples

- "What are your hobbies?" — 흔한 대화 시작
- "I don't have many hobbies." — 솔직한 답
- "It's just a hobby of mine." — 취미 강조

## Related Terms

- [[friend]] — shared activities
- [[date]] — shared activity with romantic interest

## Cultural Notes

In dating conversations, hobbies are a **safe topic** to start
with. Common English hobby mentions: hiking, reading, cooking,
gaming, traveling, photography, music, sports.
