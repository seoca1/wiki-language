# cute

**Part of Speech:** adjective
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 귀여운 — Attractive in a youthful, endearing way.

## Examples

- "That's a cute outfit." — 패션 칭찬
- "You look cute when you smile." — 부드러운 칭찬
- "He's so cute." — 가볍고 친근한 호감 표현

## Related Terms

- [[beautiful]] — formal beauty
- [[handsome]] — traditional male beauty
- [[pretty]] — slightly formal

## Cultural Notes

"Cute" in American English = attractive in a youthful/adorable way.
Suitable for early stages of dating. Less formal than "beautiful" —
can feel friend-zone-ish if used too much. Pair with other compliments.
