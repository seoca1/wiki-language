# Beef

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** The meat of a cow or bull.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /biːf/
- Syllables: beef (1 syllable)
- Rhyme: "leaf", "chief"

**Etymology:** From Old French "buef" (ox/cow), from Latin "bos" (cow). The word "corned beef" comes from the practice of preserving with salt grains (corns).

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I want some beefs" → uncountable noun
- ❌ "The beef is very tendery" → correct spelling
- ✅ "Beef is expensive in Japan."
- ✅ "Roast beef is a British classic."

**Cuts of Beef:**
- **Filet mignon** — very tender, lean
- **Ribeye** — marbled, flavorful
- **Sirloin** — between lean and fatty
- **Brisket** — tough, good for slow cooking

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 2000 English words)

- "Beef stew is warming in winter." — 소고기 스튜는 겨울에 따뜻해
