# Egg

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** An oval object produced by birds, often used as food.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ɛɡ/
- Syllables: egg (1 syllable)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000)
