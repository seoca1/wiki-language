# Butter

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A pale yellow dairy product made from cream.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈbʌtər/
- Syllables: but-ter (2 syllables)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500)

## Examples

- "I spread butter on my toast." — 토스트에 버터 발라
