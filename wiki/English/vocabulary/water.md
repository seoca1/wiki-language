# Water

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A colorless, transparent liquid that is essential for life; the liquid that comes from taps, bottles, etc.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈwɔːtər/
- Syllables: wa-ter (2 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (WA-ter)

**Etymology:** From Old English "wæter", from Proto-Germanic "*watōr". Related to German "Wasser", Dutch "water".

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I want a water" → unless you mean "a bottle of water"
- ❌ "The water is too cold" → correct usage
- ✅ "Can I have some water?"
- ✅ "Water is essential for life."

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 500 English words)

## Examples

- "Can I have some water?" — 물 한 잔 주세요
- "Water is essential for life." — 물은 생명에 필수야
