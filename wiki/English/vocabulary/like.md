# like

**Part of Speech:** verb / noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 좋아하다 — To find pleasant or attractive; mild attraction.

## Examples (Verb)

- "I like you." — 호감 표현 (약함)
- "Do you like him?" — 관심 묻기
- "I really like your style." — 칭찬

## Examples (Noun — social media)

- "I hit like on his photo." — SNS 반응
- "Lots of likes!" — 인기도

## Related Terms

- [[love]] — stronger feeling
- [[miss]] — absence
- [[comfortable]] — feeling at ease
- [[really-like-you]] — expression of moderate attraction

## Cultural Notes

English escalates: **like → really like → love** (escalation matters).
Saying "I love you" too early can be uncomfortable. **"I really like
you"** = established feelings, ready to move forward.
