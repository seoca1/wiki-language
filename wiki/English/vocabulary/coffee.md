# Coffee

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A hot or cold drink made from roasted coffee beans, or the beans themselves.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈkɔːfi/
- Syllables: cof-fee (2 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (COF-fee)

**Etymology:** From Turkish "kahve", from Arabic "qahwa" (wine). Originally meant "wine" in Arabic; coffee was called "the wine of Islam."

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I want a coffee please" → British usage, but usually "I want coffee" (uncountable)
- ❌ "Two coffees, please" → American usage (countable cups)
- ✅ "I need my morning coffee."
- ✅ "Would you like some coffee?"

**Types:**
- **Espresso** — concentrated, Italian style
- **Latte** — espresso with steamed milk
- **Cappuccino** — espresso with foamed milk
- **Americano** — espresso diluted with water

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 500 English words)

## Examples

- "I can't function without my morning coffee." — 아침 커피 없으면 못 움직여
- "Let's go for coffee sometime." — 언젠가 커피 한잔 하자
