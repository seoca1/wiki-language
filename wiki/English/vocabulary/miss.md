# miss

**Part of Speech:** verb
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 그리워하다 — To feel the absence of someone; yearn for.

## Examples

- "I miss you." — 보고 싶어 (연인)
- "I miss my family." — 가족 그리움
- "I really missed you." — 강한 그리움

## Related Terms

- [[love]] — affection
- [[like]] — general fondness
- [[comfortable]] — feeling at home with

## Cultural Notes

"I miss you" is **strongly associated with romantic relationships**
in English. Saying it to a friend is unusual in US/UK English.
Compare: Korean "보고 싶어" is more flexible.
