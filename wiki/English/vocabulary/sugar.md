# Sugar

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A sweet crystalline substance obtained from various plants.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈʃʊɡər/
- Syllables: su-gar (2 syllables)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500)
