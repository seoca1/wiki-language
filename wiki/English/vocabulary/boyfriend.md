# boyfriend

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 남자친구 — A regular male companion with whom one has a romantic relationship.

## Examples

- "She's my boyfriend." — 소개
- "Where's your boyfriend?" — 사교 자리
- "Is he your boyfriend?" — 확인

## Related Terms

- [[girlfriend]] — female partner
- [[date]] — the activity
- [[love]] — feeling

## Cultural Notes

**Gender-neutral alternative**: "Partner" is increasingly common,
especially in LGBTQ+ contexts and professional settings.

**Public vs private**: "boyfriend" implies a defined relationship.
"So..." / "Someone I'm seeing" = earlier stage, undefined.
