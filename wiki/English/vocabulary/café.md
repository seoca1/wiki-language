# café

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 카페 — A small restaurant serving coffee and light refreshments.

**Note:** Often spelled "cafe" without accent in modern English, especially US.

## Examples

- "Want to grab coffee at that new café?" — 흔한 초대
- "Meet me at the café on 5th." — 장소 지정
- "This café has great ambiance." — 분위기 칭찬
