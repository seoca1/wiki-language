# Restaurant

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A place where people pay to sit and eat meals prepared on the premises.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈrɛstərɒnt/
- Syllables: res-tau-rant (3 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (RES-tau-rant)

**Etymology:** From French "restaurant" (a restorative thing), from Latin "restaurare" (to restore).

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500)

## Examples

- "Let's eat at a restaurant." — 음식점에서 먹자
- "I work at a restaurant." — 음식점에서 일해
