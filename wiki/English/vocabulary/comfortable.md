# comfortable

**Part of Speech:** adjective
**Level:** B1

**Definition:** 편한 — Feeling at ease; not anxious.

## Examples

- "I feel comfortable around you." — 편안함 표현
- "You're easy to talk to." — 대화 편안함
- "Are you comfortable with this?" — 동의 확인

## Cultural Notes

"Comfortable" in dating context = **emotional safety**. Strong
indicator of compatibility. Opposite: "awkward" = bad sign.
