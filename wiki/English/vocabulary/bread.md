# Bread

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A food made of flour, water, and yeast mixed and baked.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /brɛd/
- Syllables: bread (1 syllable)
- Sound: same as "bred"

**Etymology:** From Old English "brēad" (bit, crumb). Related to German "Brot".

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I ate three breads this morning" → uncountable, use "three pieces of bread"
- ✅ "I eat bread every day."
- ✅ "Bread is a staple food."

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000 English words)

## Examples

- "I toast some bread every morning." — 아침마다 빵을 굽어
- "Bread is a staple in many cultures." — 빵은 많은 문화권의 주식이야
