# date

**Part of Speech:** noun / verb
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 데이트 — A social or romantic appointment or engagement.

## Examples (Noun)

- "Are we going on a date?" — 의도 확인
- "First date jitters" — 첫 데이트 긴장
- "What a great date!" — 성공적 데이트

## Examples (Verb)

- "I've been dating someone for a year." — 교제
- "We're just dating, not committed." — 비공식
- "Are you dating anyone?" — 교제 여부

## Related Terms

- [[café]] — common date venue
- [[like]] — feelings

## Cultural Notes

**"Date" vs "hang out"**: in US English, "date" implies romantic
intent. "Hang out" is ambiguous. **Clarify intent early** to avoid
misunderstandings.
