### sport

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** 스포츠

**Pronunciation / IPA:** /spɔːrt/

**Etymology:** From Old French *desport* "pastime, recreation."

#### Examples

- I love watching sports on TV.
- What sport do you play?

#### Related Terms

- Related: [[sports-vocabulary]]

#### Cultural Notes

Sports culture is a major part of English-speaking societies. In the US, football (American), basketball, and baseball dominate; in the UK, football (soccer), cricket, and rugby are central.

### football

**Part of Speech:** noun

**Definition:** 미식축구 (US) / 축구 (UK)

**Pronunciation / IPA:** /ˈfʊtbɔːl/

**Etymology:** From *foot* + *ball*.

#### Examples

- American football is very popular in the US.
- We watched the football match on Sunday.

#### Cultural Notes

"Football" is famously ambiguous: it means American football in the US, soccer in the UK/most of the world. Always clarify with context or use "soccer" when meaning association football.
