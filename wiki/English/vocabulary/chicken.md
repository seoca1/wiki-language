# Chicken

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** A domesticated bird kept for its meat and eggs, or the meat of this bird.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈtʃɪkɪn/
- Syllables: chi-cken (2 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (CHI-cken)

**Etymology:** From Old English "cicen" (young bird), diminutive of "cić" (bird).

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I ate a chicken for dinner" → "I ate chicken" (general) or "I ate a chicken breast"
- ❌ "The chicken is too expensives" → correct spelling
- ✅ "I prefer chicken to beef."
- ✅ "Grilled chicken is healthy."

**Types:**
- **Chicken breast** — lean white meat
- **Chicken thigh** — darker, more flavorful
- **Chicken wings** — often served as appetizer
- **Chicken nuggets** — processed

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 1000 English words)

## Examples

- "I had chicken for dinner." — 저녁에 닭고기 먹었어
- "Do you want chicken or beef?" — 닭고기 갈래 아니면 소고기?
- "Chicken is a good source of protein." — 닭고기는 좋은 단백질원이야
