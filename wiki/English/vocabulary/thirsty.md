# Thirsty

**Part of Speech:** adjective
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** Feeling a need to drink something.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /ˈθɜːrsti/
- Syllables: thirst-y (2 syllables)
- Stress: first syllable (THIRST-y)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ (top 1500)

## Examples

- "I'm thirsty." — 목이 말라
- "This food makes me thirsty." — 이 음식 먹으니까 목이 마른다
