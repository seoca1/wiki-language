# funny

**Part of Speech:** adjective
**Level:** A2

**Definition:** 웃긴 — Causing laughter or amusement.

## Examples

- "You're so funny." — 유머 칭찬
- "He's the funniest person I know." — 강한 호감
- "That's not funny." — 진지할 때

quality in dating surveys. **Warning**: sarcasm doesn't always
translate well across cultures.
