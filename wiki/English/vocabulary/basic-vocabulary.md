# Basic Vocabulary — Greetings, Numbers, Colors, Family, Adjectives

**Source:** [[basic-vocabulary]]

Core everyday vocabulary: greetings, basic responses, numbers, colors, family terms, common adjectives. These are the highest-frequency words in spoken and written English and form the foundation for any further learning.

## Greetings & Politeness

### hello

**Part of Speech:** interjection (greeting)

**Definition:** 인사 — used when meeting someone

**IPA:** /həˈloʊ/

#### Examples

- Hello, how are you? — 안녕, 어떻게 지내? (casual, universal)
- Hello, this is John speaking. — 여보세요, 존입니다 (phone, formal)

#### Related Terms

- [[hi]] — informal variant
- [[goodbye]] — opposite
- [[thanks]] — gratitude

### hi

**Part of Speech:** interjection (greeting)

**Part of Speech:** interjection (farewell)

## Basic Responses

### yes

**Part of Speech:** adverb (affirmation)

**Definition:** 긍정 — affirmative response

**IPA:** /jɛs/

#### Examples

- Yes, I agree. — 네, 동의합니다
- Yes, please. — 네, 부탁해요

---

### no

**Part of Speech:** adverb (negation)

**Definition:** 부정 — negative response

**IPA:** /noʊ/

#### Examples

- No, thank you. — 아니요, 괜찮습니다
- No, I don't think so. — 아니, 그건 아닌 것 같아

---

### sorry

**Part of Speech:** adjective / interjection (apology)

**Definition:** 사과 — expression of regret

**IPA:** /ˈsɔri/

#### Examples

- Sorry, I'm late. — 미안, 늦었어 (casual)
- I'm sorry for your loss. — 조의를 표합니다 (formal condolence)
