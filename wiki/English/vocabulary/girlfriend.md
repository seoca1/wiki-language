# girlfriend

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** 여자친구 — A regular female companion with whom one has a romantic relationship.

## Examples

- "Meet my girlfriend." — 소개
- "Is she your girlfriend?" — 확인
- "I don't have a girlfriend." — 솔로 선언

Same conventions as [[boyfriend]]. Gender-neutral "partner" is
increasingly preferred.
