# Meat

**Part of Speech:** noun
**Level:** A1

**Definition:** The flesh of an animal, used as food.

**Pronunciation:**
- IPA: /miːt/
- Syllables: meat (1 syllable)
- Sound: same as "meet" and "mete"

**Etymology:** From Old English "mete" meaning "food" or "nourishment", not specifically animal flesh. The specific meaning for animal flesh developed in Middle English.

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ "I want some meats" → uncountable noun, use "some meat"
- ❌ "The meat is deliciouss" → correct spelling
- ✅ "I eat meat every day."
- ✅ "This is a red meat." (beef, lamb)
- ✅ "Chicken is a white meat."

**Types of Meat:**
- **Red meat:** beef, lamb, pork
- **White meat:** chicken, turkey, fish
- **Processed meat:** bacon, sausage, ham

**Collocations:**
- lean meat
- raw meat
- cooked meat
- ground meat (minced meat)

**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (top 500 English words)

## Examples

- "I don't eat meat." — 나는 고기를 안 먹어
- "Do you prefer red meat or white meat?" — 붉은 고기 아니면 흰 고기 선호해?
- "The steak is a quality cut of meat." — 그 스테이크는 품질이 좋은 고기 Cuts
