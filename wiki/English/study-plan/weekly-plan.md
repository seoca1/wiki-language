# English Weekly Study Plan

**Level:** B2-C1 (Upper-Intermediate to Advanced)
**Weekly commitment:** 3-4 hours (weekday 30min × 5 + weekend 60min × 2)
**Started:** 2026-08-19
**Cycle:** 4-week rotation (one grammar axis + one vocabulary theme + one output per cycle)

---

## Plan philosophy

- **Microlearning on weekdays**: 30 min sessions minimize friction
- **Deep work on weekends**: 1-hour focused sessions
- **4-week rotation**: each cycle completes one grammar axis + one vocab theme + one output deliverable
- **Output obligation**: every Sunday, ship a 5-sentence English summary to a public blog draft

## Weekly distribution

### Mon–Fri (30 min/day)

| Day | Block 1 (10 min) | Block 2 (10 min) | Block 3 (10 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Vocab (Anki 15 new) | 1 idiom analysis | Light reading (1 page novel/news) |
| Tue | Vocab review (Anki) | 1 grammar point | Listening (podcast 5-10 min) |
| Wed | Vocab (Anki 15 new) | 1 idiom analysis | Light reading |
| Thu | Vocab review (Anki) | 1 grammar point | Listening |
| Fri | Weekly vocab review | 1 idiom analysis | Free reading/watching (YouTube, etc.) |

**Recommended tools:**
- Vocab: Anki or Quizlet (CEFR-graded decks)
- Idioms: 1 entry/day to `expressions/` wiki
- Reading: contemporary fiction (e.g., *Klara and the Sun*, *The Goldfinch*)
- Listening: *6 Minute English* (BBC), *All Ears English*, *TED Talks Daily*

### Saturday (60 min) — Deep session

1. **Deep reading (25 min)**: 1 chapter of novel or long essay
   - Add 1-3 unknown words to `vocabulary/` (avoid over-collection)
2. **Grammar consolidation (20 min)**: the week's grammar axis
   - Update `grammar/` page if needed
3. **Idiom consolidation (15 min)**: 5 idioms with usage examples

### Sunday (60 min) — Output + review

1. **Writing/speaking (25 min)**: short English text 5-8 sentences
   - Optional: italki / Preply 25-min session (1×/week)
2. **Weekly review (20 min)**: Anki full review + idiom recap
3. **Blog draft (15 min)**: convert wiki content to markdown → publish to blog
4. **Next week plan (with LLM)**:
   ```
   "Based on what I learned this week, draft next week's plan.
   Identify 3 weaknesses and recommend supplementary materials."
   ```

---

## 4-week rotation

### Block A · Conditional & hypotheticals (weeks 1-4)

- **Grammar axis**: conditionals (0/1/2/3 + mixed), wish, would rather
- **Vocab axis**: hypothetical, counterfactual, regret expressions
- **Reading**: short stories with conditional-heavy narrative
- **Output**: "Three things I'd change about my past" essay (200 words)
- **Grammar page**: `grammar/english-conditionals.md`

### Block B · Modals of deduction (weeks 5-8)

- **Grammar axis**: must/might/could/can't + should/ought to + had better
- **Vocab axis**: certainty, probability, advice
- **Reading**: opinion essays (Guardian, NYT op-eds)
- **Output**: opinion essay on a current event
- **Grammar page**: `grammar/english-modal-verbs.md`

### Block C · Passive & cleft sentences (weeks 9-12)

- **Grammar axis**: passive voice, it-clefts, wh-clefts
- **Vocab axis**: formal/academic register
- **Reading**: academic articles, research summaries
- **Output**: 300-word formal essay
- **Grammar page**: `grammar/english-passive-voice.md`

### Block D · Integration · free project (weeks 13-16)

- **Grammar axis**: complex (conditionals + modals + passive)
- **Vocab axis**: free (user interest area)
- **Reading**: user choice
- **Output**: free-topic essay or video script
- **Grammar page**: user's choice
