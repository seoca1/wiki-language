# English Learning Resources Catalog

Static (time-independent) catalog of recommended resources for English learning. Dynamic progress (lessons/grammar/vocab/Phase plans/KPIs) lives in the upstream openclaw plan only; this page is a curated reference.

---

## 1. Textbooks & courses

For systematic grammar + CEFR progression, especially A1-B1.

- **English File (Oxford)**: communicative, multi-level (A1-C1). Strong on speaking.
- **Headway (Oxford)**: classic, clear grammar explanations. Good for self-study.
- **Cambridge English Grammar in Use (intermediate)**: single-volume grammar reference.
- **Practical English Usage (Swan)**: edge cases and learner errors.

Related wiki themes: [[basic-vocabulary]], [[english-prepositions]], [[tense-aspect-en]]

## 2. Apps

For daily short sessions during commute.

- **Duolingo**: gamified daily learning. Good for entry and retention.
- **Busuu**: A1-B1 CEFR-aligned curriculum.
- **HelloTalk**: language exchange with native speakers.
- **Tandem**: 1:1 matching with native English speakers.
- **Anki**: SRS flashcards. Best for long-term vocab retention.

Related wiki themes: [[daily-life-vocabulary]], [[idioms-proverbs]], [[idioms-proverbs]]

## 3. Websites

For dictionary lookups + grammar reference + drills.

- **Cambridge Dictionary**: definitions + examples + grammar notes.
- **Grammarly**: writing assistant (free tier available).
- **Purdue OWL**: comprehensive writing/grammar reference.
- **EnglishClub**: ESL-focused lessons by level.

Related wiki themes: [[english-prepositions]], [[tense-aspect-en]], [[articles-en]]

## 4. YouTube channels

For free listening + grammar video lessons.

- **English with Lucy**: native UK English. Vocabulary, pronunciation, idioms.
- **BBC Learning English**: official, levels A1-C1, free lessons.
- **EngVid**: 10+ teachers covering grammar topics.
- **Rachel's English**: US pronunciation focused.
- **mmmEnglish**: Australian English accent + grammar.

Related wiki themes: [[basic-vocabulary]], [[business-vocabulary]], [[english-modal-verbs]]

## 5. Podcasts

For commute listening.

- **6 Minute English (BBC)**: short, level-tagged, current topics.
- **All Ears English**: intermediate, US accent.
- **TED Talks Daily**: advanced, ideas-focused.
- **The English We Speak (BBC)**: idioms + phrasal verbs weekly.
- **Luke's English Podcast**: upper-intermediate to advanced, British.

Related wiki themes: [[business-vocabulary]], [[emotions-personality-vocabulary]], [[travel-vocabulary]]

## 6. Reading sources

For extensive reading (graded readers + authentic material).

- **News in Levels**: 3 difficulty levels of current news.
- **British Council Learn English**: free reading exercises by level.
- **Project Gutenberg**: free classic literature.
- **Medium / The Conversation**: opinion essays for advanced learners.

Related wiki themes: [[literature-passages]], [[../sources/literature-passages]]

## 7. TV / movies (Netflix, etc.)

For audio-visual input.

- **The Crown**: British English, formal register, history.
- **Friends**: US casual English, everyday conversation.
- **The Office (US)**: workplace English, humor.
- **Ted Lasso**: US/British mix, sports + idioms.
- **Fleabag**: British English, sharp dialogue.

Related wiki themes: [[emotions-personality-vocabulary]], [[../sources/literature-passages]]

## Caveats

- This catalog is **time-independent**. Dynamic progress/lessons/KPIs live in upstream openclaw plan.
- Resource suitability per level requires personal calibration.
