# Chinese Cuisine Culture

**Overview**: A comprehensive guide to Chinese food culture — the 8 great culinary traditions, dining etiquette, regional variations, and modern Chinese food trends.

## Key Points

### The 8 Great Cuisines (八大菜系)

China is traditionally divided into **8 great regional cuisines** (八大菜系, bā dà cài xì), each with distinct flavors, techniques, and famous dishes.

| Cuisine | Chinese | Region | Flavor Profile | Famous Dishes |
|---------|---------|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **Sichuan (四川)** | Chuāncài | 西南 (SW) | 麻辣 (numbing-spicy) | 麻婆豆腐, 宫保鸡丁, 水煮鱼 |
| **Hunan (湖南)** | Xiāngcài | 华中 (Central) | 辣 (purely spicy, not numbing) | 剁椒鱼头, 毛氏红烧肉, 腊味合蒸 |

| **Zhejiang (浙江)** | Zhècài | 华东 (East) | 清鲜 (fresh, tender) | 西湖醋鱼, 龙井虾仁, 东坡肉 |

### The Four Tastes (四味)

Chinese cuisine traditionally emphasizes **4 tastes**:
- **香 (Xiāng)** — Fragrant
- **鲜 (Xiān)** — Umami / fresh
- **甜 (Tián)** — Sweet
- **辣 (Là)** — Spicy
- (Modern addition: 麻 (Má) — Numbing from Sichuan pepper)

### Regional Eating Habits

#### North China (北方)

- **主食 (staple)**: 面食 (miànshí, wheat-based) — 馒头 (steamed bun), 面条 (noodles), 饺子 (dumplings)
- **口味**: 偏咸 (xián, salty)
- **菜量**: 大份 (large portions)
- **식사**: 가족 식사 (family-style, shared dishes)
- **특색**: 饺子 (dumplings), 北京烤鸭 (Peking duck), 炸酱面 (zhajiangmian)

#### South China (南方)

- **主食**: 米饭 (mǐfàn, rice) — 위주
- **口味**: 偏甜 (tián, sweet) / 偏清淡 (light)
- **菜量**: 소분 (smaller portions)
- **식사**: 1인 1위 (individual plates) 가능
- **특색**: 早茶 (Cantonese brunch), 粥 (congee), 米粉 (rice noodles)

#### East China (华东, Yangtze Delta)

- **특색**: 甜 (sweet), 鲜 (fresh, light)
- **음식**: 小笼包 (Shanghai), 蟹黄汤包 (crab roe soup dumpling)
- **식사**: 精致 (refined, smaller portions)

#### Southwest China (西南)

- **특색**: 麻辣 (numbing-spicy)
- **음식**: 火锅 (hot pot), 串串 (skewers), 麻辣烫
- **식사**: 1인 1锅 (individual pot) 흔함

#### Tea Etiquette

- **敬茶 (jìng chá)**: "Pour tea respectfully" — host pours, guest accepts with both hands
- **叩指礼 (kòu zhǐ lǐ)**: "Knock finger courtesy" — guest taps index+middle finger on table to thank for tea pouring (originated with Emperor Qianlong, but tradition persists)
- **倒茶 (dào chá)**: "Pour tea" — host's duty
- **添茶 (tiān chá)**: "Refill tea" — when host's cup is empty
- **洗茶 (xǐ chá)**: "Wash tea" — first pour discarded for cleanliness

### Food Etiquette (餐桌礼仪)

- **长辈 (zhǎngbèi) 먼저**: Elders first
- **부채 (Buchae, 公筷)**: Public chopsticks — 공유 음식은 부채로
- **음식 담을 때**: 빈 자리/옆에 담기 (위로 적층 X)
- **국 (soup) 젓기**: 젓가락으로 저어서 부채로 떠서
- **생일/명절 음식**: 어른/손님 먼저
- **잔치 잔치**: 干杯 (gānbēi) = "Bottoms up!" (전체 한 번에) vs 随意 (suíyi) = "drink freely" (자유)
- **뱃지 (X)**: 음식 남기지 않기 (光盘行动 chī xíngdòng = empty plate action)
- **음식 칭찬**: 好吃 (hǎochī) 자주 표현

### Chinese vs Western Food Culture

| Aspect | Chinese (中餐) | Western (西餐) |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| **Style** | Shared dishes (family-style) | Individual plates (plated) |
| **Soup** | 1 huge pot | 1 small cup |
| **Bread** | 馒头, 包子, 饼 | Bread basket (pre-meal) |
| **Cooking** | Stir-fry, steam, braise | Bake, grill, fry |
| **Flavor** | Layered (salt, sweet, spicy, umami) | 1-2 main flavors |
| **Order** | All at once, share | Sequential (app, main, dessert) |
| **Drink** | Hot tea, warm water | Cold water, wine |
| **Payment** | Often split (AA) | Often one person |
| **Tip** | Generally not | Expected 15-20% |
| **Leftovers** | Common (打包 dǎbāo) | Rare |
