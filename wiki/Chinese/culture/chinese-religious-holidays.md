# Chinese Traditional Holidays

**Overview:** Chinese traditional holidays (传统节日, chuántǒng jiérì) follow the lunar calendar (农历, nónglì) and blend folk religion, Confucian ancestor veneration, and Daoist/Buddhist elements. The Big Three — 春节 (spring), 端午 (summer), 中秋 (autumn) — anchor the seasonal cycle.

## Key Points

- **Spring Festival (春节, Chūnjié)**: Lunar New Year, 正月初一. 15-day festival ending 元宵节 (Lantern Festival). World's largest annual human migration (春运, chūnyùn, ~3 billion trips). Family reunion dinner (年夜饭), red envelopes (红包), couplets (春联).
- **Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋节, Zhōngqiūjié)**: 8th lunar month, 15th day. Moon gazing (赏月, shǎngyuè), mooncakes (月饼, yuèbǐng). Symbolizes family reunion (团圆, tuányuán). One of three "golden weeks" (黄金周).
- **Dragon Boat Festival (端午节, Duānwǔjié)**: 5th lunar month, 5th day. Commemorates poet 屈原 (Qū Yuán, ~340-278 BCE). Customs: 粽子 (zòngzi, glutinous rice dumplings), 龙舟 (lóngzhōu) races, 艾草 (àicǎo, mugwort) hung on doors.
- **Qingming (清明节, Qīngmíngjié)**: April 4-6 (solar). Tomb-sweeping (扫墓, sǎomù), ancestor veneration, 青团 (green rice dumplings). National holiday since 2008.
- **Double Ninth (重阳节, Chóngyángjié)**: 9th lunar month, 9th day. "Elders' Day" (老人节, lǎorénjié) — climb mountains (登高, dēnggāo), drink chrysanthemum wine (菊花酒, júhuājiǔ). "Double 9" = 久久 (jiǔjiǔ, "long-lasting").
- **Qixi (七夕, Qīxī)**: 7th lunar month, 7th day. "Chinese Valentine's Day." Weaving Maid (织女) and Cowherd (牛郎) legend. Symbol of 缘分 (yuánfèn).
- **Religious overtones**: Buddhism (盂兰盆, Yúlánpén, 7th month 15th), Daoism (上元节, Shàngyuán, 1st month 15th), Confucian ancestor rites. Folk practices intermix with Taoist/Buddhist philosophy.
- **Off-grid dates**: 国庆节 (Oct 1, National Day) and 元旦 (Yuándàn, January 1) are solar/Gregorian additions.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 설날 (음력 1월 1일) + 추석 (음력 8월 15일) = 중국 春节 + 中秋. 쫄쫄이 (압주) vs 중국 粽子. 한국 단오 (5월 5일) 사라짐 vs 중국 端午 유지.
- **Japonés**: 일본 お正月 (おしょうがつ) = 중국 春节 — 명칭 다름, 시기 비슷. 일본 お盆 (おぼん) = 중국盂兰盆. 일본 七夕 (たなばた) = 중국 七夕.
- **Inglés**: Western Christmas (12월 25일) 과 중국 春节 비교 — 모두 가족 응집의 최대 명절. Easter = 양력 고정 vs 端午 = 음력 변동.

## Ejemplos

«春节是中国最重要的节日,全家人都要回家吃年夜饭.»
"Chūnjié shì Zhōngguó zuì zhòngyào de jiérì, quánjiārén dōu yào huíjiā chī niányèfàn."
"Spring Festival is China's most important holiday. All family members must return home for the New Year's Eve dinner."

«中秋节的时候,我们一边吃月饼,一边赏月.»
"Zhōngqiūjié de shíhou, wǒmen yìbiān chī yuèbǐng, yìbiān shǎngyuè."
"During Mid-Autumn Festival, we eat mooncakes while admiring the moon."

«端午节是为了纪念爱国诗人屈原.»
"Duānwǔjié shì wèile jìniàn àiguó shīrén Qū Yuán."
"The Dragon Boat Festival commemorates the patriotic poet Qu Yuan."
