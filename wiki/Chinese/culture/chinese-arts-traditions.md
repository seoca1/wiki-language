# Chinese Arts Traditions

**Overview:** Chinese arts traditions (传统艺术, chuántǒng yìshù) include painting (国画), calligraphy (书法), opera (京剧), tea art (茶艺), and traditional medicine (中医). These are passed as formal disciplines (国艺, guóyì) and remain cultural pillars alongside modern art.

## Key Points

- **Guohua (国画, guóhuà)**: "National painting" — ink on rice paper (宣纸) or silk. Two main types:
  - 写意 (xiěyì, "freehand") — expressive, suggestive
  - 工笔 (gōngbǐ, "meticulous") — detailed, fine-brush
  - Three traditional subjects: 山 (mountain), 水 (water), 花鸟 (flowers-birds)
  - Tools (文房四宝, wénfáng sìbǎo): 笔 (brush), 墨 (inkstick), 纸 (paper), 砚 (inkstone)
- **Calligraphy (书法, shūfǎ)**: Five main scripts:
  - 篆书 (zhuànshū) — seal
  - 隶书 (lìshū) — clerical
  - 楷书 (kǎishū) — regular (modern standard)
  - 行书 (xíngshū) — semi-cursive
  - 草书 (cǎoshū) — cursive
  - Master calligraphers: 王羲之 (Wang Xizhi, 303-361), 颜真卿 (Yan Zhenqing), 柳公权 (Liu Gongquan)
- **Peking opera (京剧, Jīngjù)**: Originated 1790s, combining four regional styles (徽/汉/秦/昆). Roles: 生 (shēng, male), 旦 (dàn, female), 净 (jìng, painted face), 丑 (chǒu, clown). Famous works: 《霸王别姬》(Farewell My Concubine). UNESCO Intangible Heritage 2010.
- **Tea art (茶艺, cháyì)**: 茶艺师 (cháyìshī) certified practitioner. Steps: 烫壶 (warm pot), 温杯 (warm cup), 投茶 (add tea), 冲泡 (pour), 倒茶 (serve). Implies deep aesthetic appreciation beyond 喝茶 (drinking tea).
- **Traditional Chinese medicine (中医, Zhōngyī)**: 历史 5,000+ years. Core concepts:
  - 阴阳 (yīnyáng) — yin-yang balance
  - 五行 (wǔxíng) — five elements (金木水火土)
  - 经络 (jīngluò) — meridian channels
  - 主要疗法: 中药 (herb medicine), 针灸 (zhēnjiǔ, acupuncture), 推拿 (tuīná, massage), 拔罐 (báguàn, cupping)
  - 经典医书: 《黄帝内经》(Yellow Emperor's Classic), 《本草纲目》(Compendium of Materia Medica)
- **Buddhist/Daoist arts**: 佛教 (Fójiào) sculpture (云冈, 龙门); 道教 (Dàojiào) tai chi (太极拳 + 太极剑).

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 書藝 (서예) = 중국 书法 직계. 한국 丹青 (단청) vs 중국 国画. 한국 한의학 (韓醫學) = 중국 中医 직계. 한국 장애 (jangge) = 中国 酿酒 가술.
- **Japonés**: 일본 書道 (しょどう) = 중국 书法 — 같은 글씨 예술, 다른 진화. 일본 浮世絵 (ukiyo-e) vs 중국 国画. 일본 柔道 vs 중국 太极拳 — 같은 무술 철학.
- **Inglés**: Western oil painting vs Chinese ink painting — different mediums, different traditions. Western "pharmacy" vs Chinese 中药房 (中药药房). Western classical music vs Chinese 古琴 (gǔqín, 7-string).

## Ejemplos

«他每天练习书法一个小时,已经坚持了二十年.»
"Tā měitiān liànxí shūfǎ yī gè xiǎoshí, yǐjīng jiānchí le èrshí nián."
"He practices calligraphy for an hour every day. He's kept it up for twenty years."

«京剧的脸谱颜色代表不同的人物性格.»
"Jīngjù de liǎnpǔ yánsè dàibiǎo bùtóng de rénwù xìnggé."
"The face-paint colors in Peking opera represent different character personalities."

«中医和西医各有优势,很多人会结合使用.»
"Zhōngyī hé xīyī gè yǒu yōushì, hěn duō rén huì jiéhé shǐyòng."
"TCM and Western medicine each have their advantages. Many people combine them."
