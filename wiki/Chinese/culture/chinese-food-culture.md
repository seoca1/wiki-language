# Chinese Food Culture: Eight Cuisines and Hot Pot

**Overview:** Chinese cuisine encompasses eight major regional traditions (八大菜系, Bā dà càixì), each with distinct flavors and techniques, plus communal eating customs.

## Key Points

- **Eight Major Cuisines (八大菜系)**:
  - **川菜 (Chuāncài, Sichuan)**: Spicy, numbing (麻辣, málà) — Sichuan peppercorn. Mapo Tofu, Kung Pao Chicken.
  - **鲁菜 (Lǔcài, Shandong)**: Salty, fresh. Sweet & sour carp.
  - **粤菜 (Yuècài, Cantonese)**: Light, fresh. Dim sum, roast goose, wonton.
  - **苏菜 (Sūcài, Jiangsu)**: Sweet, elegant. Sweet & sour ribs.
  - **浙菜 (Zhècài, Zhejiang)**: Fresh, tender. West Lake Vinegar Fish.
  - **闽菜 (Mǐncài, Fujian)**: Light, umami. Buddha Jumps Over the Wall.
  - **湘菜 (Xiāngcài, Hunan)**: Spicy, pure. Chairman Mao's favorite.
  - **徽菜 (Huīcài, Anhui)**: Salty, aromatic.
- **Hot Pot (火锅, huǒguō)**: Communal cooking at table. Types:
  - **麻辣火锅 (Chongqing)**: Spicy + numbing
  - **鸳鸯锅 (Yuan-yang)**: Split pot — spicy/clear
  - **涮羊肉 (Beijing)**: Lamb, clear broth
  - **潮汕牛肉火锅 (Chaoshan)**: Fresh sliced beef
- **Dining Etiquette**:
  - **Round tables** — central lazy Susan (转盘, zhuǎnpán). Sharing dishes family-style.
  - **Seating hierarchy**: Seat of honor (主位, zhǔwèi) faces the door. Host sits opposite.
  - **Toasting (敬酒, jìngjiǔ)**: Stand up, two hands on glass, lower than senior's glass (敬酒 vs 罚酒).
  - **Chopsticks (筷子, kuàizi)**: Never stick upright in rice (funeral).
  - **Loud slurping**: Considered polite, especially for noodles (signals enjoyment).
  - **Paying the bill (买单, mǎidān)**: Often a competition for who pays.
- **Tea Culture**:
  - **功夫茶 (Gōngfū chá)**: Gongfu tea ceremony (Fujian, Chaozhou)
  - **Longjing (龙井)**: Hangzhou green tea
  - **Pu'er (普洱)**: Yunnan aged tea
  - **茶水免费 (chá shuǐ miǎnfèi)**: Free tea in restaurants

## Language Connections

- `火锅` (huǒguō) — Hot pot
- `饺子` (jiǎozi) — Dumplings
- `八大菜系` (bā dà càixì) — Eight cuisines
- `川菜/粤菜/鲁菜` — Sichuan/Cantonese/Shandong
- `麻辣` (málà) — Spicy + numbing
- `筷子` (kuàizi) — Chopsticks
- `敬酒` (jìngjiǔ) — Toasting
- `功夫茶` (gōngfū chá) — Gongfu tea
