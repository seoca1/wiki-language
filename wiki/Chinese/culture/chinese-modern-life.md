# Chinese Modern Life: WeChat, Alipay, and Delivery

**Overview:** Modern Chinese life is dominated by WeChat (super app), mobile payments (Alipay/WeChat Pay), and extremely fast delivery.

## Key Points

- **WeChat (微信, Wēixìn)**: Super app — messaging, social media, payments, mini-programs, games, services. >1 billion users.
  - **Moments (朋友圈)**: Social feed (like Instagram)
  - **WeChat Pay (微信支付)**: Mobile payment
  - **Mini Programs (小程序)**: Apps within WeChat (no download needed)
  - **Official Accounts (公众号)**: Subscription content
- **Mobile Payment (移动支付)**:
  - **Alipay (支付宝)** — #1 by transaction volume
  - **WeChat Pay** — #1 by users
  - **UnionPay (银联)** — Traditional card
  - Cash (现金) decreasing in cities but still common in rural areas
  - **QR code payment** standard — even street vendors
- **Delivery Services (外卖, wàimài)**:
  - **Meituan (美团)** — #1 food delivery
  - **Ele.me (饿了么)** — #2 food delivery
  - **30-minute delivery**: Common in cities
  - **SF Express (顺丰)**, **JD Logistics (京东物流)**: Package delivery
  - **Hema (盒马)**: 30-min grocery delivery (Alibaba)
- **E-commerce (电商)**:
  - **Taobao (淘宝)**: C2C marketplace
  - **JD.com (京东)**: B2C + logistics
  - **Pinduoduo (拼多多)**: Group buying (lower prices)
  - **Tmall (天猫)**: B2C premium
  - **Live streaming sales (直播带货)**: Influencers selling live
- **Transportation**:
  - **DiDi (滴滴)**: Ride-hailing
  - **Bikeshare (共享单车)** — Mobike, Ofo, Hello
  - **High-speed rail (高铁, gāotiě)** — extensive network
  - **Subway (地铁)** in major cities
- **Apps (Apps ecosystem)**:
  - **Douyin (抖音)**: TikTok equivalent — short video
  - **Bilibili (B站)**: Video platform
  - **Xiaohongshu (小红书)**: Lifestyle/RED
  - **Weibo (微博)**: Microblogging

## Language Connections

- `微信` (wēixìn) — WeChat
- `支付宝` (zhīfùbǎo) — Alipay
- `美团` (měituán) — Meituan
- `淘宝` (táobǎo) — Taobao
- `外卖` (wàimài) — Takeout
- `高铁` (gāotiě) — High-speed rail
- `滴滴` (dīdī) — DiDi (ride-hailing)
- `抖音` (dǒuyīn) — Douyin (TikTok)
