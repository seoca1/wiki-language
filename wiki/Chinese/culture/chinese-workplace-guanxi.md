# Chinese Workplace Culture: Guanxi and 996

**Overview:** Chinese workplace culture centers on relationships (guanxi, 关系) and is shaped by the demanding 996 work culture, with growing pushback and alternatives.

## Key Points

- **Guanxi (关系, Guānxi)**: Personal relationships and networks. Critical for business success. Maintained through:
  - **饭局 (fànjú)**: Business dinners — essential for relationship-building
  - **送礼 (sònglǐ)**: Gift-giving (often luxury items, tea, alcohol)
  - **面子 (miànzi, "face")**: Social standing/reputation. Critical to maintain.
  - **关系网 (guānxi wǎng)**: Personal network
- **Hierarchy (等级, děngjí)**:
  - **领导 (lǐngdǎo)** — Leader/Manager
  - **老板 (lǎobǎn)** — Boss
  - **老板/老板娘 (lǎobǎnniáng)** — Female boss
  - **同事 (tóngshì)** — Colleague
  - **下属 (xiàshǔ)** — Subordinate
  - **前辈/后辈** — Senior/junior
- **996 Work Culture**:
  - **996** = 9am-9pm, 6 days/week (72 hours)
  - Started in tech industry ~2016
  - **"996.ICU"** (2019) — Worker's protest GitHub repo
  - **"Tang ping" (躺平)** — "Lie flat" movement (2021) — rejecting overwork
  - **"Nei juan" (内卷)** — "Involution" — rat race concept
  - Government regulation (2021): 996 officially illegal, but enforcement weak
- **Drinking Etiquette (酒文化, jiǔ wénhuà)**:
  - **干杯 (gānbēi)**: Bottoms up
  - **敬酒 (jìngjiǔ)**: Toasting seniors (two hands, lower glass)
  - **罚酒 (fájiǔ)**: Penalty drinking (games, etc.)
  - **茅台 (Máotái)**: Famous baijiu (白酒) for toasting
- **Boss Culture (老板文化)**: Many Chinese companies still paternalistic — boss treats employees like family, expects loyalty.
- **DingTalk (钉钉) / WeChat Work (企业微信)**: Workplace communication tools.

## Language Connections

- `关系` (guānxi) — Personal connections
