# Chinese Tech Workplace

**Overview:** China's tech-sector workplace (科技职场, kējì zhíchǎng) is defined by 996 work culture (9am-9pm, 6 days), Big Tech giants (BAT + 字节跳动), and rising worker pushback — 内卷 (involution), 躺平 (lying flat), and 老板 (laoban) paternalism shape the modern Chinese working life.

## Key Points

- **996 culture (996 工作制, jiǔliùliù gōngzuòzhì)**: Work 9am-9pm, 6 days/week. Publicly endorsed by Jack Ma (Alibaba, 2019). Illegal under PRC Labor Law (8h/day, 44h/week max). 2021 court ruling affirmed 996 violates labor law. **007**: 0:00-0:00, 7 days — even more extreme.
- **Big Tech giants (大厂, dàchǎng)**:
  - **华为 (Huáwéi, Huawei)** — founder 任正非 (Rén Zhèngfēi). Military-style culture, "wolf culture" (狼性文化).
  - **阿里 (Ālǐ, Alibaba)** — 马云 (Jack Ma). 996 push, 六脉神剑 values (six values).
  - **腾讯 (Téngxùn, Tencent)** — 马化腾 (Pony Ma). Gaming/WeChat empire.
  - **字节跳动 (Zìjié Tiàodòng, ByteDance)** — 张一鸣 (Zhang Yiming). Douyin/TikTok parent. Fast iteration, OKR culture.
  - Other: 京东 (Jingdong), 美团 (Meituan), 拼多多 (Pinduoduo), 百度 (Baidu).
- **内卷 (nèijuǎn, "involution")**: Hyper-competition without proportional gain. Originally an anthropological term (Clifford Geertz, 1960s) for agrarian labor spirals. Rediscovered 2020 — describes "rat race" where everyone works harder but no one advances.
- **躺平 (tǎngpíng, "lying flat")**: Anti-996 movement (2021). Rejects 加班 (jiābān, overtime) culture. Baidu user post went viral: "躺平即是正义" (lying flat is justice). Counterpoint to 鸡娃 (jī wá, "chicken-fetus" — push kids hard).
- **Laoban (老板, lǎobǎn)**: "Boss" — implies owner/manager. Paternalistic relationship: laoban often summons employees to dinner (请客), contributes to weddings, but expects loyalty. 老板文化 (lǎobǎn wénhuà) vs Western corporate VP.
- **Office slang**: 加班 (overtime), 摸鱼 (mōyú, "slacking"), 996icu (GitHub repo 2019 listing 996 firms), 猝死 (cùsǐ, sudden death — work-stress fatalities), 离职率 (lízhílǜ, attrition rate).
- **Government+**: 国企 (guóqǐ, state-owned enterprise) — protected, lower stress, lower upside. 编制 (biānzhì, civil-service roster) = "iron rice bowl."

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 996 vs 한국 加班 (야근) — 같은 과로 문화. 한국 삼성 (Samsung) 문화 vs 중국 华为 (Huawei) — 둘 다 군대식 회사. 한국 N-po generation (N포세대) vs 中国 躺平 — 같은 청년 저항.
- **Japonés**: 일본 過労死 (karōshi, 過労死) 와 中国 猝死 — 같은 과로 사망. 일본 終身雇用 (しゅうしんこよう) vs 中国 终身制 — 둘 다 평생 고용 유산. 일본 社畜 (しゃちく) vs 中国 打工人 (dǎgōngrén) — 같은 회사 노예.
- **Inglés**: "Hustle culture" (US startup) ↔ 996 / 内卷. "Quiet quitting" (US 2022) ↔ 躺平 (CN 2021) — same anti-hustle sentiment. Western HR vs Chinese 人事 (rénshì).

## Ejemplos

«他在一家大厂工作,每天晚上九点以后才能下班.»
"Tā zài yī jiā dàchǎng gōngzuò, měitiān wǎnshang jiǔ diǎn yǐhòu cái néng xiàbān."
"He works at a big tech company. He can only leave work after 9 PM every day."

«她决定躺平,不再追求过高的职业目标.»
"Tā juédìng tǎngpíng, bù zài zhuīqiú guògāo de zhíyè mùbiāo."
"She decided to lie flat, no longer pursuing overly ambitious career goals."

«内卷让很多人感到压力很大,工作越来越累却没有加薪.»
"Nèijuǎn ràng hěn duō rén gǎndào yālì hěn dà, gōngzuò yuèláiyuè lèi què méiyǒu jiāxīn."
"Involution makes many people feel enormous pressure. Work gets harder but raises don't come."
