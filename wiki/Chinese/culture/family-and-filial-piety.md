# Chinese Family & Filial Piety

**Overview**: A comprehensive guide to Chinese family structure, filial piety (孝), generational dynamics, and the evolving 4-2-1 family structure in modern China.

## Key Points

### Filial Piety (孝) — The Foundational Virtue

孝 (xiào) is the foundational virtue of Chinese society, originating from Confucianism (孔子 Kǒngzǐ, 551-479 BCE).

**Classic Text**: 《孝经》 (Xiào Jīng, Classic of Filial Piety) — attributed to Confucius

**Core Principles**:
- 孝悌 (xiào tì) — "Filial piety and fraternal respect"
- 百善孝为先 (bǎi shàn xiào wéi xiān) — "Among all virtues, filial piety comes first"
- 身体发肤, 受之父母 (shēntǐ fà fū, shòu zhī fùmǔ) — "Body, hair, skin — received from parents" (cannot damage)

### The 24 Examples of Filial Piety (二十四孝)

Classical tales of extreme filial piety:
1. **孝感动天 (xiào gǎn dòng tiān)**: Shun's filial piety moved Heaven
2. **戏彩娱亲 (xì cǎi yú qīn)**: Laozi in colored clothes entertained parents
3. **鹿乳奉亲 (lù rǔ fèng qīn)**: Yan Zilui wore deer skin to get milk for parents
4. **百里负米 (bǎi lǐ fù mǐ)**: Zhong You carried rice 100 miles
5. **啮指痛心 (niè zhǐ tòng xīn)**: Mencius' heart ached when mother bit finger
6. **芦衣顺母 (lú yī shùn mǔ)**: Min Ziqian wore reed-woven coat
7. **亲尝汤药 (qīn cháng tāng yào)**: Emperor Wen tasted medicine first
8. **拾葚异器 (shí shèn yì qì)**: Cai Shun collected mulberries in different baskets for different parents
9. **埋儿奉母 (mái ér fèng mǔ)**: Guo Ju buried son to feed mother (extreme!)
10. **卖身葬父 (mài shēn zàng fù)**: Dong Yong sold himself to bury father
11. **刻木事亲 (kè mù shì qīn)**: Ding Lan carved wooden statue of deceased father
12. **涌泉跃鲤 (yǒng quán yuè lǐ)**: Jiang Chong leaped into spring to fetch carp
13. **怀橘遗亲 (huái jú yí qīn)**: Lu Ji hid tangerines for mother
14. **扇枕温衾 (shān zhěn wēn qīn)**: Huang Xiang fanned father, warmed mother
15. **行佣供母 (xíng yōng gòng mǔ)**: Jiang Ge worked as servant to feed mother
16. **闻雷泣墓 (wén léi qì mù)**: Wang Pou wept at mother's grave at thunder
17. **哭竹生笋 (kū zhú shēng sǔn)**: Meng Zong cried bamboo into growth
18. **卧冰求鲤 (wò bīng qiú lǐ)**: Wang Xiang lay on ice to fetch carp
19. **恣蚊饱血 (zì wén bǎo xuè)**: Wu Meng let mosquitoes bite him
20. **扼虎救父 (è hǔ jiù fù)**: Yang Ean strangled tiger to save father
21. **乳姑不怠 (rǔ gū bù dài)**: Cui Nansha nursed grandmother
22. **投江觅父 (tóu jiāng mì fù)**: Kong Rong saved father
23. **扇枕温衾 (shān zhěn wēn qīn)**: Huang Xiang (duplicate)

### Modern Family Structure (现代家庭)

| Generation | Birth | Family Size | Characteristics |
|------------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **祖父母 (zǔfùmǔ)** | 1940s-60s | 4-6 children | Traditional; rural; many grandchildren |
| **父母 (fùmǔ)** | 1970s-90s | 1 child (1-child policy) | Filial piety + small family |
| **90/00后 (children)** | 1990s-2010s | 1 (most) | Heavy family pressure (4-2-1) |

### The 4-2-1 Problem (4-2-1 困境)

After 1-child policy (1979-2015):
- **4** grandparents + **2** parents + **1** child = **1 child supports 6 people**
- "4-2-1 综合征" (4-2-1 zōnghézhēng) — Social, financial, emotional pressure
- "421家庭" = "1 child carries 6 dependents"

#### Financial Impact

- **教育投资 (jiàoyù tóuzī)**: Education investment heavy — "鸡娃" (jī wá) = "chicken-baby" (intensive parenting)
- **房产 (fángchǎn)**: Housing — 6 people's savings for 1 home
- **医疗 (yīliáo)**: Medical care for 4 elderly + 1 child
- **결혼 자금 (jiéhūn zījīn)**: Wedding funds (huge, regional)

#### "4-2-1 Tiger Mom" Phenomenon

- **鸡娃 (jī wá)**: "Chicken-baby" — intensive parenting (study, music, sports)
- **海淀妈妈 (Hǎidiàn māmā)**: "Haidian mom" (elite Beijing district) — extreme education
- **虎妈 (Hǔmā)**: "Tiger mom" — strict, academic-focused
- **鸡血 (jī xuè)**: "Chicken blood" — hyper-motivating

### The 421 Crisis: Adult Children Burden

- 421家庭 (421 jiātíng) = 4 grandparents + 2 parents + 1 child
- **"啃老" (kěn lǎo)**: "Gnawing on elderly" — adult children depending on parents
- "上不去,下不来" (shàng bù qù, xià bù lái) = "Can't rise, can't fall"
- "三明治一代" (sānmíngzhì yīdài) = "Sandwich generation" — supporting both parents and children

### Filial Piety Practices Today

#### 1. 物质孝 (Wùzhì xiào) — Material Filial Piety

- **给钱 (gěi qián)**: Give money to parents
- **买礼物 (mǎi lǐwù)**: Buy gifts (health products, clothing)
- **改善生活 (gǎishàn shēnghuó)**: Improve parents' living conditions
- **帮忙带孩子 (bāngmáng dài háizi)**: Help with grandchildren (most common!)

#### 2. 精神孝 (Jīngshén xiào) — Spiritual Filial Piety

- **陪伴 (péibàn)**: Companionship (most valued by elderly)
- **常回家看看 (cháng huíjiā kàn kàn)**: Visit home often
- **电话问候 (diànhuà wènhòu)**: Phone calls
- **关注健康 (guānzhù jiànkāng)**: Care about health

#### 3. 顺从 (Shùncóng) — Obedience (declining)

- **传统 (chuántǒng)**: 父母在, 不远游 (fùmǔ zài, bù yuǎn yóu) — "When parents live, don't travel far" (Confucius)
- **现代 (xiàndài)**: 90/00后 (1990s/2000s-born) less obedient
- **冲突 (chōngtū)**: "代沟" (dàigōu) = "Generation gap" — common
- **"啃老" vs "独立"**: "Gnawing elderly" vs "Independent" — tension

### Family Disputes (家庭纠纷)

#### **婆媳关系 (Pó xí guānxi) = "Mother-in-law vs Daughter-in-law"**

Famous tension in Chinese families:
- **婆 (pópo)**: Mother-in-law
- **媳 (xífù)**: Daughter-in-law
- **冲突领域**:
  - 育儿 (yù'ér) — Child-rearing methods
  - 家务 (jiāwù) — Housework division
  - 婆家 vs 娘家 (Pójiā vs Niángjiā) — Husband's family vs wife's family
  - 房产 (fángchǎn) — Property

#### Solutions

- **分开住 (fēnkāi zhù)**: Live separately (most common)
- **保持距离 (bǎochí jùlí)**: Maintain distance
- **中间人 (zhōngjiān rén)**: Mediator (often husband or elder)

#### **妈宝 (Mā bǎo) = "Mama's Boy"**

- **Definition**: Adult male still highly dependent on mother
- **Characteristics**: Mother makes decisions, son agrees
- **女友/妻 (girlfriend/wife) 경고**: "Mama's boy" — dating concern
- **Related**: 妈宝男 (mā bǎo nán), 妈宝男 = "Mama's boy"
- **Opposite**: 独立 (dúlì) = "Independent"

#### **啃老 (Kěn lǎo) = "Gnawing Elderly"**

- Adult children depending on parents financially
- **房子 (fángzi)**: House bought by parents
- **车子 (chēzi)**: Car bought by parents
- **婚礼 (hūnlǐ)**: Wedding paid by parents
- **孙辈 (sūnbèi)**: Grandchildren raised by grandparents
- **Statistics**: 60%+ of urban Chinese adult children receive parental support

### Family & Generational Taboos (家庭禁忌)

- **不要给长辈倒酒 (bùyào gěi zhǎngbèi dǎo jiǔ)**: Don't pour alcohol for elder with single hand
- **不要踩门槛 (bùyào cǎi ménkǎn)**: Don't step on doorframe (brings bad luck)
- **不要在父母面前打哈欠 (bùyào zài fùmǔ miànqián dǎ hāqiàn)**: Don't yawn in front of parents
- **不要直呼长辈名字 (bùyào zhí hū zhǎngbèi míngzì)**: Don't call elders by first name
- **春节禁忌 (chūnjié jìnjì)**: Spring Festival taboos:
  - Don't sweep floor (sweeps away luck)
  - Don't break things (broken = broken luck)
  - Don't say 死 (sǐ, death) or 病 (bìng, illness)
  - Don't wear black/white (mourning colors)

### Modern Chinese Family Issues

#### 421压力 (421 yālì) = "421 Pressure"

- Single child supports 6 (2 parents + 4 grandparents)
- **Financial**: 房产 (real estate) + 医疗 (medical)
- **Emotional**: 长辈期望 (parental expectations)
- **Career**: "鸡娃" (intensive parenting) requires full-time
- **Marital**: Late marriage, low fertility (1.0 births/woman 2023)

#### 丁克 (Dīngkè) = "DINK" (Double Income No Kids)

- **丁克 (Dīngkè)**: China-coined term for DINK couples
- 2020: 5% of Chinese couples
- **Reasons**: Career, financial, environmental, lifestyle
- **Social pressure**: Still high (父母 push for grandkids)
- **Trend**: Increasing in 90/00后

#### 不婚 (Bù hūn) = "Not Marrying"

- **不婚主义 (bù hūn zhǔyì)**: "Marriage-free ideology"
- 单身经济 (dānshēn jīngjì) = "Single economy" — booming industry
- 2023: 25% of urban Chinese 25-34 are single
- **Reasons**: 经济压力 (economic pressure), 自我实现 (self-realization), 自由 (freedom)

#### 离婚 (Lí hūn) = "Divorce"

- 2020: 离婚率 (líhūn lǜ) = 离婚/结婚 ≈ 40% (大城市)
- **冷却期 (lěngquè qī) = "Cooling-off period"** (2021 법률): 30일
- **Reasons**: 家暴 (jiābào, domestic violence), 感情破裂 (gǎnqíng pòliè, broken emotions), 出轨 (chūguǐ, infidelity)

### Famous Chinese Family Concepts

#### 家 (Jiā) = "Home/Family"

- **核心 (core)**: 家 = family + household + lineage + home
- **"修身齐家治国平天下" (xiūshēn qí jiā zhì guó píng tiānxià)**: "Cultivate self, regulate family, govern state, bring peace to world" — Confucian order
- **家庭 (jiātíng)** vs **家族 (jiāzú)**: Nuclear family vs. extended family/clan
- **家风 (jiāfēng)**: "Family style" — family values passed down
- **家训 (jiāxùn)**: "Family instructions" — ancestral teachings
- **家谱 (jiāpǔ)**: "Family genealogy"

### Family Decision-Making (家庭决策)

#### Traditional

- **Father/eldest male decides** — patriarchal
- **长者为大 (zhǎngzhě wéi dà)**: "Elders are greatest"
- **男尊女卑 (nán zūn nǚ bēi)**: "Male honored, female lowly" (ancient)
- **嫁出去的女儿 (Jià chūqù de nǚ'ér)**: "Married-out daughter" (not family after marriage)

#### Modern

- **夫妻共同决策 (fūqī gòngtóng juécè)**: Joint couple decision
- **孩子也要问 (háizi yě yào wèn)**: Ask children
- **老人不管 (lǎorén bù guǎn)**: Elders don't interfere
- **核心家庭 (héxīn jiātíng)**: Nuclear family (parents + 1 child)
- **夫妻 (fūqī)** = "Married couple" = core unit

### Family Financial Dynamics

- **AA制 (AA zhì)**: "Going Dutch" — split bill (newer couples)
- **公婆养 (gōngpó yǎng)**: Parents-in-law support
- **啃老 (kěn lǎo)**: Adult children depending on parents
- **月光族 (yuèguāng zú)**: "Moonlight tribe" — spend all salary each month
- **房奴 (fáng nú)**: "House slave" — heavy mortgage burden
- **车奴 (chē nú)**: "Car slave"
- **卡奴 (kǎ nú)**: "Card slave" (credit card debt)
- **教育支出 (jiàoyù zhīchū)**: Education spending (high % of family budget)

### Family & Career

- **"421" Career Sacrifice**: Working adult child sacrifices career to care for 2 parents + 4 grandparents
- **"早退" (zǎo tuì)**: "Early retirement" (return to hometown for parents)
- **"小镇做题家" (xiǎozhèn zuòtíjiā)**: "Small-town test-taker" — first-gen college grad
- **"躺平" (tǎng píng)**: "Lying flat" — quitting competition
- **"润学" (rùn xué)**: "Running study" — going abroad

### Chinese Family Memes (家庭梗)

- **"我爸是李刚" (Wǒ bà shì Lǐ Gāng)**: "My dad is Li Gang" — famous drunk driving case (2010) → became meme for entitled rich kids
- **"我有一个朋友" (Wǒ yǒu yī gè péngyou)**: "I have a friend" — actually means "me"
- **"我爸说了" (Wǒ bà shuō le)**: "My dad said" — appeals to authority
- **"妈见打" (mā jiàn dǎ)**: "Mom sees, beat" — bad idea when mom angry
- **"我爸" (wǒ bà)**: "My dad" — increasingly used sarcastically by single-child generation

### Family Planning (计划生育)

#### 1-Child Policy (1979-2015)

- **独生子女 (dú shēng zǐ nǚ)**: "Only son/daughter"
- **独生子女证 (dúshēng zǐnǚ zhèng)**: One-child certificate
- **Effects**: 4-2-1 structure, gender imbalance, aging

#### 2-Child Policy (2016-2021)

- **二孩政策 (èrhái zhèngcè)**: "Two-child policy"
- **Effects**: Modest increase, expensive cities still 1 child

#### 3-Child Policy (2021-present)

- **三孩政策 (sānhái zhèngcè)**: "Three-child policy"
- **Effects**: Birth rate continuing to drop (2023: 6.4/1000 — historic low)

## 示例

"孝" (xiào): 핵심 유교 가치. 부모 봉양 + 조상 숭배가 핵심. 부모보다 자신의 욕구 우선은 비도덕적.
"家族" (jiāzú, 家族): 중국 가족은 종족 그룹 (clan) 단위. 명절 단위로 가족이 모여 사제 행사.
조부모 부양 (养老): "百善孝为先, 原心亏" — 모든 선행 중 孝 가 으뜸, "원심" (원래 마음)에서 출발.
