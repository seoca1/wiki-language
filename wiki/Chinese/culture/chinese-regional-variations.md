# Chinese Regional Variations

**Overview:** China is a vast, linguistically and culturally diverse country. Mandarin (普通话) is the official lingua franca, but regional variations include Cantonese (粤语), Min (闽南语), and Wu (吴语). Eight great cuisines (八大菜系) and stark north-south divisions (南北差异) reflect this regional diversity.

## Key Points

- **Linguistic diversity (语言多样性, yǔyán duōyàngxìng)**:
  - **普通话 (Pǔtōnghuà)**: Standard Mandarin, official in PRC since 1955. Based on Beijing dialect. ~1.1 billion speakers.
  - **粤语 (Yuèyǔ, Cantonese)**: Pearl River Delta, Hong Kong, Macau. ~80 million speakers. 9 tones (vs Mandarin's 4). Preserves more classical Chinese.
  - **闽南语 (Mǐnnányǔ, Minnan/Hokkien)**: Fujian, Taiwan, Southeast Asia Chinese diaspora. ~50 million. Multiple subgroups.
  - **吴语 (Wúyǔ)**: Shanghai, Suzhou, Zhejiang. ~80 million. Soft tone inventory.
  - **客家话 (Kèjiāhuà, Hakka)**: Scattered mountains/SE Asia. ~50 million.
  - **北方方言 (Běifāng fāngyán)**: Northern dialects — generally mutually intelligible with Mandarin.
- **North-South divide (南北差异, nán-běi chāyì)**:
  - **Noodles (面) vs Rice (米)**: Northern staple = wheat (馒头, 饺子, 面条); Southern = rice (米饭, 米粉)
  - **Heating (供暖)**: North has centralized heating (暖气, nuǎnqì); South often unheated
  - **Tofu preference (甜咸之争)**: 甜豆腐脑 (southern sweet) vs 咸豆腐脑 (northern savory)
  - **Bathing culture**: 北方澡堂 (běifāng zǎotáng) communal bathhouses vs southern shower norm
- **Eight great cuisines (八大菜系, bā dà cài xì)**:
  - 川菜 (Chuāncài, Sichuan) — spicy-numbing (麻辣)
  - 粤菜 (Yuècài, Cantonese) — light, fresh
  - 鲁菜 (Lǔcài, Shandong) — savory, salty
  - 苏菜 (Sūcài, Jiangsu) — fresh, slightly sweet
  - 浙菜 (Zhècài, Zhejiang) — fresh, tender
  - 闽菜 (Mǐncài, Fujian) — seafood-forward
  - 湘菜 (Xiāngcài, Hunan) — purely spicy
  - 徽菜 (Huīcài, Anhui) — oily, flavorful
- **Other regional cuisines**: 京菜 (Beijing), 沪菜 (Shanghai), 东北菜 (Dongbei), 西北 (Lamian), 粤式早茶 (yum cha).
- **汉字 simplification (简体 vs 繁体)**: Mainland uses 简体, Taiwan/HK uses 繁體. e.g., 国 (Simp.) vs 國 (Trad.) = "country."

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국도 방언 차이 큼 (경상도/전라도/제주도). 중국 粤 vs 闽 vs 吴 차이 = 한국 경상 vs 전라 차이. 한국 한자 简体 vs 일본 旧字体 vs 중국 繁体 — 셋 다 한자 변형.
- **Japonés**: 일본 標準語 (ひょうじゅんご) vs 関西弁 (かんさいべん) — 중국 普通话 vs 方言 와 비슷. 일본 漢字 旧字体 vs 简体 vs 繁体 — 한자 사용권 차이.
- **Inglés**: "Rhotic vs non-rhotic" English accents vs Chinese dialect tones. Single standard language (English) vs dominant + regional dialects (Chinese).

## Ejemplos

«广东人喜欢喝早茶,吃各种点心.»
"Guǎngdōng rén xǐhuan hē zǎochá, chī gèzhǒng diǎnxīn."
"Guangdong people like to drink morning tea and eat various dim sum."

«四川菜又麻又辣,广东菜比较清淡.»
"Sìchuān cài yòu má yòu là, Guǎngdōng cài bǐjiào qīngdàn."
"Sichuan food is numbing and spicy; Cantonese food is comparatively light."

«上海人讲吴语,香港人讲粤语,北京人讲普通话.»
"Shànghǎi rén jiǎng Wúyǔ, Xiānggǎng rén jiǎng Yuèyǔ, Běijīng rén jiǎng Pǔtōnghuà."
"Shanghai people speak Wu, Hong Kong people speak Cantonese, Beijing people speak Mandarin."
