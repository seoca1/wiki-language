# Chinese Family Structure

**Overview:** Chinese family structure (家庭结构, jiātíng jiégòu) has shifted dramatically since the late 1970s. The one-child policy (计划生育, jìhuà shēngyù) created the 4-2-1 family (421困境), reshaping eldercare, kinship terms, and intergenerational expectations.

## Key Points

- **Traditional joint family (家族, jiāzú)**: Multi-generational household under a patriarch (家长). Clans maintained ancestral halls (祠堂, cítáng) and shared surname (姓氏, xìngshì) lineage. Replaced gradually by the nuclear family (家庭, jiātíng) since the 20th century.
- **One-child policy (计划生育, 1979-2015)**: Formally the "family planning policy" (独生子女政策). Urban couples allowed one child (1个孩子), rural couples two if first was a girl. Penalties for violations; exceptions for ethnic minorities. Estimated to have prevented ~400 million births.
- **4-2-1 family (421困境, sì èr yī kùnjìng)**: 4 grandparents + 2 parents + 1 child = a single adult child bears the eldercare burden for up to 6 elders. By 2020s, ~180 million 独生子女 (dúshēngzǐnǚ) face this structural pressure.
- **Filial piety (孝, xiào)**: Confucian duty of children to respect and care for aging parents. Reinforced by law through the 2013 Elder Rights Law (老年人权益保障法). Verbal: 孝顺 (xiàoshùn) = "filial and compliant."
- **Kinship terms (亲属称谓, qīnshǔ chēngwèi)**: Highly specific — 爷爷 (paternal grandfather) vs 外公 (maternal grandfather); 堂 (paternal cousins) vs 表 (maternal cousins). Titles preferred over first names for elders.
- **Modern shifts**:
  - 空巢 (kōngcháo, "empty nest") elderly living alone in cities
  - 啃老 (kěnlǎo, "gnawing the old") adult children financially dependent on parents
  - 二孩 (èrhái, "second child") policy (2016) and 三孩 (sānhái, 2021) — low uptake due to cost
  - 失独 (shīdú, "lost the only child") families — survivors of the only-child generation with no descendants
- **Simplified vs Traditional**: 家族 → 家族 (same in both); 計劃生育 (Trad.) vs 计划生育 (Simp.).

- **Coreano**: 한국도 1인 가구 (1인 가구) 33% (2023) 로 증가, 저출산 (저출산) 심각. 孝 (효) 는 한자文化권 공통. 중국 421困境 vs 한국 1-2-3 가족 (1-2-3 가족) — 둘 다 저출산 후 세대 부담.

## Ejemplos

«这个小伙子是家里的独生子,六个老人都靠他养老.»
"Zhège xiǎohuǒzi shì jiālǐ de dúshēngzǐ, liù gè lǎorén dōu kào tā yǎnglǎo."
"This young man is the family's only child. Six elders all rely on him for eldercare."

«我家有四口人:爷爷、奶奶、爸爸、妈妈.»
"Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén: yéye, nǎinai, bàba, māma."
"My family has four people: paternal grandfather, paternal grandmother, father, mother." (Note: 口 kǒu = measure word for households/people)

«孝顺父母是中国的传统美德.»
"Xiàoshùn fùmǔ shì Zhōngguó de chuántǒng měidé."
"Filial piety toward parents is a traditional Chinese virtue."
