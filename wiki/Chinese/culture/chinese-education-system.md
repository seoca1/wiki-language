# Chinese Education System

**Overview:** China's education system (教育制度, jiàoyù zhìdù) is built on 9-year compulsory education (九年义务教育), centered on the high-stakes gaokao exam (高考), and topped by elite 211/985 universities. It is exam-driven (应试教育, yìngshì jiàoyù) and intensely competitive.

## Key Points

- **Compulsory structure (学制, xuézhì)**:
  - 小学 (xiǎoxué) — primary, 6 years (ages 6-12)
  - 初中 (chūzhōng) — junior secondary, 3 years (ages 12-15)
  - 高中 (gāozhōng) — senior secondary, 3 years (ages 15-18)
  - 大学 (dàxué) — university, 4 years (undergrad)
- **9-year compulsory education (九年义务教育, jiǔnián yìwù jiàoyù)**: 6+3 = 9 years. Free, mandatory since 1986 (Compulsory Education Law). Tuition officially waived; parents still pay for 兴趣班 (xìngqùbān, interest classes) and uniform fees.
- **Gaokao (高考, gāokǎo)**: National college entrance exam held June 7-9. ~13 million test-takers annually (2024: 13.42 million). Single exam determines university slot. Subject mix: 语文 (Chinese), 数学 (Math), 英语 (English), plus 文综/理综 (liberal arts/science composite).
- **211/985 universities**:
  - 211 工程 (211 Project, 1995) — ~115 top universities
  - 985 工程 (985 Project, 1998) — ~39 elite universities (Peking, Tsinghua, Fudan, etc.)
  - Joining the 985+211 bracket = enormous prestige, higher budget, top-tier faculty
- **Junior secondary entrance (中考, zhōngkǎo)**: Gateway exam from 初中 to 高中. Determines whether student enters 普通高中 (academic track) or 职高 (zhōnggāo, vocational track).
- **Primary to junior secondary (小升初, xiǎoshēngchū)**: Increasingly competitive in cities; banned public-school "择校" (xuéxiào selection) but parents hire 补习班 (bǔxíbān) tutors.
- **Exam-driven / 应试教育 (yìngshì jiàoyù)**: Criticized for rote memorization, lack of creativity. Reform debates: 素质教育 (sùzhì jiàoyù, "quality education") push since 1990s.
- **Vocabulary**: 补课 (bǔkè) = tutoring; 状元 (zhuàngyuán) = top scorer; 高考移民 (gāokǎo yímín) = relocating for easier provincial exam.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국의 수능 (수능) 과 중국 高考 (gāokǎo) 비슷 — 모두 1 회 시험으로 대학 결정. 한국의 사교육 (사교육) 과 중국 补习班 (bǔxíbān) 모두 과열. 985 = 한국의 SKY (서울대/고려대/연세대).
- **Japonés**: 일본 共通テスト (2021 도입, 이전 センター試験) — 중국 高考 보다는 어려움 적음, 국공립 분산. 일본 偏差値 (へんさち) vs 중국 分数线 (fēnshùxiàn).
- **Inglés**: SAT/ACT (US) 은 여러 번 응시 가능, 高考 는 1년 1회. UK A-levels 는 subject 단위, 高考 는 통합 시험.

## Ejemplos

«高考是中国学生最重要的考试,决定他们能上哪所大学.»
"Gāokǎo shì Zhōngguó xuéshēng zuì zhòngyào de kǎoshì, juédìng tāmen néng shàng nǎ suǒ dàxué."
"The gaokao is the most important exam for Chinese students. It determines which university they can attend."

«她的目标是考上北京大学,这是中国最著名的985大学之一.»
"Tā de mùbiāo shì kǎoshàng Běijīng Dàxué, zhè shì Zhōngguó zuì zhùmíng de 985 dàxué zhī yī."
"Her goal is to get into Peking University, one of China's most famous 985 universities."

«为了中考,李明每天上三个补习班.»
"Wèile zhōngkǎo, Lǐ Míng měitiān shàng sān gè bǔxíbān."
"To prepare for the zhongkao, Li Ming attends three tutoring classes every day."
