# Chinese Communication: Mianzi and Indirect Refusal

**Overview:** Chinese communication emphasizes mianzi (面子, "face") and indirect expression to preserve social harmony, often avoiding direct refusal.

## Key Points

- **Mianzi (面子)**: "Face" — social standing and reputation. Critical to maintain. Influences behavior in business, family, social.
- **Lian (脸)**: "Face" more related to moral standing. Losing 脸 = shame. Saving 脸 = dignity.
- **Indirect Communication Patterns**:
  - **Direct refusal = rude** — even if you can't comply, soften the response
  - **"我再想想 (wǒ zài xiǎngxiǎng)"** — "Let me think about it" = likely no
  - **"这有点难 (zhè yǒudiǎn nán)"** — "This is a bit difficult" = no
  - **"以后再说 (yǐhòu zàishuō)"** — "We'll talk later" = no
  - **"不太方便 (bù tài fāngbiàn)"** — "Not very convenient" = no
- **Forms of Address**:
  - **老师 (lǎoshī, "teacher")**: Respected professional (not just teachers)
  - **经理 (jīnglǐ)** — Manager
  - **主任 (zhǔrèn)** — Director
  - **先生/女士 (xiānshēng/nǚshì)** — Mr./Ms.
  - **姓 + Title** — Surname + professional title (王经理, Wang jingli)
- **Saving Face**:
  - **Public criticism avoided** — even when wrong
  - **Indirect correction** — "Maybe we could consider..."
  - **Loud disagreement** = loss of face
- **Family Hierarchy**:
  - **父母 (fùmǔ)** — Parents
  - **长辈/晚辈 (zhǎngbèi/wǎnbèi)** — Elder/junior
  - **孝 (xiào)** — Filial piety — respect for parents/elder
  - **哥哥/姐姐/弟弟/妹妹** — Sibling terms
- **Digital Communication**:
  - **WeChat (微信)**: Dominant messaging
  - **QQ**: Older
  - **Douyin (抖音)**: TikTok equivalent
  - **Weibo (微博)**: Microblogging

## Language Connections

- `面子` (miànzi) — Face
- `关系` (guānxi) — Connections
- `老师` (lǎoshī) — Teacher (honorific)
- `先生/女士` (xiānshēng/nǚshì) — Mr./Ms.
- `长辈/晚辈` (zhǎngbèi/wǎnbèi) — Elder/junior
- `孝` (xiào) — Filial piety
- `微信` (wēixìn) — WeChat
