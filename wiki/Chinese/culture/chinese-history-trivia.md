# Chinese History Trivia

**Overview:** Chinese civilization (中华文明, Zhōnghuá wénmíng) spans 5,000+ years. Key trivia: the Spring and Autumn period (春秋战国), the Silk Road (丝绸之路), the four great inventions (四大发明), the Great Wall (长城), and the evolution of Chinese characters (汉字演变).

## Key Points

- **Spring and Autumn / Warring States (春秋战国, Chūnqiū Zhànguó, 770-221 BCE)**:
  - 春秋 (Chūnqiū): 770-476 BCE, named after Confucius's 《春秋》 chronicle
  - 战国 (Zhànguó): 475-221 BCE, seven major states (齐楚燕韩赵魏秦) vying for supremacy
  - 百家争鸣 (Bǎijiā zhēngmíng, "hundred schools of thought"): Confucianism (孔子), Daoism (老子), Legalism (韩非子), Mohism (墨子)
  - Ended by Qin Shi Huang (秦始皇) unification 221 BCE
- **Silk Road (丝绸之路, sīchóu zhī lù)**: 2nd century BCE trade routes connecting Chang'an (长安) to Rome. Goods: silk (丝绸), tea, porcelain, jade. 21st-century revival: 一带一路 (One Belt, One Road, 2013) — BRI.
- **Four great inventions (四大发明, sì dà fā míng)**:
  - 造纸术 (zàozhǐshù) — paper, attributed to 蔡伦 (Cai Lun, 105 CE)
  - 印刷术 (yìnshuāshù) — woodblock printing, later movable type (毕昇, 1040 CE)
  - 火药 (huǒyào) — gunpowder, 9th century CE (Tang dynasty)
  - 指南针 (zhǐnánzhēn) — compass, Han dynasty (4th century BCE lodestone spoon)
- **Great Wall (长城, Chángchéng)**: Built/rebuilt across many dynasties (Qi, Qin, Han, Ming). Most famous = Ming Wall (1368-1644). ~21,196 km total (UNESCO 2012). Started 春秋 (Qi state) + completed 秦始皇 (Qin Shi Huang, 221 BCE).
- **Chinese character evolution (汉字演变, hànzì yǎnbiàn)**:
  - 甲骨文 (jiǎgǔwén) — oracle bone script, ~1250-1046 BCE
  - 金文 (jīnwén) — bronze inscriptions, ~1046-256 BCE
  - 篆书 (zhuànshū) — seal script, Qin dynasty
  - 隶书 (lìshū) — clerical script, Han dynasty
  - 楷书 (kǎishū) — regular script, Tang dynasty (still standard)
  - 简体字 (jiǎntǐzì) — simplified, PRC 1956
  - 繁體字 (fántǐzì) — traditional, Taiwan/HK
- **Imperial dynasties (朝代, cháodài)**: 夏/商/周/秦/汉/三国/晋/南北朝/隋/唐/宋/元/明清 — 24 official dynasties before 1912 Republic.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: 한국 고조선 (BC 2333) — 중국 漢 (Han) 와 동일 시기. 한국 漢字 (한자) 수입 시기는 한漢 — 甲骨文 와 무관. 한국 世宗大王 (1446) 한글 창제 vs 중국 简体字 (1956) — 둘 다 문자 간소화.
- **Japonés**: 일본 弥生 시대 (300 BCE) — 중국 战国 후기 + 漢 초기 동시기. 일본 漢字 (漢字) = 중국 漢字 직접 수입. 일본 遣隋使 (607) — 율령/한자/불교 수입.
- **Inglés**: Roman Empire (West) = none; Han dynasty (China) = 同期 200 BCE - 200 CE. Silk Road connected Rome (West) and Han (East). Christopher Columbus (1492) vs 鄭和 (Zhèng Hé, 1405-1433) — centuries earlier Chinese maritime voyages.

## Ejemplos

«四大发明是造纸术、印刷术、火药和指南针.»
"Sì dà fā míng shì zàozhǐshù, yìnshuāshù, huǒyào hé zhǐnánzhēn."
"The four great inventions are paper-making, printing, gunpowder, and the compass."

«长城是世界上最长的墙,总长超过两万一千公里.»
"Chángchéng shì shìjiè shàng zuì cháng de qiáng, zǒngcháng chāoguò liǎngwàn yìqiān gōnglǐ."
"The Great Wall is the world's longest wall. Its total length exceeds 21,000 kilometers."

«丝绸之路连接了中国和罗马帝国.»
"Sīchóu zhī lù liánjié le Zhōngguó hé Luómǎ Dìguó."
"The Silk Road connected China and the Roman Empire."
