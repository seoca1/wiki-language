# Chinese Pop Culture

**Overview:** Chinese pop culture (流行文化, liúxíng wénhuà) is a global force. Distinct forms: C-drama (中剧/电视剧), web novels (网络文学/网文), ancient-style music (古风), short-video platforms (抖音 Douyin), and 流量明星 (traffic stars) with massive online fanbases.

## Key Points

- **Gu feng (古风, gǔfēng)**: "Ancient style" — music, fashion, and aesthetic blending Han dynasty / Tang dynasty motifs. Sang in polyphonic 戏腔 (xìqiāng, opera-style) voice. Artists: 周杰伦 (early 2000s), 银临, 三无Marblue. Revival driven by young female audiences (Z世代).
- **Web novels (网络文学, wǎngluò wénxué)**: Online serialized fiction. Started 2003 with 起点中文网 (Qidian). Genres: 玄幻 (xuanhuan), 仙侠 (xianxia), 都市 (urban), 修真 (cultivation). 阅文集团 (China Literature) listed HK 2017. Translated globally by Wuxiaworld (200+ MTL chapters daily).
- **C-drama (中剧, Zhōngjù / 电视剧, diànshìjù)**: TV dramas. Sub-genres: 古装剧 (historical), 现代剧 (modern), 甜宠 (sweet pet), 耽改 (BL adaptation). Recent global hits: 琅琊榜 (Nirvana in Fire, 2015), 长安十二时辰 (The Longest Day in Chang'an, 2019), 庆余年 (Joy of Life, 2019).
- **Douyin (抖音, Dǒuyīn)**: ByteDance's domestic short-video app (2016). TikTok is the international version. ~750 million MAU in China. Driving #1 trending music, viral dances, livestream 带货 (huòdài, sales).
- **Fan culture (饭圈, fànquān)**: 流量明星 (liúliàng míngxīng, "traffic stars") — celebrities whose value is measured in social-media engagement. 蔡徐坤, 王一博, 易烊千玺. Fan-clubs (粉丝团) organize mass voting, LED ads, charity donations.
- **Idol survival shows**: 创造营 (Produce Camp), 青春有你 (Youth With You) — popularized by Chinese adaptations after Korean 원격 example.
- **Gaming**: 王者荣耀 (Honor of Kings, 2015) — most popular mobile MOBA globally. 原神 (Genshin Impact, 2020) — multi-region hit. Milestone: first Chinese game to cross $1B mobile revenue.
- **Sports / music crossovers**: Rap of China (中国新说唱, 2017+), Street Dance of China (这就是街舞). Idol-stage formats.

## Cross-language Connections

- **Coreano**: K-pop (韩国 아이돌) vs 中国 流量明星 — 같은 idol 산업, 다른 시장. 한국 电视剧 (K-drama) vs 중국 电视剧 (C-drama) — 둘 다 글로벌 수출. 한국 综艺 vs 중국 网綜.
- **Japonés**: 일본 アニメ (anime) vs 중국 动画 (donghua) — 둘 다 동아시아 만화 토대. 일본 ライトノベル (light novel) vs 중국 网络小说 (wǎngluò xiǎoshuō) — 모두 라이트판타지.
- **Inglés**: Western Netflix originals vs Chinese iQiyi/Youku originals. TikTok global vs Douyin domestic. K-pop training system → China idol training 模式.

## Ejemplos

«他在抖音上看到了一个很有趣的短视频,马上分享到朋友圈.»
"Tā zài Dǒuyīn shàng kàndào le yī gè hěn yǒuqù de duǎnshìpín, mǎshàng fēnxiǎng dào péngyou quān."
"He saw an interesting short video on Douyin and immediately shared it to his WeChat Moments."

«最近她在追一部很火的古装剧,每天晚上都等着更新.»
"Zuìjìn tā zài zhuī yī bù hěn huǒ de gǔzhuāng jù, měitiān wǎnshang dōu děngzhe gēngxīn."
"Recently she's been binge-watching a hit historical drama. She waits for new episodes every night."

«我喜欢看仙侠小说,主角会经历很多奇遇.»
"Wǒ xǐhuan kàn xiānxiá xiǎoshuō, zhǔjué huì jīnglì hěn duō qíyù."
"I like reading xianxia novels. The protagonist experiences many adventures."
