# Travel Basics — Source Material

**Type:** Curriculum reference
**Theme:** Travel & Tourism
**Target:** A1-A2 English learners

## Overview

This document is a curated reference of essential travel vocabulary for English-language learners. Sources include:

- Oxford 3000 (CEFR A1-B2 word list)
- Cambridge English Vocabulary in Use (Travel)
- BBC Learning English — Travel section
- EF Standard English Test travel prep

## Categories

### 1. Airport & Flight

- Documents: passport, ticket, boarding pass
- Luggage: suitcase, carry-on, baggage
- Locations: gate, terminal, runway
- Actions: check in, board, land, take off, arrive, depart

### 2. Accommodation

- Booking: reservation, book, confirm, cancel
- Room types: single, double, twin, suite
- Hotel areas: lobby, reception, elevator
- Actions: check in, check out, stay

### 3. Dining

- Ordering: menu, order, recommend
- Service: waiter, waitress, bill, tip
- Courses: appetizer, main course, dessert, drink

### 4. Transportation

- Public: subway, metro, bus, train, taxi
- Walking: walk, cross, turn
- Ticketing: ticket, transfer, platform

### 5. Sightseeing

- Places: museum, monument, park, beach
- Activities: visit, explore, photograph
- Help: guide, tour, map

### 6. Directions

- Asking: Where is...?, How do I get to...?
- Instructions: straight, left, right, next to

## Useful Phrases (CEFR A1-A2)

- Excuse me / Pardon me
- How much does this cost?
- Where is the bathroom?
- Could you help me?
- I don't understand.
