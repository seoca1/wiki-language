## Context

영어 학습 관점에서, 한국인이 일본 여행 시 만나는 상황을 영어로 어떻게 표현하는지 정리한 자료. 한국어 원어민이 일본에 가서 영어로 소통하는 시나리오를 다룬다 (일본어는 영어 통역이 가능한 경우가 많음).

일부 일본어 단어는 한자를 한국인이 읽을 수 있어 학습 효율이 높음.

## Day 1: At the Airport

### Vocabulary

| English | Note | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| airport | 공항 | "Where is the airport?" |
| passport | 여권 | "May I see your passport?" |
| immigration | 입국심사 | "Please go to immigration." |
| customs | 세관 | "Customs declaration form." |
| luggage / baggage | 짐 | "Check your luggage." |
| baggage claim | 수하물 찾는 곳 | "Baggage claim is on the left." |
| exit | 출구 | "Exit is over there." |
| information desk | 안내소 | "Information desk, please." |
| currency exchange | 환전 | "Where is currency exchange?" |

### Phrases

- **Excuse me, where is the airport?** — 실례합니다, 공항은 어디인가요?
- **Where is immigration?** — 입국심사는 어디인가요?
- **How much is it?** — 얼마인가요?
- **Thank you very much** — 정말 감사합니다
- **I don't speak Japanese** — 일본어를 못합니다

## Cultural Notes (여행 매너)

### 1. No Tipping
Tipping is not customary in Japan. It can even be considered rude.

### 2. Remove Shoes
In homes, traditional restaurants (座敷/zashiki), and some temples/shrines, remove your shoes.

### 3. Chopstick Etiquette
- Don't stick chopsticks upright in rice (resembles funeral ritual)
- Don't pass food chopstick-to-chopstick
- Lift soup bowls with one hand when drinking

### 4. Photo Etiquette
Museums often prohibit photography. Shrines/temples have limited photography zones.

### 5. Onsen (Hot Spring) Etiquette
- Don't dip your towel in the water
- Don't put your hair in the water
- Wash thoroughly before entering the bath

## Learning Tips

1. **Use Kanji when possible**: Many words are Sino-Korean, so you can guess meanings
2. **Polite form first**: Always use です/ます when unsure
3. **Memorize numbers**: 1-10, prices, time, dates
4. **Shorten syllables**: Some phrases can be very short ("駅" = "えき")
5. **Polite expressions**: すみません works for both "excuse me" and "thank you"
