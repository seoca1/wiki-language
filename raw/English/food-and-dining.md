## 8. Essential Phrases

- "I'm hungry" — 배고파요
- "I'm thirsty" — 목말라요
- "I'm full" — 배불러요
- "Can I see the menu?" — 메뉴판 보여주세요
- "The check, please" — 계산서 주세요
- "It's delicious!" — 맛있어요!
- "I'm vegetarian" — 저는 채식주의자예요
- "I'm allergic to nuts" — 견과류 알레르기 있어요

## 9. Cultural Notes

- Tipping: 15-20% standard in US, included in UK/EU
- Fast food culture: very different from European sit-down dining
- Coffee: drip (US) vs espresso (Europe) — order differently
- "Grab a bite" = quick meal, often fast food
- Brunch: late morning (10am-1pm) — popular weekend meal
- Restaurant reservation: required in upscale, not in casual
